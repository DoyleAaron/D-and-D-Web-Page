# The Graveyard Arcana

## Classification

Ancient magical cemetery and restricted research site

## Overview

**The Graveyard Arcana** is a walled burial ground containing generations of Kluimont mages, navigators, artificers, and unidentified dead from the kingdom's early formation. It lies close to the old island routes but is screened by low hills and permanent silver mist.

Unlike an ordinary cemetery, the Graveyard was designed to preserve traces of magical knowledge alongside the dead. Memorial stones record spell diagrams, unfinished theories, oaths, warnings, and fragments of personal research. Centuries of overlapping enchantment have saturated the ground.

## Geography & Conditions

- Black and pale stone markers stand in concentric, irregular fields.
- Silver mist persists even in wind and bright sun.
- Spoken spells may echo, weaken, or briefly reproduce an unrelated magical effect.
- Compasses turn toward particular graves according to unknown patterns.
- The outer wall contains only one recognised gate, watched by Kluimont custodians.

## History

The cemetery predates the current Arcane Synod. Early wizard councils feared that burying powerful practitioners in ordinary ground would waste knowledge or allow dangerous remains to be stolen. They established the Graveyard as both memorial and controlled archive.

Later generations added wards, classification systems, and sealed vaults. Records were repeatedly reorganised or hidden during political changes, leaving parts of the site poorly understood even by its custodians.

## Access

- Families may visit approved memorial fields on designated days.
- Scholars require sponsorship, a declared research purpose, and supervision.
- Removing soil, stone, remains, or written fragments is prohibited.
- Certain inner sections are closed even to most Synod members.
- Unauthorised entry is treated as both grave robbery and theft of state arcana.

## Known Features

- **The Echo Fields:** Public memorial area where minor magical traces sometimes repeat.
- **The Navigator Stones:** Graves aligned to vanished stars and old sea routes.
- **The Sealed Faculty:** Inner burial vaults of early arcane rulers.
- **The Unnamed Ring:** Markers whose inscriptions were deliberately removed.
- **The Custodian House:** Archive, gatehouse, and residence of the site's wardens.

## Reputation

Kluimonters treat the Graveyard with a mixture of reverence and professional caution. Foreign stories exaggerate it into a city of undead scholars, while officials insist it is simply a secure cemetery affected by harmless residual magic. Neither description fully accounts for the restrictions.

## DM Notes

- This page intentionally leaves the inner vaults undefined.
- The site can support historical research, dangerous magical residue, political secrets, or genuine undead later without requiring any of them now.
