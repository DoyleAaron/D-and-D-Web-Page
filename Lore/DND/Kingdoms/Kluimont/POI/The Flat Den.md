# The Flat Den

## Classification

Abandoned dragon den and collapsed nesting ground

## Overview

**The Flat Den** is a broad, low cave complex beneath an unusually level shelf of stone on Kluimont's mainland. The entrance is difficult to see from a distance because the overhang follows the surrounding ground rather than rising like a normal hill.

The caverns were once occupied by a mature dragon and several fledglings. The remains of the young dragons still lie within. The adult is absent, and no reliable account explains whether she died elsewhere, abandoned the nest, or continues to watch it from another refuge.

## Geography & Conditions

- The den begins with low, wide chambers worn smooth by a large creature repeatedly entering and turning.
- Claw marks, heat-darkened stone, shed scales, and fused mineral patches remain throughout the complex.
- Several passages have collapsed, leaving pockets of stale air and unstable ceilings.
- Rainwater gathers in shallow reflective pools across the lowest floor.
- Animals avoid the immediate area even though the den appears empty.

## The Fledgling Remains

The bones of multiple young dragons are distributed between separate nesting hollows. Some are nearly complete; others were disturbed long ago by scavengers or treasure seekers. Damage to the stone suggests violence occurred inside the den, but the surviving evidence does not reveal who or what caused it.

Kluimont scholars restrict removal of bones, scales, or eggshell fragments. This protection is officially ethical and academic, though demand for dragon-derived materials makes the site a regular target for illegal collectors.

## History & Reputation

Mainland communities knew of the den before Kluimont established modern borders and avoided the flat ridge except when leaving offerings. Later expeditions confirmed the fledgling remains but found no adult corpse.

The site's name comes from surveyors who expected a hill or mountain above a dragon lair and instead found an almost perfectly level stone shelf.

## Visible Features

- **The Low Mouth:** Wide entrance only a few feet high at its outer edge.
- **The Turning Floor:** Smooth central chamber scored by immense claws.
- **The Nest Hollows:** Separate chambers containing fledgling remains.
- **The Fallen Gallery:** Major collapse blocking the deepest mapped route.
- **The Watching Ledge:** Exterior shelf with an unobstructed view across the surrounding country.

## Unanswered Questions

- What killed the fledglings?
- Why is the mother's body absent?
- Did she leave willingly, flee an attacker, or move deeper underground?
- Why do some marks in the den appear substantially newer than the bones?

## DM Notes

- The adult dragon's fate is intentionally unresolved.
- The blocked gallery can remain a simple collapse or become evidence that the mother still uses a deeper entrance later.
