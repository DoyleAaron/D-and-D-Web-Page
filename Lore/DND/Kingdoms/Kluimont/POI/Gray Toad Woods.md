# Gray Toad Woods

## Classification

Dense lowland forest and goblin-inhabited wet woodland

## Overview

**Gray Toad Woods** spreads across wet ground on Kluimont's eastern mainland. Pale-barked trees, stagnant pools, curtains of moss, and enormous populations of gray toads give the forest its name.

The woods are inhabited by goblin families, wandering fey, petty spirits, alchemists, smugglers, and other mischievous or secretive creatures. Most encounters are inconvenient rather than deadly, but travellers who treat the forest as harmless are quickly corrected.

## Geography & Conditions

- Shallow pools and mud conceal the true depth of the forest floor.
- Raised roots form natural paths that disappear after heavy rain.
- Gray toads range from thumb-sized animals to specimens large enough to threaten livestock.
- Fog collects beneath the canopy and carries sound in misleading directions.
- Insects, spores, irritating sap, and minor magical effects make unprepared camps uncomfortable.

## Inhabitants

- Goblin settlements are built on timber platforms, root islands, and hidden dry ground.
- Small fey bargain for food, buttons, songs, secrets, or promises that sound less important than they are.
- Alchemists gather toad secretions, fungi, moss, and mineral-rich water.
- Smugglers use paths marked in ways only regular travellers recognise.
- Larger predators follow the abundant small life into the woods.

## Economy

Gray toad secretions are used in antitoxins, dyes, hallucinogens, paralytic compounds, and arcane inks. Licensed gatherers work from the forest edge, while independent collectors negotiate directly with goblin communities.

Kluimont taxes legal ingredients but makes little effort to control the entire woodland. This leaves room for both profitable cooperation and a substantial black market.

## Customs for Travellers

- Do not kill a marked toad; goblin families use paint and thread to identify valuable breeding stock.
- Leave a small food offering when using a dry platform that was already prepared.
- Never follow lights that move against the wind.
- If a path marker laughs, turn it upright before continuing.
- Agreements made with forest residents should be stated precisely.

## Known Features

- **The Croaking Mere:** Largest wetland clearing and breeding ground for giant gray toads.
- **Rootstep Paths:** Elevated routes maintained by local goblins.
- **The Pale Still:** Alchemical collection site where mineral water rises through white clay.
- **Laughing Marker:** Network of minor enchanted signs that redirect or mock travellers.
- **Drytree Market:** Irregular trading ground used by goblins, alchemists, and smugglers.

## Reputation

Mainland Kluimonters regard Gray Toad Woods as useful, irritating, and best entered with a local agreement. Foreigners often mistake the goblin presence for simple lawlessness, overlooking the detailed customs that keep gatherers and residents from constantly fighting.
