# Erymont

## Classification

Hostile fortified camp and unofficial island settlement

## Population

Approximately **700 permanent inhabitants**, fluctuating as raiding groups arrive or depart

## Overview

**Erymont** occupies a small, isolated Rimduff island claimed by Kluimont on maps but effectively outside its law. The settlement is a reinforced tribal camp inhabited primarily by ogres, goblins, orcs, and other groups driven from more controlled ports.

Erymont is not a haven for ordinary outcasts. Its dominant warbands are openly predatory, ruthlessly territorial, and hostile to almost anyone who reaches the island without established protection.

## Population & Culture

- Ogres form the physical centre of the strongest warbands.
- Goblin families maintain traps, scavenging operations, crude workshops, and lookout systems.
- Orcs, bugbears, and other inhabitants join individual bands according to strength, kinship, or survival.
- There is no single lasting ruler. Authority belongs to whichever chiefs can hold the central enclosure and command enough fighters.
- Law consists of personal allegiance, immediate retaliation, and customs enforced by violence.

## Geography & Defences

- Steep rock, rough surf, and narrow landing places make approach difficult.
- Timber walls, sharpened stakes, scavenged ship parts, and stone-filled barricades reinforce the central camp.
- Lookouts maintain smoke signals and crude throwing engines above the safest beach.
- Traps cover likely paths from the shore.
- Captured timber is too poor and local shipbuilding too crude to support serious vessels.

## Boats & Travel

Erymont has no proper fleet. Residents use leaking fishing boats, rough rafts, stolen dinghies, and badly repaired hulls for short coastal journeys. Most cannot safely cross open water in poor weather.

This limitation is one reason neighbouring powers leave Erymont alone: its inhabitants are dangerous on their island but possess limited ability to project that danger elsewhere.

## Reputation

Sailors avoid the island completely. Crews forced ashore risk capture, torture, execution, or being eaten by the camp's more brutal inhabitants. Warning marks on regional charts are treated more seriously than Kluimont's formal claim.

Kluimont has considered clearing Erymont more than once. Officials repeatedly conclude that an assault would cost ships and soldiers while gaining an island of little strategic or economic value.

## Visible Features

- **The Stake Harbour:** Narrow landing enclosed by crude defensive works.
- **The High Camp:** Main fortified enclosure built from timber, stone, bone, and shipwreck material.
- **The Smoke Ridge:** Lookout line visible from surrounding channels.
- **The Broken Fleet:** Collection of barely seaworthy boats dragged above the tide.
- **The Feast Pit:** Central gathering and intimidation ground used by dominant warbands.

## DM Notes

- Erymont should remain dangerous without requiring a unified villain or hidden sophistication.
- Individual inhabitants may be less cruel than the ruling warbands, but outsiders have no safe way to identify them on arrival.
