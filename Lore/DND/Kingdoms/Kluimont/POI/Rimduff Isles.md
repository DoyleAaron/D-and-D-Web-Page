# Rimduff Isles

## Classification

Island chain separating Kluimont's mainland from its capital island

## Overview

The **Rimduff Isles** are a broken stretch of inhabited and uninhabited islands between mainland Kluimont and the island holding Qrey and Kluimont City. Reefs, narrow channels, fog banks, and local currents make the chain both a defensive barrier and a natural home for sailors who prefer distance from central authority.

The isles are claimed by Kluimont, but control varies by harbour. Some communities cooperate closely with Qrey, some preserve practical autonomy, and Tarora functions as an illegal pirate refuge under Captain GreyHorse.

Older charts sometimes spell the name **Rimdiff**. Both forms refer to the same island chain.

## Major Settled Islands

- **The Capital Island:** Holds Kluimont City, Qrey, the ruins of Lloy, and Tempawa Forest.
- **Tarora's Island:** A small island almost entirely covered by the pirate town of Tarora.
- **Aresphia and Kilson's Island:** A fertile island with a harbour village, inland farms, and a major temple to Valkur.
- **Yuosburn and Ukrolis's Island:** The second-largest island, divided by dense pathless forest and opposing strategic outlooks.
- **Erymont:** A smaller isolated island occupied by a hostile fortified tribal camp.

## Navigation

- Reef channels change with storms and volcanic sediment.
- Local pilots use tide colour, bird movement, and partially submerged markers rather than charts alone.
- Qrey maintains approved routes for whitelisted vessels approaching the capital island.
- Pirate crews maintain separate paths through shallower water and may move navigation markers to mislead pursuers.
- Sudden magical weather associated with Tempawa Forest can make otherwise safe channels impassable.

## Economy

- Fishing, pilotage, ship repair, smuggling, piracy, shrine travel, and naval contracts sustain the islands.
- Legitimate vessels stop at Aresphia for food and at Yuosburn for military clearance or current route information.
- Tarora sells services to crews avoiding official ports.
- Kluimont collects fees where practical and accepts informal arrangements where direct rule would cost more than it returns.

## Culture

Rimduff islanders identify strongly with their specific harbour before the wider kingdom. Seamanship is a basic life skill, hospitality is governed by local custom, and mainland laws are often treated as suggestions until backed by a ship or official payment.

Bagi's old lighthouse laws still influence many communities:

1. Keep sworn agreements.
2. Charge fair tolls for safe passage.
3. Give food and shelter to sailors in genuine distress.

Pirates do not always obey these rules, but even they understand the consequences of breaking them openly.

## Security & Politics

- Qrey protects the approved route to the capital and monitors every major vessel.
- Eimgend's navy patrols the wider channels.
- Yuosburn hires privateers to watch Islefield-facing waters.
- Tarora shelters illegal crews but suppresses violence that would invite occupation.
- Smaller settlements survive by remaining useful, neutral, or difficult to reach.

## Reputation

Foreign sailors call the Rimduff Isles beautiful, profitable, and exhausting. A crew can find a safe meal, a stolen cargo, a naval inspection, and a reef capable of sinking them all within the same day's travel.
