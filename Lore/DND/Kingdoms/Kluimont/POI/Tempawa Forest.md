# Tempawa Forest

## Classification

Ancient warded forest on Kluimont's capital island

## Overview

**Tempawa Forest** covers much of the western capital island. Its old-growth canopy, copper-green clearings, standing stones, and unreliable paths separate Qrey's controlled coast from the most secretive parts of Kluimont's interior.

The forest is the first known home in Kluimont of **[Bagi, the Brass Tempest](../../../Character%20Lore/Beasts%20&%20Creatures/Bagi%20the%20Brass%20Tempest.md)**. Long before modern borders, Bagi used the surrounding waters and woodland clearings while enforcing his customs of honest tolls, kept oaths, and hospitality for sailors in distress.

## Geography & Conditions

- Ancient trees form a dense western belt broken by rocky clearings and shallow streams.
- Metallic moss, green-stained bark, and warm gusts occur even when the wider island is cool.
- Paths shift through illusion, deliberate warding, or subtle changes in the forest itself.
- Compasses drift, clocks lose time, and spoken names sometimes echo from distant stones.
- Entry is prohibited without high-level authority from Qrey or Kluimont City.

## Reputation

Kluimont officially attributes the forest's strange behaviour to **fey acoustics**, unstable ley lines, and sensitive national warding infrastructure. Foreign sailors instead tell stories about a captive brass dragon whose breathing changes the weather.

Residents of Qrey generally avoid discussing the subject. Storm damage in 1220 made complete denial impossible, but public records remain deliberately vague.

## History

- Bagi used Tempawa as a refuge while protecting western sea lanes before Kluimont fully formed.
- As Kluimont's arcane councils grew more secretive, they studied the dragon, the forest, and their shared magical patterns.
- Around 1101 C.C., the Arcane Synod bound Bagi through meteoric standing stones, clockwork rituals, and controlled names.
- The binding fed a national ward grid and helped accelerate Kluimont's arcane development.
- In 1220 C.C., Bagi partially disrupted the system, causing the three-night Storm of Unbound Chains and the destruction of Lloy.

## Known Features

- **The Meteoric Stones:** Restricted standing stones forming part of the binding network.
- **The Copper Clearings:** Areas where plants and exposed rock develop green metallic stains overnight.
- **The Oath Paths:** Old routes where travellers traditionally state their purpose and offer food before proceeding.
- **The Storm Scar:** Forest damaged during Bagi's 1220 attempt to break the grid.
- **The Quiet Perimeter:** Heavily patrolled outer zone where sound and magic are monitored.

## DM Notes

- Bagi remains bound within Tempawa Forest and is still working against the system.
- The forest's odd behaviour comes from a mixture of Bagi's magic, the Synod's ward grid, and older natural magic.
- Kluimont's prohibition protects the binding site, not merely dangerous terrain.
