# Lloy - Destroyed

### **Kingdom:**

Kluimont

### **Settlement Type:**

Destroyed Village / Military Training Ground

### **Administration:**

The abandoned coast is controlled by Qrey's island wardens and scheduled for use by Kluimont military units.

### **Population:**

**0 permanent residents** (approximately 1,100 before its destruction)

### **Wealth:**

☆☆☆☆☆ (0 stars)

- No civilian economy remains.
- Salvage is forbidden without military authority, and useful stone has already been incorporated into training structures.

### **Military:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars, current use)

- The ruins serve as a live training ground for urban assaults, evacuation drills, ward failures, and coastal landings.
- Qrey units rotate through temporary camps rather than maintaining a permanent garrison.
- Warning markers and unstable magical residue make unscheduled entry dangerous.

### **Happiness:**

Not applicable.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Lloy lies on the eastern coast of Kluimont's capital island, across difficult ground from Qrey and the capital road.
- Ruined stone houses descend toward a shattered harbour and a beach littered with fused sand.
- Sea wind passes directly through the empty streets, and storms arrive with little shelter.
- Vegetation has returned unevenly. Some blocks are overgrown while others remain barren around damaged ward stones.

### **History:**

- Lloy was a modest fishing and supply village serving the eastern coast of the capital island.
- In 1220 C.C., Bagi the Brass Tempest's struggle against Kluimont's binding grid produced the **Storm of Unbound Chains**.
- A concentrated surge struck Lloy. Wind, lightning, and failing ward magic destroyed the harbour and most buildings before evacuation could be completed.
- Kluimont closed the site, relocated survivors, and later converted the ruins into a military exercise ground.
- Official histories describe the destruction as an arcane weather disaster. Survivors and sailors generally name Bagi directly.

### **Visible Features:**

- **The Fused Quay:** Harbour stones melted together by magical lightning.
- **The Empty Bell:** A warning bell that survived although its tower collapsed.
- **The Practice Streets:** Stabilised ruins used for military exercises.
- **The Ward Crater:** Restricted impact site surrounded by copper posts.
- **The Names Wall:** Memorial maintained by relocated families from Lloy.

### **Relationships:**

- **Qrey:** Administers the ruins, training schedule, memorial access, and coastal exclusion zone.
- **Kluimont City:** Controls records concerning the failed ward grid and limits independent investigation.
- **Tempawa Forest:** The arcane disturbance that destroyed Lloy originated from the network tied to the forest.

## DM Notes

- Bagi did destroy Lloy during his attempt to break free, but the destruction was not a deliberate attack on the villagers. The settlement was caught where Kluimont's failing bindings redirected his storm.
- Military exercises also provide a convenient reason to keep civilians away from surviving ward components.
