>_All star ratings (Wealth, Military, Happiness) are in **perspective to other settlements of the same size category**._

# Yuosburn

### **Kingdom:**

Kluimont

### **Settlement Type:**

Village - military watch harbour

### **Government:**

A Watch-Reeve appointed by Qrey, advised by resident families and the commanders of contracted ships.

### **Population:**

**1,500 permanent residents**, plus rotating sailors, hired pirates, and mercenaries

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Human (37%): Officials, sailors, traders, and island families.
- Orc and Goblin (30%): Guards, crews, builders, and residents.
- Dwarf (8%): Fortification engineers and armorers.
- Halfling and Gnome (8%): Navigators, clerks, cooks, and signal technicians.
- Elf (5%): Interpreters and observers familiar with Islefield.
- Other (12%): Mercenaries, mixed families, and foreign specialists.

### **Wealth:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Qrey pays well for reconnaissance, signal duty, naval supplies, and information about Islefield shipping.
- Hired privateers spend freely while stationed in the village.
- Contracts enrich ship owners and officers more than ordinary households, but wages remain high by village standards.

### **Military:**

★★★★½ (4.5 stars)

- Yuosburn watches the Islefield-facing side of the Rimduff approaches and relays warnings toward Qrey.
- Kluimont hires pirates as scouts and deniable patrol crews, then stations disciplined mercenaries ashore to keep them under control.
- Signal towers, hidden anchorages, shore weapons, and fast cutters allow rapid response to rogue ships.

### **Happiness:**

★★★☆☆ (3 stars)

- Good wages and reliable supplies make Yuosburn materially comfortable.
- Constant surveillance, armed visitors, and underhanded dealings create distrust.
- Residents accept the arrangement because misconduct is punished quickly enough to keep daily life stable.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Yuosburn occupies the Islefield-facing shore of the second-largest Rimduff island after the capital island.
- The island is long and narrow, with a dense old forest dividing Yuosburn from Ukrolis on the opposite coast.
- No maintained path crosses the forest. Travel between the two villages is conducted by sea.
- Watchtowers stand above a sheltered harbour and command wide views of southern shipping lanes.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Military contracts, ship supply, fishing, signalling work, and intelligence payments dominate the economy.
- Civilian traders carry legal goods, while hired pirates quietly intercept smugglers or gather information beyond Kluimont waters.
- Captured contraband is officially catalogued, though a portion routinely disappears before reaching Qrey.
- The village imports most food and timber because the dividing forest is not logged extensively.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Southwatch:** Main signal tower facing Islefield's sea routes.
- **Contract Quay:** Dock where private captains receive sealed assignments and payment.
- **The Discipline Yard:** Mercenary compound used to restrain or punish hired crews that break local law.
- **The Forest Wall:** Dense interior treeline marked by warnings and old boundary stones.

### **Relationships:**

- **Qrey:** Direct military patron and recipient of Yuosburn's reports.
- **Ukrolis:** Shares the island but has no land connection; relations are distant and sometimes competitive.
- **Islefield:** Watched continuously as both trading neighbour and potential threat.
- **Tarora:** Useful source of deniable crews, although GreyHorse captains are never trusted completely.

## DM Notes

- Yuosburn's official patrols and hired pirates sometimes perform the same work under different flags.
- The mercenary garrison exists partly to punish privateers who mistake a Kluimont contract for immunity from Kluimont law.
