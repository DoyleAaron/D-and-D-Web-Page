# Kilson

### **Kingdom:**

Kluimont

### **Settlement Type:**

Village - inland temple settlement

### **Government:**

A temple council led by Valkur's senior wave-priests, with household elders managing farming and local disputes.

### **Population:**

**950** (the smaller and more isolated of the island's two settlements)

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Human (34%): Farmers, temple workers, and old island families.
- Orc and Goblin (33%): Farmers, builders, guards, and clergy.
- Halfling and Gnome (12%): Gardeners, craftspeople, and record keepers.
- Dwarf (8%): Stoneworkers and keepers of the temple cisterns.
- Other (13%): Mixed families and devotees who settled near the temple.

### **Wealth:**

★★½☆☆ (2.5 stars)

- Kilson relies on orchards, freshwater springs, temple visitors, carved devotional goods, and trade with Aresphia.
- The temple possesses considerable ceremonial wealth, but households live simply.
- Imported goods cost more because everything must travel through Aresphia and then inland.

### **Military:**

★★☆☆☆ (2 stars)

- The village has no harbour and little reason to maintain coastal weapons.
- Temple guardians and a household militia protect the road, springs, and sacred grounds.
- Isolation and the single approach from Aresphia provide the strongest defence.

### **Happiness:**

★★★½☆ (3.5 stars)

- Residents find stability in shared faith, productive land, and distance from the mainland.
- Outsiders are treated with suspicion and may remain only while respecting local religious rules.
- Open worship of deities other than Valkur is unwelcome, creating tension with otherwise peaceful visitors.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Kilson lies inland among low hills, springs, and dense green fields.
- It has no docks. The only dependable route follows the road from Aresphia.
- Rainwater and natural springs feed stone cisterns beneath the temple complex.
- Sea storms are heard but partly broken by the island ridge, giving Kilson calmer weather than the coast.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Fruit, herbs, freshwater, woven sailcloth, and temple carvings are sent to Aresphia.
- Imported metal, salt, fish, and manufactured goods travel back along the same road.
- Pilgrims contribute labour or supplies rather than simply paying coin.
- The village limits commercial expansion to protect its water and religious character.

### **Faith & Culture:**

- Kilson contains one of Dayner's largest temples to **Valkur**, god of sailors, courage, ships, and favourable winds.
- Worship focuses on the belief that every safe voyage begins with clean water carried from the island's springs.
- Other deities may be acknowledged privately, but public preaching, symbols, or competing shrines are strongly discouraged.
- Residents consider Aresphia's multi-faith tolerance well-intentioned but spiritually careless.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Temple of the First Wind:** Large hilltop sanctuary visible from Aresphia's bay.
- **The Seven Cisterns:** Sacred freshwater reservoirs maintained beneath the temple.
- **The Inland Bell:** Warns of fire, strangers, or ships bringing urgent news from Aresphia.
- **The Quiet Orchard:** Communal food source and burial ground for senior priests.

### **Relationships:**

- **Aresphia:** Essential trade partner and the village's only access to the sea.
- **Qrey:** Recognises the temple's influence but monitors large religious gatherings.
- **Foreign Sailors:** Welcomed as pilgrims only when they accept Valkur's rites and restrictions.
