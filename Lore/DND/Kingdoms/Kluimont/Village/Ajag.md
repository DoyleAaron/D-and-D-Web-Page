# Ajag

### **Kingdom:**

Kluimont

### **Settlement Type:**

Village - inland lake port

### **Government:**

A Dock Elder and circle of boat-owning families, with shrine keepers holding unusual influence over local decisions.

### **Population:**

**1,100**

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Orc and Goblin (39%): Boat crews, fishers, craftspeople, and old lakeside families.
- Human (33%): Traders, farmers, shrine keepers, and residents.
- Halfling and Gnome (10%): Boat builders, cooks, and shopkeepers.
- Dwarf (7%): Masons, smiths, and lock engineers.
- Other (11%): Tieflings, mixed families, and devotees drawn to local shrines.

### **Wealth:**

★★★☆☆ (3 stars)

- Ajag transfers cargo between lake boats, river barges, and roads leading toward Sord.
- Fishing, reeds, dyes, freshwater shell, and boat repair support the local economy.
- Shrine offerings and discreet religious visitors provide income that residents rarely discuss with outsiders.

### **Military:**

★★½☆☆ (2.5 stars)

- A harbour militia patrols the lake and can close the river mouth with chained booms.
- Marshy shores and local knowledge make an unsupported land assault difficult.
- The village lacks the troops or walls to resist a determined regional power.

### **Happiness:**

★★★½☆ (3.5 stars)

- Food is reliable, work is steady, and the lake provides some distance from mainland disorder.
- Residents are tolerant of unusual beliefs but suspicious of anyone who mocks or investigates them.
- Flooding, river crime, and religious disputes occasionally disturb an otherwise stable community.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Ajag stands on a freshwater lake connected to the ocean by a navigable river.
- Docks extend into deep water while canals, reed beds, and wet fields surround the settlement.
- River traffic can reach the coast, though changing depth and hidden bars require Ajag pilots.
- Warm mist is common at dawn, and seasonal rain can raise the lake rapidly.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Lake fish, reeds, pigments, pottery clay, and river transport are the village's primary trades.
- Barges carry goods toward the coast while road caravans connect Ajag to Sord.
- Local pilots charge well for guiding unfamiliar captains through the river channel.
- Some boats travel at night for reasons that have little to do with ordinary commerce.

### **Faith & Culture:**

- Ajag supports shrines to river spirits, drowned ancestors, luck-bringers, and other powers viewed as dubious elsewhere.
- Worship of the **Drowned God** exists beside less dangerous traditions and is tolerated so long as no public crime can be proven.
- Offerings are commonly floated onto the lake in sealed clay bowls.
- Residents distinguish sharply between respecting dangerous powers and trusting them.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **Mistwater Port:** Main docks for lake and river traffic.
- **The Chain House:** Controls the defensive booms across the river mouth.
- **The Floating Shrines:** Small anchored platforms bearing offerings to multiple water powers.
- **Reedmarket:** Waterside market for fish, dyes, clay goods, and river cargo.

### **Relationships:**

- **Sord:** Principal road market and destination for Ajag's inland cargo.
- **Eimgend:** Receives selected river goods and monitors suspicious religious traffic.
- **Coastal Kluimont:** Connected by river, though larger ships cannot reach the lake.

## DM Notes

- Most shrine keepers are harmless traditionalists. A smaller Drowned God congregation is genuinely dangerous but does not control the whole village.
