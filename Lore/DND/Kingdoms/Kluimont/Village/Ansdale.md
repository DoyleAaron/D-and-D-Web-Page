# Ansdale

### **Kingdom:**

Kluimont

### **Settlement Type:**

Village - Islefield border crossing

### **Government:**

A Border Reeve chosen locally and confirmed by Eimgend, working alongside Kluimont customs officers.

### **Population:**

**1,600** (a large village sustained by border traffic)

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Orc and Goblin (34%): Guards, craftspeople, traders, and farming families.
- Human (33%): Merchants, officials, farmers, and migrants.
- Elf (11%): A comparatively visible minority because of the Sable crossing.
- Halfling and Gnome (8%): Innkeepers, clerks, cooks, and caravan workers.
- Dwarf (6%): Smiths, masons, and wagon repairers.
- Other (8%): Mixed families and travellers who settled at the border.

### **Wealth:**

★★★½☆ (3.5 stars)

- Inns, customs fees, stables, guides, interpreters, and caravan services profit from the crossing.
- Islefield goods command high prices farther inside Kluimont, giving local factors reliable margins.
- Wealth fluctuates sharply whenever either kingdom tightens its border.

### **Military:**

★★★½☆ (3.5 stars)

- The border gate, customs yard, and road approaches are fortified but not designed for a long siege.
- Mixed patrols specialise in searches, smuggling interdiction, and escorting travellers away from restricted routes.
- The village militia knows the surrounding valleys and can delay a larger force until help arrives.

### **Happiness:**

★★★☆☆ (3 stars)

- Residents are accustomed to outsiders and more socially flexible than many Kluimont communities.
- Constant searches, political suspicion, and sudden trade closures make life uncertain.
- Families take pride in understanding both kingdoms while fully belonging to neither culture.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Ansdale is the first Kluimont settlement reached when crossing from Sable on the Islefield side.
- The village occupies a defensible valley where the border road narrows between wooded slopes.
- Farms and grazing land spread behind the customs district, while inns cluster close to the gate.
- Rain and mist can reduce visibility, making local guides valuable beyond the maintained road.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Ansdale exchanges Kluimont reagents, lake goods, and crafts for Islefield food, timber products, cloth, and luxury wares.
- Translators and document brokers help travellers navigate both kingdoms' laws.
- Smuggling exists, but the village depends too heavily on legal trade to tolerate open criminal control.
- Caravans usually continue toward Sord after inspection.

### **History:**

- A roadside hamlet grew around the first formal checkpoint between northern Islefield and Kluimont's mainland.
- Repeated changes in border policy expanded the customs yards, guard barracks, and lodging districts.
- Refugees and mixed families from both sides gradually made Ansdale more diverse than its size suggests.
- The settlement has learned to survive tension by enforcing rules consistently while avoiding unnecessary insult.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Sable Gate:** Primary customs crossing from Islefield.
- **Two-Law Yard:** Inspection square where both kingdoms' permitted cargo rules are displayed.
- **The Valley Lantern:** Large border inn serving merchants, guards, and sponsored travellers.
- **The Northbound Shrine:** Small multi-faith sanctuary for those leaving Islefield territory.

### **Notable People:**

- **[Amdir Aeristel](../../../Character%20Lore/Player%20Characters/Amdir%20Aeristel%20%28Aaron%29.md):** Exiled member of Islefield's royal line who has resided in Ansdale.

### **Relationships:**

- **Sable:** Immediate neighbour across the border, though Sable's sacred restrictions limit ordinary contact.
- **Sord:** Main destination for cleared caravans entering Kluimont.
- **Eimgend:** Confirms appointments and receives customs revenue.
- **Islefield:** Source of trade, travellers, and political scrutiny.
