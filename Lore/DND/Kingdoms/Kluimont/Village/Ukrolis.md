# Ukrolis

### **Kingdom:**

Kluimont

### **Settlement Type:**

Village - northeastern border harbour

### **Government:**

A locally elected Harbour Elder supported by a small Kluimont border detachment.

### **Population:**

**1,350** (growth has remained slow because of repeated border fighting)

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Human (48%): A notably high proportion for Kluimont, including families with Braewood ancestry.
- Orc and Goblin (24%): Fishers, craftspeople, guards, and established island families.
- Dwarf (10%): Masons, smiths, and traders familiar with Braewood markets.
- Halfling and Gnome (7%): Shopkeepers, navigators, and boat builders.
- Elf (3%): Rare residents and visiting factors.
- Other (8%): Mixed families and maritime workers.

### **Wealth:**

★★½☆☆ (2.5 stars)

- Fishing, cautious border trade, boat repair, and salvage sustain the village.
- Repeated damage and uncertain shipping have discouraged major investment.
- Human families with cross-border contacts provide valuable access to Braewood goods.

### **Military:**

★★★½☆ (3.5 stars)

- Old earthworks, rebuilt watchtowers, and a trained household militia reflect generations of border tension.
- The official Kluimont detachment is small but experienced.
- Residents know where to shelter when fighting reaches the coast and can evacuate by boat quickly.

### **Happiness:**

★★★☆☆ (3 stars)

- Humans are more accepted here than in many Kluimont communities because constant contact has made cooperation necessary.
- Families are resilient and strongly attached to their rebuilt homes.
- Fear of another raid or political crisis limits long-term confidence.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Ukrolis occupies the Braewood-facing side of the same long island as Yuosburn.
- A dense, pathless forest divides the two settlements. Residents travel between them by sea rather than attempting the interior.
- The harbour faces rougher northern water and receives colder winds than Yuosburn.
- Burned foundations and abandoned earthworks remain visible beyond the current village edge.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Ukrolis trades fish, repaired boats, timber gathered from the forest edge, and local crafts.
- Imports from Braewood include grain, tools, iron, and household goods.
- Commerce is frequently interrupted by patrol changes, diplomatic disputes, and accusations of smuggling.
- Residents often act as interpreters and intermediaries for Kluimont officials dealing with Braewood crews.

### **History:**

- The village developed around a sheltered landing used by both islanders and mainland traders.
- Its location near Braewood waters brought repeated raids, retaliations, and occupations too small to enter major histories.
- Each rebuilding drew more human families from nearby coasts, producing the mixed community present today.
- Kluimont tolerates this cultural closeness because Ukrolis provides information and a stable point of contact.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Rebuilt Tower:** Watchtower reconstructed several times on the same foundations.
- **The Shared Chapel:** Multi-faith shrine used by Kluimont and Braewood families.
- **Northwater Quay:** Main trading dock and border inspection point.
- **The No-Road Marker:** Stone at the forest edge recording failed attempts to establish a path to Yuosburn.

### **Relationships:**

- **Yuosburn:** Shares the island but remains separated by forest and different strategic priorities.
- **Braewood:** Source of trade, ancestry, and recurring danger.
- **Qrey:** Values Ukrolis as an observation post but invests less here than in Yuosburn.
