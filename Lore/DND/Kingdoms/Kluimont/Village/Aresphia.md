# Aresphia

### **Kingdom:**

Kluimont

### **Settlement Type:**

Village - Rimduff island harbour

### **Government:**

A sailors' council elected by resident households, with customary responsibility for both Aresphia and the road toward Kilson.

### **Population:**

**1,250** (the larger of the two settlements on its island)

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Human (38%): Fishers, cooks, farmers, and sailors.
- Orc and Goblin (31%): Sailors, dockworkers, craftspeople, and island families.
- Halfling and Gnome (11%): Cooks, navigators, brewers, and shopkeepers.
- Dwarf (7%): Masons, metalworkers, and boat repairers.
- Other (13%): Mixed families and seafarers who settled after long voyages.

### **Wealth:**

★★★☆☆ (3 stars)

- Fishing, ship provisions, safe anchorage, and food sales provide steady income.
- Aresphian preserved fish, spiced stews, seaweed breads, and citrus sauces are valued by crews throughout the Rimduff routes.
- Wealth is modest but distributed more evenly than in most Kluimont ports.

### **Military:**

★★☆☆☆ (2 stars)

- A small but passionate sailing company patrols the approaches and rescues vessels in bad weather.
- The village avoids major disputes and relies on local pilots, reefs, and neutrality more than fortifications.
- Most adult sailors can support the militia when necessary.

### **Happiness:**

★★★★½ (4.5 stars)

- The village is close-knit, well-fed, and proud of staying clear of mainland politics and pirate feuds.
- Religious festivals and shared meals form the centre of community life.
- Isolation and dependence on the sea remain the principal hardships.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Aresphia occupies a sheltered coastal bay on an island shared only with Kilson.
- A maintained inland track climbs from the docks through low fields and wet woodland to Kilson.
- The island is larger and more fertile than Tarora's, supporting gardens, orchards, and grazing animals.
- Warm rain, strong tides, and seasonal fog shape local travel.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Aresphia provisions ships travelling between Islefield and Kluimont.
- Sailors generally remain neutral, carrying food and passengers while avoiding contraband that could draw Qrey's attention.
- Trade with Kilson exchanges imported goods and fish for inland produce, fresh water, and temple crafts.
- Visiting crews are expected to eat locally, respect harbour quiet, and make an offering for safe passage.

### **Faith & Culture:**

- Aresphia is devoted primarily to **Valkur**, patron of sailors, favourable winds, and courageous voyages.
- Small shrines to Selune and local sea spirits are tolerated beside Valkur's harbour chapel.
- Great meals are treated as both hospitality and worship; refusing food without explanation is considered discourteous.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **Valkur's Landing:** Harbour chapel where departing crews receive salt and blue cord.
- **The Long Table:** Roofed communal dining hall and meeting place.
- **Pilots' Steps:** Tide-marked stairs where local navigators wait for incoming vessels.
- **The Kilson Road:** The island's only maintained inland route.

### **Relationships:**

- **Kilson:** Closest neighbour and religious partner, though Aresphia is far more welcoming to outsiders.
- **Islefield:** Provides regular stopping traffic and imported produce.
- **Qrey:** Respects Aresphia's reliable pilots and generally leaves the village to govern itself.
- **Tarora:** Avoided when possible; pirates may eat and repair here only if they obey local peace rules.
