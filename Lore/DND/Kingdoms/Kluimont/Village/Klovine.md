# Klovine

### **Kingdom:**

Kluimont

### **Settlement Type:**

Village - Braewood border crossing

### **Government:**

A Fort Reeve appointed by mainland authorities and advised by resident traders, farmers, and militia households.

### **Population:**

**1,450**

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Orc and Goblin (38%): Farmers, guards, teamsters, craftspeople, and old frontier families.
- Human (36%): Traders, officials, migrants, and families with Braewood connections.
- Dwarf (11%): Armorers, miners, masons, and Mortling-linked merchants.
- Halfling and Gnome (6%): Innkeepers, repairers, and clerks.
- Elf (2%): Mostly passing officials or specialists.
- Other (7%): Mixed families and temporary workers.

### **Wealth:**

★★★☆☆ (3 stars)

- The village profits from customs fees, caravan repair, livestock, food sales, and trade with Mortling Stronghold.
- Military goods and worked metal are especially important imports.
- Border closures and Braewood-Kluimont disputes can reduce income immediately.

### **Military:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Klovine is the first Kluimont settlement reached from the Mortling side and is fortified accordingly.
- A stone gatehouse, ditch, signal tower, and veteran militia protect the crossing.
- Guards are trained to delay organised troops, inspect military cargo, and call support from Sord or Slonmore.

### **Happiness:**

★★★☆☆ (3 stars)

- Work is steady and the village receives better protection than most Kluimont mainland communities.
- Residents endure strict inspections and the constant possibility that a distant political dispute will become a local confrontation.
- Orc, goblin, human, and dwarf families cooperate pragmatically despite inherited suspicion.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Klovine stands where the road from Mortling Stronghold enters Kluimont's eastern mainland.
- Low ridges and broken woodland funnel travellers toward the fortified checkpoint.
- Farms occupy cleared ground behind the village, while defensive sight lines are kept open toward the border.
- Summers are warm and dusty; rain turns the heavily used road into deep mud.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Braewood metalwork, tools, military equipment, and preserved food enter Kluimont through Klovine.
- Kluimont exports reagents, timber products, dyes, and unusual crafted goods in return.
- Smithies, wheelwrights, inns, and feed yards serve almost every caravan.
- Official scrutiny is intense because Mortling is both a trade partner and major military stronghold.

### **History:**

- Klovine developed around a small fortified post watching the Mortling road.
- Border skirmishes repeatedly damaged the early settlement, leading residents to build homes behind the gate rather than along the open road.
- Trade eventually became more regular than fighting, but the defensive character remained.
- Kluimont's rulers invest here because losing Klovine would expose the roads toward Sord and the southern mainland.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **Mortling Gate:** Fortified eastern entrance and inspection point.
- **The Redoubt:** Compact stone refuge large enough to shelter the village during a raid.
- **Iron Row:** Smithies and repair yards serving military and merchant traffic.
- **The Measured Field:** Cleared border ground marked at bow range from the walls.

### **Relationships:**

- **Mortling Stronghold:** Principal foreign trading partner and primary military concern.
- **Sord:** Receives most traffic after it clears the border.
- **Slonmore:** Coordinates wider mainland defence and customs policy.
- **Braewood:** Relations alternate between profitable routine and sudden suspicion.
