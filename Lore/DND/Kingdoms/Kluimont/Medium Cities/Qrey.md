>_All star ratings (Wealth, Military, Happiness) are in **perspective to other settlements of the same size category**._

# Qrey

### **Kingdom:**

Kluimont

### **Settlement Type:**

Medium City - Kluimont's third-largest city

### **Government:**

A Harbour Magistry appointed by the High Archon, with the navy, customs houses, and island wardens sharing authority.

### **Population:**

**26,500** (the only settlement besides the capital on Kluimont's capital island)

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Human (42%): Merchants, officials, sailors, and established island families.
- Orc and Goblin (18%): Dock crews, shipwrights, guards, and trading families.
- Elf (8%): Arcane specialists and closely monitored foreign residents.
- Dwarf (7%): Harbour engineers, ward-stone masons, and metalworkers.
- Halfling and Gnome (8%): Navigators, artificers, provisioners, and clerks.
- Other (17%): Tieflings, dragonborn, elemental-touched families, and visiting scholars.

### **Wealth:**

★★★★★ (5 stars)

- Every legal visitor to the capital island passes through Qrey, allowing the city to control tariffs, lodging, licensed transport, and inspection fees.
- Old island families possess immense wealth from shipping, arcane patents, and contracts with Eimgend.
- Public streets, seawalls, gardens, and guild buildings are maintained to an exceptional standard, reinforcing the citizens' pride.

### **Military:**

★★★★★ (5 stars)

- Layered sea walls, arcane batteries, reef wards, patrol ships, and controlled channels guard the harbour.
- Only whitelisted vessels may approach the inner docks. Unknown ships are intercepted beyond bow range and turned away.
- Leaving Qrey for the capital requires a separate invitation, an assigned escort, and a sealed travel writ.
- The city's garrison can isolate every dock and road within minutes through gates and magical barriers.

### **Happiness:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Citizens are wealthy, secure, and intensely proud of being the capital island's gatekeepers.
- Restrictions that frustrate foreigners are treated locally as proof of Qrey's importance.
- Lower-status dockworkers and temporary residents experience far less freedom than chartered island families.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Qrey occupies the northernmost harbour of the capital island, where reefs and high cliffs create a naturally defensible entrance.
- The walled city rises in clean terraces from deep-water docks to ward towers and wealthy residential districts.
- Sea winds keep the air mild, though magical storms from Tempawa Forest sometimes reach the outskirts.
- A guarded inland road leads toward Kluimont City. No other legal route crosses the island interior.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Qrey handles elite passengers, restricted cargo, arcane components, diplomatic shipments, and nearly all supplies bound for the capital.
- It maintains especially close commercial and military ties with Eimgend, whose fleet escorts many approved vessels.
- Harbour guilds provide pilots who know the warded channels. Foreign captains may not navigate the final approach alone.
- Luxury lodging, bonded warehouses, banking, ship repair, and magical inspection support a prosperous service economy.

### **History:**

- Qrey began as a fortified harbour serving the arcane enclaves that eventually formed Kluimont City.
- As the capital became more secretive, Qrey developed into its controlled reception point and first defensive line.
- Bagi, the Brass Tempest, once protected nearby waters from slavers and oath-breakers, and old harbour law still reflects his rules concerning fair tolls and aid to distressed sailors.
- During the **Storm of Unbound Chains** in 1220 C.C., Captain Merys Klyndar directed evacuations as arcane weather scorched nearby fields and threatened the city's outer wards.
- Reconstruction strengthened both Qrey's fortifications and its insistence that unrestricted access is a national danger.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The White List Hall:** Customs office where ships, crews, sponsors, and cargo are approved before arrival.
- **The Seven Sea Gates:** Layered harbour barriers combining chains, stone towers, and arcane seals.
- **The Invitation Court:** Issues inland travel writs to the rare visitors permitted to approach the capital.
- **Eimgend Exchange:** Warehouses and offices dedicated to trade with Kluimont's second city.
- **Stormward Heights:** Northern fortifications rebuilt after the 1220 arcane storm.

### **Relationships:**

- **Kluimont City:** Qrey protects the capital and controls all ordinary access to it.
- **Eimgend:** Closest ally, principal supply partner, and source of naval escorts.
- **Lloy:** The destroyed settlement's coast is now used for military exercises administered from Qrey.
- **Rimduff Isles:** Qrey licenses legitimate island traffic while monitoring piracy and smuggling.
- **Foreign Kingdoms:** Visitors may trade and remain in Qrey, but permission to dock never implies permission to travel farther.

## DM Notes

- The invitation system protects both the capital's political secrecy and Kluimont's hidden arcane projects.
- Qrey's wards draw indirectly on the grid connected to Bagi. Most officers know only that the system depends on restricted national infrastructure.
