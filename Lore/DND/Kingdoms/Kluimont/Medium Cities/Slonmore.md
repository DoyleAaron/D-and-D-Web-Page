# Slonmore

### **Kingdom:**

Kluimont

### **Settlement Type:**

Medium City - the smallest of Kluimont's four cities

### **Government:**

A mainland Warden and civic court appointed from Eimgend, supported by district captains with considerable practical independence.

### **Population:**

**14,200** (the first city reached after crossing west from Phine)

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Orc and Goblin (43%): The largest communities, including traders, laborers, craftspeople, and old frontier families.
- Human (29%): Merchants, officials, guards, and migrants from Braewood.
- Dwarf (8%): Masons, armorers, miners, and caravan factors.
- Halfling and Gnome (6%): Shopkeepers, alchemists, and transport workers.
- Elf (3%): Mostly diplomats, specialists, and guarded travellers.
- Other (11%): Tieflings, dragonborn, mixed families, and transient peoples.

### **Wealth:**

★★★☆☆ (3 stars)

- Border tolls, caravan yards, cheap housing, river trade, and markets serving both kingdoms keep money moving through the city.
- Smuggling, stolen goods, and narcotics form a substantial shadow economy.
- Wealth is visibly uneven. A small official and merchant class lives securely while poorer districts compete for irregular work.

### **Military:**

★★★½☆ (3.5 stars)

- Slonmore's walls and western gate are maintained because the city controls the passage from Phine into Kluimont.
- The watch is stricter than in surrounding villages and responds forcefully to violence that threatens trade.
- Corruption is common, but serious offenders cannot assume every guard has been bought.
- Orc and goblin ward companies provide much of the city's effective street-level security.

### **Happiness:**

★★½☆☆ (2.5 stars)

- Slonmore offers opportunity and relative acceptance to peoples treated poorly elsewhere.
- Crime, drug rings, overcrowding, and inconsistent public services make life difficult in poorer wards.
- Residents distrust Kluimont's distant rulers, who collect tariffs but invest little beyond the walls and gate.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Slonmore stands on Kluimont's mainland near the western end of the Phine passage.
- Roads from the border, Sord, Klovine, and the interior converge outside its walls.
- The surrounding country is warm, windy, and broken by forest, marshy river ground, and old defensive earthworks.
- The city is dense rather than grand, with stone lower floors, timber upper levels, crowded courtyards, and sprawling caravan camps.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Legal trade includes Braewood metalwork, Kluimont reagents, timber, livestock, preserved food, and river cargo.
- Cheap lodging and tolerant labour laws attract migrants who cannot easily settle in Phine or eastern Braewood.
- Smugglers exploit the same roads as legitimate caravans, feeding drug markets and networks that reach Sord and Eimgend.
- The city taxes movement heavily but struggles to tax wealth once it leaves the gate district.

### **History:**

- Slonmore grew around a fortified customs crossing during Kluimont's consolidation of its mainland territory.
- During the Coastal Raids of Western Tensions, Lady Ilena Wintshield organised the local militia and used signal towers and arcane wards to repel privateers.
- The 1005 C.C. ceasefire ended open raids but did not remove distrust of Braewood.
- Repeated waves of orc, goblin, human, and mixed-race migration transformed the settlement into Kluimont's most diverse mainland city.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Western Gate:** Fortified checkpoint facing the road from Phine.
- **The Low Courts:** Busy civic courts handling trade disputes, violent crime, and border offences.
- **The Lantern Warrens:** Dense district known for cheap rooms, unlicensed apothecaries, and drug traffic.
- **Wintshield Tower:** Old coastal-raids signal tower preserved as a militia post.
- **The Open Yard:** Market and caravan ground where species and citizenship matter less than coin and conduct.

### **Relationships:**

- **Phine:** Principal eastern trade partner and historic rival across the Braewood border.
- **Sord:** Most traffic into Kluimont's mainland passes through both settlements.
- **Klovine:** Secondary crossing toward Mortling Stronghold.
- **Eimgend:** Administrative superior and preferred destination of Slonmore's successful merchants.
- **Mainland Villages:** Relies on them for food, labour, and river access while offering limited protection in return.
