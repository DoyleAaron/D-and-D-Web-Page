# Sord

### **Kingdom:**

Kluimont

### **Settlement Type:**

Town - mainland crossroads hub

### **Government:**

The Crossroad Magistracy, a civic office balancing Kluimont law, merchant interests, and the commanders of the town gates.

### **Population:**

**7,400** (unusually large for a town because of its central mainland position)

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Human (35%): Merchants, officials, innkeepers, and caravan families.
- Orc and Goblin (34%): Teamsters, guards, craftspeople, traders, and long-established residents.
- Dwarf (9%): Smiths, wheelwrights, bankers, and metal factors.
- Halfling and Gnome (8%): Provisioners, cooks, clerks, and repair specialists.
- Elf (3%): Mostly passing merchants and officials.
- Other (11%): Tieflings, dragonborn, mixed families, and temporary workers.

### **Wealth:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Roads to Slonmore, Eimgend, Ajag, Ansdale, Klovine, and the wider mainland converge around Sord.
- Tolls, inns, stables, repairs, storage, and wholesale markets generate considerable income.
- Wealth is distributed extremely unevenly. Successful families usually relocate to Eimgend, leaving property agents and poorer workers behind.

### **Military:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Stringent gate searches, armed road patrols, cargo seals, and frequent identity checks protect the crossroads.
- Theft is common enough that guards treat unattended property as an invitation and suspicious movement as probable crime.
- The town can close individual roads with fortified tollhouses while keeping other routes open.

### **Happiness:**

★★★☆☆ (3 stars)

- Work is plentiful and the town is culturally mixed, but noise, crowding, theft, and intrusive security wear on residents.
- Poor districts resent watching every successful merchant depart for Eimgend.
- Strong neighbourhood shrines and mutual-aid groups provide stability beyond the official government.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Sord occupies a broad central rise on Kluimont's mainland where the kingdom's principal road corridors intersect.
- Warehouses, stable yards, shrines, and markets spread beyond the older walls in every direction.
- The warm climate and regular traffic make dust a constant feature in dry weather and mud equally constant during rain.
- The settlement has no single dominant border; its identity comes from movement through it.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Caravan services are the foundation of Sord's economy.
- The town handles goods moving between Braewood, Islefield, Eimgend, and Kluimont's scattered mainland communities.
- Theft, fencing, false cargo seals, and protection schemes operate alongside legitimate guilds.
- Religious travellers also visit Sord's many small shrines, including a respected sanctuary of Bahamut.

### **History:**

- Sord began as a guarded meeting of old military roads and informal market camps.
- Kluimont formalised the crossroads after mainland territory expanded, building tollhouses and a central court.
- The town repeatedly outgrew its walls but never gained city status, partly because Eimgend benefits from keeping Sord politically subordinate.
- Its mixed population and constant travel have made it more open than Kluimont's island settlements, though no less suspicious.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Sixfold Crossing:** Central junction where the oldest roads meet.
- **The Seal House:** Inspects cargo, issues road seals, and investigates forged documents.
- **The Long Stable:** An immense cooperative yard capable of housing entire caravans.
- **The High Road Market:** Wholesale market for goods moving between kingdoms.
- **The Platinum Shelter:** Temple of Bahamut known for feeding travellers who swear to keep the peace.

### **Notable People:**

- **[Varin](../../../Character%20Lore/Player%20Characters/Varin%20%28Aiden%29.md):** Cleric of Bahamut and one of Sord's best-known adventuring sons.

### **Relationships:**

- **Eimgend:** Destination of Sord's wealthiest families and source of most formal authority.
- **Slonmore:** Shares the heavily travelled western corridor from Phine.
- **Ansdale and Klovine:** Border routes that bring Islefield and Braewood traffic into the crossroads.
- **Ajag:** River and lake goods enter Sord's road network through Ajag traders.
