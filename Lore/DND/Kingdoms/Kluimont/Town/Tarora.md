# Tarora

### **Kingdom:**

Kluimont in claim; self-governing in practice

### **Settlement Type:**

Town - pirate harbour occupying most of a small Rimduff island

### **Government:**

Captain GreyHorse rules through a compact of ship captains, dock bosses, smugglers, and families descended from Tarora's first pirate settlers.

### **Population:**

**4,800 permanent residents**, often rising above **6,000** when pirate crews shelter in port

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Human (31%): Sailors, craftspeople, traders, and established island families.
- Orc and Goblin (29%): Crews, dockworkers, guards, and ship-owning clans.
- Halfling and Gnome (10%): Navigators, smugglers, chandlers, and repair specialists.
- Dwarf (8%): Armorers, shipwrights, and fortification crews.
- Elf (3%): Rare permanent residents, usually attached to a crew or trade house.
- Other (19%): Tieflings, dragonborn, aquatic peoples, and visitors from distant ports.

### **Wealth:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Stolen cargo, smuggling, ship repair, ransom brokerage, and unregulated auctions bring substantial wealth into Tarora.
- Coin moves quickly and ownership is difficult to prove.
- Captains and dock bosses live well, while ordinary residents depend on unpredictable arrivals and dangerous work.

### **Military:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Tarora has no conventional army, but numerous armed crews, concealed shore weapons, and experienced captains can defend the island rapidly.
- Navigation markers can be moved to mislead hostile ships onto reefs.
- Captain GreyHorse can compel temporary cooperation when the town itself is threatened.

### **Happiness:**

★★★½☆ (3.5 stars)

- Residents value independence, profit, and the ability to disappear into a crowd that asks few questions.
- Violence is controlled by local rules rather than absent. Theft from townspeople without permission is punished more harshly than theft abroad.
- Life becomes precarious whenever Kluimont increases patrols or rival captains refuse GreyHorse's authority.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Tarora covers nearly all usable ground on its small Rimduff island.
- Houses, workshops, ropewalks, taverns, and warehouses rise directly behind crowded docks.
- The island interior is little more than a rocky ridge carrying signal fires and defensive positions.
- Warm rain, salt wind, shallow reefs, and sudden fog make local pilots essential.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Tarora repairs ships, launders cargo, brokers ransoms, recruits crews, and supplies illegal voyages.
- Legitimate fishers and traders operate beside notorious pirates, making complete blockades politically inconvenient.
- Goods from every Dayner kingdom appear in its markets, often with identifying marks removed.
- Kluimont officials periodically collect fees or confiscate a token shipment, preserving the appearance of control.

### **History:**

- Tarora began as a small island village with a sheltered anchorage.
- Pirates fleeing coordinated hunts attacked the village, seized its stores, and discovered the island was easier to defend than to abandon.
- The raiders settled permanently, absorbed survivors, and expanded the harbour until the town covered almost the entire island.
- Over generations, Tarora became the Rimduff Isles' best-known refuge for illegal crews.
- Captain GreyHorse later imposed a common harbour code strong enough to reduce destructive fighting without making the town lawful.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **GreyHorse Quay:** The best-defended dock and centre of the captain's authority.
- **The Unnamed Market:** Auction ground where sellers are not required to explain a cargo's origin.
- **The Masked Mast:** Public meeting point marked by a silver-painted dragon horn.
- **Three-Rope Yard:** Ship repair complex shared by several rival crews.
- **The Ridge Fires:** Signal positions capable of warning the entire island or guiding allies through fog.

### **Relationships:**

- **Kluimont:** Claims Tarora and occasionally patrols it, but usually prefers fees and information to occupation.
- **Qrey:** Treats Tarora vessels with intense suspicion and requires individual whitelist approval.
- **Aresphia:** A quieter stopping point whose sailors generally avoid Tarora's disputes.
- **Eimgend:** Source of both contraband customers and naval pressure.

### **Notable People:**

- **[Captain GreyHorse](../../../Character%20Lore/Modern%20Characters/Captain%20GreyHorse.md):** Notorious pirate ruler recognised by his horned silver-dragon mask.
