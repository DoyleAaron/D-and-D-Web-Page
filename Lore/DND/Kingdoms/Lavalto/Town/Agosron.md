>_All star ratings (Wealth, Military, Happiness) are in **perspective to other settlements of the same size category**._

# Agosron

### **Kingdom:**

Independent Lavalto

### **Settlement Type:**

Town

### **Government:**

The Pureblood Court, an insular council of Lavalto's oldest vampiric families.

### **Population:**

**3,400 registered residents**, with an additional unrecorded labor population

### **Racial Demographics:**

Public records are deliberately unreliable. Most visible residents are human, tiefling, or orc, while the number of vampires is officially described only as a small noble class.

### **Wealth:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Agosron controls rich obsidian cuts, bloodstone deposits, geothermal works, and concealed storehouses.
- Wealth belongs almost entirely to the pureblood estates. Ordinary residents possess little beyond issued housing and rations.
- The town trades through private ships and intermediaries rather than open markets.

### **Military:**

★★★★★ (5 stars)

- Cliff emplacements, concealed archers, spellcasters, and night patrols cover every approach from the water.
- Ships not displaying an approved flag and recognition signal are warned once, if at all, before being attacked from shore.
- There is no direct public road to Darwaeton or Flison. Inland tracks are watched, trapped, and omitted from most maps.

### **Happiness:**

½☆☆☆☆ (0.5 stars)

- The pureblood families enjoy complete authority and considerable luxury.
- Fear, magical compulsion, isolation, and rigid household control define life for everyone beneath them.
- Public celebrations are displays of obedience rather than genuine civic festivals.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Agosron occupies Lavalto's southern volcanic coast beneath steep black ridges.
- Its harbour is narrow, naturally sheltered, and almost invisible until a vessel enters the final approach.
- The settlement rises rapidly from fortified quays to walled estates, quarries, and smoke-covered terraces.
- Heat, sulfurous air, ashfall, and volcanic tremors are stronger here than in either Darwaeton or Flison.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Obsidian mining, alchemical extraction, geothermal smithing, and estate agriculture support the town.
- Almost all trade is private and carried on ships owned by the pureblood houses.
- Foreign merchants cannot simply dock. Cargo is transferred offshore or handled by pre-approved crews.
- Labor is abundant despite the town's small official population, allowing Agosron to expand at an unnatural pace.

### **History:**

- Agosron is a relatively new settlement, founded after Lavalto's old families sought a defensible refuge beyond Darwaeton's public politics.
- The first estates were isolated coastal compounds. Quarries, walls, docks, and worker districts expanded around them within only a few generations.
- The Pureblood Court gradually withdrew from outside diplomacy and turned Agosron into the most inaccessible settlement on the island.
- Its existence is known abroad, but reliable descriptions of its population, streets, or rulers are rare.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Veiled Harbour:** Fortified inlet protected by signal towers and hidden artillery positions.
- **The Court of First Blood:** Meeting hall and ceremonial centre of the pureblood families.
- **The Obsidian Steps:** Terraced road connecting the harbour to the upper estates.
- **The Silent Yards:** Vast work compounds screened by basalt walls and permanent smoke.
- **The Red Flag Tower:** Signal station that authenticates approaching ships through flags, lantern patterns, and magical responses.

### **Relationships:**

- **Darwaeton:** Recognised as Lavalto's diplomatic centre but not permitted to supervise Agosron's internal affairs.
- **Flison:** Limited contact and mutual distrust. Neither settlement maintains a safe direct road to the other.
- **Foreign Kingdoms:** No formal public trade. All contact is conducted through selected agents or pureblood vessels.

## DM Notes

- Vampires form roughly **28%** of the true population, the highest proportion of any Dayner settlement.
- Much of the unrecorded population consists of magically dominated slaves of many ancestries. They built most of modern Agosron and continue to operate its mines, estates, and fortifications.
- Approved ships fly a pureblood flag and use a changing secondary signal. Copying the flag alone is not enough to approach safely.
