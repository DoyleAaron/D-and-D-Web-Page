# Darford Cavity

## Classification

Collapsed lava chamber, ritual site, and avoided landmark

## Overview

**Darford Cavity** is a vast opening in Lavalto's inner volcanic country. From above it resembles a black wound in the earth: an uneven sinkhole surrounded by scorched stone, old posts, and paths that stop well short of the rim.

For generations, the cavity has been associated with sacrifice. Some offerings were animals or valuables; darker accounts describe prisoners, enemies, and chosen victims being taken to the edge during periods of eruption or political fear.

## Geography & Conditions

- The opening descends through layers of basalt into a network of old lava tubes.
- Warm air rises from below even during cool weather, often carrying sulfur, damp stone, and an unexplained metallic smell.
- Sound behaves strangely around the rim. Voices may return several seconds late or seem to answer from the wrong direction.
- The nearest maintained tracks curve around the area rather than approaching it.
- Vegetation grows poorly near the cavity, except for pale moss and wiry red-rooted shrubs.

## Reputation

Lavaltons disagree on what Darford Cavity actually is:

- Some say it is haunted by those sacrificed there.
- Others believe the ground itself is cursed and remembers every death.
- Infernal cults claim it is a threshold leading toward the Nine Hells.
- Miners insist it is only a dangerous volcanic chamber made frightening by old crimes and toxic fumes.

Despite those disagreements, almost everyone avoids it. Even armed patrols prefer to add hours to a journey rather than camp within sight of the rim.

## Historical Use

The site's earliest carvings predate the modern settlements of Lavalto. Tharnic symbols appear beside later infernal marks, family crests, and crude tally cuts. This suggests that different rulers reused the cavity for punishment, sacrifice, and intimidation over many centuries.

The old families publicly deny using Darford in the present era. Weathered chains, replaced posts, and paths cleared more recently than the surrounding ground make that denial difficult to trust.

## Visible Features

- **The Ash Ring:** A broad circle where no permanent vegetation grows.
- **The Offering Posts:** Blackened stone uprights with grooves worn by rope or chain.
- **The Broken Stair:** Ancient steps descending partway down one wall before ending at a collapse.
- **The Breathing Fissure:** A narrow crack that releases waves of hot air at irregular intervals.
- **The Names:** Hundreds of initials, prayers, warnings, and curses carved into the safer sections of the rim.

## DM Notes

- Darford has unquestionably been used for sacrifices, including by vampiric and infernal groups.
- Whether it contains a true portal to the Hells is deliberately unresolved. It can remain a cursed lava network, become an unstable planar thin spot, or conceal a sealed ritual gate if needed later.
- Apparitions at the rim may be actual spirits, magical residue, hallucinations caused by fumes, or a mixture of all three.
