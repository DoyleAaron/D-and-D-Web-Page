# Lavalto Volcano

## Classification

Active volcanic massif and the geographic heart of Lavalto

## Overview

The **Lavalto Volcano** dominates the island and the surrounding lake. Its connected peaks, vents, lava fields, and steep inner slopes cover much of Lavalto, with the settled coast forming only a narrow ring around them. Even on clear days, a plume hangs over the highest caldera.

The volcano is both the source of Lavalto's wealth and the reason ordinary life there remains dangerous. Fertile ash supports unusual crops along the lower slopes, while obsidian, sulfur, rare ores, mineral salts, and alchemical plants sustain the island's trade.

## Geography & Conditions

- The volcanic range is nearly **60 kilometres across**, with multiple vents surrounding the principal caldera.
- Lower slopes contain forest, terraced farms, quarries, hot springs, and abandoned lava channels.
- Higher elevations are unstable fields of ash, glass, broken basalt, steam vents, and sudden fissures.
- Tremors are common enough that local buildings use flexible timber braces within heavy basalt shells.
- Rain mixing with ash can turn roads into deep, fast-moving mud with little warning.

## Settlements & Roads

- **Darwaeton** occupies the principal northeastern harbour and controls most legal access inland.
- **Flison** guards the northwestern passage from an old Tharnic fortress.
- **Agosron** lies on the hostile southern coast and controls its own concealed approaches.
- Roads rarely cross the inner slopes. Most travel follows the coast or uses guarded estate tracks.

## Resources

- Obsidian and volcanic glass
- Sulfur, pumice, and mineral salts
- Heat-fed forges and bathhouses
- Rare medicinal and poisonous plants
- Small deposits of magically reactive ore
- Rich soil on older, stable lava terraces

## History

The Tharnic Empire fortified parts of Lavalto to control surrounding passages and exploit volcanic resources. Many old roads, foundations, and sealed tunnels beneath the slopes date to this period.

In **1191 C.C.**, a violent eruption became known as the **Night of Red Embers**. Lava and fire damaged Darwaeton's port, farmland burned, and an uprising against the island's rulers was crushed during the disaster. Whether ritual magic caused or merely worsened the eruption remains disputed.

## Beliefs & Reputation

Lavaltons treat the volcano with practical respect rather than simple fear. Households keep ash masks and evacuation packs, bells mark dangerous tremors, and many communities leave offerings at old vent shrines.

Foreign stories are less measured. Sailors describe red lights moving against the wind, chanting beneath the caldera, and doors cut into rock where no road leads.

## Known Sites

- **The High Caldera:** Active central crater, normally inaccessible without specialist equipment or magical protection.
- **The Red Ember Fields:** Scarred terrain left by the 1191 eruption.
- **The Glass Slopes:** Obsidian-rich western ridges worked by licensed quarry crews.
- **The Lower Caverns:** A broad name for lava tubes, old mines, shrines, and sealed chambers beneath the mountain.
- **Darford Cavity:** An avoided sacrificial site associated with the volcano's darkest traditions.

## DM Notes

- Father Maltrius Kael maintains interests in caverns beneath the volcano.
- Lore concerning Balthazar's hidden lair and the Sealed God belongs to the deeper volcanic complex, but exact entrances should remain flexible.
- The cause of the Night of Red Embers can remain partly ambiguous: a ritual may have triggered the eruption, intensified a natural event, or exploited one already beginning.
