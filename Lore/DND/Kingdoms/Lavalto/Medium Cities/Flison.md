>_All star ratings (Wealth, Military, Happiness) are in **perspective to other settlements of the same size category**._

# Flison

### **Kingdom:**

Independent Lavalto

### **Settlement Type:**

Medium City - commonly called Lavalto's second city

### **Government:**

A military castellany owing formal allegiance to the Darwaeton Council. The office of Basalt Castellan is controlled by Flison's old fortress families.

### **Population:**

**9,600** (a compact but heavily fortified city in the northwestern corner of Lavalto)

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Orc and Goblin (34%): Long-established dock, military, and labor families.
- Tiefling and Other (24%): Including fire-touched families and descendants of imperial auxiliaries.
- Dwarf (18%): Masons, miners, armorers, and fortress engineers.
- Human (17%): Mostly Lavalton families; mainland humans are treated as outsiders.
- Elf (4%): Traders, captives' descendants, or heavily protected foreign visitors.
- Halfling and Gnome (3%): Craftspeople, provisioners, and navigators.

### **Wealth:**

★★★☆☆ (3 stars)

- Flison exports worked basalt, volcanic glass, sulfur, and small quantities of rare ore.
- Legal foreign trade is minimal and conducted almost exclusively with approved Kluimont vessels.
- The fortress families are comfortable, while most residents live on military wages, mining income, or dock labor.

### **Military:**

★★★★½ (4.5 stars)

- Tharnic walls, layered sea gates, cliff batteries, and narrow approaches make Flison difficult to assault.
- Nearly every household owes service to the garrison, harbour watch, or reserve militia.
- The city still maintains signal towers built to control the western sea passage and watch for elven fleets.

### **Happiness:**

★★☆☆☆ (2 stars)

- Residents possess fierce local pride and value strength, ancestry, and service to the city.
- Military discipline, dangerous streets, and suspicion of outsiders make daily life harsh.
- Elves and mainland humans are frequent targets of verbal abuse, intimidation, and occasional assault. Authorities rarely intervene unless trade or public order is threatened.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Flison occupies high cliffs in Lavalto's northwestern corner, overlooking the narrow waters between the island and Kluimont.
- The upper city is contained within the old imperial fortress. Newer districts descend in steep terraces toward a small, fortified harbour.
- Ash-bearing winds and sudden squalls are common. The western slopes are cooler than Darwaeton but remain humid and volcanically active.
- A maintained road connects Flison to Darwaeton. Patrols use it regularly, but civilian traffic is light and many islanders refuse to make the crossing after dark.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- The city survives through quarrying, military contracts, fishing, glassworking, and restricted maritime trade.
- Kluimont buys volcanic reagents and stonework while supplying timber, grain, lamp oil, and arcane components.
- Foreign ships are searched thoroughly and confined to the lower harbour district.
- Inns and warehouses exist for approved traders, but there is little effort to make outsiders comfortable.

### **History:**

- Flison began as a Tharnic stronghold built to control the western passage and support imperial campaigns against elven territories.
- During the War of the Sundered Chains, its garrison held longer than most western imperial positions. Refugees, auxiliaries, and soldiers remained after the empire's collapse.
- Generations of isolation turned imperial military identity into local tradition. Anti-elven prejudice remains especially strong, while resentment of mainland humans reflects old memories of abandonment and invasion.
- Darwaeton eventually brought Flison under Lavalto's shared authority, but the city retained its own command structure and internal laws.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Black Crown:** The central Tharnic fortress and seat of the Basalt Castellan.
- **The Western Chains:** A pair of harbour towers capable of closing the inner channel with an immense chain.
- **The Legion Steps:** A steep ceremonial road lined with damaged imperial reliefs.
- **Kluimont Quay:** The only district where foreign merchants may rent warehouses or remain overnight without a local sponsor.
- **The Ember Yard:** Parade ground, militia mustering place, and public court for serious crimes.

### **Relationships:**

- **Darwaeton:** Formal superior and primary road connection. Flison accepts council directives but resents interference.
- **Kluimont:** Only regular foreign trading partner. Relations are practical, guarded, and profitable.
- **Agosron:** Viewed as dangerously extreme even by Flison standards. There is no dependable direct route between them.
- **Islefield:** Historic enemy in local memory. Elven ships are rarely admitted without Kluimont sponsorship.

## DM Notes

- Hidden Tharnic chambers beneath the Black Crown can support future imperial lore without requiring a current plot.
- The fortress families selectively preserve imperial history and omit the empire's reliance on slavery.
