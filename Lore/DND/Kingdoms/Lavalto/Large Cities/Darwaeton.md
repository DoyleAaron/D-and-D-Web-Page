>_All star ratings (Wealth, Military, Happiness) are in **perspective to other settlements of the same size category**._

# Darwaeton

### **Kingdom:**

Independent Lavalto

### **Settlement Type:**

Large City

### **Government:**

The Darwaeton Council, chaired publicly by Mayor Adrian. Lord Adrian Silwynn and Lavalto's old families hold substantial influence beyond the council chamber.

### **Population:**

**85,000** (Lavalto's largest settlement and the island's diplomatic and commercial centre)

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Human (64%): Dockworkers, craftspeople, merchants, administrators, and most of the outer districts.
- Orc and Goblin (10%): Laborers, sailors, caravan guards, and residents of the volcanic wards.
- Dwarf (6%): Stoneworkers, miners, engineers, and metal merchants.
- Elf (4%): Primarily foreign traders, diplomats, and established merchant families.
- Halfling and Gnome (5%): Shopkeepers, navigators, alchemists, and service workers.
- Other (11%): Tieflings, dragonborn, travelling peoples, and the city's concealed undead population.

### **Wealth:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Darwaeton controls most legal exports of volcanic glass, rare minerals, medicinal plants, and alchemical reagents from Lavalto.
- Its harbour earns considerable income from docking fees, warehousing, ship repair, and discreet trade between rival kingdoms.
- Wealth is concentrated around the council, old families, and merchant houses. Protection levies and blood debts keep poorer districts dependent.

### **Military:**

★★★½☆ (3.5 stars)

- The city watch is modest for a settlement of this size, but the harbour batteries, basalt walls, and fortified approaches are formidable.
- Private household guards and magically compelled defenders can reinforce the public garrison without warning.
- Darwaeton relies on intelligence, sabotage, and fear as much as open force. Invaders rarely know the city's true strength.

### **Happiness:**

★★☆☆☆ (2 stars)

- Merchants and favoured families enjoy safety, festivals, and profitable access to foreign goods.
- Ordinary residents endure heavy levies, arbitrary disappearances, and the expectation that powerful families are never questioned.
- The city remains lively despite its fear. Markets, shrines, guild halls, and crowded dockside taverns give many residents a genuine civic identity.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Darwaeton spreads along Lavalto's northeastern harbour beneath steep volcanic ridges.
- Basalt towers, black-glass roofs, canals, and smoke-stained terraces give the city a dark appearance even in daylight.
- The climate is warm and humid. Ashfall is common, and tremors are treated with practiced calm.
- A maintained road leads northwest to Flison. Tracks toward Agosron and the inner volcano are sparse, guarded, or deliberately omitted from public maps.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Darwaeton is the only Lavalto settlement that trades regularly with all three major kingdoms.
- Exports include obsidian, pumice, sulfur, rare ores, exotic herbs, alchemical salts, and finely worked volcanic glass.
- Imports include grain, timber, livestock, cloth, and worked steel, without which the densely populated city could not survive.
- Information is a secondary currency. Brokers, private couriers, and the Wayfarers' Accord all thrive beside conventional merchants.

### **History:**

- The harbour grew around older Tharnic foundations and became the island's principal port after the empire collapsed.
- Between 1050 and 1080 C.C., the Silwynn-aligned families consolidated control over the city and crushed a merchant coalition that threatened their authority.
- In 1061 C.C., Braewood adventurers attempted to expose vampiric practices in the city. The event became known as the **Darwaeton Purge** and ended with the intruders dead or driven away.
- The **Night of Red Embers** in 1191 C.C. destroyed part of the port and triggered a failed uprising. Lord Adrian financed the reconstruction, leaving much of the city indebted to him.
- Darwaeton has since rebuilt into a neutral-looking trade power whose rulers carefully prevent any foreign kingdom from gaining decisive influence on Lavalto.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Council Hall:** Administrative centre, diplomatic chamber, and the public seat of Mayor Adrian.
- **Wavecrest Hall:** The Wayfarers' Accord guildhall serving Lavalto and the lake routes.
- **The Ash Market:** A sprawling market for imported food, volcanic goods, alchemical materials, and less official merchandise.
- **The Black Quays:** Deep-water docks, warehouses, customs houses, and fortified harbour towers.
- **The Lantern Library:** A respected archive used by scholars, council members, and visiting dignitaries.
- **The Undercity:** Old drains, lava tubes, forgotten Tharnic masonry, and sealed chambers beneath the modern streets.

### **Relationships:**

- **Flison:** Connected by the island's only dependable intercity road. Trade is limited and travellers are uncommon.
- **Agosron:** Nominally under the same Lavalto authority, but its pureblood rulers tolerate little interference from Darwaeton.
- **Kluimont:** A valuable source of shipping, grain, timber, and arcane materials. Relations are profitable but distrustful.
- **Braewood:** Important trading partner and frequent source of diplomatic friction.
- **Islefield:** Purchases volcanic reagents and treats Darwaeton cautiously, particularly where elven travellers are concerned.

## DM Notes

- Darwaeton's public council is genuine, but vampires and their dependants control enough seats, debts, and private guards to determine any decision that matters.
- Lord Adrian Silwynn is the city's long-term power broker. Whether Mayor Adrian is a separate subordinate, a public identity, or part of a deliberate naming confusion can remain flexible until needed.
- Blood taxes, enthralled agents, hidden catacombs, and infernal rites sustain the old families' control.
