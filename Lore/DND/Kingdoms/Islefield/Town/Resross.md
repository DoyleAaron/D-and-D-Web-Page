# Resross

### **Kingdom:**

Islefield, historically administered through Kluimont proxies and now firmly Islefield-controlled

### **Settlement Type:**

Town - river port, penal settlement, and Iroware Heights access point

### **Government:**

Warden Halden Cosse manages the prison and crown logistics, while a small civic council governs the free settlement.

### **Population:**

**Approximately 4,500**

- 1,900 permanent settlers, workers, guards, and families
- 1,400 prisoners
- 1,200 transient labourers, officials, traders, and seasonal residents

### **Racial Demographics:**

The free population is predominantly human and elf, with smaller dwarf, half-elf, halfling, gnome, orc, and goblin communities. Prison demographics reflect convictions from across Islefield rather than the local population.

### **Wealth:**

★★★☆☆ (3 stars)

- Ore, prison labour, river shipping, guard supply, inns, and access to the Heights sustain the town.
- The river port allows Yhey merchants to move heavy cargo without relying entirely on mountain roads.
- Crown contracts are reliable, but most wealth flows through officials, mine factors, and supply owners.

### **Military:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Resross Hold, guard barracks, chained work parties, river patrols, and mountain road posts create a heavy security presence.
- The town is designed to prevent escape and protect ore transport rather than resist a national army.
- Dragon alarms and evacuation procedures are maintained despite no confirmed attack in living memory.

### **Happiness:**

★★½☆☆ (2.5 stars)

- Free residents are practical, calm, and generally safe from ordinary crime.
- Brutal prisoner treatment and the normalisation of forced labour cast a constant shadow over civic life.
- The looming Heights and regular dragon sightings make smaller disputes feel unimportant to many locals.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Resross lies southeast of Yhey on a river at the northern foot of the Iroware Heights.
- Its port serves Yhey and receives ore, supplies, prisoners, and mountain traffic.
- A northern trail enters the Heights, while a narrower branch reaches old Tharnic workings and the Ossian Vault area.
- The land is drier and harsher than southern Islefield, with sudden wind, exposed rock, and large temperature changes.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Ore extraction, prison supply, river transport, guard wages, foundry contracts, and rough lodging dominate the economy.
- Prison work gangs move material from the Heights under armed supervision.
- Yhey purchases most ore and sends food, tools, officials, and replacements upriver.
- Independent guides serve authorised travellers entering the lower mountain trails.

### **History:**

- Resross began as a work camp established to extract ore using prisoners kept far from easy escape routes.
- Guards' families, merchants, inns, a shrine, and permanent labourers gradually formed a civilian settlement beside the prison.
- The river landing expanded into a port supporting Yhey and the mountain operations.
- Old Tharnic ruins throughout the Heights attracted periodic surveys, including the abandoned facility later used by Valdrek Ossian.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **Resross Hold:** Walled prison compound on the edge of town.
- **The Iron Tap:** Functional, quiet inn used by guards, traders, and travellers.
- **Mistwater Port:** River docks handling ore and Yhey-bound cargo.
- **The Foundry Gate:** Guarded entrance to the primary Heights route.
- **Vessa Dorn's Shrine:** Multi-faith sanctuary and respected source of local knowledge.
- **The North Trail:** Difficult route into the Iroware Heights and nearby Tharnic sites.

### **Relationships:**

- **Yhey:** Administrative, military, and commercial lifeline.
- **Iroware Heights:** Source of ore, danger, dragon sightings, and old imperial ruins.
- **Kluimont:** Historical administrative influence still visible in records and some families.
- **Guatpool:** Shares experience living beneath the range and its copper dragon.

## DM Notes

- Prison treatment includes common lashings, deaths recorded as sentence complications, and deliberate public avoidance by free residents.
- The copper dragon in the Iroware Heights has not attacked Resross in living memory. Its exact relationship to Guatpool's offerings remains flexible.
- The Ossian Vault lies roughly six miles from Resross along a narrow branch from the Heights road.
