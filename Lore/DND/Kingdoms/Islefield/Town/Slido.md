# Slido

### **Kingdom:**

Islefield

### **Settlement Type:**

Town - major inland trading crossroads

### **Government:**

A Merchant Reeve elected by chartered districts and confirmed by the crown, with road captains responsible for each major approach.

### **Population:**

**8,600** (Islefield's largest town and comparable to a small city)

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Human (41%): Traders, carriers, workers, craftspeople, and residents.
- Elf (31%): Merchants, officials, landowners, and cultural leaders.
- Half-Elf (10%): Guild members, performers, and mixed families.
- Halfling and Gnome (9%): Innkeepers, cooks, repairers, traders, and entertainers.
- Dwarf (4%): Smiths, wagon builders, and merchants.
- Orc, Goblin, and Other (5%): Smaller resident communities and regular travellers.

### **Wealth:**

★★★★½ (4.5 stars)

- Slido connects routes toward Yhey, Eighwood, Wrando, Okcaster, Osegas, Praso, and the wider Islefield interior.
- Warehousing, livestock, food, repairs, finance, guides, and lodging serve enormous traveller volume.
- Wealth is visible across several districts rather than concentrated in a single elite quarter.

### **Military:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Separate road companies patrol each approach and share a central dispatch system.
- The town watch is large, experienced, and particularly focused on caravan theft and organised crime.
- Slido's broad roads and supply yards make it an important military staging point despite lacking major walls.

### **Happiness:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Residents are proud of their distinct crossroads culture, crowded festivals, food, music, and practical openness.
- Constant traffic provides opportunity but also noise, theft, rising rents, and disputes between visitors.
- Civic identity is stronger than allegiance to any single racial or noble tradition.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Slido stands near the centre of a web of river and road routes.
- The town spreads widely around markets, stable fields, warehouses, and bridge approaches rather than forming a dense walled centre.
- Wrando lies close enough to move river cargo efficiently between Slido and Islefield City.
- The climate is warm and humid, with road conditions varying sharply during heavy rain.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Slido is Islefield's principal inland redistribution town.
- Livestock fairs, caravan auctions, storage contracts, and currency exchange operate throughout the year.
- Goods can move toward the capital by Wrando's river, toward Kluimont through Yhey, toward Rye through Eighwood and Avinin, or west toward coastal settlements.
- Longer routes connect with Osegas and Braewood markets through controlled crossings.

### **History:**

- Several roadside markets merged where older routes intersected near reliable water.
- The crown formalised the road network but allowed merchant districts considerable freedom to preserve trade.
- Slido repeatedly approached city size without seeking city status, as its charter grants tax and governance advantages residents do not want to lose.
- Its culture developed from generations of travellers who stayed, married locally, and brought customs from across Dayner.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Many Roads Market:** Vast central trading district divided by route and commodity.
- **Reeve's Exchange:** Government hall, merchant court, and dispatch centre.
- **The Cattle Ring:** Famous livestock and riding-animal market.
- **Riverward Yard:** Cargo depot for goods moving through Wrando.
- **The Seven Kitchens:** Food district representing routes from across Islefield and neighbouring kingdoms.

### **Relationships:**

- **Wrando:** River connection to Islefield City and one of Slido's closest partners.
- **Eighwood:** Route toward Avinin and Rye.
- **Yhey:** Northern trade corridor and source of ore and Kluimont goods.
- **Okcaster and Praso:** Western and southern village routes.
- **Osegas:** Longer interkingdom connection supporting religious and commercial travel.
