# Avinin

### **Kingdom:**

Islefield

### **Settlement Type:**

Town - northern point of the Rye-Antstel-Avinin trade triangle

### **Government:**

A royal Town Warden advised by craft guilds, caravan factors, and officers responsible for the Eighwood road.

### **Population:**

**5,200**

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Elf (39%): Officers, merchants, craftspeople, and established families.
- Human (38%): Traders, workers, farmers, and carriers.
- Half-Elf (9%): Guild members, officials, and mixed families.
- Halfling and Gnome (7%): Artisans, innkeepers, and market organisers.
- Dwarf and Other (7%): Smiths, engineers, traders, and smaller communities.

### **Wealth:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Avinin redirects trade between Rye, Eighwood, and northern interior roads.
- Craft fairs, wagon building, textiles, preserved food, and military logistics generate reliable income.
- Guild families are prosperous without matching the wealth of Rye's bridge merchants.

### **Military:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- The town forms the northern part of a deliberately planned defensive network with Rye and Antstel.
- Roads are broad enough for rapid troop movement and include fortified relay posts.
- Avinin watches approaches from Eighwood while maintaining reserves able to support Rye.

### **Happiness:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Trade is strong, streets are safe, and residents value the town's clear role in the region.
- Military planning rarely interferes with ordinary markets.
- Guild rivalry and pressure from Rye merchants are the most common civic complaints.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Avinin lies north and east of Rye, connected directly to both Rye and Eighwood.
- Together with Antstel, it creates a triangular network surrounding the bridge approaches.
- Managed fields, groves, and wide road corridors provide clear visibility around town.
- Mild weather and maintained drainage keep routes usable for most of the year.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Goods from Eighwood and Slido enter the Rye market through Avinin.
- Craft fairs attract buyers for textiles, wagons, bows, household goods, and decorated armour.
- Warehouses hold emergency supplies for the Grand Span garrison.
- Merchants can divert traffic toward Antstel when Rye is congested or politically restricted.

### **History:**

- Avinin was deliberately expanded by Islefield planners rather than growing only by chance.
- Its roads completed a defensive and commercial triangle with Rye and Antstel.
- Artisans moved into the protected corridor, establishing fairs that gave the military waypoint an independent civic identity.
- The town remains both a real community and a component of Islefield's bridge defence strategy.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The North Relay:** Fortified road station and military dispatch point.
- **Avinin Fairground:** Permanent stalls and halls used for major craft fairs.
- **The Three Banners Storehouse:** Emergency supply depot serving Avinin, Antstel, and Rye.
- **Eighwood Gate:** Northern road entrance and caravan inspection point.

### **Relationships:**

- **Rye:** Primary trade destination and defensive partner.
- **Antstel:** Completes the regional triangle and stores additional military reserves.
- **Eighwood:** Northern route partner and source of woodland goods.
- **Slido:** Connected through Eighwood's wider trade network.
