# Halten

### **Kingdom:**

Islefield

### **Settlement Type:**

Town - inland road and hospitality centre

### **Government:**

An elected Mayor confirmed by Antstel's regional administration, supported by inn, market, and carrier guilds.

### **Population:**

**6,800** (one of Islefield's fastest-grown towns)

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Human (41%): Carriers, merchants, craftspeople, farmers, and residents.
- Elf (36%): Landowners, officials, merchants, and established families.
- Half-Elf (9%): Guild members, professionals, and mixed families.
- Halfling and Gnome (8%): Innkeepers, cooks, brewers, and repair specialists.
- Dwarf and Other (6%): Smiths, masons, traders, and travellers who settled.

### **Wealth:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Inns, stables, food, markets, repairs, and caravan services turned a rest stop into a thriving town.
- Halten benefits from traffic between Praso and Antstel as travellers prepare for the long inland stretch.
- Guild wealth is visible, but service work provides income across much of the population.

### **Military:**

★★★☆☆ (3 stars)

- A competent watch protects crowded inns, warehouses, and stable districts.
- Road patrols cooperate with Antstel but are focused on theft and traveller safety rather than border warfare.
- A restored beacon tower can call assistance from surrounding posts.

### **Happiness:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Work is plentiful, festivals are frequent, and the town remains proud of having built itself through hospitality.
- Rapid growth strains water, housing, and older roads.
- Long-established families sometimes resent guild newcomers who gained wealth quickly.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Halten stands beside Antstel on the inland route toward distant Praso.
- The town occupies a shallow valley with ample pasture, wells, and room for caravan yards.
- The next major stop toward Praso requires a long trek, making Halten the natural place to rest and resupply.
- Warm summers and wet winters keep surrounding farmland productive.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Hospitality is the defining industry: lodging, food, animals, repairs, guides, and storage.
- Agricultural markets and seasonal festivals grew around the original roadside services.
- Antstel clerks and soldiers use Halten as a cheaper place to live or conduct business.
- Praso sends produce and woodland goods through Halten toward larger cities.

### **History:**

- Halten began as a crown-supported rest station intended to make the Praso-Antstel journey safer.
- Reliable wells, an inn, and protected stables attracted merchants who began meeting there rather than simply passing through.
- Markets, homes, and workshops expanded rapidly until the station received town status.
- The settlement's planned purpose remains visible in its wide central road and unusually large lodging districts.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The First Rest:** Oldest surviving inn and symbolic centre of town.
- **Halten Yards:** Vast stable and caravan complex.
- **The Restored Beacon:** Road-warning tower on the valley ridge.
- **Festival Green:** Market and celebration ground that helped drive the town's growth.

### **Relationships:**

- **Antstel:** Closest city, administrative partner, and principal market.
- **Praso:** Original reason for Halten's founding and main western road partner.
- **Rye:** Receives goods routed through Antstel.
