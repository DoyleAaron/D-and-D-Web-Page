# Beyross

### **Kingdom:**

Islefield

### **Settlement Type:**

Town - final mainland town before the capital-side crossing

### **Government:**

A crown-appointed Road Reeve working with a mixed council of merchants, farmers, and local households.

### **Population:**

**5,400**

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Human (44%): Farmers, traders, craftspeople, carriers, and established families.
- Elf (32%): Officials, landowners, merchants, and residents.
- Half-Elf (9%): Border families, professionals, and traders.
- Halfling and Gnome (7%): Innkeepers, gardeners, craftspeople, and shopkeepers.
- Dwarf (4%): Smiths, masons, and caravan workers.
- Other (4%): Smaller resident and travelling communities.

### **Wealth:**

★★★½☆ (3.5 stars)

- Beyross earns from mainland agriculture, fishing, inns, caravan services, and traffic heading toward the capital-side bridge.
- It is the last place many travellers purchase ordinary supplies before entering more expensive and strongly elven territory.
- Wealth is reasonably distributed among farms, merchants, and service businesses.

### **Military:**

★★★☆☆ (3 stars)

- Road patrols and a trained town watch protect traffic moving toward Cok and the bridge.
- Beyross is not itself on the crossing and lacks the heavy fortifications of Cok or Cheling.
- Muster grounds allow Islefield forces to assemble without congesting the bridge approach.

### **Happiness:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- The town is prosperous, culturally mixed, and less socially rigid than settlements closer to the capital.
- Residents take pride in offering a practical welcome before travellers enter formal elven territory.
- Rising prices and pressure from capital investors create some resentment.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Beyross is the last substantial town on mainland Islefield before the narrow crossing toward the land holding Islefield City.
- Cok and the guarded bridge lie roughly **30 kilometres** farther along the road.
- Farms, low coastal ground, and small fishing landings surround the town.
- The climate is warm and humid, with regular sea fog and heavy seasonal rain.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Grain, fruit, fish, road services, wagon repair, and general markets support the economy.
- Capital-bound travellers often reorganise caravans here before the controlled bridge route.
- Goods move west toward Agosgas and north toward Praso as well as east toward Cok.
- Beyross merchants specialise in practical goods that elite capital markets overlook.

### **History:**

- Beyross grew from a mainland farm market serving the road to the old crossing.
- As the capital-side settlements became wealthier and more elven, Beyross retained a mixed population and open commercial culture.
- The town expanded whenever bridge controls moved staging, lodging, or inspection activity away from Cok.
- It remains the cultural transition between broad mainland Islefield and the capital's inner region.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Last Open Market:** Large general market known for broad access and ordinary prices.
- **Reeve's Yard:** Road administration, patrol office, and caravan assembly ground.
- **Thirty-Mile House:** Famous inn named for the remaining distance to Cok.
- **Mainland Green:** Fairground and militia muster site.

### **Relationships:**

- **Cok:** Controls the actual bridge checkpoint beyond Beyross.
- **Praso:** Northern road neighbour and source of village produce.
- **Agosgas:** Coastal trading partner.
- **Islefield City:** Destination for much of the town's traffic and source of increasing investment pressure.
