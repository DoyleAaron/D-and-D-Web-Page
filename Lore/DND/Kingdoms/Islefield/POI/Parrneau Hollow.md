# Parrneau Hollow

## Classification

Secluded natural basin and lightly protected druidic site

## Overview

**Parrneau Hollow** is a deep green basin hidden among low ridges in western Islefield. From the surrounding road it appears to be ordinary woodland. Only at the final rise does the ground fall away into a sheltered bowl of meadow, springs, pale stone, and unusually still air.

No major settlement occupies the Hollow, and Islefield has never assigned it a single official purpose. Foresters, druids, shepherds, and travellers use its edges, while the central stones are left largely undisturbed.

## Geography & Conditions

- A ring of low limestone ridges shields the basin from coastal wind.
- Several small springs emerge along the inner wall and join before draining underground.
- Weathered standing stones form incomplete circles across the central meadow.
- Sound carries clearly from one side of the Hollow to the other.
- At dawn and dusk, mist collects below the rim while the surrounding hills remain clear.

## Flora & Wildlife

- Wildflowers grow in dense seasonal bands according to water level.
- Deer, foxes, ground birds, and small fey-touched animals are common.
- Old pear and plum trees suggest habitation or deliberate planting in the distant past.
- No dangerous creature is known to hold the basin permanently.

## History

The standing stones predate surviving Islefield records. Their arrangement has been interpreted as a calendar, meeting ground, burial marker, water ritual, and unfinished ward. None of those explanations has been proven.

Parrneau families once grazed animals around the rim, giving the Hollow its modern name. The family dispersed generations ago, but local shepherds preserved rules against damaging the stones or blocking the springs.

## Present Use

- Druids conduct small seasonal observances with regional permission.
- Travellers use marked sites near the rim but are discouraged from camping in the central meadow.
- Herbalists gather common plants under limits intended to preserve the basin.
- Islefield wardens inspect the stones periodically and have recorded no immediate danger.

## Known Features

- **The Broken Circle:** Largest group of standing stones.
- **Seven Springs:** Water sources emerging around the basin wall.
- **Parrneau Trees:** Old orchard whose origin is unknown.
- **The Listening Ridge:** Point where quiet speech carries across the entire Hollow.
- **The Underflow:** Place where joined spring water disappears beneath the ground.

## Reputation

Parrneau Hollow is regarded as peaceful but unfinished, as though it once had an important purpose that history forgot. Placeholder theories are plentiful; confirmed lore is deliberately sparse.

## DM Notes

- The Hollow currently has no fixed secret or plot function.
- Its stones, underground water, or vanished Parrneau family can be developed later without contradicting this entry.
