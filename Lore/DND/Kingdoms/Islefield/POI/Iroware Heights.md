# Iroware Heights

## Classification

Vast northern highland, desert, and mountain region

## Alternate Name

The range is also called the **Ironwarf Heights** in military records, mining documents, and local speech.

## Overview

The **Iroware Heights** form one of the harshest environments in Islefield. The range is not Dayner's tallest, but it covers an immense area of ridges, dry basins, broken plateaus, mineral slopes, and wind-carved passes. Its aridity contrasts sharply with Islefield's humid coasts and lush southern forests.

A copper dragon inhabits the region. Sightings are common near Yhey, Resross, and Guatpool, though reports disagree on its age, lair, and attitude toward the surrounding settlements.

## Geography & Climate

- Broad rain-shadow deserts lie between exposed ridgelines.
- Daytime heat can give way to freezing nights at higher elevation.
- Flash floods cut dry channels after rare storms.
- Copper, iron, coloured stone, salt, and scattered rare ores attract miners despite the danger.
- Reliable water is limited to mapped springs, deep ravines, and old engineered channels.

## Settlements & Access

- **Yhey** guards the northern border approaches and processes much of the region's ore.
- **Resross** provides the principal northern trail, river transport, prison labour, and guides.
- **Guatpool** lies at the lower foothills and maintains an offering tradition intended to prevent dragon attacks.
- Smaller camps appear and vanish according to water, mining success, and danger.

## The Copper Dragon

The dragon is seen circling high ridges, investigating caravans, and occasionally approaching settlements. Guatpool experienced enough attacks to begin leaving regular tribute. Resross has recorded sightings for decades without a confirmed attack in living memory.

Copper dragons are often associated with humour, tricks, conversation, and territorial games, but residents do not assume that makes this one harmless. Offerings, route customs, and warning horns are treated seriously.

## Tharnic Remains

The Tharnic Empire mined and surveyed parts of the Heights using forced labour. Ruins include roads, drainage systems, watch posts, mine entrances, sealed workshops, and underground facilities.

One abandoned mining complex was later occupied and transformed by **[Valdrek Ossian](../../../Character%20Lore/Historic%20Characters/Valdrek%20Ossian.md)** into the Ossian Vault. Many other structures remain uncatalogued because the terrain makes sustained exploration expensive.

## Known Features

- **The Northern Trail:** Main route from Resross into the lower range.
- **The Copper Shelf:** Guatpool's traditional offering site.
- **The Ossian Vault:** Tharnic facility later reshaped around the worship of Avethis.
- **The Dry Labyrinth:** Network of canyons and seasonal watercourses.
- **The High Circles:** Ridges where the copper dragon is most frequently seen.

## Resources & Dangers

- Ore, mineral salts, rare stone, fossils, and mountain herbs reward travel.
- Heat, cold, thirst, unstable ruins, flash floods, predators, and dragon encounters threaten it.
- Old Tharnic maps are valuable but unreliable after centuries of collapse and erosion.
- Guides from Resross or Guatpool significantly improve survival.

## DM Notes

- The simplest interpretation is that one copper dragon accounts for sightings and Guatpool's attacks, but additional dragons can be introduced later if needed.
- Use Iroware as the formal geographic name and Ironwarf as a well-established local or historical variant rather than treating them as separate ranges.
