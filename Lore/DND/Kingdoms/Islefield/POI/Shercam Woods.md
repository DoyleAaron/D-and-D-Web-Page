# Shercam Woods

## Classification

Most sacred elven forest in Islefield and protected birthplace of its recognised elven lineages

## Overview

**Shercam Woods** is the spiritual centre of Islefield's elven culture. The forest can be entered lawfully only through Sable, whose guardians, lineage keepers, and Birth Keepers control every recognised route.

All formally recognised Islefield elven births take place within Shercam. The forest's depth determines ceremonial status, and the royal line gives birth near the most sacred centre.

## Geography & Protection

- Dense ancient woodland surrounds layered clearings, springs, birth houses, and ritual paths.
- A powerful magical boundary turns away unauthorised travellers, closes false paths, and interferes with teleportation.
- Routes change according to season, lineage, ceremony, and permission.
- No conventional road reaches the central forest.
- The deeper woods are quieter, older, and more magically concentrated.

## Elven Birth Tradition

Islefield tradition holds that elves must be born within Shercam Woods under the care of lifelong **Birth Keepers**, commonly called elven birthers.

The established rules are:

- Birth within Shercam grants the silver hair associated with Islefield's blessed lineages.
- Elves born outside the forest lack that mark and are often shunned by the kingdom's traditional institutions.
- A family's permitted birth depth reflects recognised nobility.
- Common lineages use outer sacred clearings.
- Senior noble houses use deeper birth grounds.
- The royal line is admitted to the innermost recognised centre; Queen Atheliana Aeristel was born there.

These customs combine magic, ancestry, politics, and social control. Whether every claim made by lineage authorities is biologically absolute matters less in daily life than their power to recognise or reject a birth.

## The Birth Keepers

Birth Keepers spend their lives within Sable and Shercam. Their duties include:

- Midwifery, healing, and protection
- Maintaining birth houses and sacred springs
- Recording lineages and witnesses
- Preparing families for entry
- Protecting newborns during the return journey
- Preserving rituals that differ by depth and house

They hold unusual authority because even noble families depend on their recognition and care.

## Sacred Structure

Shercam is understood as a series of increasingly restricted layers:

- **The Outer Green:** Approved births for common elven families and the largest number of Birth Keepers.
- **The Lineage Paths:** Separate routes associated with old houses and regional traditions.
- **The Silver Springs:** Deep sites used by recognised noble families.
- **The Crownward:** Highly restricted inner forest associated with royal births.
- **The Living Centre:** Sacred heart described in ritual language but absent from ordinary maps.

## Culture & Resources

- Nothing from Shercam may be harvested for ordinary trade.
- Fallen wood, spring water, leaves, and silver moss are used only in approved rites.
- Songs, names, and genealogies are preserved orally as well as in Sable's archives.
- Elves born in the forest may return for major lineage ceremonies, but access remains controlled.

## Relationships

- **Sable:** Only lawful gate and administrative settlement.
- **Islefield City:** Royal authority protects Shercam but does not replace the Birth Keepers' ritual control.
- **Prairie Kingfisher Grove:** Shares protective natural traditions but is less sacred and more accessible.
- **Esterhull:** Restricted teleportation link to Sable supports elite and emergency travel, not direct entry into Shercam.
- **Yhey:** Provides regional military support outside the sacred boundary.

## DM Notes

- Shercam's magic should resist simple bypass by flight, teleportation, tunnelling, or approaching from another direction.
- The social system is intentionally hierarchical. Individual elves may question it even though the institutions enforce it.
- The exact nature of the Living Centre can remain undefined until needed.
