# Yhey

### **Kingdom:**

Jointly administered at the Kluimont-Islefield border; culturally and politically Islefield-dominant

### **Settlement Type:**

Medium City - Islefield's third-largest city and northern border checkpoint

### **Government:**

A divided Warden's Hall with Islefield and Kluimont wings. Islefield's noble and merchant houses hold most practical power.

### **Population:**

**28,000**

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Elf (39%): Noble houses, senior officials, officers, merchants, and guild leaders.
- Human (34%): Workers, traders, soldiers, craftspeople, and lower-district residents.
- Half-Elf (9%): Professionals and families navigating both social structures.
- Orc and Goblin (7%): Mostly Kluimont-linked residents of the lower city.
- Dwarf (5%): Foundry workers, engineers, miners, and merchants.
- Halfling, Gnome, and Other (6%): Traders, artisans, and border residents.

### **Wealth:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Border tolls, ore processing, trade, banking, military supply, and elven luxury workshops make Yhey prosperous.
- Wealth is concentrated in the elevated elven Crescent, while the Basin performs most industrial work.
- Kluimont-linked districts contribute heavily to production without receiving equivalent public investment.

### **Military:**

★★★★★ (5 stars)

- Yhey maintains one of Islefield's largest permanent border garrisons.
- The Gate of Two Crowns is staffed by both kingdoms, with alternating patrols and redundant inspection systems.
- Elite archers, mounted scouts, foundry-supported engineers, and high-level ward magic protect the city.
- Dragon sightings over the Iroware Heights are common enough to be included in watch training and alarm codes.

### **Happiness:**

★★½☆☆ (2.5 stars)

- The city is materially successful but socially divided.
- Years of raids, prisoner traffic, border violence, and political disputes have hardened residents against Kluimont and non-elven outsiders.
- Racism is more direct and brutal than in southern Islefield: harassment, unequal searches, denied service, and guard violence occur with limited consequences.
- Lower Yhey remains culturally active and tightly communal despite official neglect.

### **Geography & Districts:**

- Yhey stands directly on the territorial line north of the Iroware Heights.
- **The Crescent** occupies an elevated plateau of white stone, controlled gardens, elite residences, and elven institutions.
- **The Basin** contains foundries, markets, river docks, dense housing, and most of the city's mixed population.
- The border road, river traffic, and routes toward Resross converge below the upper city.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Ore from the Iroware Heights feeds Basin foundries and Crescent workshops.
- Border trade includes Kluimont reagents, Islefield food and timber, tools, textiles, and restricted Tharnic artefacts.
- Merchant houses profit from complexity by providing escorts, permits, currency exchange, and document services.
- Black markets thrive wherever the two administrations disagree.

### **History:**

- Yhey grew where both kingdoms claimed the same crossing and neither could remove the other without war.
- A joint administration ended repeated skirmishes, but Islefield's wealth gradually secured the upper districts and dominant civic influence.
- Generations of border trouble entrenched social division between the elven Crescent and working Basin.
- The city became Islefield's northern military anchor and principal checkpoint for Kluimont traffic.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Gate of Two Crowns:** Main border gate, staffed by both kingdoms.
- **The Warden's Hall:** Split administrative building whose two wings rarely agree.
- **The Crescent:** Elevated elven district of government, wealth, and restricted institutions.
- **The Basin:** Working city of markets, docks, foundries, and mixed neighbourhoods.
- **Aelrath's Library:** Major archive containing valuable Tharnic-era records.
- **The River Market:** Daily market and centre of legal and illegal cross-border trade.

### **Relationships:**

- **Kluimont:** Essential trading neighbour and principal object of local suspicion.
- **Resross:** Receives supplies, officials, prisoners, and travellers through Yhey.
- **Eighwood:** Southern road connection toward Avinin and Slido.
- **Sable:** Nearby sacred territory whose restrictions reinforce Yhey's elven hierarchy.
- **Iroware Heights:** Source of ore, dragon sightings, danger, and old Tharnic ruins.
