>_All star ratings (Wealth, Military, Happiness) are in **perspective to other settlements of the same size category**._

# Cheling

### **Kingdom:**

Islefield

### **Settlement Type:**

Medium City - southern soft border of the capital region

### **Government:**

A royal Prefect supported by merchant assessors, road wardens, and an elven civic council.

### **Population:**

**18,200** (larger than Agosgas, slightly smaller than Antstel, and well below Yhey)

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Elf (48%): Officials, landowners, officers, guild leaders, and established families.
- Human (34%): Merchants, workers, craftspeople, farmers, and travellers.
- Half-Elf (7%): Administrators, traders, and professional families.
- Halfling and Gnome (5%): Innkeepers, artisans, clerks, and specialists.
- Dwarf (3%): Smiths, engineers, and merchants.
- Other (3%): Small resident and transient communities.

### **Wealth:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Cheling profits from every road leading between Islefield City, the southern settlements, and the mainland crossing.
- Warehousing, inspections, high-quality inns, craft markets, and supply contracts create broad prosperity.
- Elven families dominate property and senior guild positions despite the city's mixed workforce.

### **Military:**

★★★★½ (4.5 stars)

- Cheling is heavily militarised as the final screening point before the capital's inner territory.
- Patrols inspect suspicious cargo and monitor travellers without routinely closing the road by race or nationality.
- Barracks, mounted wardens, archers, and layered warning wards can reinforce the capital quickly.

### **Happiness:**

★★★½☆ (3.5 stars)

- Streets are safe, work is reliable, and travellers can pass without special racial permission.
- Elves receive better service, more respectful treatment, and easier access to institutions.
- Classism and racism are expressed through language, social exclusion, pricing, and contempt rather than formal travel bans or routine violence.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Cheling stands north of Ulsall and Esterhull on the final major approach to Islefield City.
- Broad roads, inspection gardens, barracks, and warehouses surround an older civic centre.
- The landscape is fertile and humid, with managed groves separating military sites from residential districts.
- River and road traffic both feed the city, though most travellers continue by land toward the capital.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Cheling is a distribution hub for food, luxury goods, timber products, court supplies, and southern estate traffic.
- Craft markets are known for fine textiles, jewellery, legal documents, and household goods.
- Merchants can trade freely, but contracts involving the capital are reviewed more carefully.
- Ulsall inns and Esterhull estates depend on Cheling wholesalers.

### **History:**

- Cheling grew where capital patrols once met caravans arriving from the south.
- Temporary inspection camps became permanent markets, barracks, and administrative offices.
- The city expanded as Islefield chose screening and observation over a visibly closed capital border.
- Its mixed population reflects centuries of trade, while its social hierarchy reflects proximity to the elven court.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Green Review:** Landscaped checkpoint complex handling travellers and cargo bound for the capital.
- **Prefect's Hall:** Civic government, courts, and road administration.
- **The Southern Exchange:** Warehouse and wholesale district.
- **The White Barracks:** Headquarters of the capital approach wardens.
- **The Open Garden:** Public market where foreign merchants may trade without entering elite guild houses.

### **Relationships:**

- **Islefield City:** Cheling protects and supplies the capital's southern approach.
- **Ulsall:** Rest village and next stop toward Esterhull.
- **Esterhull:** Wealthy client whose visitors and supplies are screened in Cheling.
- **Cok and Beyross:** Sources of mainland and bridge traffic entering the southern network.
