# Agosgas

### **Kingdom:**

Islefield

### **Settlement Type:**

Medium City - fortified Kluimont-facing coastal city

### **Government:**

A royal Coast-Warden supported by naval officers, merchant houses, and an elected harbour council.

### **Population:**

**16,400** (smaller than Cheling and Antstel but firmly a city rather than a village)

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Elf (41%): Officers, landowners, merchants, and established coastal families.
- Human (37%): Sailors, fishers, craftspeople, traders, and residents.
- Half-Elf (8%): Merchant and naval families.
- Halfling and Gnome (5%): Navigators, innkeepers, artisans, and ship suppliers.
- Dwarf (4%): Ship engineers, smiths, and stoneworkers.
- Orc, Goblin, and Other (5%): Small resident communities and foreign traders.

### **Wealth:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Agosgas trades with Kluimont and Islefield City while serving as a naval and customs centre.
- Fine beaches, protected coves, fishing, salt, ship repair, and coastal estates support a varied economy.
- Merchant and officer families are prosperous, though outer working districts receive less investment.

### **Military:**

★★★★★ (5 stars)

- The city is heavily militarised because it faces Kluimont routes and protects Islefield's outer coast.
- Cliff towers, fast patrol ships, trained archers, and signal stations cover the beaches and harbour.
- Residents take visible pride in naval service, coastal readiness, and the city's record of resisting raids.

### **Happiness:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Strong civic identity, reliable trade, attractive coastline, and public festivals support high morale.
- Military duties and suspicion of Kluimont create pressure, but residents generally view them as necessary.
- The best beaches remain public by royal tradition, preventing estates from closing the entire shore.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Agosgas occupies Islefield's outer coast facing the principal routes toward Kluimont.
- Pale beaches alternate with defensive cliffs, sheltered coves, and wooded ridges.
- Okcaster lies north along the same shore, while roads and river routes connect inland toward Slido and the capital.
- The climate is warm, breezy, and humid, with dangerous seasonal storms.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Exports include fish, salt, seaweed products, timber craft, wine, and naval supplies.
- Kluimont vessels bring reagents, unusual crafted goods, and island products under heavy inspection.
- Trade with Islefield City moves by road, river, and protected coastal shipping.
- Beach inns and restorative estates serve wealthy domestic visitors without replacing the city's working harbour economy.

### **History:**

- Agosgas began as several fishing villages around a defensible natural harbour.
- Islefield fortified the coast as Kluimont expanded its naval influence and pirate traffic increased.
- The harbour settlements merged behind shared walls, eventually receiving city status.
- Naval service became central to local identity, producing a proud culture distinct from both the capital and inland cities.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Coast-Warden's Citadel:** Government seat and naval command.
- **Brightwater Harbour:** Fortified commercial and military port.
- **The Open Sands:** Public beaches protected from private enclosure.
- **The Kluimont Exchange:** Closely monitored foreign trading quarter.
- **The Signal Crown:** Chain of cliff towers communicating with Okcaster and inland patrols.

### **Relationships:**

- **Kluimont:** Important trade partner and primary military concern.
- **Islefield City:** Receives coastal goods and directs naval policy.
- **Okcaster:** Northern coastal neighbour whose extremist groups concern Agosgas officials.
- **Slido:** Major inland distribution partner.
- **Beyross:** Links Agosgas to southern mainland traffic.
