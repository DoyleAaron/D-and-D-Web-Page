# Antstel

### **Kingdom:**

Islefield

### **Settlement Type:**

Medium City - archive city and Grand Span reserve

### **Government:**

An Archscribe-Governor appointed by the crown, advised by archive guilds, civic houses, and the commander of the Rye reserve garrison.

### **Population:**

**18,600** (similar in size to Cheling and one of the three settlements supporting the Rye crossing)

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Elf (43%): Archivists, officers, landowners, scholars, and senior officials.
- Human (38%): Merchants, soldiers, craftspeople, workers, and border families.
- Half-Elf (8%): Professionals, scribes, and mixed border households.
- Dwarf (4%): Engineers, bookbinders, metalworkers, and traders.
- Halfling and Gnome (4%): Clerks, artisans, innkeepers, and printers.
- Other (3%): Smaller resident communities and travellers.

### **Wealth:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Antstel earns from archives, legal copying, education, military supply, bridge traffic, and skilled craft.
- Its scribes authenticate treaties, property records, cargo seals, and historic claims used throughout Islefield.
- Wealth is more broadly distributed than in the capital but sharply divided by guild membership and education.

### **Military:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Antstel houses reserve soldiers, mounts, engineers, and supplies intended to reinforce Rye and the Grand Span.
- Roads toward Rye and Avinin are patrolled heavily and designed for rapid troop movement.
- The garrison is substantial without dominating every part of civilian life.

### **Happiness:**

★★★½☆ (3.5 stars)

- The city has strong cultural institutions, reliable work, and a more mixed population than southern elven centres.
- Crime is not high by wider Dayner standards, but theft, fraud, and smuggling exceed what Islefield elites consider acceptable.
- Humans and half-elves can rise through guilds more readily here, though elven families still control senior offices.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Antstel sits close to Rye and Avinin, forming the southern point of a deliberately planned defensive and trading triangle.
- Broad avenues connect archives, barracks, markets, and caravan yards.
- The surrounding land is gently rolling and heavily cultivated, with maintained groves breaking the wind.
- The climate is warm and mild, supporting year-round movement toward the bridge.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Copying, record authentication, education, printing, bookbinding, legal services, and military supply are central industries.
- Cargo moving toward Rye often has documents inspected or corrected in Antstel.
- Avinin routes goods north toward Eighwood, while Antstel handles eastward movement to the bridge.
- Scholars purchase Tharnic documents, though ownership and export are regulated.

### **History:**

- Antstel began as a royal archive and supply station serving the approaches to the Grand Span.
- Civilian districts grew around scribes, soldiers' families, legal courts, and merchants seeking reliable documentation.
- Islefield deliberately expanded the roads linking Antstel, Avinin, and Rye for both trade and defence.
- Border contact produced a mixed culture that is less formal than the capital but still distinctly Islefield.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Great Shelves:** Public-facing archive complex containing legal and historical records.
- **The Reserve Fields:** Barracks, stables, and supply grounds for forces assigned to Rye.
- **The Three Roads Court:** Commercial square where routes to Rye, Avinin, and the interior divide.
- **The Seal Guild:** Authenticates documents, cargo marks, and official copies.
- **The Mixed Quarter:** Densely populated district known for workshops, food, music, and the city's highest crime rate.

### **Relationships:**

- **Rye:** Antstel supplies reserve troops, records, and logistical support.
- **Avinin:** Completes the three-settlement trade and defence arrangement around the bridge approaches.
- **Halten:** Links Antstel to the longer inland route toward Praso.
- **Braewood:** Source of travellers, records disputes, and contraband crossing the Grand Span.
