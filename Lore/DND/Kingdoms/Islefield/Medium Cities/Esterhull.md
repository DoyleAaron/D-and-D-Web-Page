>_All star ratings (Wealth, Military, Happiness) are in **perspective to other settlements of the same size category**._

# Esterhull

### **Kingdom:**

Islefield

### **Settlement Type:**

Medium City - Islefield's smallest and Dayner's southernmost city

### **Government:**

The Emeritus Circle, a council of retired elven ministers, generals, magistrates, and senior court figures operating under royal charter.

### **Population:**

**9,800** (small in population but exceptionally wealthy and exclusive)

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Elf (88%): Retired elites, their households, descendants, tutors, artists, and staff.
- Half-Elf (5%): Established families with court sponsorship.
- Human (3%): Vetted specialists and hereditary household retainers.
- Halfling and Gnome (2%): Gardeners, chefs, healers, and artisans under patronage.
- Other (2%): Individually sponsored residents with exceptional standing.

### **Wealth:**

★★★★★ (5 stars)

- Esterhull is the richest city per resident in Islefield and likely in Dayner.
- Generations of retired elites brought pensions, estates, art collections, libraries, and investments into a very small population.
- Public spaces, private schools, magical services, and household care operate at a standard associated elsewhere with royal courts.

### **Military:**

★★★★½ (4.5 stars)

- The city has a modest visible guard but extensive magical safeguards, concealed wards, and highly experienced retired officers.
- Outsiders rarely reach the gates without being detected and redirected long beforehand.
- A restricted teleportation circle links Esterhull to Sable for emergencies, ceremonies, and approved travel.

### **Happiness:**

★★★★½ (4.5 stars)

- Crime is almost nonexistent, services are exceptional, and residents enjoy security and leisure.
- Children raised here receive elite education and social access comparable to minor royalty.
- The city's comfort depends on extreme exclusion, inherited privilege, and a workforce whose place is carefully controlled.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Esterhull stands near Islefield's southern coast beyond Ulsall and the ruins of Stanron, outside the Prairie Kingfisher Grove.
- Sea cliffs, managed woodland, old estates, and magically protected gardens surround the city.
- The climate is warm, humid, and moderated by coastal wind.
- Roads are immaculate but discreetly controlled. Travellers without an invitation are turned back before seeing the main gates.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Esterhull produces little bulk trade; its economy centres on wealth management, luxury craft, education, healing, art, and private patronage.
- Supplies arrive through Cheling and Ulsall under long-term contracts.
- Master artisans accept commissions from across Islefield but rarely admit clients in person.
- Estates invest heavily in the capital, Rye, and elven agricultural projects.

### **History:**

- The area began as a collection of southern retreats used by aging elven nobles and royal advisers.
- A royal charter formalised shared roads, wards, and administration without opening the estates to general settlement.
- Retired political and military figures gradually transformed the enclave into a small city whose influence greatly exceeds its population.
- Magical protections were expanded after several attempts at espionage and kidnapping, producing the near-impenetrable approach used today.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Emeritus Hall:** Council chamber and archive of retired state service.
- **The Southern Gardens:** Interconnected estates, conservatories, and walking grounds.
- **The Quiet Gate:** Main entrance, reached only after several concealed ward checkpoints.
- **The Silver School:** Elite academy serving resident children and a handful of royal invitees.
- **The Sable Circle:** Restricted teleportation chamber linked to the holy-ground gate village.

### **Relationships:**

- **Ulsall:** Principal rest stop and service village on the northern approach.
- **Cheling:** Supplies most ordinary goods and screens traffic travelling farther south.
- **Stanron:** The ruined royal burial settlement is treated with solemn respect.
- **Sable:** Connected by protected teleportation for sacred and emergency travel.
- **Islefield City:** Many residents retain influence at court despite officially retiring.

## DM Notes

- Esterhull's safeguards should feel preventive rather than openly hostile: misdirected roads, polite messengers, sleep wards, and teleport redirection before visible force.
