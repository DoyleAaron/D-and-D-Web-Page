# Praso

### **Kingdom:**

Islefield

### **Settlement Type:**

Village - elven road and farming community

### **Government:**

A village Elder chosen by resident households and confirmed through the regional elven land council.

### **Population:**

**1,000**

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Elf (67%): Farmers, herbalists, craftspeople, and established woodland families.
- Human (20%): Farmers, labourers, traders, and resident families.
- Half-Elf (7%): Mixed households and local professionals.
- Halfling, Gnome, and Other (6%): Gardeners, artisans, and travellers who settled.

### **Wealth:**

★★★☆☆ (3 stars)

- Orchards, herbs, fine woodcraft, food, and road services provide a stable village economy.
- Praso benefits from lying between Halten and Beyross without becoming a major market itself.
- Wealth is modest and relatively evenly distributed.

### **Military:**

★★½☆☆ (2.5 stars)

- Woodland scouts and a household militia protect the village and road.
- Praso relies on warning systems and nearby towns rather than walls.
- Elven archery traditions give the small militia more capability than its numbers suggest.

### **Happiness:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- The village is quiet, productive, and strongly communal.
- Residents value distance from city politics while remaining connected to useful markets.
- Outsiders are treated politely but may find local customs difficult to enter.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Praso lies on the route connecting Halten and Beyross.
- Orchards, small groves, hedged fields, and low woodland surround the settlement.
- The road passes close enough to support inns without dividing the residential centre.
- Warm rain and fertile soil support herbs and fruit.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Praso sells fruit, herbs, preserved food, bows, carved household goods, and simple lodging.
- Halten carries its products toward Antstel, while Beyross opens routes toward the capital-side crossing and coast.
- Residents resist large warehouses or industrial development.

### **History:**

- Elven farming families established Praso near reliable springs and an old woodland path.
- The path became a maintained road between Halten and Beyross.
- The village accepted limited traveller services while preserving its small scale and elven character.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Orchard Hall:** Village meeting place surrounded by communal fruit trees.
- **Praso Springs:** Protected water source feeding homes and gardens.
- **The Bent Bow:** Small road inn known for quiet rooms and local food.
- **Hedgeway Market:** Weekly exchange for village produce and passing goods.

### **Relationships:**

- **Halten:** Main eastern market and hospitality hub.
- **Beyross:** Southern road partner and access to wider mainland trade.
- **Slido:** Reached through the wider road network for bulk sales.
