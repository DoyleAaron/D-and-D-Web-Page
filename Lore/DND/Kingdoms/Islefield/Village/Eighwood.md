# Eighwood

### **Kingdom:**

Islefield

### **Settlement Type:**

Village - woodland road relay

### **Government:**

A Road Elder and council of foresters, carriers, and resident households.

### **Population:**

**1,300**

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Elf (48%): Foresters, scouts, craftspeople, officials, and old families.
- Human (33%): Carriers, farmers, workers, and residents.
- Half-Elf (8%): Guides, traders, and mixed households.
- Halfling and Gnome (6%): Innkeepers, craftspeople, and provisioners.
- Dwarf and Other (5%): Smiths, road workers, and smaller communities.

### **Wealth:**

★★★½☆ (3.5 stars)

- Traffic between Yhey, Avinin, and Slido supports inns, guides, repairs, forestry, and storage.
- Carefully managed timber and woodland products supplement road income.
- The village is comfortable but limits logging to preserve the surrounding forest.

### **Military:**

★★★☆☆ (3 stars)

- Scouts patrol roads toward Yhey and watch for smugglers avoiding official checkpoints.
- A small militia protects the relay yards and can guide larger Islefield forces through woodland routes.
- Avinin and Yhey provide reinforcement when needed.

### **Happiness:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Eighwood combines steady traveller income with a quiet woodland setting.
- Residents value cooperation between its mixed families and dislike Yhey's harsher social divisions.
- Road crime and military searches occasionally disturb village life.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Eighwood lies between Yhey, Avinin, and Slido at a junction surrounded by ancient trees.
- Maintained clearings hold inns, stables, and relay yards while homes extend into managed woodland.
- The climate is humid and shaded, with frequent mist and heavy leaf cover.
- Roads remain usable through raised stone beds and careful drainage.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Lodging, animal exchange, wagon repair, guides, timber craft, mushrooms, resin, and woodland herbs support the village.
- Yhey traffic brings ore and Kluimont goods south.
- Avinin carries goods toward Rye, while Slido distributes them across the interior.
- Eighwood guides are valued for avoiding flooded or unsafe forest routes.

### **History:**

- A road relay was established where three regional routes met beneath a distinctive stand of tall trees.
- Foresters and soldiers' families settled around it, followed by traders and craftspeople.
- The village expanded without clearing the whole forest, producing its scattered present layout.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Threeway Relay:** Stable, courier post, and road office.
- **The High Canopy Inn:** Large timber inn built around living trees.
- **The Raised Roads:** Stone-supported approaches designed for wet weather.
- **Foresters' Hall:** Manages timber use, scouts, and woodland boundaries.

### **Relationships:**

- **Yhey:** Northern city and source of guarded border traffic.
- **Avinin:** Route toward Rye and the Grand Span.
- **Slido:** Largest trading partner and inland distribution hub.
