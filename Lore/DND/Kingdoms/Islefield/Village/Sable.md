>_All star ratings (Wealth, Military, Happiness) are in **perspective to other settlements of the same size category**._

# Sable

### **Kingdom:**

Islefield

### **Settlement Type:**

Village - sacred gate to Shercam Woods

### **Government:**

The Gate Keepers, Birth Keepers, and senior guardians of Shercam tradition, answerable directly to the royal household.

### **Population:**

**1,200**, all elves

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Elf (100%): Guards, keepers of tradition, Birth Keepers, ritual attendants, and their families.
- Non-elves may approach designated outer offices but may not enter the village or Shercam Woods.

### **Wealth:**

★★★½☆ (3.5 stars)

- Sable receives direct royal support, ceremonial gifts, and supplies from across Islefield.
- Residents produce little for ordinary markets; their work is religious, protective, genealogical, and medical.
- Material display is restrained despite access to high-quality goods.

### **Military:**

★★★★★ (5 stars)

- Every approach is warded, watched, and designed to turn away unauthorised travellers before they reach the inner gate.
- Guardians combine elite archery, druidic magic, royal authority, and control of the forest's only recognised entrance.
- The village can call support from Yhey and use protected routes unavailable to outsiders.

### **Happiness:**

★★★½☆ (3.5 stars)

- Residents possess immense cultural status and a clear lifelong purpose.
- Duty is restrictive: many keepers spend their entire lives at Sable or within Shercam Woods.
- The prohibition on outsiders and weight of tradition leave little room for individual departure or dissent.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Sable stands at the only lawful entrance to Shercam Woods and beside the crossing toward Ansdale in Kluimont.
- The village is arranged around layered gates, cleansing gardens, archive halls, and forest paths.
- Magical protection surrounds both Sable and the woods, making unauthorised routes turn, close, or return travellers to where they began.
- The climate is warm and green despite the harsher country farther north.

### **Culture & Sacred Role:**

Sable is the gate to the holiest place in Islefield elven tradition. All recognised Islefield elven births are conducted within Shercam Woods under the care of lifelong **Birth Keepers**, commonly called elven birthers.

Tradition teaches that:

- Elves born outside Shercam Woods do not receive the kingdom's characteristic silver hair.
- Such births are treated as a break from sacred lineage and may lead to social rejection within Islefield.
- The deeper within the forest a child is born, the greater the family's recognised nobility.
- The royal line gives birth near the sacred centre; Queen Atheliana was born at the innermost recognised site.

### **Economy & Duties:**

- Sable receives families, verifies ancestry, prepares birth processions, maintains records, and protects returning parents and children.
- Guardians control all material entering the woods.
- Healers, midwives, genealogists, ritualists, cooks, and ward tenders form most of the population.
- Ordinary foreign trade is conducted through outer representatives rather than inside the village.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Elven Holy Gate:** Only recognised entrance to Shercam Woods.
- **The Lineage Hall:** Genealogical archive determining access and ceremonial depth.
- **The Birth Keepers' Court:** Residence, school, and medical centre for the lifelong birthing order.
- **The Outer Office:** Boundary where non-elven diplomats and traders must stop.
- **The Silver Path:** Protected route leading into the forest's layered sacred grounds.

### **Relationships:**

- **Shercam Woods:** Sable exists to protect and administer access to the sacred forest.
- **Ansdale:** First Kluimont settlement beyond the nearby border, with contact kept formal and limited.
- **Yhey:** Provides regional military support and receives travellers unable to enter Sable.
- **Esterhull:** Connected through a restricted teleportation circle used for approved sacred and emergency travel.
- **Islefield City:** The royal household directly supervises Sable's highest offices.

## DM Notes

- The silver-hair and shunning rules are Islefield's established magical and cultural doctrine. Whether every biological claim is objectively true can remain uncertain; the social consequences are real regardless.
