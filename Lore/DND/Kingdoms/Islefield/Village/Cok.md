# Cok

### **Kingdom:**

Islefield

### **Settlement Type:**

Village - bridge checkpoint, often called a small town by travellers

### **Government:**

A Checkpoint Captain commands the garrison while a household Elder manages civilian matters.

### **Population:**

**1,350**, including soldiers and their families

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Elf (49%): Officers, soldiers, officials, and established families.
- Human (35%): Soldiers, workers, traders, and residents.
- Half-Elf (8%): Military and civilian households.
- Halfling, Gnome, Dwarf, and Other (8%): Craftspeople, suppliers, and specialists.

### **Wealth:**

★★★½☆ (3.5 stars)

- Garrison wages, inspection fees, food, lodging, and bridge traffic sustain the village.
- Most businesses serve soldiers or travellers waiting for clearance.
- Civilian wealth depends heavily on royal spending.

### **Military:**

★★★★★ (5 stars)

- Cok exists to house soldiers who man the checkpoint where the bridge reaches Islefield's capital-side territory.
- Gate towers, road barriers, archers, inspection yards, and rapid signals make it one of the strongest villages in the kingdom.
- Reinforcements can arrive from Cheling or the capital corridor.

### **Happiness:**

★★★½☆ (3.5 stars)

- Employment and security are reliable.
- Inspections, shift bells, crowded barracks, and constant official oversight limit privacy.
- Soldiers' families give Cok a more permanent community than most checkpoints.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Cok sits beside the small bridge separating mainland Islefield from the land holding the capital-side settlements.
- Beyross lies roughly 30 kilometres back along the mainland road.
- The village is compact, with barracks and inspection yards occupying more ground than civilian homes.
- River mist and humid coastal weather are common.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Inns, kitchens, stables, smithing, document services, and supply contracts dominate local trade.
- Cargo is inspected before continuing toward Cheling and Islefield City.
- Travellers rejected at the checkpoint are directed back toward Beyross rather than allowed to camp indefinitely.

### **History:**

- A military post has guarded the crossing since Islefield's early consolidation.
- Civilian households developed around permanent troop rotations and bridge maintenance.
- Cok was granted village status but retained the scale and routines of a fortress checkpoint.
- Improved roads shifted most commercial staging to Beyross, leaving Cok focused on security.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Capital-Side Gate:** Main fortified checkpoint.
- **The Inspection Yards:** Separate enclosures for travellers, animals, and cargo.
- **The Barracks Green:** Garrison housing and drill ground.
- **The Return House:** Office assisting rejected travellers with lawful routes and appeals.

### **Relationships:**

- **Beyross:** Last major mainland service town before the crossing.
- **Cheling:** Next major security layer on the route to the capital.
- **Islefield City:** Directs policy and funds the garrison.
