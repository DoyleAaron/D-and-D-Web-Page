# Okcaster

### **Kingdom:**

Islefield

### **Settlement Type:**

Village - isolated coastal ideological enclave

### **Government:**

A closed council divided between elven purity elders and human Tharnic restorationists.

### **Population:**

**1,100**

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Elf (52%): Purity families, fishers, farmers, hunters, and local leaders.
- Human (39%): Tharnic restorationist families, workers, and residents.
- Half-Elf (5%): Socially marginal despite local ancestry.
- Other (4%): Mostly temporary workers or residents unable to leave easily.
- Orcs and goblins are not permitted to settle.

### **Wealth:**

★★½☆☆ (2.5 stars)

- Fishing from small shore boats, coastal farming, weaving, and trade through Agosgas and Slido sustain the village.
- Okcaster has no proper port, limiting larger commerce.
- Ideological isolation and refusal to trade with many communities restrict growth.

### **Military:**

★★★½☆ (3.5 stars)

- Hunting groups, armed households, cliff lookouts, and hidden coastal paths make the village dangerous to approach casually.
- Residents drill more often than their economic position requires.
- There is no major fortification or fleet, and Agosgas could overpower the village if ordered to intervene.

### **Happiness:**

★★☆☆☆ (2 stars)

- Favoured families find belonging and certainty in rigid doctrine.
- Social surveillance, purity tests, suspicion, and hostility toward outsiders create an oppressive atmosphere.
- Young residents have limited contact with other communities and little freedom to challenge local beliefs.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Okcaster lies north of Agosgas on the same shore.
- Rocky beaches and shallow water prevent construction of a useful port.
- Homes and meeting halls sit above coastal cliffs among wind-bent trees.
- Inland tracks connect toward Slido, while shoreline paths lead south to Agosgas.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Small boats fish close to shore and beach their hulls rather than using docks.
- Grain, tools, and manufactured goods arrive by road.
- Local councils discourage dependence on merchants belonging to groups they consider inferior.
- Smuggling and private exchange persist because ideology cannot provide everything the village needs.

### **Beliefs & Culture:**

Two extremist traditions dominate Okcaster:

- **Elven purity circles** teach that elves should withdraw from or rule over shorter-lived peoples.
- **Tharnic restorationists** glorify the empire's order and military power while denying or justifying its enslavement and atrocities.

The movements disagree on history, race, and who should rule. They cooperate around the belief that orcs, goblins, and other so-called lesser peoples should be eradicated.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Pale Grove:** Meeting ground of the elven purity elders.
- **The Restored Standard:** Hall displaying reconstructed Tharnic symbols and military relics.
- **Cliffwatch:** Lookout and militia assembly point above the shore.
- **The Empty Strand:** Boat beach used in place of a harbour.

### **Relationships:**

- **Agosgas:** Main source of goods and authority; city officials monitor Okcaster uneasily.
- **Slido:** Inland market whose diversity is treated with suspicion but remains economically necessary.
- **Kluimont:** Object of hostility, especially because of its large orc and goblin population.

## DM Notes

- Okcaster's two factions are allies of convenience, not a unified ideology.
- Their exterminationist beliefs are real, but their ability to act beyond the village remains limited by Agosgas and Islefield law.
