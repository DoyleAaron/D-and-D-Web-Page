# Wrando

### **Kingdom:**

Islefield

### **Settlement Type:**

Village - river port and capital trade relay

### **Government:**

A Harbour Elder elected by boat owners, warehouse households, and permanent residents.

### **Population:**

**1,400**

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Human (42%): Boat crews, workers, merchants, fishers, and residents.
- Elf (38%): Officials, traders, landowners, and river families.
- Half-Elf (8%): Pilots, clerks, and mixed households.
- Halfling and Gnome (7%): Warehouse keepers, cooks, repairers, and shopkeepers.
- Dwarf and Other (5%): Smiths, engineers, and smaller communities.

### **Wealth:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Wrando's river connects directly to Islefield City, allowing quick movement of goods into the centre of the kingdom.
- Warehousing, boats, tolls, fishing, ferries, and Slido cargo create strong income for a village.
- Harbour families hold most commercial property, though regular work benefits the wider population.

### **Military:**

★★★☆☆ (3 stars)

- River wardens inspect cargo and patrol for smugglers or sabotage directed toward the capital.
- A chain boom can close the channel during emergencies.
- Slido and capital patrols can reinforce Wrando quickly by road or water.

### **Happiness:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars)

- Work is reliable, the village is close to Slido, and river travel makes supplies inexpensive.
- Flooding, inspections, and competition for dock space are recurring frustrations.
- Residents take pride in being the fastest ordinary trade route to the capital.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Wrando stands on a navigable river that runs directly toward Islefield City.
- Slido is close by, allowing road cargo to transfer efficiently onto boats.
- Docks, warehouses, and raised homes line the river while fields occupy higher ground.
- Heavy rain can raise water quickly, so flood markers and elevated walkways are common.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Capital-bound food, craft goods, timber, documents, and passengers pass through Wrando.
- Return boats carry luxury goods, official cargo, and manufactured items toward Slido.
- Fishing and ferry work supplement warehouse income.
- Pilots are licensed because the capital approach includes controlled channels and warded checkpoints.

### **History:**

- Wrando began as a fishing village where the river was deep enough for reliable cargo boats.
- Slido's growth transformed the village into a transfer point for inland trade.
- Islefield invested in docks and security once the river became a major capital supply route.
- Residents resisted annexation into Slido and retained village government under a harbour charter.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **Capital Quay:** Main loading point for direct river traffic.
- **The Chain House:** Controls the emergency river boom.
- **Highwater Walk:** Raised commercial street used during floods.
- **Slido Yard:** Road-to-river cargo exchange outside the residential centre.

### **Relationships:**

- **Slido:** Closest market and primary source of cargo.
- **Islefield City:** Direct river destination and source of regulation.
- **Agosgas and Okcaster:** Coastal goods sometimes enter the capital route through western roads to Wrando.
