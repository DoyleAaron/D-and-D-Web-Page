# Stanron - Destroyed

### **Kingdom:**

Islefield

### **Settlement Type:**

Destroyed Village / Royal Elven Burial Site

### **Administration:**

The royal household and a closed order of burial keepers based between Sable and Esterhull.

### **Population:**

**0 permanent public residents** (approximately 900 before its destruction)

### **Wealth:**

☆☆☆☆☆ (0 stars as a settlement)

- Grave goods and royal memorials may be materially valuable, but none are recognised as available wealth.
- Removal of even ordinary stone or vegetation is treated as desecration.

### **Military:**

★★★★☆ (4 stars, protective presence)

- Hidden wards, burial keepers, and royal patrols protect the site.
- Approaches appear neglected but are monitored before travellers reach the inner graves.
- Access requires direct royal authorisation.

### **Happiness:**

Not applicable.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Stanron lies near the southern routes connecting Esterhull, Ulsall, and the Prairie Kingfisher Grove.
- Ruined homes surround an older necropolis of pale stone, silver-leaved trees, and sunken ceremonial paths.
- Coastal humidity has covered ordinary buildings in vegetation while royal tomb structures remain unnaturally preserved.
- No maintained public road enters the inner site.

### **History:**

- Stanron began as a village serving the burial keepers of Islefield's royal line.
- Only true members of the royal family, rather than ordinary nobles, were interred in its deepest grounds.
- The settlement was destroyed long ago in an event removed from public histories.
- Survivors were relocated, the roads were closed, and the royal household took direct control of the ruins.
- Only the royal family and highest burial keepers are believed to know what actually destroyed Stanron.

### **Cultural Importance:**

Stanron is not treated as an abandoned village that might be reclaimed. It is a sealed royal necropolis. Elven families lower their voices when passing its outer roads, and even Esterhull's retired elite do not claim authority there.

The destruction did not end burials entirely. Rare royal processions still travel under magical concealment, though outsiders cannot confirm where within the site they go.

### **Visible Features:**

- **The Empty Village:** Overgrown homes and service buildings outside the sacred boundary.
- **The Silver Path:** Ceremonial road whose stones remain clean despite neglect.
- **The Royal Trees:** Memorial trees associated with individual royal dead.
- **The Sealed Descent:** Deepest burial entrance, inaccessible without royal rites.
- **The Broken Bell:** Village warning bell left where it fell during the destruction.

### **Relationships:**

- **Esterhull:** Nearest city and occasional source of approved support personnel.
- **Ulsall:** Travellers are warned there not to leave the maintained road near Stanron.
- **Sable and Shercam Woods:** Supply burial keepers and preserve related royal traditions.

## DM Notes

- The cause of Stanron's destruction is intentionally undefined because the user's canon states that only the royal family knows.
- Any future explanation should account for why the necropolis survived while the village did not and why the truth remains politically dangerous.
