# Ulsall

### **Kingdom:**

Islefield

### **Settlement Type:**

Village - luxury rest stop

### **Government:**

A Hospitality Council formed by inn houses, healers, bath keepers, and resident elders.

### **Population:**

**1,150**

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Elf (61%): Inn owners, healers, household staff, artisans, and residents.
- Human (23%): Workers, cooks, stable staff, and families.
- Half-Elf (7%): Healers, managers, and resident households.
- Halfling and Gnome (6%): Cooks, masseuses, brewers, and craftspeople.
- Other (3%): Sponsored specialists and long-term residents.

### **Wealth:**

★★★★★ (5 stars)

- Ulsall contains some of Islefield's finest inns, bathhouses, masseuses, and restorative services.
- Wealthy travellers between Cheling and Esterhull pay heavily for privacy and comfort.
- Much of the village's wealth is reinvested in gardens, water systems, staff training, and luxury supplies.

### **Military:**

★★☆☆☆ (2 stars)

- A small road watch and discreet private guards protect guests.
- Ulsall depends on Cheling patrols and Esterhull's wider safeguards against serious attack.
- Security prioritises prevention, confidentiality, and removing trouble quietly.

### **Happiness:**

★★★★½ (4.5 stars)

- Wages, public cleanliness, food, and housing are excellent for a village.
- Work can be demanding during elite travel seasons, and service hierarchies are rigid.
- Residents take genuine pride in hospitality recognised across Islefield.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Ulsall lies between Cheling and Esterhull on the protected southern road.
- Warm springs and carefully channelled water feed bathhouses and gardens.
- The village is compact, quiet, and screened from the road by trees and landscaped walls.
- Side paths near Stanron are marked closed and avoided.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Lodging, massage, bathing, healing, fine food, stabling, and discreet courier service dominate the economy.
- Cheling supplies ordinary goods while Esterhull patrons fund the highest-end establishments.
- Ulsall exports trained hospitality workers to elite households throughout Islefield.

### **History:**

- Ulsall began as a simple protected rest stop on the long southern road.
- Retired elites travelling to Esterhull demanded better beds, food, and recovery services.
- Competing inns gradually transformed the stop into a wealthy specialist village.
- Strict quality customs prevent rapid expansion that might undermine its reputation.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **The Silver Reed:** Celebrated inn and bathhouse favoured by retired officials.
- **Seven Hands House:** Training hall for masseuses and restorative practitioners.
- **The Quiet Stables:** Secure lodging and care for valuable mounts.
- **Stanron Marker:** Formal warning where the forbidden burial road diverges.

### **Relationships:**

- **Esterhull:** Main source of wealthy patrons.
- **Cheling:** Supplier, security partner, and northern road connection.
- **Stanron:** Sacred neighbouring ruin that residents never treat as an attraction.
