# Guatpool

### **Kingdom:**

Islefield

### **Settlement Type:**

Village - Iroware foothill and mineral-spring settlement

### **Government:**

A village Elder advised by spring keepers, herders, and the families responsible for dragon offerings.

### **Population:**

**850** (growth has been repeatedly stunted by dragon attacks and mountain danger)

### **Racial Demographics:**

- Human (39%): Herders, farmers, workers, and resident families.
- Elf (38%): Spring keepers, scouts, landholders, and residents.
- Half-Elf (8%): Guides and mixed households.
- Dwarf (7%): Stoneworkers, miners, and spring engineers.
- Halfling, Gnome, and Other (8%): Healers, craftspeople, and smaller communities.

### **Wealth:**

★★½☆☆ (2.5 stars)

- Mineral springs, hardy livestock, mountain herbs, guiding, and limited stonework support the village.
- Dragon damage and fear of investment prevent large-scale growth.
- Spring visitors provide seasonal income but rarely remain long.

### **Military:**

★★☆☆☆ (2 stars)

- Lookouts, caves, warning horns, and evacuation drills are more important than direct defence.
- A village militia can handle raiders or animals but cannot defeat a mature dragon.
- Islefield patrols pass through irregularly because the foothill roads are difficult.

### **Happiness:**

★★½☆☆ (2.5 stars)

- Residents value the springs, dramatic landscape, and strong community.
- Fear of another dragon attack limits construction, travel, and confidence.
- The offering tradition provides reassurance but also resentment among families who contribute most.

### **Geography & Climate:**

- Guatpool sits at the foot of the Iroware Heights beside a naturally warm mineral pool.
- Dry highland wind meets humid Islefield air, producing sudden storms and sharp temperature changes.
- The village is built low against rock and includes several prepared shelters.
- Mountain trails begin nearby but are less reliable than the northern Resross route.

### **Economy & Trade:**

- Mineral bathing, medicinal salts, mountain herbs, goats, wool, and guide work form the economy.
- Small quantities of ore and unusual stone are traded when routes remain safe.
- Food and manufactured goods arrive from lower Islefield.
- Offerings to the dragon consume livestock, metalwork, coin, and crafted objects that might otherwise support growth.

### **Dragon Tradition:**

A copper dragon inhabits the Iroware Heights. Older attacks convinced Guatpool that walls and weapons could not guarantee safety. The village now leaves regular offerings at a high stone shelf beyond the last homes.

The arrangement is not a formal treaty. Attacks have decreased, but residents cannot prove whether the dragon accepts the tribute, ignores them for unrelated reasons, or simply enjoys the performance.

### **Notable Locations:**

- **Guat Pool:** Mineral spring giving the village its name.
- **The Offering Shelf:** High stone platform where tribute is left.
- **Shelter Row:** Reinforced communal chambers built into the foothill.
- **The Copper Horn:** Alarm sounded only for confirmed dragon sightings.

### **Relationships:**

- **Iroware Heights:** Source of resources, climate, danger, and the copper dragon.
- **Resross:** Northern mountain access point and source of news about dragon sightings.
- **Yhey:** Market for minerals and highland products.

## DM Notes

- The copper dragon's motives and whether it truly demands the offerings remain open.
- It may be the same dragon observed from Resross, which is the simplest geographic interpretation.
