# Session 25 Summary - The Spehlina

## Into the Lake

- The party and their hired allies sailed out to confront the Spehlina.
- The Spehlina appeared as a corrupted lake guardian, twisted by Maltrius's influence and the spreading infernal corruption.
- The recruited side crews and support characters helped make the fight manageable, though the battle was still extremely dangerous.

---

## The Battle

- The party made relatively easy work of the Spehlina compared with the scale of the threat.
- Morjor and Amdir were both pushed into death saving throws during the fight.
- Varin kept the party alive at critical moments.
- The main party survived, but side characters died in the battle.

---

## Death Throes

- When the Spehlina died, its stored corruption exploded outward across the lake.
- The blast killed side characters and nearly took Morjor and Amdir with it.
- Varin used the corrupted gem on another character rather than on Amdir or Morjor.
- The lake was left corrupted by the Spehlina's death.

---

## Varin's Purification

- After the explosion, Varin used his draconic arm to purify the corrupted lake.
- This marked the end of the Spehlina threat and restored the lake from the worst of the released corruption.
- The session ended after the lake was purified.

---

## Major Outcomes

- The Spehlina was killed.
- Several allied or side characters died in the aftermath.
- Morjor and Amdir survived death saving throws.
- Varin saved lives and purified the lake with his arm.
- The party completed the mission and could return to claim their reward.
