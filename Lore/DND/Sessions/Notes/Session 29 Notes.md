# Session 29 Summary - Eye Golem and Ossian

## Deeper Into the Ossian Vault

- The party continued through the Ossian Vault after clearing most of its central and eastern sections.
- The remaining major threats were the Eye Golem, Ossian, Vasskren, and the sealed chamber beyond them.
- The dungeon's time distortion remained active: **1 hour inside equalled 1 day outside**.

---

## Dungeon Time

- This stretch added about **3 hours and 30 minutes inside the dungeon**.
- That meant about **3.5 days passed outside**.
- Combined with the earlier dungeon time, the vault expedition had become a serious calendar pressure point.

---

## The Eye Golem

- The party reached the Eye Golem chamber, a circular room covered in carved open eyes.
- The Eye Golem was the last of the major sentinel golems still active.
- Its witness-light could incapacitate creatures caught in its cone, forcing the party to deal with positioning and cover rather than simply looking away.
- The party defeated the Eye Golem.

---

## Eye Chamber Loot

After the Eye Golem fell, the party could claim the chamber's major finds:

- **Tome of Avethis**, an unsettling Old Elvish manuscript tied to the watching principle and the cult's founding.
- **220 gp** in old Tharnic gold.
- **Shard of Discernment**, the Eye Golem's core, able to sense active deception once per day.

---

## Ossian's Chamber

- The party then reached Ossian's chamber.
- Ossian lay in a sarcophagus surrounded by preserved worshippers.
- Disturbing Ossian, the worshippers, or the Cloak of the Watcher's Veil triggered the necromancer encounter.
- The fight included Ossian and his wights.

---

## Ossian Defeated

- The party defeated Ossian.
- When Ossian fell, the remaining active wights crumbled.
- The Eye Door reopened, and the dungeon began moving toward its final sealed chamber.
- Ossian's defeat cleared the way for the Vasskren decision and the door to Room 17.

---

## Major Finds From Ossian's Chamber

- **Cloak of the Watcher's Veil**, the Islefield mission objective.
- **Ossian's Journal**.
- **340 gp** in Tharnic coins.
- A possible **Scroll of Speak with Dead** from the reliquary.
- A ceremonial iron key, if recovered, that could serve as a fallback for the sealed door.

---

## Major Outcomes

- The Eye Golem was defeated.
- Ossian was defeated.
- The dungeon's undead presence was heavily broken.
- The party obtained or gained access to major Ossian and Avethis lore.
- Vasskren and the sealed door remained for the next session.
