# Session 23 Summary - Return to Blackthorn House

## Scouting the Manor

- After escaping Blackthorn House once, the party returned with a more careful plan.
- Varin scouted the manor in fly form, giving the group a safer look at the vampire-held house before they committed to another entry.
- The house was now on alert after the previous escape, but Liora was still believed to be somewhere inside.

---

## Second Break-In

- The party broke back into Blackthorn House through the rear of the building.
- They used sunlight and radiant magic to weaken or disable the vampires long enough to move through the house.
- Instead of trying to fully clear the manor room by room, they focused on reaching the lower levels and finding Liora.

---

## Basement Rescue

- The party pushed into the basement, where Liora was being held.
- A golem guarded the lower level and became the main obstacle between the party and the rescue.
- The party destroyed the golem and freed Liora from captivity.

---

## Major Outcomes

- Liora was rescued from Blackthorn House.
- The party recovered from the failed first assault and turned the situation into a successful extraction.
- The vampires at Blackthorn House were disrupted, but the wider Maltrius threat remained active.
- With Liora safe, the party could finally return to Dawerton and deal with the promised payment and the Spehlina threat.
