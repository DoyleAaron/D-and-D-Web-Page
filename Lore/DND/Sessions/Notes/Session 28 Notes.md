# Session 28 Summary - Into the Ossian Vault

## Journey to the Vault

- After the Islefield delegation's offer, the party travelled south toward the Ironwarf Heights.
- The job was presented as a search for an important item supposedly connected to the Balthazar artifact network.
- The destination was the Ossian Vault, an old Tharnic facility later reshaped by Ossian into something stranger.

---

## Time in the Dungeon

- The party spent **12 hours inside the dungeon** during this stretch.
- **4 of those hours included a rest**.
- Because the Ossian Vault has unusual time behavior, the outside-calendar impact should be confirmed carefully against when the party entered and exited.

---

## Dungeon Progress

The party made strong progress through the central and eastern parts of the vault.

- Explored and looted the **Room 2** area tied to the Amethyst Golem and ghoul aftermath.
- Reached **Room 6**, the Grand Hall.
- Explored **Room 13**, the main work area.
- Explored **Room 14**, the conference room.
- Found **Room 18**, Ossian's hidden wall room.
- Passed through **Room 9** but did not properly search it, leaving loot and notes behind.

---

## Fights and Dangers

The dungeon included undead and construct threats, including:

- A gargoyle.
- Weeping angels.
- The Amethyst Golem.
- Skeletons.
- Ghouls.

These encounters made the vault feel less like an abandoned ruin and more like a guarded structure still reacting to intruders.

---

## Important Finds

- Morjor found the **3 blue-tipped iron rods** in Room 18.
- The party recovered several keys, scrolls, coins, gems, and Ossian-linked documents.
- Room 14's records hinted at the Eye, the inner chamber, and the idea that anyone who reaches the chamber may be marked by being witnessed.
- Room 18 confirmed Ossian had a hidden workspace and extra arcane conductor components.

---

## Unresolved Threads

- Room 9 was passed through but not searched.
- Room 3 still held **Ossian's Cipher Journal** if the party reached and searched it later.
- Room 4 remained tied to the Eye Golem key route.
- The party had not yet reached **Room 17**, the true Eye Chamber / Sight of Avethis reveal.

---

## Major Outcomes

- The party committed to the Ossian Vault expedition.
- They cleared a significant portion of the dungeon.
- They gathered loot, keys, and lore pointing toward Ossian, Avethis, and the Eye.
- The dungeon time count became important: **12 hours inside**, including **4 hours with a rest**.
