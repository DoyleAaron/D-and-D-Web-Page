# Session 27 Summary - Islefield's Offer

## After the Spehlina

- With the Spehlina defeated and the reward claimed, the party had a short window to recover in Dawerton.
- The wider threat was not over: the summit was approaching, magical items were still being moved or consumed, and Maltrius remained active.
- The party's success made them harder for political powers to ignore.

---

## Islefield Contact

- An Islefield delegation approached the party after the Spehlina arc.
- The lead envoy, **Tharion Aelindre**, represented the Islefield Second Council.
- His offer involved a supposedly important item in southern Islefield, near the Ironwarf Heights and Resross.
- The stated reason was a possible connection to the Balthazar artifact network.

---

## Political Tension

- The offer carried extra weight because of Amdir and Morjor's ties to Islefield.
- Any previous travel restrictions or political issues were temporarily suspended for the job.
- The delegation was clearly watching the party's capabilities and had its own agenda beyond the official explanation.

---

## Toward the Ossian Vault

- The party accepted or moved toward the Islefield job, setting up the dungeon arc that would eventually lead to Vasskren.
- This marked the transition from Dawerton and the lake crisis into the most recent dungeon expedition.

---

## Major Outcomes

- Islefield directly approached the party.
- The next major objective became the southern Islefield dungeon near the Ironwarf Heights.
- The party's political entanglements deepened ahead of the queens' summit.
- The path toward the Vasskren dungeon began.
