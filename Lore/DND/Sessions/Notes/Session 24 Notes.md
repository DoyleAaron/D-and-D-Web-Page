# Session 24 Summary - Liora's Payment and the Spehlina Crew

## Returning with Liora

- After rescuing Liora from Blackthorn House, the party brought her back to Darwaeton.
- Liora followed through on the promised business from the Queen's support and began arranging the 10,000 gp payment.
- The rescue confirmed that Maltrius's forces were acting directly around Darwaeton, not only from Lavalto.

---

## Spehlina Preparations

- With the payment request in motion, the party shifted focus to the Spehlina.
- The creature was still a major threat to the lake and the coming summit plans.
- The party went to the port to recruit help for a lake battle rather than face the creature alone.

---

## Hiring Allies

The prep around the docks centered on finding ships, fighters, and divine support for the fight.

- **Captain Harlan Dusk** and the crew of **The Ironwake** were available through Captain Kirk's contacts.
- **Grint Copperhand** and the Copperhand sellswords could be hired as paid muscle.
- **Seyla Vane** and the Driftwood Four could bring another adventuring crew and **The Luckpenny**.
- **Rekka Redtooth** was a possible Bahamut-aligned ally, especially if Varin asked.
- Local clerics, including figures like **Father Edric Ironvow**, **Brother Aldwin Oathheart**, and **Mother Thessaly Brightwater**, were potential sources of radiant support.

---

## Major Outcomes

- Liora survived and resumed helping the party.
- The 10,000 gp payment process was set in motion.
- The party committed to the Spehlina mission.
- The port recruitment turned the next fight into a larger lake operation with hired and allied support.
