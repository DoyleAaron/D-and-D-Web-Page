# Session 30 Summary - Vasskren and the Door

## Final Vault Battle

- After defeating the Eye Golem and Ossian, the party faced Vasskren.
- Vasskren was the final major guardian between the party and the sealed chamber deeper in the Ossian Vault.
- The battle centered on defeating Vasskren and claiming the pieces needed to open the door.

---

## Vasskren Defeated

- The party fought Vasskren.
- The party defeated Vasskren.
- With Vasskren beaten, the party was able to unlock the sealed door.

---

## The Door Opens

- The sealed door to Room 17 could be opened through Vasskren's five cores or Ossian's fallback key.
- Opening the door gave the party access to the Eye Chamber, the Sight of Avethis, and the deepest lore hidden in the vault.

---

## The Sight of Avethis

- The Eye Chamber offers a permanent one-use boon: each character may choose to see any point in time, past or future, as a witness.
- The party identified three visions they want to see.

### Vision Requests

1. How the great queen of Islefield lost the ring.
2. When the Balthazar items were last used.
3. What made Prisoner 7 become Prisoner 7.

---

## Room 17 Revelations

The Eye Chamber contains Ossian's Research Codex, which reveals the vault's deepest truths:

- Gaia made three great powers: a ring, a crown, and an amulet.
- The Chart of the Threefold Way was a guide to locate where the powers were hidden.
- The act of hiding them forced even Gaia to lose exact knowledge of where they rested.
- Gaia made the Eye as a companion and watcher.
- Ossian identifies the Eye as Avethis.
- The central reveal: **Gaia is Balthazar**.
- Ossian's final clue points toward records of Gaia beneath Islefield, in a sealed lower archive.

---

## Major Outcomes

- Vasskren was defeated.
- The sealed door was unlocked.
- The party gained access to the Eye Chamber and the Sight of Avethis.
- The party chose three major vision questions.
- The vault revealed the Gaia/Balthazar connection and pointed toward Islefield's hidden lower archive.
