# Session 26 Summary - Reward and Spehlina Aftermath

## Return to Darwaeton

- After killing the Spehlina and purifying the lake, the party returned to Darwaeton.
- The docks were tense and unsettled after the distant shock of the lake battle and the Spehlina's death throes.
- The surviving crews and allies returned with the party, while the cost of the battle settled in.

---

## Claiming the Reward

- The party claimed the 10,000 gp reward for completing the Spehlina mission.
- The payment was handled through the crown's local representative, with the money available for the party to split and spend.
- The victory strengthened the party's standing with Braewood and Darwaeton.

---

## Divine and Infernal Fallout

- The Spehlina's death created immediate fallout for the party's larger divine and infernal threads.
- Varin's connection through his draconic arm became central after he used it to purify the lake.
- The party's success also drew more attention from forces tied to Maltrius, Balthazar, Bahamut, and the missing magical items.

---

## Major Outcomes

- The Spehlina mission was officially completed.
- The party received the 10,000 gp reward.
- Darwaeton and the lake were spared the worst long-term effects of the corruption.
- The campaign pivoted from the lake crisis toward the summit, missing items, and outside political attention.
