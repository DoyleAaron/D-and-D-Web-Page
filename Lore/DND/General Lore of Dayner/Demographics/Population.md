# Population of Dayner

Dayner's total settled population is approximately **2.25 million**. These figures are estimates assembled from crown censuses, port records, temple rolls, tax ledgers, and local counts. They include people living on farms, estates, small hamlets, islands, and seasonal routes rather than only the named settlements shown on maps.

Population records should not be treated as exact. Kluimont restricts information, Lavalto conceals residents, and many Braewood and Islefield communities report households rather than individuals.

## Braewood

Braewood is the largest realm by land area and population. Its named cities and towns are only part of the total; large numbers live on crown estates, farms, military lands, mining enclaves, and remote roads.

| People | Approximate Share | Approximate Number |
|---|---:|---:|
| Humans | 55% | 580,000 |
| Dwarves | 20% | 210,000 |
| Elves | 5% | 50,000 |
| Halflings and Gnomes | 5% | 50,000 |
| Orcs, Goblins, and Half-Orcs | 5% | 50,000 |
| Other peoples | 10% | 110,000 |

- Humans predominate in the central and southern heartlands.
- Northern Braewood contains Dayner's largest concentration of dwarven communities, especially around mines, clanholds, and metalworking settlements.
- Legal status does not guarantee equal treatment. Local prejudice varies sharply, with some northern and military communities particularly hostile toward orcs, goblins, tieflings, and people viewed as outsiders.
- Long distances and a large standing army encourage cities to maintain their own supplies, militias, and surrounding rural populations.

## Islefield

Islefield combines dense cities with extensive farmland, managed woodland, fishing communities, estates, and sacred territories. Its large rural population explains why named settlements account for only part of the kingdom's total.

| People | Approximate Share | Approximate Number |
|---|---:|---:|
| Elves | 40% | 300,000 |
| Humans | 40% | 300,000 |
| Dwarves | 5% | 37,500 |
| Halflings and Gnomes | 5% | 37,500 |
| Orcs, Goblins, and Half-Orcs | 3% | 22,500 |
| Other peoples | 7% | 52,500 |

- Elven populations are especially concentrated around [[Kingdoms/Islefield/POI/Shercam Woods|Shercam Woods]], [[Kingdoms/Islefield/Village/Sable|Sable]], the capital region, and wealthy southern enclaves.
- Humans are common across farms, trade corridors, mixed cities, and northern districts.
- Social standing depends on ancestry, class, sponsorship, and location. [[Kingdoms/Islefield/Medium Cities/Yhey|Yhey]] is openly divided, while [[Kingdoms/Islefield/Town/Beyross|Beyross]] and [[Kingdoms/Islefield/Town/Slido|Slido]] are more mixed.
- Elves born outside Shercam's recognised traditions may face exclusion even when they are citizens of Islefield.

## Kluimont

Kluimont's population is spread across the guarded capital island, the [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/POI/Rimduff Isles|Rimduff Isles]], and loosely governed mainland territory. Named settlements account for most residents, with the remainder living in minor islands, farms, forest communities, vessels, and transient camps.

| People | Approximate Share | Approximate Number |
|---|---:|---:|
| Humans | 40% | 140,000 |
| Orcs, Goblins, and Half-Orcs | 25% | 87,500 |
| Elves | 5% | 17,500 |
| Dwarves | 5% | 17,500 |
| Halflings and Gnomes | 5% | 17,500 |
| Other peoples | 20% | 70,000 |

- Kluimont has Dayner's highest proportion of orc and goblin residents. Acceptance is greater than elsewhere but does not eliminate poverty, exploitation, or local prejudice.
- Humans remain the largest single population and the most common public face of trade and administration.
- Tieflings, dragonborn, genasi, aquatic peoples, and other less common ancestries are drawn by arcane work, maritime trade, smuggling, and looser mainland customs.
- Entry to [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Capital/Kluimont City|Kluimont City]] is tightly controlled through [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Medium Cities/Qrey|Qrey]], so population and migration records from the capital island are unusually guarded.

## Lavalto

Lavalto is independent of the three kingdoms and has an estimated population of **100,000**. Almost all residents live in or around [[Kingdoms/Lavalto/Large Cities/Darwaeton|Darwaeton]], [[Kingdoms/Lavalto/Medium Cities/Flison|Flison]], and [[Kingdoms/Lavalto/Town/Agosron|Agosron]]. Small farms, estates, mines, shrines, and work camps account for the remainder.

| People | Approximate Share | Approximate Number |
|---|---:|---:|
| Humans | 60% | 60,000 |
| Orcs, Goblins, and Half-Orcs | 10% | 10,000 |
| Elves | 5% | 5,000 |
| Dwarves | 5% | 5,000 |
| Halflings and Gnomes | 5% | 5,000 |
| Other and undeclared peoples | 15% | 15,000 |

- Darwaeton contains the overwhelming majority of the island's registered population and its most diverse public districts.
- Flison has unusually large orc, goblin, tiefling, and dwarf communities shaped by its fortress history.
- Agosron's official count is unreliable. Restricted estates, unregistered workers, and a concealed vampire population make any exact figure doubtful.
- The ruling undead are counted inconsistently or concealed within household records, so ancestry tables describe the visible population more reliably than the true one.
