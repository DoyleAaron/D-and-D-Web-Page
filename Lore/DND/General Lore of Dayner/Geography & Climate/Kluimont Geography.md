# Kluimont Geography

## Position

[[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Kluimont|Kluimont]] is a northwestern arcane and maritime kingdom divided across three geographic systems: the capital island, the [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/POI/Rimduff Isles|Rimduff Isles]], and a broad mainland territory west of Braewood and north of Islefield.

## Capital Island

The capital island contains [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Capital/Kluimont City|Kluimont City]], [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Medium Cities/Qrey|Qrey]], the ruins of Lloy, and [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/POI/Tempawa Forest|Tempawa Forest]]. Qrey controls ordinary access. The island interior is restricted through roads, wards, patrols, and invitation law.

Tempawa's western old-growth forest contains unstable magical conditions associated publicly with fey acoustics and privately with Bagi the Brass Tempest.

## Rimduff Isles

The Rimduff Isles form a broken chain between the capital island and mainland Kluimont. Reefs, fog, shallow channels, and local currents make pilots essential.

Major island communities include:

- [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Town/Tarora|Tarora]], a pirate harbour occupying most of its island
- [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Village/Aresphia|Aresphia]] and [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Village/Kilson|Kilson]], sharing a fertile island
- [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Village/Yuosburn|Yuosburn]] and [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Village/Ukrolis|Ukrolis]], divided by dense pathless forest
- [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/POI/Erymont|Erymont]], an isolated hostile camp

## Mainland Territory

Mainland Kluimont contains Eimgend, Slonmore, Sord, Ajag, Ansdale, Klovine, Gray Toad Woods, and scattered farms or enclaves. Government investment is strongest around roads and border gates and weakest in remote communities.

The mainland includes river systems connecting Ajag's lake to the coast, wet woodland, productive valleys, and strategic crossings toward both neighbouring kingdoms.

## Major Geographic Features

- [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/POI/Tempawa Forest|Tempawa Forest]] - Ancient warded forest and centre of Kluimont's dragon secret.
- [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/POI/Rimduff Isles|Rimduff Isles]] - Island chain of naval routes, independent communities, and pirate waters.
- [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/POI/Gray Toad Woods|Gray Toad Woods]] - Wet woodland inhabited by goblins, alchemists, smugglers, and fey creatures.
- [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/POI/The Flat Den|The Flat Den]] - Abandoned dragon nesting complex on the mainland.
- [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/POI/The Graveyard Arcana|The Graveyard Arcana]] - Restricted magical cemetery on the capital island.

## Climate

Kluimont is warm and maritime. Sea wind moderates temperature, but storms and fog can arrive quickly. The mainland has wetter river country and denser lowland forest, while small islands range from fertile and sheltered to bare and wave-beaten.

Magical weather around Tempawa can disrupt normal forecasts. Compasses, clocks, and ward-sensitive devices are least reliable near the forest and capital-island channels.

## Travel and Access

- Qrey's approved channels are the only ordinary route to the capital island.
- Eimgend is the largest open naval and commercial hub.
- Sord connects most mainland roads.
- Slonmore receives traffic from Phine; Klovine receives traffic from Mortling; Ansdale receives traffic from Sable.
- Island residents often travel by sea even when settlements share the same island because interiors may be forested, steep, or deliberately pathless.
