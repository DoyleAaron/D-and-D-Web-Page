# Islefield Geography

## Position

[[Kingdoms/Islefield/Islefield|Islefield]] occupies the southern and southwestern mainland. Its territory includes humid coasts, rich farmland, broad rivers, sacred forests, bridge corridors, and the dry northern highlands.

## Major Regions

### Capital-Side South

The land around Islefield City, Cheling, Cok, Ulsall, Esterhull, and the Prairie Kingfisher Grove is warm, humid, heavily managed, and strongly influenced by elven institutions. Roads and magical safeguards become more controlled closer to the capital and elite southern enclaves.

### The Grand Span Corridor

[[Kingdoms/Islefield/Large Cities/Rye|Rye]], [[Kingdoms/Islefield/Medium Cities/Antstel|Antstel]], and [[Kingdoms/Islefield/Town/Avinin|Avinin]] form a planned commercial and defensive network around the crossing to Braewood. Farmland and broad military roads dominate the surrounding country.

### Western Coast and Interior Roads

[[Kingdoms/Islefield/Medium Cities/Agosgas|Agosgas]], Okcaster, Beyross, Praso, Wrando, and Slido link the outer coast with the capital river and central roads. Beaches and sheltered coves give way inland to managed woodland, farms, and busy crossroads.

### Northern Border Country

Yhey, Sable, Resross, Guatpool, and the Iroware Heights occupy a harsher region of border roads, dry foothills, mountain trails, mines, and sacred restrictions. The climate here contrasts sharply with Islefield's southern plains.

## Major Geographic Features

- [[Kingdoms/Islefield/POI/Prairie Kingfisher Grove|Prairie Kingfisher Grove]] - Magical prairie-forest transition supplying much of Islefield's renewable wardcraft.
- [[Kingdoms/Islefield/POI/Shercam Woods|Shercam Woods]] - Most sacred elven forest and recognised birthplace of Islefield's elven lineages.
- [[Kingdoms/Islefield/POI/Iroware Heights|Iroware Heights]] - Vast dry highland also called the Ironwarf Heights in local and military records.
- [[Kingdoms/Islefield/POI/Parrneau Hollow|Parrneau Hollow]] - Sheltered basin containing old stones, springs, and lightly protected druidic ground.
- [[Kingdoms/Braewood/POI/The Grand Span|The Grand Span]] - Major bridge connecting Rye to Kadena.

## Climate

- Southern coasts are warm, humid, and moderated by sea wind.
- Central plains receive enough rain for reliable grain, orchards, pasture, and managed woodland.
- The capital region benefits from magical water management and protective natural systems.
- The Iroware Heights lie in a rain shadow, producing desert basins, exposed ridges, hot days, and cold nights.
- Northern and border roads experience sudden wind, dust, flash floods, and mountain storms.

## Rivers and Trade

Rivers connect the capital to Wrando and the interior, move ore toward Yhey, and support farmland throughout the kingdom. Slido acts as the main inland road distributor, while Rye handles the largest foreign land crossing.

## Sacred Geography

Islefield treats some landscapes as institutions rather than wilderness:

- Shercam Woods controls recognised elven birth and lineage traditions.
- Sable protects the only lawful entrance to Shercam.
- The Prairie Kingfisher Grove sustains protective wards and royal symbolism.
- Stanron remains a sealed royal necropolis rather than an abandoned settlement available for reclamation.

## Borders

- **Braewood:** Connected through the Grand Span and southern routes.
- **Kluimont:** Crossings near Yhey, Sable, and Ansdale combine trade with intense scrutiny.
- **Lavalto:** Accessible through the lake and northern roads; volcanic influence reaches the highlands and surrounding water.
