# Braewood Geography

## Position

[[Kingdoms/Braewood/Braewood|Braewood]] occupies the eastern and central mainland and is Dayner's largest realm by land area and population. Its territory extends from northern peninsulas and dwarven uplands through the central river heartland to warm southern farmland and coast.

## Major Regions

### Northern Peninsula and Uplands

The north is drier, rockier, and more exposed than southern Braewood. Dwarven settlements, mines, old Tharnic workings, narrow lakes, and wind-scoured roads are common. [[Kingdoms/Braewood/Medium City/Strane|Strane]], [[Kingdoms/Braewood/Medium City/Barge|Barge]], and the northern towns serve as the principal centres.

### Central Heartland

Braewood City, the main river, Cleamond, Sheyton, and Mortling occupy the political and military core. Rivers and maintained roads carry food, troops, ore, and manufactured goods through this region.

### Southern Country

The south is warmer and more fertile. Trade corridors connect Eaveton, Gitstin, Osegas, Kadena, and numerous farming or fishing villages. The southern road network is denser than the north but still shaped by long distances and local supply needs.

### Eastern Coast

Braewood's eastern coast supports fishing, salt, ports, naval patrols, and trade around Ardismouth, Ioxyhull, Oyard, and Rens. Weather can change quickly where warm inland air meets stronger sea wind.

## Major Geographic Features

- [[Kingdoms/Braewood/POI/Meriver Mountains|Meriver Mountains]] - Important northern heights associated with mines, passes, and dwarven territory.
- [[Kingdoms/Braewood/POI/The Grand Span|The Grand Span]] - The immense bridge connecting Kadena to Rye in Islefield.
- [[Kingdoms/Braewood/POI/Wynlants Cavity|Wynlants Cavity]] - Deep sinkhole and cave system with Tharnic and rebel history.
- [[Kingdoms/Braewood/POI/Buchrior Hollows|Buchrior Hollows]] - Forested hollows associated with dwarven memory and autonomy.
- [[Kingdoms/Braewood/POI/Debourg Grotto|Debourg Grotto]] - Restricted cavern region tied to the vanished Debourg settlement.
- [[Kingdoms/Braewood/POI/Plalisle Den|Plalisle Den]] - Ancient site connected to Balthazar and the Chart of the Threefold Way.

## Climate

Braewood is not uniformly cold or dry:

- Northern coasts and uplands are windy, fog-prone, and colder in winter.
- Central regions are warm but seasonally dry, with productive river valleys.
- Southern plains and coasts are mild, fertile, and suitable for grain, livestock, orchards, and fishing.
- Storms affect the exposed eastern coast, while drought and dust are greater concerns inland and north.

## Settlement and Travel Pattern

Long distances encourage every major city to maintain food stores, militias, repair yards, and local government. Braewood's army protects strategic roads, but frontier routes remain vulnerable to weather, monsters, banditry, and regional disputes.

The largest corridors connect:

- Braewood City, Cleamond, Eaveton, and Mortling
- Eaveton, Gitstin, Osegas, and Kadena
- Barge, the northern towns, and dwarven mining country
- Coastal ports and the main river network

## Borders

- **Islefield:** Connected most visibly by the Grand Span and southern trade corridors.
- **Kluimont:** Western and northwestern crossings include the Phine-Slonmore and Mortling-Klovine routes.
- **Lavalto:** Reached across the central lake and river systems rather than by a continuous land border.
