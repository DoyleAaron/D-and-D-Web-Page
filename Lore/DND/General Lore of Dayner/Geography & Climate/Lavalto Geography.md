# Lavalto Geography

## Position

[[Kingdoms/Lavalto/Lavalto|Lavalto]] is an independent volcanic island within the Great Lavalto Lake. The island is dominated by a connected volcanic massif nearly 60 kilometres across, leaving most permanent settlement on a dangerous ring of coastal and lower-slope ground.

## Settled Coast

### Darwaeton

[[Kingdoms/Lavalto/Large Cities/Darwaeton|Darwaeton]] occupies the principal northeastern harbour. It controls most legal foreign trade, island diplomacy, and access to maintained roads.

### Flison

[[Kingdoms/Lavalto/Medium Cities/Flison|Flison]] stands in the northwestern corner on high cliffs overlooking the passage toward Kluimont. Its old Tharnic fortress, layered sea gates, and restricted harbour make it difficult to approach.

### Agosron

[[Kingdoms/Lavalto/Town/Agosron|Agosron]] lies on the southern volcanic coast beneath steep ridges. Its concealed harbour and hostile shore defences isolate it from ordinary travel.

## Major Geographic Features

- [[Kingdoms/Lavalto/POI/Lavalto Volcano|Lavalto Volcano]] - Active massif of calderas, vents, ash fields, quarries, lava tubes, and unstable high slopes.
- [[Kingdoms/Lavalto/POI/Darford Cavity|Darford Cavity]] - Collapsed lava chamber associated with sacrifice, hauntings, curses, and infernal stories.
- **The Great Lavalto Lake** - Ring of water connecting the island to Braewood, Islefield, and Kluimont trade routes.
- **The Red Ember Fields** - Scarred terrain left by the 1191 eruption.
- **The Glass Slopes** - Obsidian-rich ridges worked by licensed quarry crews.

## Climate and Hazards

Lavalto is warm, humid, and geothermally active.

- Ashfall can reduce visibility and contaminate water or crops.
- Tremors are common enough to shape construction practices.
- Heavy rain on ash can create sudden mudflows.
- Sulfurous air, steam vents, glass-sharp ground, and unstable lava tubes make inland travel dangerous.
- Older stable lava terraces support fertile soil, unusual plants, grazing, and estate agriculture.

## Roads and Movement

A maintained road connects Darwaeton and Flison, though civilian traffic is light. Agosron has no open direct road to either city. Estate tracks and volcanic routes are guarded, trapped, omitted from maps, or usable only with local knowledge.

Ships generally provide safer movement around the island than crossing the interior. Even coastal approaches require pilots because of reefs, vents, patrols, and restricted harbours.

## Resources

Lavalto exports obsidian, volcanic glass, sulfur, pumice, mineral salts, rare ores, alchemical plants, and heat-worked craft. It imports much of its grain, timber, livestock, cloth, and ordinary manufactured goods.

## Historical Layers

The Tharnic Empire fortified and exploited parts of the island. Old walls, roads, foundations, mines, and sealed chambers survive beneath later construction. Modern rulers reused many of those sites rather than building on untouched ground.

The Night of Red Embers in 1191 damaged Darwaeton, burned farmland, and reshaped parts of the coast. Whether ritual magic caused or intensified the eruption remains disputed.
