# Valkur - The Mighty

| | |
|---|---|
| **Domains** | Sailors, ships, courage, favourable winds |
| **Alignment** | Chaotic Good |
| **Symbol** | A cloud crossed by three lightning bolts |
| **Worship Centres** | Aresphia, Kilson, the Rimduff Isles |

Valkur is the patron of sailors who face dangerous water through skill and courage rather than conquest. His followers value capable crews, aid for vessels in distress, and the belief that fear is natural but must not command the helm.

## Aresphia and Kilson

On the island shared by [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Village/Aresphia|Aresphia]] and [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Village/Kilson|Kilson]], Valkur shapes everyday life.

- Aresphia maintains a practical harbour chapel and blesses crews leaving its bay.
- Kilson protects one of Dayner's largest temples to Valkur and treats water from its sacred springs as the beginning of every safe voyage.
- Aresphia permits neighbouring faiths more easily, while Kilson discourages competing public worship.

## Sailor Ethics

Valkur's followers admire bravery but reject pointless recklessness. Common teachings include:

- Keep the crew alive before protecting pride.
- Aid genuine distress when the sea allows it.
- A captain who ignores skilled counsel is not courageous.
- No oath made on safe land should be forgotten in dangerous water.

## Relationships

- **Selune:** Frequent ally in navigation and traveller protection.
- **The Drowned God:** Opposed because courage at sea is not the same as worshipping death beneath it.
- **Bagi's lighthouse laws:** Not formal Valkur doctrine, but many sailors consider the customs compatible.
