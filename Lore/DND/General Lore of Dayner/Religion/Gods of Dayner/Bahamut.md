# Bahamut - The Platinum Dragon

| | |
|---|---|
| **Domains** | Justice, protection, honour, metallic dragons |
| **Alignment** | Lawful Good |
| **Symbol** | A platinum dragon's head in profile |
| **Worship Centres** | Sord, scattered temples and military chapels |

Bahamut teaches that strength creates an obligation to protect rather than permission to dominate. Paladins, clerics, metallic dragons, soldiers, judges, and people seeking redemption are drawn to his worship.

## Worship in Dayner

Bahamut is recognised in every realm but has special importance in Kluimont because of the kingdom's history with metallic dragons. [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Town/Sord|Sord]] maintains the Platinum Shelter, a sanctuary known for feeding travellers who swear to keep the peace.

His followers often cooperate with Tyr's churches but place greater emphasis on protection, mercy, personal honour, and the possibility of becoming better than one's past.

## Varin

[[Character Lore/Player Characters/Varin (Aiden)|Varin]] is Bahamut's best-known active follower in the current campaign record. His divine connection was disrupted by infernal corruption but restored during the Spehlina crisis. His draconic arm and willingness to use divine power to purify rather than merely destroy became central to the lake's recovery.

## Relationships

- **Tyr:** Ally in justice, though Tyr's institutions favour law and judgement more strongly.
- **Asmodeus:** Direct enemy through opposition to tyranny, corruption, and infernal contracts.
- **Metallic dragons:** Revered as exemplars or servants without being treated as interchangeable with Bahamut himself.
- **Avethis:** Neither enemy nor natural ally; witness without judgement conflicts with Bahamut's demand to act against injustice.
