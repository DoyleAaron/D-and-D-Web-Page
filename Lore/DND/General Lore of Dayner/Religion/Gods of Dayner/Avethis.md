# Avethis - The Undying Gaze

| | |
|---|---|
| **Domains** | Witness, observation, memory, time |
| **Attitude** | Neutral and non-intervening |
| **Symbol** | An open eye with an hourglass pupil |
| **Known Centre** | The Eye Chamber beneath the Ossian Vault |

Avethis watches without judging. Past and future are not moral categories to the Eye; they are moments available to witness. Avethis does not promise protection, punishment, or guidance. It offers knowledge and leaves the consequences to the observer.

## Origin

The [[General Lore of Dayner/History/Recovered Books and Tomes/Ossian's Research Codex|Research Codex]] recovered from the Ossian Vault records that Gaia made the Eye as a companion and watcher at the dawn of the world.

Gaia is the being now known as Balthazar. The Eye remained after its maker moved on and, through ages of uninterrupted witness, became a god in its own right.

Avethis therefore differs from deities associated with nature, justice, war, or celestial realms. It was made for a purpose before becoming divine.

## The Cult of Ossian

[[Character Lore/Historic Characters/Valdrek Ossian|Valdrek Ossian]] devoted himself to Avethis and converted an abandoned Tharnic facility into a temple surrounding the older Eye Chamber.

His cult taught:

- To see is to know.
- The record does not disappear when a person dies.
- Witness is not the same as judgement.
- Knowledge changes the observer even when the Eye does nothing else.

Ossian surrounded the chamber with undead, constructs, writings, and Vasskren, but Avethis itself was not the attacking guardian.

## The Sight of Avethis

The Eye Chamber offered each visitor a permanent one-use boon: the ability to witness a chosen point in past or future. The gift does not guarantee context, safety, or a pleasing answer.

## Religious Questions

Scholars disagree over whether Avethis should be called a god, an ascended construct, an ancient spirit, or a surviving part of Balthazar's work. The distinction matters to institutions but not to the Eye, which continues to witness regardless of classification.

## Relationships

- **Balthazar / Gaia:** Maker and first subject of witness.
- **Bahamut and Tyr:** Their demand for moral action contrasts with Avethis's refusal to judge.
- **Ossian:** Devotee and researcher, not Avethis's creator.
