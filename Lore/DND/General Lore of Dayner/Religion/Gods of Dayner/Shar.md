# Shar - The Mistress of Night

| | |
|---|---|
| **Domains** | Darkness, loss, night, secrets, forgetfulness |
| **Alignment** | Neutral Evil |
| **Symbol** | A black disk encircled by violet |
| **Worship Centres** | Hidden shrines, selected Lavalto circles |

Shar attracts people shaped by grief, secrecy, resentment, and the desire to escape memory. Her doctrine teaches that loss reveals the emptiness beneath mortal attachments and that darkness can conceal pain as well as power.

## Worship in Dayner

Shar's worship is usually private or clandestine. Small cells appear in cities where disappearances, grief, and political secrecy create fertile ground. Some vampire households tolerate or adopt her rites, though Lavalto's undead do not follow one unified faith.

Followers vary from mourners seeking numbness to cultists who deliberately spread despair or erase memory. That range makes early identification difficult.

## Relationship to Selune

Selune is Shar's traditional opposite. Their conflict appears in sailor stories, temple art, dream interpretation, and rituals concerning memory or night travel.

## Relationship to the Red Scarves

Observers have noted similarities between Shar's language of secrecy and some Red Scarf teachings, but no confirmed public record proves the organisation serves Shar directly.
