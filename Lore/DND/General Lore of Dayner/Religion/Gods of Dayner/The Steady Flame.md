# The Steady Flame

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Multi-faith tradition rather than a single deity |
| **Domains** | Endurance, community, shelter, persistence |
| **Symbol** | A flame that does not bend or go out |
| **Worship Centres** | Strane, isolated settlements, shared shrines |

The Steady Flame is a practical religious tradition used where one community cannot maintain separate temples for every faith. Different gods receive distinct shrines beneath one roof, while the central flame represents the community's persistence through hardship.

## Principles

- No traveller in genuine need is refused warmth without cause.
- Different faiths may share care, food, and burial duties without surrendering doctrine.
- The shared flame belongs to the community, not one priesthood.
- Endurance is sacred when it protects life rather than merely prolonging suffering.

## Worship in Dayner

The best-known formal temple stands in [[Kingdoms/Braewood/Medium City/Strane|Strane]]. Smaller versions appear in military posts, remote villages, and places where migration has created mixed communities.

Darwaeton also supports multi-faith shrines serving sailors, workers, travellers, and residents who do not belong to the island's powerful infernal or vampiric circles.

## Clergy

Steady Flame clergy may be dedicated to one god while accepting duties to the shared temple. Others serve the communal tradition itself as healers, caretakers, mediators, and keepers of the central fire.

## Relationships

The Steady Flame can include shrines to Bahamut, Tyr, Selune, Valkur, and many local gods. Predatory or sacrificial cults are generally excluded because shared worship depends on trust and mutual safety.
