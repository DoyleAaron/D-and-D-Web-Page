# Asmodeus - Lord of the Nine Hells

| | |
|---|---|
| **Domains** | Tyranny, contracts, hierarchy, infernal power |
| **Alignment** | Lawful Evil |
| **Symbol** | A red pentagram or ruby-tipped sceptre |
| **Worship Centres** | Lavalto's infernal circles, hidden cults elsewhere |

Asmodeus is an archdevil rather than a conventional god, but his power rivals divine beings and he accepts worship. His doctrine treats hierarchy as natural, contracts as weapons, and power as proof of the right to rule.

## Worship on Lavalto

Infernal practice is most visible among powerful vampire households and priests who combine undeath with contract magic. It exists beside civic government, household cult, and ordinary public shrines rather than replacing them.

Father Maltrius Kael is the most important known infernal priest in the current record. His influence extends through vampire factions, corrupted magic, and plans surrounding the summit and Balthazar artefacts.

## The Spehlina

The [[Character Lore/Beasts & Creatures/The Spehlina|Spehlina]] was corrupted by infernal power and used as a threat against the lake and summit. The party defeated the creature and purified the water, ending the immediate crisis without eliminating the wider infernal network.

## Contracts and Corruption

Asmodean influence rarely begins with open worship. It appears through:

- Bargains made under impossible pressure
- Hierarchies promising safety in exchange for obedience
- Gifts that create dependence
- Rituals that turn debt into magical compulsion
- Corruption framed as efficient order

## Relationships

- **Bahamut and Tyr:** Direct enemies through opposition to tyranny and infernal corruption.
- **Shar:** Occasional overlap in secretive circles, but their doctrines differ sharply.
- **Avethis:** Asmodeus seeks control and action; Avethis only witnesses.
