# Tyr - The Maimed God

| | |
|---|---|
| **Domains** | Justice, law, war, courage |
| **Alignment** | Lawful Good |
| **Symbol** | Balanced scales resting on a warhammer |
| **Worship Centres** | Braewood courts, military chapels, garrisons |

Tyr represents justice upheld despite personal cost. His followers include judges, soldiers, investigators, paladins, prison chaplains, and officials who believe order must answer to a moral standard.

## Worship in Dayner

Tyr is strongest in Braewood, where courts and military institutions need a faith capable of blessing force while limiting its misuse. His chaplains commonly serve beside priests of Bahamut and other lawful gods.

The gap between Tyr's ideals and Braewood's actual institutions is a frequent source of conflict. A lawful sentence is not automatically just, and priests disagree over Crown authority, dwarven autonomy, prison treatment, and discrimination.

## Father Halden

Father Halden was a priest who fell to corruption and was later slain during the Darwaeton investigation. His fate is cited as a warning that office, training, and a righteous symbol do not make a servant immune to manipulation.

## Relationships

- **Bahamut:** Close ally; Tyr emphasises judgement and law while Bahamut emphasises protection and honour.
- **Asmodeus:** Enemy, particularly where law is twisted into tyranny or infernal contract.
- **The Steady Flame:** Tyr's priests may share isolated multi-faith temples where no dedicated chapel exists.
