# Selune - The Moonmaiden

| | |
|---|---|
| **Domains** | Moon, stars, navigation, wanderers, dreams |
| **Alignment** | Chaotic Good |
| **Symbol** | A pair of eyes surrounded by seven silver stars |
| **Worship Centres** | Coastal settlements, traveller shrines, Islefield |

Selune protects travellers, navigators, and those who move through darkness without surrendering to it. Her temples and shrines often provide shelter rather than political authority.

## Worship in Dayner

Sailors use Selune's stars for navigation, while travellers leave offerings at roadside shrines before night journeys. Islefield's coastal and forest traditions frequently associate moonlight with safe passage, healing, and revelation.

Selune's worship overlaps comfortably with Valkur in tolerant ports: she guides the route while Valkur gives courage to sail it.

## Opposition to Shar

Selune and Shar are treated as divine opposites in many traditions. Their conflict represents light against concealment, memory against chosen forgetting, and guidance against despair.

Not every Selunite hunts Shar's followers, but organised attempts to spread supernatural darkness or erase memory draw direct opposition.
