# The Drowned God

| | |
|---|---|
| **Domains** | Ocean depths, drowning, sacrifice, ancient seas |
| **Alignment** | Commonly treated as Chaotic Evil |
| **Symbol** | A barnacle-covered altar or tentacles rising from deep water |
| **Worship Centres** | Hidden coastal cults and selected Ajag shrines |

The Drowned God is a disputed power worshipped by cults that treat drowning as return, revelation, or transformation. It may be a true deity, an ancient sea entity, several local beings sharing one name, or a religious mask used by predatory cult leaders.

## Worship in Dayner

Drowned God cells appear in ports, flooded basements, isolated fishing communities, and river settlements. [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Village/Ajag|Ajag]] tolerates many dubious water shrines, including a smaller dangerous congregation associated with the Drowned God.

Followers may use:

- Barnacle-covered altars
- Blood and fish offerings
- Ritual immersion or near-drowning
- Summoning attempts in flooded chambers
- Language describing death as a return to the true sea

## Distinction from Sailor Faiths

Most sailors reject the Drowned God. Selune and Valkur protect travellers upon the water; the Drowned God glorifies what waits beneath it.

A respectful offering to sea conditions is not proof of cult membership. Coastal communities distinguish ordinary caution from traditions that seek sacrifice or transformation through drowning.
