# Gods and Faiths of Dayner

Dayner has no single universal religion. Temples, household rites, military chapels, sailor traditions, sacred forests, and hidden cults overlap across every realm. Some powers are widely recognised gods; others are regional beings, infernal patrons, or shared traditions whose divine status remains disputed.

## Major Faiths

- [[General Lore of Dayner/Religion/Gods of Dayner/Bahamut|Bahamut, the Platinum Dragon]] - Justice, protection, honour, and metallic dragons.
- [[General Lore of Dayner/Religion/Gods of Dayner/Tyr|Tyr, the Maimed God]] - Law, courage, justice, and war.
- [[General Lore of Dayner/Religion/Gods of Dayner/Selune|Selune, the Moonmaiden]] - Moonlight, navigation, travellers, and dreams.
- [[General Lore of Dayner/Religion/Gods of Dayner/Valkur|Valkur, the Mighty]] - Sailors, ships, courage, and favourable winds.
- [[General Lore of Dayner/Religion/Gods of Dayner/Shar|Shar, the Mistress of Night]] - Darkness, loss, secrets, and forgetfulness.
- [[General Lore of Dayner/Religion/Gods of Dayner/Asmodeus|Asmodeus, Lord of the Nine Hells]] - Tyranny, contracts, infernal hierarchy, and power.

## Ancient and Regional Powers

- [[General Lore of Dayner/Religion/Gods of Dayner/Avethis|Avethis, the Undying Gaze]] - Witness, memory, observation, and time seen without judgement.
- [[General Lore of Dayner/Religion/Gods of Dayner/The Drowned God|The Drowned God]] - Ocean depths, drowning, sacrifice, and dangerous coastal cults.
- [[General Lore of Dayner/Religion/Gods of Dayner/The Steady Flame|The Steady Flame]] - A shared multi-faith tradition based on endurance and communal worship.

## Religious Institutions

### Braewood Military Chapels

Braewood's army maintains multi-faith chaplains, with Tyr, Bahamut, and local martial traditions especially visible. Chaplains bless troops, conduct funerals, advise commanders, and provide care during campaigns.

### Islefield Sacred Traditions

Islefield's faith is inseparable from land, ancestry, and ritual access. [[Kingdoms/Islefield/POI/Shercam Woods|Shercam Woods]], [[Kingdoms/Islefield/Village/Sable|Sable]], and the [[Kingdoms/Islefield/POI/Prairie Kingfisher Grove|Prairie Kingfisher Grove]] are governed through sacred custom as much as royal law.

### Kluimont's Mixed Worship

Kluimont combines formal temples, sailor shrines, arcane study, dubious local gods, and traditions preserved by orc, goblin, human, and island communities. Sord has a respected Bahamut sanctuary, while Aresphia and Kilson are major centres of Valkur worship.

### Lavalto's Infernal and Civic Faiths

Lavalto contains ordinary multi-faith shrines alongside open or semi-open infernal practice among powerful vampire households. Asmodeus is the most politically dangerous organised faith, but Shar, the Drowned God, and local volcanic traditions also appear.

## Recent Religious Developments

- Varin's actions during the Spehlina crisis strengthened his association with Bahamut and restored his damaged divine connection.
- The Spehlina was defeated and the lake purified, weakening the immediate infernal corruption but not removing Father Maltrius Kael's wider influence.
- The Ossian Vault confirmed that Avethis is older than Ossian's cult and that Gaia, now known as Balthazar, made the Eye as a companion and witness.
- Religious scholars now face a difficult question: whether a created being that became divine through ages of witness belongs inside existing pantheons at all.

## Religious Tensions

- Followers of Bahamut and Tyr oppose infernal and predatory cults, but disagree over law, mercy, and authority.
- Selune and Shar remain symbolic opposites in many traditions.
- Sailor communities may honour both Selune and Valkur while rejecting the Drowned God.
- Multi-faith settlements value practical cooperation, while isolated temples such as Kilson restrict competing public worship.
- Avethis does not command morality or intervention, making its followers unsettling even when they are not openly hostile.
