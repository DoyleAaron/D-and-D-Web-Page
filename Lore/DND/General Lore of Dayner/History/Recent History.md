# Recent History of Dayner

## Timeline (975-1225 C.C.)

### Year 975–990: The Post-Binding Era

- **975**:
    - **Braewood** emerges from a minor succession crisis. King Roden II officially ascends the throne after quelling a brief rebellion by his uncle.
    - [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Kluimont|Kluimont]] re-establishes control over the [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/POI/Rimduff Isles|Rimduff Isles]], which had become semi-independent pirate havens during Braewood's internal turmoil. This marks the start of Kluimont's renewed push for maritime security.
- **980**:
    - **Islefield** invests heavily in new farmland irrigation. Elven nobility from the Shercam Woods sponsor magical greenways that increase crop yields, boosting the kingdom’s economic power.

### Year 1000–1010: The Western Tensions

- **1000**:
    - The dwarves of **northern Braewood** negotiate **partial autonomy** for their clans. They gain rights to manage their mines and hold dwarven moot councils. Braewood’s monarchy endorses this to secure a steady supply of minerals for the royal armory.
- **1002–1005**:
    - A brief conflict, **the Western Tensions**, flares up between Braewood border barons and Kluimont’s mainland enclaves, sparked by arguments over fishing rights and trade tariffs. There’s **no formal war**, but small skirmishes and raids escalate.
    - Islefield brokers a **ceasefire** in 1005, granting Kluimont limited free-trade passages along certain coastal routes.

### Year 1050–1080: Rise of Darwaeton Influence

- **1053**:
    - The ruling families of [[Kingdoms/Lavalto/Large Cities/Darwaeton|Darwaeton]] solidify influence over the growing settlement of [[Kingdoms/Lavalto/Medium Cities/Flison|Flison]]. They quietly eliminate a merchant coalition that threatened their secrecy.
- **1061**:
    - Adventurers from Braewood claim to have discovered ominous “blood rituals” in a cavern near the Lavalto caldera. Their allegations reach the Braewood court, but no formal action is taken—diplomats fear losing the island’s vital trade route.
- **1072**:
    - Darwaeton’s vampiric lords strike a **trade accord** with Islefield, offering precious volcanic minerals in exchange for safe maritime routes. Tensions lessen, but rumors of dark magic swirl.

### Year 1100–1110: The Dragon Rumors & Kluimont’s Arcane Boom

- **1101**:
    - Stories begin circulating of an **ancient brass dragon** being subdued in [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/POI/Tempawa Forest|Tempawa Forest]]. Kluimont's leadership denies these rumours, but sightings of unusual arcane experimentation multiply.
- **1105**:
    - **Arcane academies** in Kluimont expand rapidly, surpassing even some of Braewood’s famed colleges. Scholars from Islefield and even dwarven smith-mages from northern Braewood visit under strict security protocols.
- **1109**:
    - A small scandal erupts when a Braewood diplomat is **expelled** from Kluimont’s main city. Whispers claim they tried to spy on hidden research labs or the rumored dragon site.

### Year 1130–1150: The Great Resource Dispute

- **1132**:
    - **Islefield** and **Braewood** clash over farmland boundaries near their mutual border. Islefield’s expanding farmland projects push into territories claimed by Braewood’s local barons.
- **1135–1138**:
    - **The Great Resource Dispute** becomes a trade war. Tariffs spike, caravans are harassed, and tensions build into a short, sharp conflict with minimal actual battlefield engagements.
    - **1140**: A truce is negotiated. Braewood recognizes some of Islefield’s farmland expansions in exchange for reduced grain prices and permission to traverse the area freely.

### Year 1170–1180: Scandal & Dynastic Shakeups

- **1172**:
    - A major scandal in Braewood reveals that **Prince Ralston**, a cousin to the current monarch, conspired with certain dwarven iron lords to divert weapon shipments. The scheme aimed to undermine the monarch’s power.
    - The dwarves disclaim knowledge of the treasonous intent; tensions rise between the monarchy and dwarf clans. Prince Ralston is exiled, and the dwarves pay restitution.
- **1179**:
    - The Islefield monarchy transitions peacefully to [[Character Lore/Modern Characters/Queen Atheliana Aeristel|Queen Atheliana Aeristel]], an elf of the Shercam Woods lineage. Her coronation cements the forest-based aristocracy's influence over the broader kingdom.

### Year 1190–1200: Lavalto’s Night of Red Embers
- **1191**:
    - A disastrous **volcanic flare** ravages farmland near Darwaeton. Townsfolk speak of **blood-red embers** lighting the sky and claim a sacrificial ritual awakened the lava flows.
    - Despite fear and rumors, Darwaeton’s vampiric rulers rally to rebuild the damaged port, offering financial aid (possibly sourced from hidden coffers or unscrupulous deals).
- **1195**:
    - Kluimont quietly sends an emissary to Darwaeton, possibly to barter for volcanic reagents used in advanced arcane experiments. A new trade channel opens, though details remain secretive.

### Year 1205–1215: Strains on Multiple Fronts
- **1207**:
    - **Braewood** experiences a major internal revolt in its northern regions, led by a rogue dwarven clan lord who accuses the monarchy of reneging on certain mining-rights agreements. The revolt ends swiftly thanks to Braewood’s large standing army, but distrust festers.
- **1210**:
    - **Orcish enclaves** in Kluimont’s mainland territory mount small raids against nearby Braewood border towns, claiming harassment and forced relocations by local human lords. Kluimont officially disavows the attacks, but orcish leaders blame Braewood aggression for inciting them.
- **1212**:
    - Islefield intensifies **coastal patrols** to block an uptick in Rimduff Isle piracy. The elven navy's presence leads to friction with Kluimont, which sees it as an infringement on open seas.

### Year 1220–1225: Modern Day Conflicts & Uneasy Peace

- **1220**:
    - The **Storm of Unbound Chains** strikes Kluimont's forest interior. Qrey's outskirts are threatened, [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Village/Lloy|Lloy]] is destroyed, and rumours spread that [[Character Lore/Beasts & Creatures/Bagi the Brass Tempest|Bagi the Brass Tempest]] nearly broke free.
- **1222**:
    - Islefield finalizes a **defensive pact** with certain dwarven clans from Braewood, aimed at fostering cooperation if Braewood and Kluimont’s conflicts escalate. Braewood’s monarchy condemns this act as meddling.
- **1224**:
    - Darwaeton’s leadership entertains envoys from both Braewood and Islefield, each seeking advantageous trade deals or alliances. The vampiric household plays both sides diplomatically, stoking tension.
- **1225**:
    - The continent enters a period of intense artefact competition, diplomatic preparation, and covert action before a planned queens' summit.

## Campaign-Era Events of 1225 C.C.

### The Chart Recovered

The party recovered the [[General Lore of Dayner/History/Recovered Books and Tomes/Chart of the Threefold Way|Chart of the Threefold Way]] from [[Kingdoms/Braewood/POI/Plalisle Den|Plalisle Den]]. Early investigation revealed that the codex responds to water carrying the signature of Balthazar-gifted magic.

### Darwaeton Investigation

A Braewood diplomatic mission reached Darwaeton. The party encountered the [[General Lore of Dayner/Organizations/The Darwaeton Council|Darwaeton Council]], investigated shared nightmares and undead disturbances, and became entangled with Liora, Mayor Adrian, and Lavalto's competing vampire factions.

### Rimduff Rescue

The party travelled into the Rimduff Isles to rescue Captain Kirk's daughter from Captain Iona. The confrontation exposed the continuing reach of pirate fleets and personal vendettas within Kluimont's island waters.

### Blackthorn House

After returning toward Darwaeton, the party attacked [[Kingdoms/Lavalto/Large Cities/Blackthorn House|Blackthorn House]], defeated the Valdris brood and its constructs, and rescued [[Character Lore/Modern Characters/Liora|Liora]]. The conflict confirmed that Father Maltrius Kael's influence reached close to Darwaeton's civic leadership.

### The Spehlina Crisis

The corrupted [[Character Lore/Beasts & Creatures/The Spehlina|Spehlina]] threatened traffic, settlements, and the planned summit. The party and recruited allies defeated the creature and purified the lake, preventing the worst effects of its corruption. The mission's 10,000-gold reward was paid in Darwaeton.

### The Ossian Vault

An Islefield delegation led by [[Character Lore/Modern Characters/Tharion Aelindre|Tharion Aelindre]] commissioned the party to recover the Cloak of the Watcher's Veil near Resross and the Iroware Heights.

Inside the Ossian Vault, time passed at roughly one day outside for every hour within. The party defeated the Eye Golem, [[Character Lore/Historic Characters/Valdrek Ossian|Valdrek Ossian]], and [[Character Lore/Beasts & Creatures/Vasskren the Undying Sentinel|Vasskren]]. They opened the Eye Chamber and recovered the deepest surviving records of Ossian's research.

### The Gaia-Balthazar Revelation

[[General Lore of Dayner/History/Recovered Books and Tomes/Ossian's Research Codex|Ossian's Research Codex]] revealed that Gaia made Avethis and the three powers associated with the Threefold Way. Its central conclusion is that **Gaia is Balthazar**. The codex also points toward a sealed lower archive beneath Islefield.

## Present Position

As of the latest public record:

- The Spehlina crisis is over, but its corruption and intended use remain politically important.
- The queens' summit is still approaching.
- The party possesses new knowledge about Balthazar, Avethis, and the Threefold artefacts.
- Kluimont continues to guard its dragon and arcane secrets.
- Lavalto remains divided among civic, pureblood, and infernal interests.
- Islefield's hidden records have become the next major source of historical and political pressure.
