# War of the Sundered Chains

The **War of the Sundered Chains** ended the Tharnic Empire's control of much of Dayner. Dwarven rebellion formed its core, but humans, elves, orcs, and other peoples joined as imperial rule fractured.

## 650 C.C. - Battle of Iron Break

### Context

The Tharnic Empire was near its height and depended heavily on enslaved dwarven labour for mines and foundries. Iron Break guarded rich ore veins in the northern territories that later became part of Braewood.

### Key Figures

- [[Character Lore/Historic Characters/General Arach Tharne|General Arach Tharne]] led the imperial legion.
- Warchief Gorim Stonefist led the dwarven defenders.

### Battle

Imperial heavy infantry forced the narrow approaches with rams and disciplined formations. The dwarves used tunnels and high ground but lacked enough free fighters to hold the fortress.

### Outcome

The Empire won. Defenders were killed, captured, or returned to forced labour. The victory secured imperial access to northern iron for decades and became one of the grievances later invoked by the rebellion.

## 715 C.C. - Siege of Treadfast Hold

### Context

By 715 C.C., [[Character Lore/Historic Characters/High Thane Broldir Flameforged|High Thane Broldir Flameforged]] had united several dwarven clans and allied them with other imperial subjects. Treadfast Hold was an imperial fortress notorious for supervising enslaved workers.

### Key Figures

- High Thane Broldir Flameforged commanded the coalition.
- [[Character Lore/Historic Characters/Legate Sienna Valtoris|Legate Sienna Valtoris]] commanded the fortress.

### Battle

Rebels disrupted supplies and used concealed tunnels to breach the fortifications from below. The garrison had prepared for a conventional siege and could not reorganise before coalition forces entered the hold.

### Outcome

Treadfast fell and Valtoris was killed. News of the victory encouraged wider uprisings and transformed scattered resistance into open war.

## 720 C.C. - Hammerfall Uprising

### Context

The uprising brought together dwarven heavy infantry, elven archers, human rebels, and orcish shock troops against the Empire's main field army.

### Key Figures

- High Thane Broldir Flameforged led the dwarven centre.
- [[Character Lore/Historic Characters/Princess Araleth Oakheath|Princess Araleth Oakheath]] coordinated elven support.
- [[Character Lore/Historic Characters/Imperial Warmaster Dravarian|Imperial Warmaster Dravarian]] led the imperial host.

### Battle

The coalition used rocky ground to contain the imperial advance. Dwarves held the centre, elven archers controlled ridges, and orcish troops struck the flanks. Dravarian's frontal assault failed to break the line.

### Outcome

The imperial legion was shattered and Dravarian died while attempting to rally it. Hammerfall marked the point after which imperial collapse became likely rather than merely possible.

## 728 C.C. - Last Stand at Barash Gorge

### Context

The Empire attempted to preserve one of its last mountain routes as rebel armies closed around its remaining strongholds.

### Key Figures

- Clan Chief Madra Emberhelm commanded the forward rebel assault.
- [[Character Lore/Historic Characters/Grand Inquisitor Pellaro|Grand Inquisitor Pellaro]] commanded the imperial defence.

### Battle

Imperial ballistae controlled the gorge from above. Rebel forces staged a diversion below while dwarven sappers collapsed side passages and destroyed the defenders' elevated positions.

### Outcome

The imperial force was nearly annihilated. Pellaro was captured and reportedly tried and executed by dwarven judges. The loss opened the route toward the final collapse of Tharnic rule in 730 C.C.

## Legacy

The war remains central to dwarven political identity and to modern suspicion of concentrated human authority. It also demonstrates that the kingdoms did not emerge from separate peoples: the coalition that destroyed the Empire was mixed long before Braewood, Islefield, and Kluimont drew borders.
