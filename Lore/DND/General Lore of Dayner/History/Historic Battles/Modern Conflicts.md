# Modern Conflicts

These conflicts occurred after the three kingdoms had formed and continue to shape modern diplomacy.

## 1061 C.C. - The Darwaeton Purge

### Context

A Braewood expedition attempted to expose vampiric practices in [[Kingdoms/Lavalto/Large Cities/Darwaeton|Darwaeton]].

### Key Figures

- Knight-Captain Rell Stravus led the Braewood group.
- [[Character Lore/Modern Characters/Lady Seraphina Caylester|Lady Seraphina Caylester]] represented Lavalto's vampiric elite.

### Outcome

The expedition used infiltration and attacks within the catacombs. Illusions, thralls, and local defenders trapped the intruders. Stravus died and the survivors withdrew.

Darwaeton demanded an apology, while Braewood quietly abandoned the investigation to preserve trade. The event remains the clearest historical warning against foreign intervention on Lavalto.

## 1135-1140 C.C. - Great Resource Dispute

Braewood and Islefield fought over farmland expansion and movement through their shared border country. [[Character Lore/Historic Characters/General Yon Garathorn|General Yon Garathorn]] directed Braewood forces, while Archdruid Elowen Farendar supported Islefield with nature magic and concealment.

Neither side won decisively. The 1140 settlement recognised some Islefield expansion, reduced grain prices for Braewood, and preserved movement through agreed corridors.

## 1191 C.C. - Night of Red Embers

### Context

The Lavalto Volcano erupted violently as rebellion spread around Darwaeton. Local tradition claims a dark ritual triggered or intensified the event.

### Key Figures

- [[Character Lore/Modern Characters/Lord Adrian Silwynn|Lord Adrian Silwynn]] led the ruling response.
- Acolyte Luriana Emberveil was associated with volcanic ritual magic.

### Outcome

Lava damaged Darwaeton's port and destroyed farmland. Vampiric households used controlled militia and enthralled defenders against rebels. The uprising failed, and reconstruction loans left much of the city indebted to Lord Adrian's faction.

## 1207 C.C. - Dwarven Revolt of Northpeak

A northern dwarven coalition seized Northpeak after accusing the Braewood Crown of violating mining agreements. Clan Chief Haldar Magmathorn used tunnels and high ground against Commander Riona Blackwyn's siege forces.

Weeks of fighting ended in an uneasy truce and a promise to reconsider mining law. The revolt ended, but disputes over autonomy and taxation continued.

## 1220 C.C. - Storm of Unbound Chains

### Context

An arcane storm erupted around [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/POI/Tempawa Forest|Tempawa Forest]] as [[Character Lore/Beasts & Creatures/Bagi the Brass Tempest|Bagi the Brass Tempest]] disrupted the binding network connected to him.

### Key Figures

- [[Character Lore/Modern Characters/High Archon Lysanthe Kilmora|High Archon Lysanthe Kilmora]] directed Kluimont's response.
- Captain Merys Klyndar coordinated evacuation and defence around Qrey.

### Outcome

The storm lasted three nights, damaged fields, threatened Qrey, and destroyed [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Village/Lloy|Lloy]]. Kluimont contained the immediate disaster and strengthened restrictions around the forest and capital island.

Official accounts describe an unexplained magical weather event. The connection to Bagi is widely suspected but remains politically explosive.
