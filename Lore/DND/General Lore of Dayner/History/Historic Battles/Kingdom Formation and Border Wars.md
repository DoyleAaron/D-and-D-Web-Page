# Kingdom Formation and Border Wars

After the Tharnic Empire collapsed, local rulers, clans, ports, and surviving institutions fought over farmland, mines, roads, and sea lanes. These conflicts shaped the borders of the three modern kingdoms.

## 750 C.C. - Skirmish at the Ember Fields

Human warlords and dwarven clans contested farmland still scarred by imperial war. Baroness Yara Whiteshield used light cavalry against supply routes, while Thane Gorlun Kazador answered with earthworks and crossbow lines.

Neither side gained a decisive advantage. A negotiated division of farmland became an early example of dwarven autonomy existing beside human territorial rule.

## 802 C.C. - Naval Battle of Sunken Crags

### Context

Coastal lords attempting to unify the northwestern sea routes faced pirate rulers who rejected central fees and authority.

### Key Figures

- [[Character Lore/Historic Characters/Sea Lord Helmar Lynth|Sea Lord Helmar Lynth]] led the proto-Kluimont fleet.
- [[Character Lore/Historic Characters/Pirate Queen Aris Steel-Eye|Pirate Queen Aris Steel-Eye]] commanded the reef strongholds.

### Battle and Outcome

Lynth combined war galleys, ballistae, and navigational magic against smaller pirate vessels using shallow reefs. His flagship sank Aris's main sloop and broke the confederacy's organised resistance.

The victory strengthened maritime consolidation and helped create the political system that later became Kluimont. Pirate independence survived in smaller forms throughout the Rimduff Isles.

## 915 C.C. - Battle of Red Wheat

### Context

During the Year of Withered Fields, Braewood soldiers seized grain intended for Islefield communities.

### Key Figures

- Colonel Varic Holt led the Braewood cavalry.
- Sir Aldarion Evensong defended the Islefield caravan.

### Battle and Outcome

Braewood cavalry attempted a quick interception. Islefield archers and weather magic turned the road to mud, slowing the attack. Evensong mortally wounded Holt and the caravan remained under Islefield control.

The clash damaged local ceasefires but did not become a full war.

## 962 C.C. - Skirmish of Dwarven Watch

A northern Braewood watchtower was attacked by mercenaries who claimed royal authority. Clanlord Fandrin Ironflare's defenders discovered an attempted infiltration and used high ground and defensive engines to repel it.

Captured survivors could not conclusively prove Crown involvement. The uncertainty became more politically damaging than a clear answer and deepened dwarven distrust over mining rights and taxation.

## 1002-1005 C.C. - Coastal Raids of the Western Tensions

### Context

Braewood border barons and Kluimont's mainland communities disputed fishing rights, tariffs, and coastal control. Privateers on both sides attacked lightly defended settlements without a formal declaration of war.

### Key Figures

- Lord Damon Briarfall was suspected of financing Braewood raiders.
- Lady Ilena Wintshield organised Slonmore's militia and coastal warning system.

### Outcome

Kluimont's arcane support made continued raids increasingly costly. Islefield brokered a ceasefire in 1005 C.C., granting limited trade access and ending the worst attacks without resolving mutual suspicion.

## Legacy

These conflicts established patterns that remain visible in 1225 C.C.:

- Dwarven communities demand local authority before accepting Crown protection.
- Kluimont treats control of sea lanes as a security issue rather than ordinary commerce.
- Islefield frequently uses food, roads, and diplomacy to achieve what neighbours pursue through force.
- Privateers and unofficial agents allow kingdoms to exert pressure while denying responsibility.
