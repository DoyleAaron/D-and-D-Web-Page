## Opening the Chart

The codex does not open by ordinary force. Its concealed material responds to water carrying the magical signature of a Balthazar-gifted artefact. Early investigation revealed routes and coordinates only after the correct magical interaction.

## What the Vault Records Confirmed

[[General Lore of Dayner/History/Recovered Books and Tomes/Ossian's Research Codex|Ossian's Research Codex]] identifies the Chart as a guide to three great powers:

1. A ring
2. A crown
3. An amulet

Gaia made the Three and then hid them so completely that even their maker could no longer point directly to their resting places. The Chart preserves a path without functioning as a simple ordinary map.

## Campaign Importance

- The Chart is one part of the political struggle surrounding Balthazar's creations.
- Its routes connect the early Plalisle vault to later discoveries beneath Islefield.
- Multiple factions know the book matters without sharing the same understanding of why.
- The Chart's relationship to the ring, crown, and amulet makes control of it more dangerous than possession of an isolated magical book.

## Open Questions

- Which route or archive reveals the current location of each power?
- What occurs if all three powers are brought together?
- Why did Gaia choose to lose exact knowledge of the hiding places?
- Who currently understands the Chart well enough to use it safely?
