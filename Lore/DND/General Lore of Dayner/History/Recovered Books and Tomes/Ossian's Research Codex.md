## Confirmed Revelations

Ossian's life's work records the deepest truths recovered from the vault:

- The Eye called **Avethis** existed before Ossian and before the Tharnic facility.
- Gaia made Avethis as a companion and watcher.
- Gaia made three great powers: a ring, a crown, and an amulet.
- The [[General Lore of Dayner/History/Recovered Books and Tomes/Chart of the Threefold Way|Chart of the Threefold Way]] was created to guide seekers toward where those powers had been hidden.
- Hiding the powers caused even Gaia to lose exact knowledge of their locations.
- **Gaia is Balthazar.** Ossian writes both names together in the final revelation.
- Additional records concerning Gaia may survive beneath Islefield in a sealed lower archive.

## Avethis

Ossian describes Avethis as a witness rather than a judge. The Eye observes past and future without intervening. Over immense time, the companion made by Gaia became a god through the depth and duration of its witnessing.

The Eye Chamber's Sight offered each visitor a permanent one-use opportunity to witness a chosen point in time.

## Why the Codex Matters

Before the codex was recovered, Balthazar could be understood as an ancient wizard who created powerful artefacts. Ossian's work changes that scale: Gaia is described as the earth made capable of acting, creating, and speaking through a name known in the modern age as Balthazar.

The codex also turns the Threefold artefacts from unrelated treasures into one deliberate system.
