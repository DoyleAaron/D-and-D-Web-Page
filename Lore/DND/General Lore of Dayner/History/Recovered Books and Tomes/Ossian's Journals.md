# Ossian's Journals

## Overview

Several separate journals, ciphered notebooks, and working records were recovered from the Ossian Vault. They should be treated as a collection rather than one book.

## Main Documents

### Cipher Journal

A thirty-page journal written in Ossian's personal cipher and kept in a fireproof case. Once decrypted, it records his discovery that:

- The site contained structures older than the Tharnic mine.
- Islefield surveyors knew more about the facility than later records admitted.
- Gaia was not merely a historical person or conventional deity.
- The being called Gaia was present at the site and made something there.

### Chamber Journal

Ossian's ordinary journal was kept near his sarcophagus. It records his devotion to Avethis, his preparation of the vault, and his belief that reaching the Eye required deliberate persistence.

### Working Notes

Conference-room documents, wall inscriptions, construction notes, and secondary-workspace records track:

- The four sentinel golems
- Vasskren's construction
- The sealed Eye Chamber
- The five cores needed to open the final door
- The time distortion affecting the vault
- Ossian's concern that outside institutions already knew of the site's importance

## Importance

The journals bridge the gap between the doctrinal [[General Lore of Dayner/History/Recovered Books and Tomes/Tome of Avethis|Tome of Avethis]] and the complete [[General Lore of Dayner/History/Recovered Books and Tomes/Ossian's Research Codex|Research Codex]]. They show Ossian reaching his conclusions gradually rather than receiving one complete revelation.

## Reliability

Ossian was obsessive, undead, and influenced by Avethis, but his notes often distinguish observation from interpretation. His conclusions are dangerous precisely because later discoveries confirm several of them.
