# Mirelle's Correspondence

## Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Letters, directives, and covert political messages |
| **Associated Figure** | Mirelle / Myrelle |
| **Period** | Summit-era campaign, 1225 C.C. |

## Confirmed Themes

The correspondence shows multiple factions racing to control information and magical objects before the queens' summit.

Recurring subjects include:

- The book and its connection to the fountain or location strategy
- Competing attempts to secure Balthazar-linked items
- Pressure on Amdir and other party members
- The summit as both a diplomatic event and an opportunity for covert transfer or theft
- Lavalto and Kluimont as possible routes around crown control

## Nature of the Collection

This is not one bound volume. It is a living body of letters whose value comes from comparison. A single message may mislead; repeated names, dates, destinations, and requests reveal broader intent.

## Reliability

The letters confirm what their writers wanted recipients to believe and do. They do not automatically prove every accusation or political claim within them.

## Relationship to Recovered Codices

Mirelle's network knew the Chart mattered before the Ossian Vault revealed the complete Gaia-Balthazar connection. The correspondence therefore records the political race around incomplete knowledge rather than the full truth.
