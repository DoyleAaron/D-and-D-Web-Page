## Contents

The Tome concerns Avethis, the principle of witness, and the beliefs that formed around the Eye. It treats observation as sacred even when no judgement or intervention follows.

Recurring ideas include:

- The Eye sees without turning away.
- Past and future are equally available to a complete witness.
- Knowledge changes the person who receives it even when the witness does nothing else.
- Death does not remove a person from the record of what occurred.

## Physical and Magical Qualities

The manuscript is large, iron-bound, and difficult to read without Old Elvish or magical translation. Extended study assists research into Avethis, Ossian, and the vault, but readers may become fixated on unanswered questions or knowledge they should leave alone.

## Relationship to the Research Codex

The Tome records doctrine and worship. [[General Lore of Dayner/History/Recovered Books and Tomes/Ossian's Research Codex|Ossian's Research Codex]] records historical and cosmological conclusions. Reading only the Tome explains what Ossian believed; reading the Codex explains why he believed it.
