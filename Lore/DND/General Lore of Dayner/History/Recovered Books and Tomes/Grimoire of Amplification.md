# Grimoire of Amplification

## Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Personal arcane grimoire and spellcasting focus |
| **Received** | Reward connected to the Darwaeton mission |
| **Bearer** | [[Character Lore/Player Characters/Amdir Aeristel (Aaron)|Amdir Aeristel]] |

## Confirmed Function

- The Grimoire functions as an arcane focus.
- It can amplify a spell once per day, dramatically increasing damage or healing according to the effect established when it was awarded.
- Its magic is distinct from the Threefold artefacts and should not be treated as one of Balthazar's three powers without further evidence.

## Unknown History

The book's original author, school, and long-term stability have not been established. Hidden pages, restrictions, or side effects remain possible but unconfirmed.

## Political Context

The Grimoire entered the party's possession through Darwaeton-era political dealings. That history makes its provenance relevant even if the item is not part of the Balthazar network.
