# Historic Battles of Dayner

This index collects Dayner's best-known wars, uprisings, raids, and violent political crises. Detailed accounts are grouped by era.

## Era Collections

- [[General Lore of Dayner/History/Historic Battles/War of the Sundered Chains|War of the Sundered Chains]] covers the Tharnic Empire's expansion, dwarven enslavement, and the rebellion that destroyed imperial rule.
- [[General Lore of Dayner/History/Historic Battles/Kingdom Formation and Border Wars|Kingdom Formation and Border Wars]] covers the unstable centuries in which Braewood, Islefield, and Kluimont emerged.
- [[General Lore of Dayner/History/Historic Battles/Modern Conflicts|Modern Conflicts]] covers Darwaeton, the Great Resource Dispute, the Night of Red Embers, northern revolt, and Kluimont's arcane storm.
