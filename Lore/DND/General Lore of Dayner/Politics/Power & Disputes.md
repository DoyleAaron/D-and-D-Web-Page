# Power and Disputes

Dayner has three recognised kingdoms and one independent volcanic realm. None can dominate the others without accepting unacceptable economic, military, or magical risk.

## Balance of Power

### Braewood: Conventional Strength

[[Kingdoms/Braewood/Braewood|Braewood]] fields the largest army, controls extensive farmland and mines, and can sustain long campaigns better than its neighbours. Its weakness is internal: dwarven autonomy, distant governors, military expense, and uneven treatment of non-human communities repeatedly strain the Crown.

### Islefield: Wealth and Access

[[Kingdoms/Islefield/Islefield|Islefield]] is the wealthiest realm per resident. Food, timber, shipping, bridge traffic, and protective magic give it leverage without requiring open conquest. Its elven institutions are stable but deeply hierarchical, producing resentment in mixed cities and along its borders.

### Kluimont: Hidden Arcane Capacity

[[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Kluimont|Kluimont]] has fewer conventional soldiers but possesses advanced wards, naval assets, magical research, and projects controlled by the [[General Lore of Dayner/Organizations/The Arcane Synod|Arcane Synod]]. Its secretiveness protects those advantages while preventing trust.

### Lavalto: Strategic Scarcity

[[Kingdoms/Lavalto/Lavalto|Lavalto]] is not unclaimed; it is independent and internally divided. Its rulers control volcanic resources and dangerous coastal approaches. [[Kingdoms/Lavalto/Large Cities/Darwaeton|Darwaeton]] deals openly with foreign powers, while Flison and Agosron are more hostile and isolated.

## Major Disputes in 1225 C.C.

### The Queens' Summit

Braewood and Islefield are preparing for a high-level summit after the attempted use of the [[Character Lore/Beasts & Creatures/The Spehlina|Spehlina]] against regional stability. The creature has been defeated and purified, but responsibility for its corruption and intended deployment remains politically dangerous.

### The Balthazar Artefacts

The [[General Lore of Dayner/History/Recovered Books and Tomes/Chart of the Threefold Way|Chart of the Threefold Way]], a ring, and a crown are now understood to be connected creations. Knowledge recovered from the Ossian Vault has made control of records and relics more important than ever.

### Kluimont's Dragon Secret

The 1220 Storm of Unbound Chains and the destruction of Lloy strengthened rumours that Kluimont bound [[Character Lore/Beasts & Creatures/Bagi the Brass Tempest|Bagi the Brass Tempest]] into its ward network. If proven, the revelation could fracture the Synod, provoke foreign intervention, or free a furious ancient power.

### Dwarven Autonomy

Northern Braewood's clans possess wealth, military value, and memories of Tharnic enslavement. Tax, mining rights, local law, and Islefield's contacts with dwarven groups remain persistent sources of tension.

### Islefield's Social Order

Elven authority is strongest around sacred and capital institutions. Mixed settlements benefit from Islefield's prosperity but face unequal access, class barriers, and in some places open racism. Yhey's Crescent and Basin make that division especially visible.

### Lavalto's Internal Powers

The [[General Lore of Dayner/Organizations/The Darwaeton Council|Darwaeton Council]], Lord Adrian's diplomatic faction, isolated pureblood families, and infernal religious interests do not share one strategy. Foreign kingdoms often mistake Lavalto's temporary cooperation for unified rule.

## Border Flashpoints

- **The Grand Span:** Peaceful and profitable, but heavily militarised because both kingdoms depend on it.
- **Yhey and Ansdale:** Islefield-Kluimont crossings shaped by suspicion, trade, and unequal treatment.
- **Phine and Slonmore:** Braewood-Kluimont corridor with a history of raids and privateering.
- **Mortling and Klovine:** Military trade route where ordinary inspections can become diplomatic incidents.
- **Rimduff waters:** Kluimont patrols, Islefield observation, pirate autonomy, and Qrey's restricted channels overlap.
