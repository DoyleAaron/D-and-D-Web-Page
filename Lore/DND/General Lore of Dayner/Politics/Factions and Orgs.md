# Factions and Organizations

Dayner's institutions overlap across borders. Crowns and councils govern territory, while guilds, criminal networks, religious orders, and arcane bodies exercise power through contracts, knowledge, trade, and influence.

## Major Organizations

- [[General Lore of Dayner/Organizations/The Arcane Synod|The Arcane Synod]] - Kluimont's secretive governing circle of senior mages and custodians of restricted arcane projects.
- [[General Lore of Dayner/Organizations/The Darwaeton Council|The Darwaeton Council]] - Darwaeton's civic government and diplomatic face on independent Lavalto.
- [[General Lore of Dayner/Organizations/The Red Scarves|The Red Scarves]] - A criminal and occult network rooted in Sheyton with influence beyond the city.
- [[General Lore of Dayner/Organizations/The Wayfarers Accord|The Wayfarers' Accord]] - A continent-wide adventurers' guild built around contracts, rankings, and mutual recognition.

## Kingdom Governments

- [[Kingdoms/Braewood/Braewood|Braewood]] - A human-led monarchy backed by Dayner's largest conventional military.
- [[Kingdoms/Islefield/Islefield|Islefield]] - An elven-led monarchy whose power rests on wealth, resources, wards, and diplomacy.
- [[Kingdoms/Kluimont/Kluimont|Kluimont]] - An arcane kingdom divided between a guarded capital island, the Rimduff Isles, and loosely governed mainland territory.
- [[Kingdoms/Lavalto/Lavalto|Lavalto]] - An independent volcanic island whose settlements answer to overlapping civic and vampiric powers.
