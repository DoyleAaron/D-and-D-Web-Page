## Description

Valdrek Ossian was a necromancer, scholar, and genuine devotee of Avethis. He occupied an abandoned Tharnic mining facility and reshaped it into a temple of witnessing, surrounding the ancient Eye Chamber with undead guardians, constructs, records, and sealed passages.

By the time the party found him, Ossian had been waiting in his chamber for decades. His desiccated body rested among six preserved worshippers while he wore the Cloak of the Watcher's Veil.

## Work in the Vault

Ossian fused Tharnic engineering with Avethis-touched magic to create the vault's four sentinel golems:

- The Elven Tree Golem.
- The Blue Fire Golem.
- The Amethyst Golem.
- The Eye Golem.

His final guardian was Vasskren, an enormous undead construct assembled from the accumulated bones of those who died within the vault.

## Research

Ossian's journals and codex preserved discoveries that had been lost elsewhere:

- The Eye called Avethis existed before Ossian's cult.
- Gaia created the ring, crown, and amulet associated with the Threefold Way.
- The artifacts were deliberately hidden so completely that even their maker could not point directly to them.
- Gaia is the being now known as Balthazar.
- Records tied to Gaia may remain beneath Islefield in a sealed lower archive.

## Final Encounter

The party awakened Ossian in his chamber and defeated him alongside his wight followers. When he fell, the surviving wights crumbled and the way toward the final vault guardian began to open.

## Legacy

Ossian was dangerous, but his obsession also made him one of the campaign's most important record keepers. He turned the vault into a deadly barrier around truths he believed should only be reached by people willing to fight for them.

## Notable Quote

> "The Eye has already seen you. It sees everything. Welcome to the record."
