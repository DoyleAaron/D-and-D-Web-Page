## Description

Seyla Vane is a quick-witted adventurer who constantly weighs risk against reward. She respects competence, protects her crew, and refuses to spend lives merely to satisfy someone else's ambition.

## Spehlina Mission

Seyla led the Driftwood Four and could bring the Luckpenny into the lake operation. Her crew offered speed, ranged support, monster-hunting knowledge, and arcane firepower.

She was willing to take the contract for substantial payment and salvage rights, but her cooperation depended on believing the party had a credible plan.

## Notable Quote
