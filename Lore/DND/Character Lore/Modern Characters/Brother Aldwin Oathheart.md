## Description

Brother Aldwin Oathheart is an earnest young cleric serving Dawerton's local multi-faith shrine. He is eager to help, committed to radiant faith, and inexperienced in real battle.

## Spehlina Mission

Aldwin volunteered for the Spehlina operation without demanding payment. His radiant magic could cut through corruption, but the lake battle represented the first truly terrifying fight of his life.
