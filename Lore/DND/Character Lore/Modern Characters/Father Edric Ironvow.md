## Description

Father Edric Ironvow is a stern and honorable priest who speaks in short sentences and judges people by their actions. He carries a worn copy of Tyr's Laws and refuses payment for work he considers just.

## Spehlina Mission

Edric could be recruited by framing the mission as resistance against infernal corruption and protection of the summit. His battlefield prayers empowered nearby allies with radiant force, making a full crew far more dangerous to corrupted creatures.

## Notable Quote

> "Tyr's work is not for sale."
