## Description

Dorek Ashfall is the quiet tracker of the Driftwood Four. He specializes in aberrations and coastal terrain, preferring observation, preparation, and precise longbow fire over attention or speeches.

## Spehlina Mission

Dorek had studied the Spehlina from a distance and understood that it was an aberrant, corrupted guardian rather than a normal predator. He was privately sympathetic to saving it, though he followed Seyla Vane's lead and rarely volunteered his opinion.
