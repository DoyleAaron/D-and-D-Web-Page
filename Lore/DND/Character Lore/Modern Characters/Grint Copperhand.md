## Description

Grint Copperhand is a practical dwarven mercenary who treats danger as a contract and survival as part of maintaining his reputation. He counts payment openly, avoids grand causes, and expects clear terms before drawing his greataxe.

## Spehlina Mission

Grint and five Copperhand sellswords were available as hired muscle for the Spehlina operation. Unlike the clerics and faithful volunteers, Grint cared little about whether the creature was killed or purified; he cared that the work was paid and competently led.

## Notable Quote

> "Gods don't pay wages."
