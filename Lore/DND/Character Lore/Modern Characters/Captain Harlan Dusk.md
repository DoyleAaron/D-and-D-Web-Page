## Description

Harlan Dusk is a semi-retired naval veteran who now runs cargo routes aboard the Ironwake. He is gruff, pragmatic, fiercely loyal, and prone to complaining even while committing himself fully to a dangerous job.

## Spehlina Mission

Harlan was one of the captains available to support the party's lake operation against the Spehlina. The Ironwake brought experienced sailors, a ballista, and harpoon guns suited to restraining a massive aquatic target.

## Notable Detail

Harlan calls Captain Kirk "old barnacle" and treats their long friendship as both a burden and an obligation he would never abandon.
