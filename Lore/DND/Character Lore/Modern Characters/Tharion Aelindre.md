## Description

Tharion Aelindre is a tall, controlled high elf with silver-grey hair, violet eyes, and muted green court clothing marked by the Second Council's silver kingfisher pin. He carries himself with deliberate precision and rarely raises his voice.

## History

Tharion served as a junior council aide during Amdir Aeristel's banishment proceedings and personally read the formal charges. Amdir publicly insulted the Aelindre family during the hearing, an incident Tharion has never forgotten.

## Current Role

Tharion approached the party in Darwaeton after the Spehlina mission. He offered temporary relief from Islefield travel restrictions and commissioned the party to recover the Cloak of the Watcher's Veil from the Ossian Vault.

He is a capable Way of Shadow monk and a careful political operator. His composure, silence, and exact language make it difficult to tell where diplomacy ends and surveillance begins.

## Notable Quote

> "I see your exile has not changed your instinct for spectacle."
