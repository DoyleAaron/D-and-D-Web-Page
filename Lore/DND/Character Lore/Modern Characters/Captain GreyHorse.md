# Captain GreyHorse

## Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Pirate Captain and Harbour-Lord of Tarora |
| **Race** | Unknown |
| **Location** | Tarora, Rimduff Isles |
| **Affiliation** | Tarora Captains' Compact |

## Description

**Captain GreyHorse** is the notorious pirate who holds Tarora's competing crews together. He is not a king and avoids the title, but no major ship is repaired, hidden, sold, or condemned in Tarora without his people hearing of it.

GreyHorse built his authority by making the pirate harbour predictable enough to survive. Crews may feud at sea, but fighting on protected docks, stealing from permanent residents, or drawing a foreign fleet to the island can bring every signatory of the Captains' Compact against the offender.

## Appearance

GreyHorse is known above all for his mask. Its face resembles a severe silver dragon, weathered rather than polished, with a genuine **silver dragon horn** fixed along one side. The horn curves back above the temple and makes his silhouette unmistakable even across a crowded quay.

He wears layered sea leathers, a long storm coat, and practical armour assembled from several ports. Almost nobody in Tarora claims to have seen his uncovered face, and the conflicting descriptions sound deliberately planted.

## Personality

- Controlled and theatrical without wasting words
- Ruthless toward anyone who endangers Tarora as a refuge
- Patient when bargaining over ships, prisoners, or information
- More interested in enforceable agreements than personal loyalty
- Intensely protective of the mask and the history of its horn

## Role in Tarora

- Arbitrates disputes between pirate captains
- Controls access to GreyHorse Quay and the island's best repair facilities
- Organises collective defence when Kluimont or a rival fleet approaches
- Sets the harbour's rules for ransom, stolen cargo, and protected residents
- Maintains informants in Eimgend, Qrey, and several foreign ports

## Reputation

Kluimont officials call GreyHorse a criminal when useful and an island intermediary when practical. Merchants regard him as dangerous but capable of honouring a precise bargain. Pirates know that his punishments are severe, public, and usually tied to a rule the offender knowingly broke.

The origin of the silver dragon horn is unknown. Stories claim it was won in battle, inherited from a dragon hunter, purchased from an arcane collector, or given freely by a dragon whose life GreyHorse once spared.

## Relationships

- **Tarora:** His power base and the one community he consistently protects.
- **Kluimont:** A hostile accommodation built on information, bribes, and mutual inconvenience.
- **Qrey:** GreyHorse vessels require exceptional sponsorship to enter its harbour.
- **Other Pirates:** Useful allies until they violate the Compact or challenge his control openly.

## DM Notes

- GreyHorse's ancestry, face, and the true origin of the horn are intentionally open for later development.
- The horn should be genuine silver dragon horn, but it need not have come from a dragon GreyHorse killed.
