## Description

Aleth Varne is a broad-shouldered high elf in polished silver plate, with close-cropped grey-blond hair and a deliberate scar across his left cheekbone. His armour bears Islefield's kingfisher insignia.

## Character

Aleth speaks rarely and treats every word as a decision. His duty is to keep Tharion alive and uphold Islefield's sovereignty, even when orders and personal morality do not align cleanly.

## Capabilities

A veteran Oath of the Crown paladin, Aleth specializes in holding dangerous enemies in place, shielding allies, resisting fear, and delivering powerful divine strikes against undead or fiends.

## Role in the Party's Story

Aleth accompanied the Islefield delegation that recruited the party for the Ossian Vault expedition. He served as the delegation's immovable defensive presence and a reminder that the offer carried the authority of a powerful state.

## Notable Quote

> "You could kill me. Understand that the consequences would follow you into Islefield for the rest of your lives."
