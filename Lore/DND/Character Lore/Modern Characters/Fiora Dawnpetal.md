## Description

Fiora Dawnpetal is the youngest and most openly enthusiastic member of the Driftwood Four. She is excitable, chaotic, and deeply fond of destructive magic.

## Spehlina Mission

The prospect of fighting an enormous lake creature thrilled Fiora more than it frightened her. She brought ranged fire, force, and thunder magic to the operation and was easier to recruit through an appeal to adventure than through promises of money.

## Notable Quote

> "The magic is enough."
