## Description

Brell Hammerstone is the Driftwood Four's shield and frontline anchor. He speaks little, follows Seyla Vane's calls, and can be relied upon to hold his ground when a fight becomes dangerous.

## Spehlina Mission

Brell joined the lake operation despite being terrified of deep water, a fear he refused to admit. His heavy armour, shield, and stubborn discipline made him better suited to standing between danger and his crew than moving easily across a ship.
