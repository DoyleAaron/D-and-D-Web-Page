## Description

Sister Maren Tidecaller is the calm, soft-spoken chaplain aboard the Ironwake. She navigates by moon and stars, treating their light as Selune's guidance, and remains steady even when a battle turns chaotic.

## Spehlina Mission

Maren accompanied Captain Harlan Dusk as part of the Ironwake crew. Her healing, restorative magic, and radiant attacks made her one of the operation's most important support figures.

She strongly favored saving the corrupted guardian rather than killing it and regarded the Spehlina's suffering as a spiritual wrong rather than a simple monster hunt.
