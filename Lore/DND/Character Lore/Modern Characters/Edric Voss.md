## Description

Edric Voss was a Darwaeton carpenter before one of Father Maltrius Kael's brood fed on him and forced vampire blood into his body. He woke confused, starving, and unable to control what he had become.

The hunger drove Edric to kill his wife, Maren, and their young son, Tobin. Horrified by his actions, he covered their bodies and hid in the family home, fighting against the urge to feed again.

## The Turned Man

When found, Edric was gaunt and bloodstained, with trembling hands and red-rimmed eyes. He shifted unpredictably between hunger, desperate flight, and paralysing guilt. Even after knocking a town guard unconscious, he resisted feeding on him.

Edric's fate exposed the human cost of Maltrius's expansion. He was dangerous, but he was also a victim who understood exactly what had been taken from him.
