# Welan Orvath

## Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race** | High Elf |
| **Role** | Knowledge cleric and intelligence gatherer |
| **Affiliation** | Islefield Second Council delegation |

---

## Description

Welan Orvath is an older-looking high elf in grey and white robes, usually carrying a heavy tome. He wears decorative reading glasses and an eye-shaped silver pendant associated with knowledge and observation.

## Role

Welan supports Tharion Aelindre's delegation through divination, languages, historical knowledge, healing, and mental magic. He is more dangerous as an intelligence gatherer than as a conventional combatant.

His habit of knowing details that were never shared openly makes conversations with him deeply uncomfortable.
