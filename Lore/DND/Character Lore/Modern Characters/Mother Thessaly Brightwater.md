## Description

Mother Thessaly Brightwater is a seasoned traveller and commanding healer who expects to be heard when divine matters are at stake. She combines restorative magic with control over storms and water.

## Spehlina Mission

Thessaly was passing through Dawerton when the party recruited support for the lake operation. She recognized Morjor and Varin's divine connections and insisted that any plan must attempt to save the Spehlina rather than simply slaughter it.

Her command of water, lightning, radiant magic, and resurrection made her one of the strongest possible allies for the mission.

## Notable Quote

> "If there is a way to save the creature rather than kill it, we take it."
