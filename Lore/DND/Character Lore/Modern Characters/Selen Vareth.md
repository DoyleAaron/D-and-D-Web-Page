## Description

Selen Vareth is a lean, quiet high elf with long dark hair worn in a braid. She wears scale mail beneath a travelling cloak and carries a composite longbow and shortsword.

## Role

Selen provides perimeter control for Tharion Aelindre's delegation. She habitually stands apart from a group, watches exits, and moves to a flanking position when danger begins.

She is an experienced archer and tactical fighter who prioritizes hostile spellcasters and anyone attempting to reach the envoy.
