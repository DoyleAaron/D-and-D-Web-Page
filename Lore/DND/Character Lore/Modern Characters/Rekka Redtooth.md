| | |

## Description

Rekka Redtooth is loud, direct, and newly devoted to Bahamut after Varin saved her life. Her faith is physical and unpolished: she prays through action, calls Varin "Holy Man," and invokes Bahamut's name while charging into danger.

## Spehlina Mission

Rekka was willing to join the Spehlina operation without payment if Varin approved. Her divine fury made her attacks carry radiant force, but she strongly opposed treating the corrupted guardian as nothing more than a target.

## Notable Quote

> "Bahamut says protect the innocent, and that thing is a prisoner!"
