## Description

The Blue Fire Golem was wrapped in cobalt flame that felt colder than ordinary fire. Its blue-white light resembled a dying star, burning with heat and supernatural cold at the same time.

## Abilities

- Projected a cold-fire aura around its body.
- Absorbed ordinary fire to repair itself.
- Released a surge that combined fire and cold across the room.
- Left behind a Cobalt Ember capable of relighting as magical blue flame.
