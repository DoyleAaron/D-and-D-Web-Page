## Description

Wrought-Iron House Golems are humanoid guardians built to resemble severe metal servants. Their polished frames, brass joints, and restrained movements suit the false respectability of Blackthorn House until they begin dragging intruders into killing positions.

## Abilities

- Hold stairs, hallways, and narrow entrances.
- Strike with heavy iron limbs.
- Use a magnetic lash to pull targets forward and knock them down.
- Resist ordinary weapons, poison, fear, and attempts to alter their form.
