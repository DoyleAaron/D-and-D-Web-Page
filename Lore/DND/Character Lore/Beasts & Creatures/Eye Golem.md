## Description

The Eye Golem was a towering brass-and-iron construct built around a single enormous glass eye. Its iris shifted through unnatural colours as it observed magic and weaponry with cold, mechanical attention.

Unlike a simple guardian, it adapted to repeated attacks. Methods that worked once quickly became less effective as the golem learned their pattern.

## Abilities

- Fired destructive beams from its central eye.
- Adapted its defences against repeated forms of damage.
- Suppressed magic within its gaze.
- Contained a cracked Arcane Lens that retained part of its adaptive power.
