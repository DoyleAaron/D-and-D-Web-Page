## Description

The Elven Tree Golem was made from an ancient elven-grown tree taken from somewhere it should never have left. Ossian petrified the wood, hardened its roots into limbs, and drove iron through its heartwood to form a spine.

Its shape still suggested something that once reached toward sunlight. In the vault, it reached only for intruders.

## Abilities

- Regenerated damaged bark unless burned.
- Grappled and restrained targets with heavy roots.
- Released poisonous spores that disrupted concentration.
- Contained a glowing Verdant Core used as a magical crafting component.
