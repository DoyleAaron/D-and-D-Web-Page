# Lava Golem

## Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Large Construct |
| **Location** | Basement of Blackthorn House |
| **Purpose** | Guard Liora and the infernal binding chamber |
| **Status** | Destroyed by the party |

---

## Description

The Lava Golem was the final basement guardian at Blackthorn House. Its stone and iron frame contained a molten infernal core, making close combat dangerous even when the construct was not actively attacking.

## Abilities

- Its heated body burned creatures that struck it at close range.
- Damage to its shell exposed a hotter, faster molten core.
- It sprayed magma across the basement, leaving the floor difficult to cross.
- It remained near Liora and prioritized anyone approaching her prison.

On destruction, it left behind an Infernal Control Shard.
