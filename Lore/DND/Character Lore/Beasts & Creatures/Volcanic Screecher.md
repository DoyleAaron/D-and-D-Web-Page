## Overview

| | |
|---|---|

---

## Description

Volcanic Screechers are large bat-like predators with charred, cracked skin and orange light glowing through their joints. Their wings trail heat shimmer, their bodies radiate painful heat, and their cries sound like steam escaping under pressure.

Increasing volcanic activity near Lavalto drove groups of screechers from their roosts and made them aggressively territorial.

## Abilities

- Swoop past enemies without slowing or exposing themselves.
- Tear prey with heated talons.
- Release an ember-charged screech that burns and deafens nearby creatures.
- Larger alpha specimens possess horned crests, brighter magma cracks, and devastating diving attacks.
