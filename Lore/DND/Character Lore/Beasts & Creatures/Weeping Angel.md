## Description

Ossian's Weeping Angels are stone statues carved as grieving elves with their faces buried in their hands. They remain completely motionless while observed. The moment every witness looks away, they cross impossible distances without any visible movement.

The Ossian Vault used pairs of the constructs to divide attention. Watching one meant losing sight of the other.

## Abilities

- Become inert stone while a living creature watches them.
- Move with unnatural speed when unobserved.
- Pass through openings far too narrow for their carved bodies.
- Seize victims with a temporal grasp that throws their actions out of sequence.
- Resist ordinary weapons, poison, psychic attacks, and attempts to petrify them further.
