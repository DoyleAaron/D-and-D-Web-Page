## Description

The Amethyst Golem was assembled from deep purple crystal and moved with the unsettling patience of something capable of thought. Light fractured across its faceted body while a psychic pressure gathered around it.

## Abilities

- Reflected weaker spells back toward their casters.
- Released crystal shrapnel when struck with a powerful blow.
- Disrupted concentration with a psionic pulse.
- Crushed grappled victims while attacking their minds.
- Left behind a Psionic Shard useful in intellect-focused magical crafting.
