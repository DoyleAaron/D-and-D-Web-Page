# Sandmaw

## Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Monstrosity |
| **Habitat** | Deserts and deep loose sand |
| **Status** | Known threat |

---

## Description

A Sandmaw is an enormous eyeless desert worm broad enough to swallow a person whole. Its pale grey-yellow body is segmented and rigid, while its circular mouth contains several rows of jagged inward-curving teeth surrounded by smaller hooked tendrils.

The first sign of an attack is often sand moving in the wrong direction. By then, the creature is already beneath its prey.

## Behaviour

Sandmaws sense movement through the ground and attack from below. They knock prey down as they surface, seize a victim in their jaws, and drag them beneath the sand to devour them. They show little self-preservation and do not willingly release captured prey.
