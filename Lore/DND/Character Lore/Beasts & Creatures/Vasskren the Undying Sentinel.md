## Description

Vasskren was Valdrek Ossian's final sentinel: a colossal undead construct assembled from a twenty-foot spine, a ribcage broad enough to fill a doorway, an oversized skull, and three independently moving arms.

One skeletal arm ended in a grasping claw, another wielded a massive stone maul, and the third was spectral, phasing through the vault's walls and floor. Pale light burned in the empty sockets of the skull as the scattered body dragged itself from beneath the Grand Hall.

## The Undying Sentinel

Vasskren's body fought as five connected parts: torso, skull, claw arm, maul arm, and spectral arm. The claw seized and held victims, the maul shattered clustered enemies, and the spectral limb reached creatures who believed distance or stone could protect them.

Destroying parts of the sentinel made the remaining body more violent. Vasskren could only be stopped by breaking the torso or destroying all three arms and forcing the unsupported body to collapse.

## Fate

The party defeated Vasskren and recovered five magical shards from its remains. Together, those shards unlocked the final door of the Ossian Vault.
