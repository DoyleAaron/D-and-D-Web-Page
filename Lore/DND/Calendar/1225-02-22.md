- The party sensed he knows more than he's saying — he is careful and controlled
- He pointed them toward **Blackthorn House** as the likely source of the spiritual disturbance

- **The party was beaten badly** — hostile spirits, aggressive undead presence, or supernatural hazards (exact encounter TBD)
