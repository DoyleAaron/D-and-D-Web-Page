# March 7–13, 1225

## The Spehlina Hunt

*The final operation before the party's return to Dawerton. Ships deployed on the lake/sea; party vs. the corrupted Spehlina with allied crews.*

---

### The Fight

- The party engaged the **Spehlina** — a corrupted lake/sea guardian being weaponised by Maltrius's forces
- **Both Amdir and Morjor almost died** during the fight — severe injuries, likely unconscious or on death saves at some point
- Allied ships (Ironwake, Luckpenny, Wavecutter) and hired crews were part of the engagement

### Varin — The Amulet & the Curse

- Varin attempted to **purify / save** the Spehlina, intending to restore rather than kill it
- He used **the cursed amulet** (the Asmodean necklace from the earlier curse arc) in the attempt
- The amulet's corruption turned the act: instead of purifying the Spehlina, it **killed it** — the very creature Varin was trying to revive/save
- However, **using the amulet broke Varin's curse** — expending or destroying the object severed the infernal link
- Varin's spellcasting returned to normal; the nightmare sequence ended

### Outcomes

### The Return

- Party returned to Dawerton harbour after the Spehlina was killed
- The explosion of the Spehlina's death was visible and audible from shore — residents knew something had happened at sea
- Luckpenny docked ahead of the party's main ships
- **Amdir received Father Maltrius's Sending spell** during the approach to shore *(see Session 27 Overview for full read-aloud)*
