# March 7-13, 1225

## Dawerton, Blackthorn House, and the Spehlina

*Across this stretch, the party returned from Braewood, rescued Liora from Maltrius's brood, recruited help at the port, and ended the Spehlina threat on the lake.*

---

### Return to Dawerton

- The party returned to Dawerton after receiving crown support and rewards in Braewood.
- They went looking for **Liora** to follow up on the Queen's payment and the next steps against the vampire threat.
- Liora was missing; the party learned she had been captured by vampires.

### Adrian's Lead

- The party spoke with **Mayor Adrian** about Liora's disappearance.
- Adrian told them where Liora had last been seen.
- His information pointed the party toward **Blackthorn House**, a manor outside Dawerton being used by Maltrius's brood.

### First Blackthorn Assault

- The party entered Blackthorn House to investigate.
- They were ambushed inside by the vampire household and its controlled servants.
- The fight turned dangerous quickly, and the party escaped by breaking a window and fleeing.
- Liora remained captured after the first attempt.

### Liora Rescued

- The party returned to Blackthorn House with a more careful plan.
- Varin scouted the manor in fly form before the group broke in through the rear.
- They used sunlight and radiant magic to disable the vampires long enough to push toward the basement.
- In the basement, they destroyed the golem guarding Liora and rescued her.

### Port Preparations

- Back in Dawerton, Liora put in the request for the promised **10,000 gp** payment.
- The party went to the port to recruit help for the Spehlina mission.
- Possible support included Captain Harlan Dusk and **The Ironwake**, Grint Copperhand's sellswords, the Driftwood Four aboard **The Luckpenny**, Rekka Redtooth, and local clerics.

### The Spehlina

- The party and their allies sailed out to fight the Spehlina.
- The fight went better than expected, though side characters died and both Morjor and Amdir were pushed into death saving throws.
- When the Spehlina died, its stored corruption exploded outward across the lake.
- Varin used the corrupted gem on another character, then used his draconic arm to purify the corrupted lake.

### Outcomes

| Outcome | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Liora | Captured, then rescued from Blackthorn House |
| Blackthorn House | Infiltrated twice; basement golem destroyed |
| Spehlina | Killed on the lake |
| Lake | Corrupted by the death blast, then purified by Varin |
| Party | Morjor and Amdir survived death saving throws |
| Reward | 10,000 gp payment request put in before the lake mission |
