# February 25 – March 6, 1225

## The Road to the Spehlina — Travel Arc

*This covers the period between the Wayfarers Guild meeting (Feb 24) and the return to Dawerton / Braewood ahead of the Spehlina hunt (Session 22 prep, starting ~March 7).*

---

### February 25–26 — Travel to Adaham
- Party departed Dawerton heading south for **Adaham**
- ~2-day journey by road
- **Got horses in Adaham** before departing on the next leg
- Adaham is the party's departure point for sea travel

### February 27 – ~March 8 — Rimduff Isle Voyage (~10 days)
- Departed Adaham by ship for the **Rimduff Isles** — roughly 2 weeks there and back
- Rekka Redtooth travels with them following her rescue on Feb 24
- Captain Kirk is present for this leg

#### The Iona Encounter
- **Captain Iona's fleet** intercepted the party's ships at or near the Rimduff Isles
- A fight broke out as the fleets came together
- **Captain Kirk crossed to Iona's ship** during the confrontation and delivered a personal speech
- Kirk **convinced Iona** to release his daughter **Marina** without a full battle
- Outcome: **minimal casualties** — the fleet engagement was stopped by Kirk's words rather than force
- Iona stood down and let Marina go free
- This resolved the long-running thread from Kirk's backstory

### ~March 8–9 — Return to Adaham
- Returned to Adaham after the Rimduff Isle voyage
- Picked up horses / resupplied

### ~March 9–10 — Travel to the Capital
- Rode from Adaham to the **Braewood capital** to collect outstanding rewards and report to the Queen
- Travel time approximately 1–2 days depending on route

### ~March 10–11 — Capital Visit
- Collected **10,000 gp** reward from the Queen (balance of the Spehlina commission, or a separate contract payment)
- Rested at the capital
- **Bram Stone revealed as a traitor** — the Queen informed the party
  - Bram's betrayal: nature/extent uncertain but the Queen had intelligence confirming it
  - The party had been working with Bram through the Blackthorn House operation

### ~March 12–13 — Return to Dawerton / Pay Commission
- Returned to **Braewood / Dawerton** from the capital
- Paid **Liora's scroll commission** — the party's 10,000 gp portion of the 15,000 gp total
  - Scroll commissioned: locator for a Balthazar Chosen
  - Completion expected in ~17–40 days (see Session 27 overview for confirmed dates: scroll returns March 31st)
- Final preparations before the Spehlina hunt
