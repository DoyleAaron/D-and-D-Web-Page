## Session 27 Begins — Return to Dawerton

*In-game start of Session 27. Party returns to shore after defeating the Spehlina.*

---

### Return to the Docks
- Party's ships arrive at Dawerton harbour
- Docks are busy and unsettled — the Spehlina's death sent a shockwave and sound blast that reached shore hours earlier
- People at the dock wall are staring at the horizon, speculating
- **The Luckpenny is already docked** when the party arrives

### Amdir — Maltrius's Message
- Before docking, **Amdir** received a *Sending* spell from **Father Maltrius**
- Maltrius was aware the Spehlina was destroyed; he chose to reframe it as an asset rather than a loss
- He instructed Amdir to travel to **Lavalto Peak** and arrive at the summit with his most powerful spell slot primed
- Amdir told no one *(see Session 27 Overview for full read-aloud and DM notes on Maltrius's actual plan)*

### Payment Scene
- The party met with **Aldec Tyne** at the docks to collect the Spehlina commission
- **10,000 gp** paid in full
- This money is now available for splitting and spending
