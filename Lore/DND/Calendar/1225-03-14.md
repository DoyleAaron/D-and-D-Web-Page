# March 14, 1225

## Spehlina Reward and Islefield Contact

*The party returned to Darwaeton after the Spehlina battle, claimed the reward, and drew direct attention from Islefield.*

---

### Return to the Docks

- The party's ships arrived at Darwaeton harbour after the Spehlina fight.
- The docks were busy and unsettled after the shockwave and sound blast from the lake.
- Survivors, hired allies, and dockside officials dealt with the aftermath of the battle.

### Payment Scene

- The party met with **Aldec Tyne** to collect the Spehlina commission.
- The **10,000 gp** reward was paid in full.
- The victory strengthened the party's standing with Braewood and Darwaeton.

### Wider Fallout

- The Spehlina's death and the lake purification shifted attention back toward the summit.
- The missing magical items and the threat behind them became more urgent.
- Varin's draconic arm and divine connection became central after the lake was purified.

### Islefield Approach

- After the reward and recovery period, an Islefield delegation approached the party.
- **Tharion Aelindre** represented the Islefield Second Council.
- The delegation offered a job tied to a supposed Balthazar-related item near the Ironwarf Heights.
- This offer set up the next expedition toward the dungeon that would eventually lead to Vasskren.
