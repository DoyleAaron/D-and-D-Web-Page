# February 23, 1225

## Blackthorn House — The Clear

### Morning — Bram Formally Recruited
- The party met with **Captain Elora** regarding Bram Stone
- **Corporal Bram Stone** was formally assigned to accompany the party for the Blackthorn House operation
- Bram is capable and willing — his local knowledge of the district is useful

### Evening — Fighting Into Blackthorn House
- The party returned to Blackthorn House with Bram
- This time they fought their way through the defences rather than retreating
- Cleared room by room — the house was heavier resistance than expected
- *See Session 23 & 24 prep files for full room-by-room enemy placements*

### The Bed & the Realm
- Deep in the house the party found **a specific bed or piece of furniture** with strange magical properties
- Touching it **transported the party to a spirit realm** — a pocket dimension tied to the house
- In this realm they encountered the **boss of the operation**: a figure known as "Father" (surname uncertain — likely connected to the Valdris name associated with the house)
- The party **killed him** in the realm fight

### Aftermath
- Returned from the realm with the house cleared
- **Liora was freed** from somewhere in the house (basement, most likely)
- The party returned to the inn and rested
