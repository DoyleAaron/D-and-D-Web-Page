# Resross — Shops & Services

Resross is a prison town. It has what it needs and not much more. The shops here serve guards, prison staff, travelers in transit, and the occasional worker from the Heights. Nobody comes to Resross to browse. They come to stock up quickly and leave.

Prices are fair. The town has no interest in gouging travelers — it just doesn't have a lot to offer.

---

## 1) The Iron Tap — Tavern & Inn

**Owner:** Brek Molna (human, 50s, ex-prison guard, took the shilling for 20 years and bought this place on his pension. Runs it the way he ran his shifts: efficiently, without drama, and with a watchful eye for anything that's about to go wrong.)  
**Style:** Clean, dim, slightly too warm from a good fire. The clientele is mostly off-duty guards and wardens. They don't bother travelers. Travelers don't bother them. This arrangement works.

Brek knows Vessa Dorn. He knows Warden Cosse. He knows the road northeast. He will answer direct questions with direct answers and will not volunteer anything.

### What Dara Knows (if asked directly)

Dara has sold Heights kits to enough people to know the rough shape of what's up there. She will say:
- *"The old mine entrance. Yes. Northeast, follow the Tharnic track."*
- *"I don't ask what people find in there. Easier that way."*
- *"Vessa at the shrine knows more than me. She always has."*

She will not ask why you're going. She doesn't want to know.

## 3) Vessa Dorn's Shrine — Ederith, Keeper of Paths (Divine Services)

**Keeper:** Vessa Dorn (human, 70s, has been in Resross longer than most buildings, keeps records that nobody else keeps, and knows things she shouldn't, which she attributes to Ederith's guidance and which is probably also just decades of paying attention)  
**Style:** A small stone shrine to Ederith — a minor god of paths, crossings, and transitions. Not widely worshipped, but appropriate for a town that exists on a road. The shrine smells of incense and dried herbs. Vessa is always there. Always awake, as far as anyone can tell.

> *She will know who you are before you tell her. Not because she's magical — she's just been watching the road for forty years and Tharion's group passed through here days ago. She puts things together.*

### What Vessa Knows

She will not lie to the party. She will also not offer information — they have to ask.

- **About the dungeon:** *"Northeast. Six miles. You'll find it."* (She pauses.) *"Come back out before you lose count of the hours."* She will not explain this further unless pressed hard — DC 14 Persuasion. If pressed: *"Time runs differently in there. I don't know the mechanism. I know that the man who came out last year thought he'd been inside three days. His hair told a different story."*
- **About Ossian:** She knows the name. *"A man who worshipped something old and paid for it."* That's all she'll say freely.
- **About Avethis:** *"An eye that doesn't close. A god, or something that became one. I don't worship it. I don't think many do anymore."*
- **About the cloak:** If the party mentions a cloak, or something Tharion wants from the dungeon, she goes quiet for a long moment. *"What someone leaves behind in a place like that — there's usually a reason it was left."*
- **About Tharion:** *"He came through. Polite. Asked me what I knew about the dungeon. I told him what I'm telling you."*
- **About Liora Cael:** If the name comes up, she simply says: *"She'll be waiting. She's patient."* She won't say for what.
