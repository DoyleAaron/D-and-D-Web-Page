# Yhey — Shops & Services

Yhey is divided. The upper district caters to elven residents and affluent visitors; the lower district handles everyone else. Prices in the upper district reflect both quality and the implicit tax of being somewhere you are tolerated rather than welcomed.

Non-elves who show money, restraint, and respect are served without incident. Non-elves who do not are asked to leave.

## 5) The Broken Hitch — Lower District Inn & Tavern

**Owner:** Maren Voss (half-elf, 50s, broad, former road merchant, bought this place because she was tired of sleeping in it. Runs it with exactly the energy she has left, which is enough.)
