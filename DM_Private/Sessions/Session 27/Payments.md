## Session 27 — Arrival at Dawerton (March 14th)

### 1. Aldec Tyne Payment — 10,000 gp
**Who:** Aldec Tyne (Braewood crown intermediary) pays the party  
**For:** Completion of the Spehlina mission  
**How:** Met at the docks or sent to a pre-arranged location (Tyne's preference is a private side room at the dockside warehouse, not out in the open). He pays in Letters of Credit drawn on the Braewood Royal Vault — each letter worth 1,000 gp, redeemable at any licensed money changer or the palace treasury. Ten letters. He hands them in a leather case, sealed with the Braewood crown stamp.

> *He doesn't count them out theatrically. He hands the case, meets eyes with whoever receives it, nods. "The queen will want to speak with you. I'll arrange it. Rest first."*

**Scene note:** Tyne also quietly mentions the Queen's request for the party to escort her to the summit. He frames it as an honour, not an order. He says it like he expects them to say yes.
