# Dawerton → The Ossian Vault — Travel Notes

**When:** Immediately after accepting Tharion's commission (~March 14–17, 1225)  
**Route:** Dawerton → [Coastal Road South] → Yhey (Islefield border city) → [Foothills Track] → Resross → [Ironwarf Heights path] → The Ossian Vault  
**Total Travel Time:** ~1 day on horse | ~3 days on foot

---

## Route Overview

| Leg | Distance | Horse | Foot | Notes |
|-----|----------|-------|------|-------|
| Dawerton → Yhey | ~20 miles | Half day | 1.5 days | Coastal road, then border crossing at Gate of Two Crowns |
| Yhey → Resross | ~15 miles | Half day | 1 day | Foothills track, less maintained |
| Resross → Dungeon | 6 miles NE | — | 2 hours | Cliff path up into Ironwarf Heights |

## Leg A — Dawerton to Yhey

**Terrain:** Well-maintained coastal road. Sea visible to the west for the first half. Road turns inland as it approaches the border. Traffic is consistent — this is an active trade corridor.

**Atmosphere:**
- Islefield carved stone markers appear after ~10 miles — eagle and branch motif, inscriptions in Elvish and Common
- Trees on the eastern roadside change noticeably: taller, older, clearly elven-cultivated
- The road is better maintained the further south they go — Islefield keeps its approaches clean

### Encounter: Elven Border Patrol

Three mounted Islefield outriders in light silver plate — kingfisher sigil on the pauldron. They match pace alongside the party without stopping, then the lead rider angles in.

**Lead rider — Sylara Vaen** (elf, appears ~22 by human standards, ice-calm, professional)

> *"You're heading south. Where to?"*

Standard procedure. She wants destination, purpose, expected duration in Islefield territory. She is not unfriendly. She is not warm.

**Key reactions:**
- **Amdir's name comes up:** She knows it. Her eyes go to him directly. She doesn't comment. She writes something down.
- **Morjor's Wayfarers Accord credentials:** She softens a fraction. The Accord has standing in Islefield.
- **Tharion's name:** The three riders exchange a brief look. She says *"Safe travels"* and they peel off immediately. He is known to them.

She does not care about criminal records from other kingdoms, merchant ledgers, or religious affiliation. She cares that they're not a Kluimont intelligence asset and not carrying infernal-attuned devices (DC 15 Arcana to know what counts as banned).

## Mandatory Scene — The Gate of Two Crowns (Arriving at Yhey)

This always happens regardless of the encounter roll. It is the border.

**The Gate:** Two carved stone pillars with intertwined crown-and-branch motifs. Permanent guard post. Twelve guards visible, six active at any given time. Heavy oak doors, iron-banded, open during daylight.

**Guard Captain — Warden Tethyn Osse** (elf, well into his second century, has processed thousands of people through this gate, genuinely unruffleable)

He checks:
1. Name and origin — written in a ledger
2. Purpose in Yhey — "Trade? Transit? Accommodation?"
3. Magical items carried — not confiscated, just logged
4. Duration of stay

**Character-specific notes:**
- **Amdir:** Will be logged under his full family name. *"Aeristel — full name for the ledger."* Stated like a toll collector calling a fare. He knows exactly who Amdir is. Amdir may feel this.
- **Morjor:** Logged as Wayfarers Accord. Osse notes this with a small nod. The Accord is respected.
- **Varin:** *"Varin — paladin, house unaffiliated."* If Varin mentions Bahamut, Osse nods with faint approval. No elaboration.

Non-elves are directed to the lower district automatically. *"The Broken Hitch on Millford Lane takes travellers. You won't find a room in the upper district."* He says this without apology.

## Leg B — Yhey to Resross

**Terrain:** The road south of Yhey becomes a foothills track within two miles. Less maintained. The coast is gone. Stone gives way to scrub grass. The Ironwarf Heights loom ahead, darker than the sky above them.

**Atmosphere:**
- Few other travelers on this leg
- The track is clearly used — bootprints, old campfire sites at one clearing — but not recently
- One point the track runs alongside a dry streambed with old Tharnic stonework along the banks (ruins of an aqueduct). The Tharnic influence becomes visible here.

**Random Encounter (roll 1d6 once for this leg — encounter on 5+):**

## Leg C — Resross to the Ossian Vault

**No encounter roll.** This is a 2-hour walk. It is an approach.

**Directions (from Vessa Dorn at the shrine, or from Tharion's briefing):** Head northeast out of Resross on the old Tharnic track. Follow it until it ends at a cut stone marker. Bear right up the slope. The entrance is a wide arch cut into the rock face.

**What the party experiences:**

**(30 minutes in):** The track narrows to a path. Soil gives way to exposed rock. The temperature drops even on a mild March day.

**(1 hour in):** The Tharnic survey marker — stone pillar, carved eagle over crossed picks. DC 13 History to read: *"Survey District NE-7. Ore extraction facility, Tharnic Colonial Authority. Under private management as of Colony Year 34."* The last line was carved by a different hand. Less precise. Later.

**(1.5 hours in):** The cliff face. The path ends. The entrance: a wide arch, 40 ft high, cut directly into the rock. Iron doors — permanently open, rusted to nothing, green-overgrown. The entrance does not look sealed. It looks forgotten.

**Passive Perception 16 (or active DC 16 search of the cliff face below the entrance):** Rusted iron pipe mouths in the rock face, ~80 ft below the entrance and to the east, partially obscured by overgrowth. Large enough for a medium creature to crawl into.

**DC 12 Perception (approaching the entrance):** The air from inside is noticeably cooler. And there is a faint smell — mineral, wet stone, and underneath it something older. Like a room sealed for a very long time.

**Boxed text for the entrance:**
> *The carvings around the entrance arch are not all Tharnic. Or rather — the administrative symbols, survey markers, are. But over them, in a different hand, someone has carved a repeated motif: an eye. Over and over, in every spare surface around the arch. They're not exactly decorative. They look more like a warning that forgot how to be frightening.*

## DM Notes

- **Time dilation begins the moment a creature enters** — via pipe outlets or the main arch. 1 hour inside = 1 day outside. The party will not know this unless they discover it inside.
- **Tharion's group is waiting at the Iron Tap in Resross.** They do not go in. Tharion's instructions: *"Retrieve the cloak. You can take anything else you find. Come back out."*
- **Pipe entry option:** Deposits the party into Room F (Cellar) among the Skeletal Workers. Bypasses the main entrance entirely. Time dilation still applies.
- **Tharion will not tell the party how long he's waiting.** He has, in fact, arranged a 4-day window before he sends a message to the council. He will say nothing about this unless directly asked.
