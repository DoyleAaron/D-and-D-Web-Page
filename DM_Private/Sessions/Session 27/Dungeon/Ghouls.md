## Actions

**Multiattack:** The ghoul makes 2 attacks: one bite and one claw, or two claws.

**Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft, one creature (not undead/construct).  
Hit: 2d6+2 (9 avg) piercing damage.

**Claw:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft, one target.  
Hit: 2d4+2 (7 avg) slashing damage.  
Target: DC 12 CON save or **paralysed** until end of their next turn. Undead/constructs are immune. On a failed save, the next attack against them has advantage.

## Tactics

Both ghouls wait until the party is partially spread out, then rush the most isolated target simultaneously. They prioritise targets who are:
- Already separated from the group
- Low AC / obvious casters
- Prone or otherwise disadvantaged

If one ghoul is turned or fleeing, the other continues attacking — they do not coordinate retreat.

**Key threat:** The paralysis on claw attacks. A paralysed player who gets bitten next round takes guaranteed crit damage (advantage on attack = crit on paralysed target). They know this instinctively.
