## Tactics

Room G patrols follow a set path. Light or noise in the furnace area triggers them. They attack the closest target and do not retreat.

Room N skeletons are "sleeping" in bunks — they do not activate until light directly hits them or a creature comes within 5 ft. A player moving carefully (DC 10 Stealth) can avoid triggering them.
