# The Ossian Vault — Dungeon Overview

**Location:** Southeast of Resross, toward the Ironwarf Heights  
**Original Purpose:** Tharnic Empire mining and ore-processing facility  
**Current Status:** Cursed by Necromancer Valdrek Ossian (deceased — sort of). Active undead, golems, constructs  
**Dungeon Level:** High (recommend party level 9+)

---

## The Curse

When Valdrek Ossian claimed the abandoned Tharnic mine and converted it to a place of worship for **Avethis**, he layered it with a temporal curse derived from the god's domain over witnessing — the idea that the Eye sees all time at once.

**Inside the dungeon, time runs differently: every 1 hour spent inside equals 1 day outside.**

This is not announced. Players will not know until they discover it. Clues exist if they look:

| Discovery Method | Clue |
|-----------------|------|
| Perception DC 14 (entry hall) | Symbols carved near the doorframe. Hourglasses. Eyes. The shape repeats. |
| Arcana DC 13 | The air itself has a faint temporal aura. Magic here feels slightly displaced. |
| Investigation DC 16 (worker tablets, lower-left) | A worker's diary fragment: *"We've been down here four hours and Seral says two days have passed above. The air is wrong here."* |
| Tharnic script tablet (mid-dungeon, DC 15 History) | *"Ossian's working corrupts the measurement of moments. Trust the eye symbols — one eye = one hour = one day."* |

**Party implication:** A 4-hour delve = 4 days outside. A full clear could mean 1-2 weeks of outside time passing. This should create urgency — especially with the scroll returning in 17 days and the summit in 40.

## Dungeon Structure Overview

The dungeon has three main wings branching from a **central entry hall**.

```
                          [ENTRY]
                             |
                    [CENTRAL HALL]
                    /      |      \
              [LEFT]   [CENTRE]  [RIGHT]
              WEST      MAIN      EAST
              WING      HALL      WING
```

The centre hall contains the most important loot and the necromancer's body. Left and right wings contain the 4 golems (2 per side) and various rooms.

> **DM Layout Note:** This dungeon is **single-level underground**. All references to "left/right/upper/lower" refer to **2D map position** (as if looking straight down at a plan). There is no second physical floor — "upper left" simply means further back-left on the map. The pipe system runs along the bottom of the map in 2D terms (see Section Q).

## CENTRE — Main Hall

### A. Entry Hall
A wide stone corridor. Tharnic carved walls — administrative records, survey logs (partial, can be translated with DC 12 History). Evidence of the place's working past: old iron hooks, collapsed shelving, rusted tools.

**Hazards:** Crumbling floor section (DC 13 Perception to spot, 2d6 fall if missed, drops into the cellar below).

**Perception DC 12:** Dust on the floor has been disturbed recently — something moves in here. Drag marks, not footprints.

**Loot:** Amid the collapsed shelving: a rusted iron lockbox (no lock — the mechanism corroded open long ago). Inside: **28 cp, 14 sp**, a small bronze Tharnic surveying token (worth 2 gp to a collector), and a broken compass rose stamp used by Tharnic cartographers.

### B. Central Chamber (Gargoyle Before Stairs)

A large vaulted chamber. Two cracked columns. The remains of a Tharnic administrative desk, stone, half-buried in rubble. At the far end: **wide stairs leading up** to the Grand Hall.

**Guarding the stairs: 1 Gargoyle.** It is perched on the column at the top of the stairs. It does not move until a creature gets within 20 ft or until attacked.

> *The gargoyle looks wrong. Not carved — collapsed into itself slightly, mouth wider than it should be. The stone is a different shade than the walls. It has been here longer than the building.*

**Gargoyle Stats (modified):**
- HP: 58 | AC: 15 | Speed: 30 ft / Fly 60 ft
- Stone Camouflage: Advantage on Stealth when motionless in stone environment
- Multi-attack: 2 claws + 1 bite
  - Claws: +4 to hit, 1d6+2 slashing each
  - Bite: +4 to hit, 1d8+2 piercing
- Damage Resistances: bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical
- Immunities: poison, psychic

When killed, it crumbles. Inside its chest cavity: a small piece of the original Tharnic cornerstone inscription mentioning **"the lower archive was sealed by the Master Ossian in the 34th year of the colony."**

**Loot (area after gargoyle):** Behind the stairs, in a hollow in the base of the cracked column: **65 gp** in old Tharnic coins, a *Potion of Climbing* (stoppered bronze vial, DC 13 Arcana to identify), and a dead cultist's satchel — moulded leather, contains a half-legible note in Ossian's hand: *"Do not wake the stones. They remember everything."*

### C. Grand Hall

Long and tall. High arched ceiling with carved eye motifs repeated along the vault — dozens of eyes, all sizes, all staring forward. The Avethis symbol is present (a single open eye with an hourglass pupil).

A stone dais at the far end. On the dais: **a massive carved eye, 4 ft across.** It appears to be purely decorative. It is not.

**The Eye — Secret Door:**
- Perception DC 17 to notice the seam around the eye
- Investigation DC 14 to find that pushing the pupil causes it to swing inward
- Opens to: **Ossian's Chamber** (the necromancer's room)
- **The door is magically sealed until all four golems are defeated.** Pushing the pupil before that point does nothing — no sound, no movement. The seam is still findable; the party simply cannot open it yet. Once the golems fall, the door becomes openable normally.
- Opening it is still the trigger for Ossian's chamber contents.

**Loot:** Along the wall behind the dais, three iron offering bowls remain mounted on brackets. Two are empty. The third contains: **40 gp** in large Tharnic silver discs, and a small *Bead of Force* (DC 15 Arcana to identify — looks like a dark glass marble).

**Tablet on the dais wall (Avethis prayer, carved in old Elvish — DC 12 Language):**
> *"He who watches all witnesses without judgment. To see is to know. To know is to transcend. The Eye does not close. The Eye does not turn away. The dead do not hide from the Eye. The living cannot."*

### D. Ossian's Chamber (Behind the Eye Door)

A large square room. Thick dust. A circular altar in the centre with the dried remains of **six worshippers** arranged around it in a ritual pose — seated, heads bowed, hands flat. In the centre of the altar: **the body of Valdrek Ossian** himself. Desiccated, ancient, robed in black and violet. He looks mummified.

**Around his neck: the Cloak of the Watcher's Veil** (the item the Islefield delegation wants). It is folded under him but visible.

**DO NOT DISTURB THE BODY.**

The moment any creature touches Ossian or the worshippers, or takes the cloak while Ossian's remains are undisturbed:
- All 6 worshippers animate as **Wights** (HP 45 each)
- Ossian rises as a **Mummy Lord** (see below)
- The door back to the Grand Hall swings shut and magically locks until Ossian is defeated

**If the party defeats the Mummy Lord Ossian:** The cloak is freely available. Also: all skeleton enemies throughout the dungeon immediately crumble (they were connected to him).

> **DM Note:** This is an optional encounter — the party can grab the cloak and the Mummy Lord only activates when touched. If they're careful and use mage hand or a stick, they can extract the cloak without triggering this. Let them think about it. If they DO fight Ossian, reward them — his defeat clears the dungeon and makes the rest much easier.

**Valdrek Ossian — Mummy Lord (modified, CR 11)**
- HP: 198 | AC: 17 | Speed: 20 ft
- Legendary Resistance (3/day)
- Mummy Rot: On successful slam attack, DC 16 CON save or cursed (can't regain HP, STR max reduced 3d6 per day until 0 → death → sand)
- Frightening Gaze: DC 16 WIS save or frightened, paralysed until end of next turn
- Legendary Actions (3/round):
  1. Slam (cost 1): +9 to hit, 2d6+4 bludgeoning + mummy rot
  2. Blinding Dust (cost 2): 15 ft radius, DC 16 CON save or blinded 1 round
  3. Channel Negative Energy (cost 3): All undead in dungeon regain 20 HP

**Loot on Ossian:**
- The Cloak of the Watcher's Veil
- A **journal** (partially legible — see tablet lore section)
- A **key** to the magically locked door (the final room)
- 340 gp in ancient Tharnic coins (worth same, collector value ×2)
- Scroll of *Speak with Dead*

### E. The Sealed Door (Final Room)

**Location:** Deep wall of Ossian's chamber, behind a tapestry depicting Avethis's eye.

**The Lock:** The door is sealed by a spell cast at **12th level**. It cannot be picked, bashed open, or dispelled by anything lower than a 12th-level caster. It glows faintly when touched.

**The Unlock Condition:** Five **Magical Cores** taken from the four limbs and head of **Vasskren** (the skeleton boss) must be placed into five indentations in the door — one at each cardinal point, and one at the centre.

| Core | Source | Slot |
|------|--------|------|
| Shard of Rending | Left Claw | North |
| Shard of Breaking | Right Maul | South |
| Shard of Withering | Spectral Arm | West |
| Shard of Vision | Eye Arm | East |
| **Shard of Witnessing** | **Skull (gem in forehead)** | **Centre** |

When all five cores are placed: the glow intensifies, then fades. The door swings open on its own.

**See: Vasskren stat sheet for how the cores are obtained.**

## LEFT WING — West (Cellar / Ore Facility)

### F. Cellar / Basement
Accessed from a door off the entry hall or through the crumbling floor trap in the entry corridor.

**Contents:**
- Old barrels, most rotted
- 6 **Skeletal Workers** (CR 1/4 each) — they shamble in a repetitive loop, mining tools in hand, pretending to work. Attack when disturbed or when the party gets too close. See [Skeletal_Workers.md](Skeletal_Workers.md) for full stats and saves.
- A **hidden side entrance** in the far wall (stone door, DC 14 Investigation to find, opens to outside — useful escape route)
- **The pipe access hatch** — see Section Q.

**Loot:** Among the rotted barrels: one intact sealed barrel marked with Tharnic trade stamps. Inside: preserved salted meat (no value), **55 sp**, a dented but functional *tinderbox*, and a leather cord with 3 small iron keys (they open the sleeping quarter lockers in Rooms N and O — no labels, but trial and error works).

*See Skeletal_Workers.md for full stats and spell save reference.*

---

### Q. Pipe System (Under the Dungeon — 2D Map: Bottom Edge)

**What it is:** A network of large iron drainage and ore-slurry pipes originally built by the Tharnic engineers to route waste water and ore runoff out of the mine and down the hillside. The pipes are large enough for a medium creature to crawl through (tight — disadvantage on attack rolls while inside).

**Access from outside:** The pipe outlets are visible on the cliff face approximately 80 ft below and to the east of the main dungeon entrance — a cluster of rusted iron mouths in the rock face, partially obscured by overgrowth. DC 14 Investigation (if searching the cliff exterior) or DC 16 Perception (passive, if passing nearby) to spot them.

**What the pipes connect to:**
- The cellar (Room F) — a large floor hatch, iron-ringed, DC 13 Strength to lift (rusted). Concealed under decades of debris; DC 15 Investigation from inside the cellar to locate.
- A secondary junction under the furnace area (Room G) — grate in the floor, locked (Thieves' Tools DC 14).
- The east mining floor (Room K) — a collapsed section partially blocked; Small creatures can pass freely, Medium creatures need a DC 13 Athletics check.

**Tactical note:** Entering via the pipes bypasses the main entrance entirely and places the party directly in the cellar (Room F) among the Skeletal Workers. No front-door encounter. The time dilation still applies the moment they enter the pipe at the cliff face.

**DM note — Liora's Door:**  
Approximately 40 ft along the pipe junction heading east (in 2D map terms, toward the right side), there is a section of pipe wall that is not pipe at all — it is a **seamless stone door** set into the cliff rock. There are no markings, no handle, no seam visible (DC 25 Investigation to detect — effectively undetectable without foreknowledge). If **Morjor** physically touches this section of wall, the door swings inward silently. He will feel no special sensation — it just opens. This leads to **Liora's prepared dungeon** (content TBD — does not need prep yet; this note marks its position only). No other party member's touch opens it.

### G. Furnace Level (Upper-Left, First Floor)

Ore smelting facilities from the Tharnic era. Three massive furnaces — cold now, but intact. Conveyor tracks (broken). Ore hoppers (empty). Iron chains hanging from the ceiling.

**Hazards:**
- A chain section collapses if more than 200 lbs stands under it (DC 14 Perception to spot, 2d8 bludgeoning)
- A furnace vent still releases gas pockets occasionally (DC 13 CON save or poisoned 1 hour, advantage if wearing nose covering)

**Undead here:** 4 **Skeletons** (standard CR 1/4) patrolling the floor.

**Tablet on furnace wall (Tharnic administrative — DC 13 History):**
> *"Production output Month 7: 340 units iron ore. 12 units silver ore. Workers: 28. Deaths this month: 3 (cave-in sector D, one fever). Overseer Brace notes workers are reporting sounds from the lower eastern wing. Investigation pending."*

### H. Golem 1 — Elven Tree Golem Chamber (Second Floor, Left)
*(See Golems.md for full stat block)*

A tall stone chamber with roots bursting through the floor and ceiling — an ancient elven tree, petrified and then somehow converted into a construct by Ossian's magic. The chamber smells of old bark and copper.

**Tablet near door (Ossian's writing — DC 14 Arcana):**
> *"The first sentinel is placed. The elf-wood took the binding well. It remembers what it was, and hates what it has become. That hatred makes it stronger."*

**Loot (after golem):** Core drops (see Vasskren? No — golem cores are different from Vasskren cores). A leather satchel wedged under a root: 120 gp, 2 healing potions (standard), a *Ring of Feather Falling* (250 gp value, useful in this dungeon).

### I. Kitchen / Break Rooms (Top-Left, Third Floor)

Workers' quarters. A kitchen with a long-cold hearth. A break room with stone benches. A washroom (bucket system, long dry). Nothing of combat interest.

**Secret Compartment in the washroom:** DC 16 Investigation to find a loose stone behind the latrine slab. Inside: a worker's **personal journal** (fully legible — reveals the timeline of Ossian's arrival and the workers' slow disappearance), and a **Potion of Greater Healing** (someone hid it for emergencies and never came back).

**Journal excerpt:**
> *Day 1 after the Master arrived: He says we are to remain. The work continues. He paid the foreman three times the rate. Day 8: Three of us tried to leave last night. They were at breakfast this morning. They don't remember trying to leave. Day 14: I am writing this because I think I might not remember writing it. The Master makes the torches burn blue. He says it's holy. The new workers he brought don't eat. Day 19: I think I'm the only one left. I found the others in the east wing. They are still standing. They are still smiling. I hid this here. If you find it, go. Now. Don't wait.*

### J. Blue Fire Golem Chamber (Second Floor, Left — Above Tree Golem)
*(See Golems.md for full stat block)*

A chamber converted into a magical forge. The walls are scorched blue-black. In the centre, encased in a ring of metal rods embedded in the floor, stands the golem — wreathed in cobalt flame.

**Gargoyle outside this room:** A second gargoyle perches on the arch above the doorway to this chamber. Same stats as the central one, HP 52. It attacks immediately when a non-Tharnic-sigil-bearing creature (i.e. the party) crosses the threshold.

**Tactical hazard:** The blue fire aura of the golem (2d6 cold damage to creatures that start turn within 5 ft) combines poorly with the doorway choke point. Players who charge in without thinking will be punished.

**Loot (after golem):** The metal rods are worth 40 gp as magical conductors (Arcana DC 12 to identify value). A **Spell Scroll of Counterspell (3rd level)** is etched onto a metal plate on the wall — requires 1 minute to carefully transcribe or can be used directly.

## RIGHT WING — East (Mining / Administrative)

### K. Mining Facilities with Track (Ground Floor, Right)

The eastern ground level mirrors the furnace area on the left — raw ore sorting, cart tracks (iron carts still on rails, moveable), a collapsed section blocked by rubble.

**Undead here:** 2 **Ghouls** who have been trapped in this wing since Ossian died. They attack immediately. See [Ghouls.md](Ghouls.md) for full stats and spell save reference.

**Loot:** The ghouls have been hoarding instinctively — in the corner behind the ore carts, amid old bones: **180 sp, 40 gp**, a *Potion of Greater Healing* (they cannot use it — just took it), a broken shortsword (salvageable blade, 5 gp), and a Tharnic administrative ring — bronze signet with an eagle crest (worth 25 gp to a Tharnic antiquities buyer, or 8 gp to a generic fence).

### L. Golem 3 — Amethyst Golem Chamber (Second Floor, Right)
*(See Golems.md for full stat block)*

A chamber of deep purple stone — amethyst deposits in the walls, floor, and ceiling. The golem stands at the far end, a hulking purple crystal construct. The room has a faint psychic resonance — characters with INT 14+ feel a slight pressure behind the eyes.

**Tablet on wall (Ossian — DC 13 Arcana):**
> *"The purple stone resists necromancy. Interesting. So I gave it something else — the memory of all who have died within these walls. It does not serve the dead. It remembers them."*

**Loot (after golem):** Scattered amethyst chips (total 180 gp in gems if collected). A **+1 Dagger** buried in the crystal debris at the golem's base.

### M. Meeting Chambers (Third Floor, Right)

A large room with a long table, still intact. Stone chairs. A cracked window opening (blocked by iron bars, but air comes through). This is where Ossian and his senior cultists met.

**Six sets of rotting robes still seated at the table — *these are permanent.* They don't animate.** But they look wrong for a long moment.

**Murals on the walls:** Full depictions of Avethis — the Eye God. A central eye with an hourglass pupil, ringed by smaller eyes, each showing a different scene (a battle, a birth, a death, a crown being placed). These are stunning artistic works, unsettling in their detail.

**Perception DC 12:** One of the mural's depicted eyes is watching the door. It moves when no one is looking directly at it. It is not dangerous — it is simply the residual divine presence of Avethis bleeding through the stonework. (This is how you introduce the concept before the party reaches the final room.)

**Loot:** Under the stone table — kicked there decades ago, possibly by someone fleeing — a locked iron document case (Thieves' Tools DC 13). Inside: **3 spell scrolls** (*Detect Magic*, *Identify*, *Comprehend Languages* — all 1st level, in good condition, written in Ossian's precise hand). Also 120 gp in old mixed coinage, and a wax-sealed vial of *Dust of Dryness* (DC 14 Arcana to identify).

**Documents on the table:**
- A crumbling letter in Ossian's hand (DC 14 History): *"The vault is prepared. The Eye will watch this place until the stones dissolve. Anyone who reaches the inner chamber and proves worthy of witness will be marked. The mark is not a curse. It is a gift. They will never be deceived again."*

### N. Sleeping Quarters — Lower Ranks (Third Floor, Right)

Bunks. Most collapsed. Skeletal remains in some. Nothing notable except 2 **Skeletons** (standard) still "sleeping" in bunks — they snap awake if the light hits them.

**Hidden compartment** behind a wall brick (DC 15 Investigation): A small lockbox (Thieves' Tools DC 12). Inside: 45 gp, a set of loaded dice (5 gp), a love letter addressed to "Mira, in the southern camp" — never sent.

### O. Sleeping Quarters — Upper Ranks (Third Floor, Right)

Better furnishings. Rugs (rotted). A wardrobe (empty). A writing desk with a cracked mirror.

**Loot:** The wardrobe isn't empty — it just looks it. False bottom (DC 14 Investigation): a sealed wooden box. Inside: **150 gp** in old Islefield gold (recognisable mint, 60+ years old), a *Ring of Warmth* (DC 13 Arcana to identify — protects against cold damage, advantage on CON saves vs cold environments), and a bundle of letters in careful handwriting addressed to someone named "Aether" — never sent, never found. Personal. Up to the party what they do with them.

**Perception DC 16:** The mirror briefly shows not your reflection but the room as it was — full of people, lit torches, laughter. Then it clears. This is another Avethis bleed-through. No mechanical effect unless a character reaches in (DC 18 Arcana — they see one random memory from the dungeon's past for 6 seconds, DM chooses).

### P. Golem 4 — Eye Golem Chamber (Fourth Floor, Right — Highest Point)
*(See Golems.md for full stat block)*

**Side Door:** There is a second door in this chamber — set into the left wall (in 2D map terms: the south wall), partially hidden behind a collapsed shelf unit. It is visible with DC 12 Perception. **This door does not unlock when the golems are defeated.** It has a different lock mechanism entirely — Thieves' Tools DC 22, or it can be broken down (AC 19, HP 40, bludgeoning only). What's behind it: a narrow passage leading to a dead-end storage room with old iron ore samples and broken tools. Nothing valuable — it was just a working area. But the party won't know that until they open it.

The highest accessible room in the dungeon. Hexagonal chamber. The ceiling is a painted dome — a massive single eye looking down, rendered in black and white. The golem stands in the centre. This is the most dangerous encounter in the dungeon before Vasskren.

**Weeping Angels in this wing:** Two weeping angels are placed in the corridor approach to the Eye Golem — see Weeping Angel entry below.

**Loot (after golem):** A **Tome of Avethis** (partial, 50 pages — grants advantage on Arcana and History checks if studied for 8 hours, but the reader must then make a DC 14 WIS save or become mildly fixated on knowing things they shouldn't — DM flavour only). Also: 220 gp in Tharnic gold discs.

## Weeping Angels (Special Undead)

**CR: 5 | Type:** Construct (Necromantic)  
These are stone statues — carved in the shape of weeping elves, faces buried in hands. Created by Ossian as guardians. They cannot move while observed.

### Stats
| | |
|--|--|
| **HP** | 85 |
| **AC** | 15 (natural stone armour) |
| **Speed** | 0 ft (while observed) / 40 ft (while unobserved) |
| **STR** | 18 (+4) |
| **DEX** | 8 (−1) |
| **CON** | 16 (+3) |
| **INT** | 3 (−4) |
| **WIS** | 10 (+0) |
| **CHA** | 1 (−5) |

**Immunities:** Poison, psychic, blinded, charmed, frightened  
**Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, piercing, slashing (nonmagical)  
**Senses:** Blindsight 30 ft | Passive Perception 10

### Stone Stillness
When any creature that can see the angel is within 60 ft, the angel is **completely unable to move or take actions**. It is treated as an object (AC 15, immunities apply).

### Quantum Movement
On its turn, if no creature within 60 ft has line of sight to it, the angel can move up to 40 ft. It does not provoke opportunity attacks. It can pass through 1-inch cracks. It cannot be seen doing this — you simply find it has moved.

### Actions (only when unobserved)

**Stone Fist:** +7 to hit | 2d8+4 bludgeoning  
**Temporal Grasp (recharge 5–6):** +7 to hit, grapple attempt (DC 15 STR/DEX to escape). While grappled, at the start of each of the angel's turns: DC 15 CON save or the target is afflicted with **temporal drain** — they lose their next round's action (they exist in a slightly offset version of time for that round, unable to act). This does not stack.

### Tactics
Angels spread out. One positions itself behind the party (using a blind moment during a fight). The other positions itself ahead. They wait for a moment when a character's attention shifts. A character watching two angels at once has to explicitly say so each round — if they don't state it, the DM may rule one moves. Make it tense. Make the players paranoid.

If both angels are being watched: stalemate. The party can walk past them. This is a valid solution — just keep your eyes on them.

**Weakness:** If an angel is hit while frozen, it takes full damage. If a character can Grapple it while it's stone-still (DC 15 STR), it cannot teleport while grappled.

## After All 4 Golems Are Defeated

When the fourth golem falls, the dungeon shakes. Dust falls from the ceiling. A low resonant tone — like a very deep bell — echoes through every corridor.

**Two things happen simultaneously:**

1. **The front entrance (main door) unlocks.** The heavy iron bar across the main entrance drops on its own with a loud clang. The party can now exit freely — or reinforcements can enter. If the party had been worried about being trapped, this is the reassurance. (It was sealed when they entered — Ossian's original warding.)

2. **The Eye Door in the Grand Hall unlocks.** The carved pupil can now be pushed. The door to Ossian's Chamber will swing open. (If the party found it earlier and was frustrated by it not opening, this is their cue.)

**What does NOT unlock:** The side door in the Eye Golem Chamber (Room P). Its mechanism is independent of the golem warding entirely.

**Then, from the Central Hall:** a grinding, cracking sound.

**Vasskren** rises from beneath the stone floor of the Grand Hall. The floor doesn't break — it separates like a curtain being pulled. Something very old and very large climbs out. The floor cracks open and it pulls itself out — a multi-limbed skeleton of enormous scale, held together by dark magical force.

> *The eye carvings along the Grand Hall go dark, one by one. And then there is movement in the floor. The stone doesn't break — it separates, like a curtain being pulled. And from the gap, something very old and very large begins to climb.*

See: Vasskren.md
