# Notes, Documents & Written Items — The Ossian Vault

*Every readable piece of writing found in the dungeon, listed by room. Includes journals, tablets, letters, plaques, and administrative records. Full text where legible.*

---

## Room 0 — Main Entrance

**Tharnic arch inscription** (administrative — DC 12 History to read):
Survey registration marks, lot numbers, and Tharnic colonial dating stamps. Standard bureaucratic record of the facility's founding. Nothing remarkable — except someone has scratched dozens of eye symbols over the top of the official text, overwriting the colonial authority with Ossian's Avethis iconography.

## Room 2 — Amethyst Golem Chamber

**Stone tablet on the chamber wall** (Ossian — DC 13 Arcana):
> *"Standard binding does not work on crystal. The stone resists the death-principle — there is no death in it, so there is nothing to leverage. I spent eleven days understanding this. ## Room 3 — Blue Fire Golem Chamber

**No surface writing survives** — the forge heat destroyed every wall inscription except the fireproof Counterspell plate on the west wall. But on that plate's shelf, inside a **sealed fireproof case**, Ossian left his private cipher journal (the one document he wanted close to a fire he could burn it in).

**Ossian's Cipher Journal** (in the fireproof case — DC 18 Investigation to crack the cipher, or *Comprehend Languages* reads it normally):

*First page (uncoded — plain Common):*
> *"If you are reading this in cipher, you are doing it wrong. If you are reading this clearly, you earned it. Everything I could not write in the other document is written here. I am sorry about approximately a third of it."*

*Decoded entries:*

> *"The Islefield surveyors who prepared the lower tunnels were not Tharnic subjects. I found their commission records in the collapsed Sector 2 archive. Paid through a Tharnic proxy. The originating account traces back to an Islefield 'Colonial Liaison Office' that does not exist in any current records — dissolved, I believe deliberately, shortly after Colony Year 31.*
>
> *Colony Year 31. I arrived in Year 34. Someone in Islefield was preparing this site three years before I came here. They knew someone like me was coming. Or they knew something was coming and wanted a person of my particular skills positioned to receive it."*

> *"The lower eastern wing was already sealed when I arrived. I had assumed I sealed it. I did not — I broke an existing seal and re-applied my own. The original seal was not Tharnic, not any method I have encountered in thirty years of practice. Much older. Applied by someone who understood what they were sealing in, and chose to seal it in rather than remove it."*

> *"Gaia. I am going to write the old name here because this is the document I will burn if I change my mind, and I have not changed my mind.*
>
> *Gaia. That is the ancient name, and the ancient name is safe — it is in a hundred dead manuscripts and frightens no one. He is not a historical figure. He is not a deity in any tradition I have studied. He is the being that the earth is when you ask it what it is. He was here. In this room. In the room below. He built in this place and he left what he built.*
>
> *What the old name connects to now — what it is called in this age, and who has been taking its instructions without knowing it — I will not write here. I have written it once, with the Eye, in the room below. That is the only place it belongs. If you want it, go down and earn it.*
>
> *The Islefield surveying party was working for someone who knew about him. Or for someone who worked for someone who knew. The chain is long and I cannot follow it further back than the second link."*

> *"The Cloak is not mine. I found it. I have documented what I found in the appropriate document in the room below. What I have not documented anywhere else: the Cloak was sealed in this archive by the same hand that sealed the archive itself. Old seal, unknown method. Same person who sealed in what was below.*
>
> *Someone sealed a witnessing device in here and walked away. I have thought about why for eleven months. The most honest answer I can produce: they sealed it here so it would be found by the right person at the right time. I do not know who the right person is. I do not know if it is me. I do not know if it is whoever reads this."*

## Room 7 — Ossian's Chamber

**Ossian's Journal** (on the stone shelf beside the sarcophagus — no check to find, DC 12 History to read; some pages water-stained but the main thread is legible; written over approximately fourteen months):

*Day 1:*
> *"The site is correct. The surveys are not. The lower tunnels have been worked since the Tharnic seal — recently, carefully, by someone who knew how to follow the old survey marks. I have examined fourteen disturbed blocks. Twelve reset cleanly. Two reset badly, as if in haste. Someone was here and left in a hurry. Note: the Cloak reacts to this place. Not to me. Not to proximity. To something in the stone that I cannot yet identify. I have checked this eighteen times. Eighteen."*

*Day 8:*
> *"I have divided the facility. My people hold the east section. The Tharnic workers hold the upper levels. They are not to cross the line I have set at the east arch. I explained this clearly. Most of them understood. Three did not, this morning. Two do now. One still doesn't. I expect this to resolve."*

*Day 20:*
> *"First sentinel placed. The elf-wood took the binding correctly — not well, correctly; I am not interested in impressions, I am interested in function. Note: it persists beyond what the binding should sustain on its own. Something here reinforces the work. I have not found the mechanism. I have noted it."*

*Day 34:*
> *"Four sentinels placed. The fourth required eyes. I will not elaborate on the procurement. Note: during the binding, every worker in the facility looked at the ceiling simultaneously. They could not explain why. Two have since refused to enter the east section. I have not required it of them. My people did not react. My people are no longer surprised by things like this. This is one of the advantages of working with people who have been through the process."*

*Day 51:*
> *"Vasskren is finished. I will not record what I used — the materials are in a separate log I have since destroyed. What I will say: the binding required something that had already chosen. Not been instructed — chosen. The result understood the parameters without being told the details. I find this notable. He will hold the inner door until someone places in correct order what he carries. The order is not arbitrary. The order follows a structure I found written below. If someone has read what I read, they will know the order. If they haven't, they won't."*

*Month 3:*
> *"The Tharnic workers have begun asking questions. Expected. They have noticed that my people do not eat, do not sleep in the conventional sense, and do not age at any observable rate. I have told them it is a religious observance. Technically this is accurate. Marren has been helpful here — she smiles at them, which they find reassuring. I have not told them that Marren's smile is no longer entirely voluntary."*

*Month 4:*
> *"I have found the origin of the Cloak. I will not record it here — this journal is not the appropriate document. What I will record: the Cloak was in the lower archive. The lower archive was sealed before the Tharnic colony existed. Someone left this here for someone who would eventually understand what they were looking at. I believe I am that person. I am not certain this is a good thing."*

*Month 7:*
> *"I have placed what I found in the archive with the Eye, in the room below the hall. It belongs there. Anyone who reads this and wants the full record: follow the sealed door in the south wall. You will need what Vasskren carries — all five pieces, in the correct slots. He will not give them without reason. Fighting him is the reason I expected. I designed the system for someone who would fight for access, not someone who would simply ask for it. I am aware this reflects something about how I think. I do not consider it a flaw."*

*Month 8:*
> *"I understand the time distortion now. The Eye does not only watch time pass. Its watching changes how time passes. A thing behaves differently when it is observed; this place is being observed constantly, perfectly, without blinking. The moments here are held too tightly. Inside, an hour remains an hour. Outside, the world moves on for a day. Observation is not a side effect. Observation is the mechanism."*

*Month 11:*
> *"The workers want to leave. I have told them the survey is ongoing. This is true. I cannot tell them what I am surveying because what I am surveying cannot be safely carried out of this place. I am aware of the irony of sealing myself in to protect knowledge from being carried out while also having sealed myself in. I made this decision several months ago and am only now committing it to this document, because I kept hoping I would change my mind."*

*Final entry:*
> *"Everything I built here was built around something that was already built. The golems, Vasskren, the door, the structure of the last fourteen months — it is a shell around something that did not need a shell. It only needed attention.*
>
> *I am not afraid. I want to be precise: not afraid. Finished. These are different states.*
>
> *The Cloak goes with me. It shouldn't leave this place but I cannot leave it here for someone who won't understand it. The record is below. The Eye is below. Everything I found, everything I understood, is in the room you reach by placing Vasskren's pieces in the door.*
>
> *If you are reading this: you are standing over it. Go down."*

**Loose sheet — tucked inside the back cover** (DC 13 Investigation to notice it is separate; ink noticeably fresher — written last):
> *"Addition. Written because I could not not write it.*
>
> *Gaia made three things here. Not golems — I made those. Not the guardian — I made him. Three things older than this facility, older than this stone, older than the language I am writing in. Records. I have called them 'items' in other documents because 'items' implies they can be moved and handled, which they can. Records implies what they actually are.*
>
> *They are not here. They have not been here for a very long time. The transfer document I found was Colony Year 31 — three years before I arrived. Someone who knew they were not ordinary survey materials removed them and filed where they went. I do not know if the person who filed it understood what they had. I suspect they knew only enough to hide them in a sealed administrative archive rather than a public collection.*
>
> *Who Gaia is, and what the name turns into when you follow it forward into this age, I have written once — in the room that is the appropriate place for that information. I have not written it here."*

**Scroll of Speak with Dead** (inside reliquary behind north tapestry — Perception DC 15 / destroy AC 13 HP 15), alongside a note in Ossian's hand:
> *"If someone found this box and destroyed it, they've earned the right to ask me questions. Use the scroll on me. I will answer what I consider relevant. I will not be offended by questions I consider irrelevant — I will simply not answer them."*

## Room 17 — The Eye Chamber

**Ossian's Research Codex** (stone alcove, wrapped in oilskin — no check to find; DC 12 History for the Common sections, a Language check / *Comprehend Languages* for the Old Elvish):

> *"Entry 1 — What this place is.*
>
> *I was wrong about why I came here. I came because the surveys mentioned unusual stone formations in the lower tunnels and a thaumic resonance no one could explain. I thought I was investigating a natural occurrence. I was not. I was following directions. Someone had arranged for a person like me to find this, in the same way you arrange furniture for a guest you expect: deliberately, in advance, with specific intent. Note: I am not troubled by this. I am interested in it."*

> *"Entry 4 — What was here first.*
>
> *The inscription on the dais in the Grand Hall predates this facility by centuries. The stone is non-local — brought here, shaped, placed, inscribed by one set of hands. The inscription reads: 'a witness was needed.' You do not say 'a witness was needed' unless you are about to do something that matters. One being. Alone. Building a place for something to watch from."*

> *"Entry 7 — The maker. The old texts give him a name: Gaia.*
>
> *I write it plainly because the name is ancient and, I am almost certain, safe. It sleeps in a hundred mouldering manuscripts and means nothing to anyone living. Gaia is not a god in the conventional sense. He does not receive worship. He is the earth. The literal earth — this ground, the rock under it, the heat at the centre, the roots that hold the whole thing together. That is him, or he is an expression of that, or the distinction between 'him' and 'it' does not survive contact with what he actually is. He came here — made himself small enough to come here — because he wanted to make something."*

> *"Entry 9 — What he made.*
>
> *Three records. He created three things in this room that are records of three acts. The first: he gave form to something formless. The second: he gave persistence to something temporary. The third: he gave boundary to something unbounded. These are not metaphors. These are descriptions of things that happened. The records he made of them: one is a book — the present age calls it the Chart of the Threefold Way. One is a ring. One is a crown. They were made here. They are no longer here."*

> *"Entry 11 — The Eye.*
>
> *He did not leave the records here for safekeeping. He left them here because this is where they were made. What he chose to leave here, deliberately, was the Eye. The Eye is not a structure. It is a being. He made it here, as a companion — something to watch while he worked, because he was alone in the way a first thing is alone: completely, without category. He made a friend out of witnessing, because witnessing was the only thing he had to give.*
>
> *My followers call it Avethis. They believe they are worshipping the Eye of some ancient pantheon. They are not. They are worshipping a thing made out of loneliness — a companion the soul of the world built so it would not have to make everything alone, and then outlived. A minor god of pure witness, present in every room of every life ever lived. I find that unbearably sad. I have not told them.*
>
> *Note: Gaia is approximately as powerful as the world, because he is the world. And because he made it, the Eye is — functionally, for anyone who encounters it — a power that does not have a smaller word than deity. I have been in this room for four days. I have not been harmed. I have been seen. Completely. Without remainder."*

> *"Entry 14 — The part I swore I would not write.*
>
> *Gaia did not die. The earth does not end. He is still here. Still moving the way a world moves. Still — and this is what took me eleven months to accept — still acting. Still making arrangements. And he wears a name now. A present name. One the living would recognise, because the living have met him, dealt with him, taken his instructions without once suspecting what they were standing in front of.*
>
> *I said I would never write it. I am writing it exactly once, here, in the one room where it belongs:*
>
> *Gaia is the one they call Balthazar.*
>
> *Balthazar is not a man who became powerful. Balthazar is what the soul of the world is calling itself in this age — the current shape of a thing older than shape. Everything laid at the archmage's feet — the vault, the Three, the chosen messengers, the long patient designs — is not the work of a wizard. It is the work of the ground you are standing on, wearing a name and a face the way you or I would wear a coat. I have written this nowhere else. If you are holding the Codex, you are holding the only place the two names sit on the same page."*

> *"Final entry.*
>
> *I am going to stay here. Because this room contains the most significant fact I have ever learned, and leaving it unguarded is not something I am willing to do. If you are reading this: you were not careless. You were thorough, and you fought for what Vasskren carried, and you placed the pieces correctly. The Eye has already seen you. It sees everything. Welcome to the record."*

**Slip of paper** (tucked between the last pages — Perception DC 16 to notice it is a separate insert; easy to miss, no heading, slightly smaller than the Codex pages):
> *"Transfer document. Colony Year 31. Three items removed from the lower eastern archive, recorded as 'survey materials, misc.' Recipient: Islefield Administrative Authority. Storage: lower archive, administrative quarter, sealed section. Signed by a name I recognise from old Second Council records. This was not routine. This was filed by someone who knew the items were not ordinary survey materials.*
>
> *The records of Gaia are in Islefield. Under it, to be precise: beneath the old administrative quarter, in a sealed lower archive. I do not know if anyone there knows what they are."*

# Exit Map — Room 8

*For when the party needs to get out and the front door is barred.*

---

## Finding Room 8

Room 8 is in the far western wing, accessible via the left-wing corridor past Rooms 1, 3, 16, and 13. It appears to be a dead-end.

**Hints pointing here:**
- Room 16: Scratch mark on the southwest wall — three lines and a westward arrow (DC 13 Perception, DC 12 History to interpret)
- Room 12: Tharnic survey map labels this section of cliff "Emergency Egress — Sector West Terminal"

**Finding the exit from inside:**
- DC 15 Investigation: faint scratch marks on the floor (boot marks — many workers came here over the decades) and mortar between blocks that's more worn than the surrounding walls
- DC 15 Investigation (left wall, knee height): a lever, set into the wall. Releasing it opens the wall panel outward silently.
- Alternative: The wall can be broken down — AC 17, HP 30, bludgeoning or piercing only.

---

## The Route Out

```
[Room 13 — Main Work Area]
          |
          | (west corridor)
          |
[Room 8 — Dead-End Corridor]
          |
          | (lever or break wall)
          |
[Narrow passage — 3 ft wide, 20 ft long]
          |
          |
[Cliff Ledge — overgrown, exterior]
          |
          | (10 minutes careful footing — narrow path descends)
          |
[Base of Ironwarf Heights]
```

**The cliff ledge:** Overgrown with scrub grass and lichen. The path down is narrow — single file, 10 minutes of careful movement. DC 12 Athletics in bad weather or darkness, DC 8 otherwise. A fall from the path is approximately 40 ft to a rocky slope (4d6 bludgeoning, DC 13 DEX save to catch a handhold and reduce to 2d6).

**At the base:** Open terrain. Resross is approximately 2 hours travel northeast. The dungeon entrance is around the cliff face to the east — visible from the base but not easily accessible without climbing back up.

**The passage stays open:** Once the wall panel is released or broken, there is no mechanism to close it from outside. The dungeon is permanently accessible via this route afterward — which cuts both ways.

---

## Sewer Alternative Exit

The sewer has no external exit within this dungeon. The drainage system exits somewhere east of the Heights — the survey map in Room 12 shows the route heading off the edge of the mapped area. Not a viable exit without further exploration (future session).
