# Weeping Angels — Character Sheet

**Location:** Corridor approach to Eye Golem Chamber (Room P approach — east wing, upper floor)  
**CR:** 5 | **XP:** 1,800 each (×2 = 3,600 total)  
**Type:** Construct (Necromantic)  
**Count:** 2

> *Stone statues carved in the shape of weeping elves — faces buried in hands. Created by Ossian as corridor guardians. They cannot move while observed. The tension is the point.*

## Special Traits

**Stone Stillness:** When any creature that can see the angel is within 60 ft, the angel **cannot move or take actions**. It is treated as an object with AC 15 and standard immunities.  
*Crucial note: "can see" means line of sight and within 60 ft. Darkness, walls, or looking away breaks this.*

**Quantum Movement:** On its turn, if no creature within 60 ft has line of sight to it, the angel can move up to 40 ft. It does not provoke opportunity attacks. It passes through 1-inch cracks. Its movement cannot be observed — it simply has moved.

---

## Actions *(only when unobserved)*

**Stone Fist:** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft.  
Hit: 2d8+4 (13 avg) bludgeoning damage.

**Temporal Grasp (Recharge 5–6):** Melee Attack: +7 to hit, one creature. On hit: grapple attempt. Escape DC 15 STR or DEX.  
While grappled, at the start of each of the angel's turns: DC 15 CON save.  
**Fail:** **Temporal drain** — the target loses their next round's action entirely. They exist in a slightly offset version of time and cannot act (no movement, no action, no reaction). *Does not stack.*

## Tactics

**Setup:** One angel in front of the party, one behind (it repositioned through a blind spot earlier — the party should have a moment of realising this).

**Goal:** Wait for a moment when a character's attention is divided. A player watching two angels simultaneously must say so explicitly each round — if they don't state it, the DM may rule one moved.

**If both are being watched:** Complete stalemate. The party can walk past them. This is a valid solution — just keep your eyes on both at all times.

**Effective counter:** Keep torches high, have someone dedicated to each angel, use *Moonbeam* or an effect that forces them to stay in place.

## Running Notes

- Make the players paranoid. Describe the sound of stone scraping. Then describe silence.
- If a player blinks, describe the angel slightly closer than before.
- A character who closes their eyes for any reason (even blinking declared out-loud) may hear the 40 ft sprint begin.
