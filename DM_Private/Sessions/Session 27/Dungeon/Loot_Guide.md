### Cloak of the Watcher's Veil [Room 7 — on Ossian]
**Type:** Wondrous Item | **Rarity:** Rare | **Attunement:** Yes  
**Appearance:** Deep grey, almost black. Inner lining is silver-grey with tiny woven eyes visible only in dim light. Wide hood. Bone clasp shaped like an hourglass. Very old — pre-Tharnic origin.

**Properties:**
- **Watcher's Sight (Passive):** Advantage on Perception checks involving watching or observing (sight only, not hearing)
- **Peripheral Awareness (Passive):** Cannot be surprised while wearing it and conscious
- **Fold Into Shadow (1/day):** Bonus action — become heavily obscured in dim light or darkness. No Stealth check needed. Lasts until you move or take an action.
- **The Watcher Knows (1/week):** Action — focus on one creature within 60 ft. For 1 minute, you always know that creature's exact position even if invisible or behind cover.

**DM Note:** The Islefield Second Council (via Tharion) wants this for political surveillance at the upcoming summit. See Cloak_of_the_Watchers_Veil.md for full context.

### Ossian's Research Codex [Room 17]
**Type:** Lore item / Wondrous Item  
**Appearance:** Complete illuminated manuscript, Common and Old Elvish. Ossian's private life's work.

**Contents:**
- The Eye (Avethis) was *made* — a companion the earth-god **Gaia** created here at the dawn of the world, which became a god in its own right over the eons. It predates the Tharnic pantheon and the elven gods.
- Gaia made the Three here — the Book (the Chart of the Threefold Way), the Ring, and the Crown — records of three acts of creation. They were removed Colony Year 31 and are now stored under Islefield.
- The name **Gaia** is written plainly throughout. The final entry — and only the final entry — reveals that Gaia is the one the present age calls **Balthazar**: the one place Ossian commits both names to a single page.
- Final entry: *"The Eye does not judge. It only sees. I understand now why that is worse."*

**Mechanical benefit:** A character who studies it for 8 hours gains advantage on History checks related to Avethis, the Tharnic Empire, or Islefield's founding. Permanent.

**DM Note:** The most important lore item in the dungeon. The party probably won't understand it fully yet. File it away.

## Notable Non-Magic Items

Partial. Dated ritual entries, increasingly fragmented toward the end. Readable with DC 12 History. Last coherent entries describe the "final binding" and a sense that something was watching him back.

30 pages in personal cipher. DC 18 Intelligence (Investigation) to crack, or *Comprehend Languages*. Contains Ossian's private discovery that Islefield funded surveys of the Ironwarf Heights site before Tharnic occupation — connects directly to the payment receipt in Room 5.

### Scroll of Speak with Dead [Room 7 — on Ossian]
Not a spell scroll (can't be transcribed). A ritual scroll — any creature can use it with 10 minutes of preparation. Allows 5 questions to a corpse. The corpse answers truthfully but only what it knew in life. Ossian's own body could be questioned with this. Single use.

*Possible questions for Ossian: Who built the original facility? What is the cloak for? Who is Balthazar? Why did you seal yourself in here?*

A Tharnic administrative document, partially overwritten by Ossian. Records payment from the "Islefield Colonial Liaison Office" for survey consulting at the Ironwarf Heights site in Colony Year 31. Ossian arrived Colony Year 34. Connects to Ossian's Cipher Journal in Room 3.

No mechanism. No ritual. When you are ready, close your eyes, and you see.

## Who is Gaia? — and why the Codex writes "Balthazar" only once

*(DM reference. In-world, the dungeon notes name only **Gaia** throughout; the single place the two names are connected is the final entry of Ossian's Research Codex in Room 17. Keep it that way at the table — let the reveal land there.)*

**Gaia = Balthazar.** Gaia is the ancient name: the earth incarnate — mother nature given a being, the soul of the world. **Balthazar is simply the current version of Gaia** — the name and face it wears in this age. The party have been hunting "the Archmage Balthazar" and his vault; the Codex is where they learn the archmage is the earth itself, made small enough to wear a man's life.

**What Gaia made here:** the Three — the Book (the Chart of the Threefold Way), the Ring, and the Crown — records of three acts of creation; and the Eye (Avethis), a companion made out of loneliness that became a god. The Three were removed Colony Year 31 and now sit under Islefield.

**What the party can learn here:** if they find and understand the Room 17 transfer slip, they can learn the Three were stored beneath Islefield's old administrative quarter, in a sealed lower archive. This is the intended location clue. It does not mean anyone currently in Islefield knows the vault exists.

**The vault beneath Islefield:**
Balthazar built his true vault beneath Islefield — for the old Queen of Islefield, whom he loved. The current Queen doesn't know it's there. Her corrupt advisor does not know it's there. No one currently in power knows the vault exists. The Second Council's records preserve old gaps and taboos around Balthazar's name, but no living councillor understands what those gaps point to. The Three were filed there in Colony Year 31.

Ossian came close to understanding what the Cloak actually witnessed. He stopped short — deliberately. He sealed himself in this dungeon rather than carry that knowledge back into the world.

> *"He built something that shouldn't exist. The Eye watched him build it. That is what the Cloak carries — not a record of Islefield's founding. A record of what he put beneath it, and why. I understand now why the oldest records avoid his name. They are afraid that saying it will remind something that the name still exists."*
> — Ossian, partial draft entry, crossed out

**For Morjor:** Balthazar told him to deliver the Ring to the Queen of Islefield at the Summit — the Queen who doesn't know the vault is under her own lands. Whatever Balthazar is, he's still giving instructions. This dungeon is the first place the party can learn that the "archmage" giving Morjor orders is the soul of the world.

**What the Second Council actually wants the Cloak for:** surveillance at the summit and political leverage. Their "Balthazar artefact" explanation is a cover story, not an accurate understanding. They do not know that the Cloak's record points to a vault beneath Islefield. The Cloak's record is sealed to anyone who wasn't present — but the right person, in the right circumstances, might read it.

*Tweak as needed. Core facts: Gaia and Balthazar are the same being, the vault is real, no current Islefield power knows the vault exists, the Second Council wants the Cloak for surveillance and leverage rather than true vault knowledge, and Ossian understood just enough to be terrified.*

## Scroll of Speak with Dead — Ossian's Answers

*5 questions maximum. Ossian answers truthfully but as a dead creature — his answers reflect only what he knew in life. He is not hostile, not distressed. He has been dead for sixty years and is at peace with it.*

*Voice: quiet, precise, slightly formal. Like a man giving a deposition.*

---

**Q: Who built the original facility?**
> *"The Tharnic Empire constructed the surface facility. The tunnels and the lower architecture were built earlier — by Islefield surveyors, under private commission. I found the payment records. I do not know who commissioned them. I know that someone in Islefield wanted this location mapped and accessible before the Empire arrived."*

---

**Q: What is the Cloak for?**
> *"It is a record. Avethis witnessed an act — I came to believe it was the act of building. The vault, the thing beneath. The Cloak holds that witnessing. It does not carry a judgment. Avethis does not judge. It carries the fact of what happened, exactly as it occurred, for as long as the Cloak survives. I spent twenty years trying to read what it witnessed. I could not. The record is sealed to anyone who was not present."*

---

**Q: Who is Balthazar?**
> *"You use his present name. I spent years using his old one. Gaia. The earth — not a god of the earth, the earth itself, given a will. What you call the Archmage Balthazar is what that will is calling itself now. He made three things here, at the start of everything, and he made the Eye to watch him do it. He built something beneath Islefield. I know Avethis watched him put it there. I wrote the whole of it once, in the room below, with the Eye. I would not write it twice. I began to feel, when I wrote it, that something noticed. I am not given to superstition. I stopped."*

---

**Q: Why did you seal yourself in here?**
> *"I finished. The work was complete. The golems were placed. The vault was consecrated to Avethis. I understood that leaving would require me to carry what I had learned about the Cloak out into the world where others could take it from me. I did not want to carry it. I chose to remain with it. My worshippers remained with me. That was their choice. I did not ask them to stay."*

---

**Q: What does the vault beneath Islefield contain?**
> *"I do not know precisely. My research indicated it seals something. Balthazar's notes — which I accessed partially through old Tharnic records — described it only as 'the consequence of the first choice.' I believe it is a binding. Something bound in place. I chose not to investigate further. Some knowledge is correctly left alone."*

---

*Additional questions the party might ask:*
- **"What should we do with the Cloak?"** → *"That is not a question I have an answer to. I acquired it. I protected it. What it is for is not mine to say."*
- **"Who is Avethis?"** → *"Made. Not born — made. By the same presence whose name I would not write. Made out of loneliness, I believe, before anything else existed to witness. It has been watching since before the first age. It became a god slowly, the way still water becomes deep. It is not a great god. It is a god of pure sight. It will not answer you. It will not help you. It will only see you. It has always already seen you."*
- **"Is there anything below the drainage tunnels?"** → *"Yes. I did not go there. The surveyors' maps showed a lower passage. They wrote 'do not chart' and stopped. I respected that."*
