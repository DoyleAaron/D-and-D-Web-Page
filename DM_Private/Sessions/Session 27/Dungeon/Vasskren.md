# Vasskren the Undying Sentinel — Final Boss

**Location:** Grand Hall (centre dungeon) — rises after Ossian is defeated  
**CR:** 15 | **XP:** 13,000  
**Type:** Undead Construct (Avethis-Anchored)

---

## Lore

Vasskren was the last thing Ossian built — not from stone or iron, but from the accumulated bones of every worker, cultist, and creature that died within the vault. He is held together not by necromancy but by Avethis's own passive presence in this place. The Eye God did not create him deliberately. Vasskren is what happens when too much death accumulates in a space that a god of observation watches without ever looking away.

He does not speak. He does not hesitate. He witnesses your efforts and finds them interesting.

> *The floor of the Grand Hall cracks in four places at once. Stone slabs push upward and topple as something below pushes through. What rises is not one creature — it is assembled. A spine twenty feet long. Ribcage wide as a doorway. Four arms pulling itself out of the stone like a man climbing out of cold water. A skull that turns, slowly, and stops when it finds you.*

---

## Multi-Limb Combat System

Vasskren has **six parts**, each with its own HP pool. **Each arm has its own initiative** — roll separately for each at combat start.

Parts can be targeted individually (player declares target: torso, left claw, right maul, spectral arm, eye arm, skull).

**The main body (torso) does not die until its HP reaches 0.** Destroying limbs weakens Vasskren but does not kill it.

**Kill condition:** Torso reaches 0 HP, OR all 4 arms are destroyed (at which point Vasskren collapses even if the torso has HP remaining — it cannot support itself).

## Part Stats

### Vasskren — Torso (Main Body)

**HP:** 240 | **AC:** 18 (divine bone plating)  
**Speed:** 30 ft  
**Initiative:** +4 (rolls first among all parts)

| Ability | Score | Modifier |
|---------|-------|----------|
| STR | 24 | +7 |
| DEX | 10 | +0 |
| CON | 22 | +6 |
| INT | 5 | −3 |
| WIS | 14 | +2 |
| CHA | 1 | −5 |

**Saving Throws:** STR +12, CON +11  
**Damage Immunities:** Poison, necrotic, psychic  
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralysed, petrified, poisoned  
**Magic Resistance:** Advantage on saves vs. spells  
**Senses:** Darkvision 120 ft, Truesight 30 ft | Passive Perception 12  
**Languages:** None

#### Legendary Resistance (2/day)
If Vasskren fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

#### Bone Collapse (Triggered)
When reduced to **150 HP or below**, the rib cage violently fragments outward:  
All creatures within 20 ft: DC 17 CON save. Fail: 4d8 piercing. Success: half.  
*(Triggers once only.)*

#### Actions
**Spine Slam (2/action):** +12 to hit | 2d12+7 bludgeoning | Reach 15 ft  
*(The torso itself swings — it has reach from its sheer size.)*

#### Legendary Actions (3/round, end of another creature's turn)
1. **Stomp (cost 1):** All creatures within 15 ft: DC 17 STR save or knocked prone.
2. **Harvest Soul (cost 2):** Ranged attack, +10 to hit, range 60 ft. Hit: 4d8 necrotic. Target max HP reduced by half the damage taken until a short rest. (Does not stack beyond −20 HP reduction per creature.)
3. **Limb Emergency (cost 3):** One destroyed limb is **reattached** at 40 HP. This can only happen once per limb, once total per combat.

### Left Claw Arm (Shard of Rending)

**HP:** 100 | **AC:** 15  
**Initiative:** +2  

**Claw Rake:** +11 to hit | 2d8+7 slashing | Reach 10 ft  
**Grapple:** On a hit with Claw Rake, target must DC 16 STR save or be **grappled** (Escape DC 16 STR). While grappled, Vasskren deals 2d8+7 slashing at start of grappled target's turn automatically.

**On Destruction:** The arm detonates into bone shards. 10 ft radius: DC 14 DEX save. Fail: 2d8 piercing. Success: half.  
**Drops:** **Shard of Rending** (door core #1 — red-tinged bone fragment, warm to the touch)

---

### Right Maul Arm (Shard of Breaking)

**HP:** 100 | **AC:** 15  
**Initiative:** +1  

**Crushing Blow:** +11 to hit | 3d8+7 bludgeoning | Reach 10 ft  
On hit: DC 17 STR save or knocked back **15 ft** and knocked **prone**.

**Shockwave (Recharge 6):** The arm slams the floor. 30 ft cone: DC 17 STR save. Fail: 4d6 bludgeoning + prone. Success: half, no prone.

**On Destruction:** The arm falls, pinning any creature in melee range. DC 14 DEX save or restrained under the arm until a DC 16 STR check (action) frees them.  
**Drops:** **Shard of Breaking** (door core #2 — iron-grey bone, heavy)

---

### Spectral Arm (Shard of Withering)

**HP:** 90 | **AC:** 13  
**Initiative:** +3  
*This arm is partially translucent — necrotic energy bleeds off it. It phases partially in and out of reality.*

**Wither Touch:** +9 to hit | 2d10 necrotic | Reach 10 ft  
Target: DC 15 CON save or **disadvantage on attack rolls** until end of their next turn (muscles wither briefly).

**Necrotic Burst (Recharge 5–6):** 20 ft radius centred on the arm's position. DC 15 CON save. Fail: 5d8 necrotic. Success: half.  
Additionally: creatures who fail the save cannot use reactions until end of their next turn.

**Phase-Through:** The spectral arm ignores opportunity attacks. It can move through walls (but not attack from inside them).

**On Destruction:** Releases a wave of cold necrotic energy. 15 ft radius: DC 14 CON save or **frightened** for 1 round.  
**Drops:** **Shard of Withering** (door core #3 — black bone fragment, leaves frost on fingers)

---

### Eye Arm (Shard of Vision)

**HP:** 90 | **AC:** 14  
**Initiative:** +5 (rolls highest among limbs)  
*The fourth arm ends in a blunt club-like appendage with a single large eye socket — an actual carved eye embedded in the bone that functions and tracks.*

**Eye Blast:** Ranged Spell Attack, +9 to hit, range 90 ft | 4d10 radiant  
Targets a single creature. On hit: DC 16 WIS save or **blinded** until end of their next turn.

**Full Witness (Recharge 5–6):** The eye opens fully. All creatures within 60 ft: DC 17 WIS save or be **incapacitated** for 1 round, overwhelmed by the sensation of being completely, utterly observed. *(Varin is immune to this — Bahamut's divine mark acts as a ward.)*

**Truesight:** The Eye Arm ignores invisibility and illusions on anything within 60 ft of Vasskren.

**On Destruction:** The eye shatters. All creatures within 10 ft: DC 13 WIS save or stunned 1 round (the sheer psychic weight of the eye dying).  
**Drops:** **Shard of Vision** (door core #4 — pale white bone, perfectly smooth, carved with a tiny eye)

---

### Skull

**HP:** 120 | **AC:** 16  
**Initiative:** +3  
*Separate from the torso — sits atop the spine but acts independently.*

**Death Wail (Recharge 5–6):** 60 ft cone. DC 17 WIS save. Fail: **frightened** for 1 minute (repeat save end of each turn) + **deafened** for the duration. Success: neither.

**Necrotic Gaze:** Ranged attack, +9 to hit, range 80 ft | 4d8 necrotic  

**Ossian's Echo (Reaction, 1/round):** When any limb is destroyed, the skull screams. Every creature within 40 ft: DC 14 WIS save or frightened until end of their next turn.

**On Destruction:** The skull cracks and falls. For the remainder of combat, Vasskren **cannot use Legendary Actions** and loses **Truesight**.
**Drops:** **Shard of Witnessing** (door core #5 — the single gem embedded in the skull's forehead, deep violet, warm, faintly humming. Does not drop until the skull is destroyed.)

## Tactical Notes

**Phase 1 (All Limbs Intact):**
- Left Claw tries to grapple the tankiest player
- Right Maul targets clustered groups
- Eye Arm picks off casters at range
- Spectral Arm phases into melee with whoever is trying to kite
- Skull uses Death Wail if 4+ players are in range
- Torso slams anyone in melee and uses Legendary Actions to recover

**Phase 2 (1–2 Limbs Destroyed):**
- Torso uses Limb Emergency on the most tactically critical destroyed limb
- Remaining arms become more erratic and aggressive
- Bone Collapse triggers if torso hits 150 HP

**Phase 3 (3+ Limbs Destroyed):**
- Torso is now vulnerable — legs are gone or impaired
- Skull starts using Necrotic Gaze every round (no Recharge needed — it's terrified in its own absent way)
- If only the skull and torso remain, the skull begins visually weeping (bone dry, but the motion is there)

## Death Scene

> *The last piece of it falls — whatever you struck last. The sound it makes is not metal or stone. It is the sound of a hundred bones settling at once, the way a forest sounds when the last tree falls.*
>
> *The eye carvings along the Grand Hall slowly begin to glow again — all of them. Not red. Not dark. A clean, pale white. Watching. Still.*
>
> *Four fragments remain on the floor, each pulsing faintly. You know, without knowing how you know, where they belong.*

**Five Shards** are now in the party's possession:
- **Shard of Rending** (left claw — red bone, warm)
- **Shard of Breaking** (right maul — iron-grey, heavy)
- **Shard of Withering** (spectral arm — black bone, frost)
- **Shard of Vision** (eye arm — pale white, carved eye)
- **Shard of Witnessing** (skull — violet gem, humming)

All five must be placed into the five indentations of the Sealed Door in Ossian's Chamber to open it.

Return to Ossian's chamber. The tapestry over the sealed door moves on its own as you approach. Place each shard in its indentation. The door opens.
