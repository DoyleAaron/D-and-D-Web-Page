**Location:** Room 7 — Ossian's Chamber  
**CR:** 11 | **XP:** 7,200  
**Type:** Undead  
**Trigger:** Physical contact with Ossian, any worshipper, or the Cloak (except Mage Hand)

> *He is seated at the altar as if in meditation — desiccated, ancient, robed in black and violet. Around his neck: the Cloak of the Watcher's Veil. He has been here for sixty years. He is not asleep. He is waiting for something worth waking for.*

## Special Traits

**Legendary Resistance (2/day):** If Ossian fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

**Magic Resistance:** Advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects.

**Rejuvenation:** If Ossian is destroyed and his heart is not destroyed separately, he reforms in 24 hours. His heart is in Room 7 — a small black iron reliquary behind the tapestry on the altar's back face (DC 14 Investigation to find). Destroying the heart prevents rejuvenation.

*Note: With the party at level 9 and Tharion + Aleth present, rejuvenation is unlikely to matter in session — but note it for any follow-up.*

## Actions

**Multiattack:** Ossian makes 2 attacks with his Rotting Fist. He can substitute one for a spell.

**Rotting Fist:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 5 ft, one target.  
Hit: 11 (2d6+4) bludgeoning damage + 21 (6d6) necrotic damage.  
Target must succeed DC **16 CON save** or be **cursed with Mummy Rot.**

---

## Mummy Rot

A cursed creature cannot regain hit points and its maximum HP decreases by **10 (3d6) for every 24 hours** it remains cursed. If this reduces maximum HP to 0, the creature dies and its body turns to dust.

Cure: *Remove Curse* removes it immediately. Lesser Restoration does not.

---

## Legendary Actions (3/round)

Ossian can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from below. Only one at the end of another creature's turn.

**Attack (1 action):** One Rotting Fist attack.  
**Blinding Dust (2 actions):** Blinding dust erupts in a 5 ft radius around Ossian. DC 14 CON save or blinded until end of next turn.  
**Blasphemous Word (2 actions):** Ossian utters a word of power. Each non-undead within 10 ft must succeed DC **16 CHA save** or be frightened until end of their next turn.  
**Channel Negative Energy (3 actions):** Each non-undead within 60 ft loses 10 (3d6) HP. Each undead within 60 ft regains 10 HP.

---

## Lair Actions (on Initiative 20)

Once per round, on initiative count 20 (losing all ties), Ossian takes one of the following Lair Actions while in Room 7:

- **Animate Altar:** The stone altar animates — one creature touching it is restrained until they succeed DC 14 STR check on their turn (object AC 13, HP 20).
- **Extinguish:** All non-magical light sources in Room 7 are extinguished simultaneously.
- **Call the Wights:** One Wight that has not yet activated this round can take a reaction to move up to its speed.

## Wights (×6, activated on trigger)

**HP:** 45 each | **AC:** 14 | **Speed:** 30 ft  
**Attacks:** Longsword (+4 to hit, 1d8+2 slashing) or Life Drain (+4 to hit, 2d6+2 necrotic — target max HP reduced by damage until long rest, if reduced to 0 max HP target dies and rises as a Wight)  
**Resistances:** Necrotic; bludgeoning/piercing/slashing from nonmagical  
**Immunities:** Poison, cold | Condition Immunities: Exhaustion, poisoned  
**Darkvision 60 ft**

If Ossian is defeated, all Wights still active crumble immediately (no save).

---

## Tactics

**Phase 1 (above 50 HP):** Ossian opens with *Spirit Guardians* and uses Blasphemous Word to push frightened creatures into the spirit guardian aura. Wights focus any isolated target.

**Phase 2 (below 50 HP):** Drops *Spirit Guardians*, casts *Blight* on the most damaged party member, uses Channel Negative Energy as a legendary action to heal himself while draining the party. Uses *Contagion* on a martial character who relies on ability checks.

**Ossian never retreats.** He does not pursue creatures who flee Room 7 — he holds the room.

---

## On Defeat

When Ossian's HP reaches 0: he collapses. All active Wights crumble immediately. The Eye Door from Room 7 swings open.

**Then, roughly 1 minute later** (if Vasskren hasn't already been triggered another way): the Grand Hall responds. Roll for atmosphere — the bell tone, the floor cracks, Vasskren begins to rise.
