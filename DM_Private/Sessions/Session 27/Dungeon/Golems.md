# Dungeon Golems — All Four Stat Blocks

All four golems were created by Necromancer Valdrek Ossian using a fusion of Tharnic engineering and Avethis-touched dark magic. They are not undead — they are **constructs**. They cannot be turned. They do not fear death. They follow the last orders Ossian gave them: protect the wings.

---

## Golem 1 — The Elven Tree Golem

**Location:** Second Floor, West Wing (above the cellar)  
**CR:** 9 | **XP:** 5,000  
**Type:** Construct  

> *It was an ancient tree — elven-grown, probably centuries old, taken from somewhere it shouldn't have been taken from. Ossian petrified it, hardened its roots into limbs, drove iron spikes through its heartwood to create a spine. It still has the shape of something that used to reach toward sunlight. Now it reaches for you.*

### Stats

| Ability | Score | Modifier |
|---------|-------|----------|
| STR | 22 | +6 |
| DEX | 8 | −1 |
| CON | 20 | +5 |
| INT | 3 | −4 |
| WIS | 10 | +0 |
| CHA | 1 | −5 |

**HP:** 178  
**AC:** 16 (natural bark-iron armour)  
**Speed:** 25 ft  
**Initiative:** −1  
**Proficiency:** +4

**Damage Immunities:** Poison, psychic  
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralysed, petrified, poisoned  
**Damage Vulnerabilities:** Fire  
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft, Passive Perception 10

### Bark Regeneration
At the start of each turn, the golem regains **10 HP** if it has at least 1 HP **and has not taken fire damage since its last turn**.

### Actions

**Multiattack:** 2 attacks per action (Root Slam + Root Grapple, or 2 Root Slams).

**Root Slam:** +10 to hit | 2d10+6 bludgeoning | Reach 10 ft

**Root Grapple:** +10 to hit | 2d10+6 bludgeoning | Target is **grappled** on hit (DC 16 STR to escape). While grappled, target is **restrained**.

**Spore Cloud (Recharge 5–6):** 30 ft cone. DC 16 CON save or **poisoned** for 1 minute. On a failed save, target also has disadvantage on concentration checks. Repeat save at end of each of their turns.

### Green Core (Kill Condition)
Deep in the golem's chest cavity, a pulsing **verdant core** glows. It is not visible unless the golem is reduced below 60 HP, at which point bark splits and the core becomes exposed.

- Dealing **30+ fire damage in a single hit** to the exposed core destroys it instantly, killing the golem.
- The core itself can be removed (Athletics DC 14 or Thieves' Tools DC 12) from a dead golem. It is a **Verdant Core** — a magical component (components value: 200 gp, or can be used in crafting a *Necklace of Adaptation* with a skilled alchemist).

### Tactics
The Tree Golem tries to grapple the heaviest-armoured target and hold them while swinging at squishier members with Root Slam. It positions itself to block the doorway out. Characters who deal fire damage will quickly become its priority — it instinctively moves to neutralise fire users.

## Golem 2 — The Blue Fire Golem

**Location:** Second Floor, West Wing (above Tree Golem, separate chamber)  
**CR:** 11 | **XP:** 7,200  
**Type:** Construct (Elemental-Infused)

> *It should not be cold. A thing made of fire should be warm. But the cobalt flames that wrap this construct burn at a temperature beyond cold — something older than cold, the blue-white of stars dying. The room is warm on paper. In your bones, you are freezing.*

### Stats

| Ability | Score | Modifier |
|---------|-------|----------|
| STR | 20 | +5 |
| DEX | 10 | +0 |
| CON | 18 | +4 |
| INT | 5 | −3 |
| WIS | 10 | +0 |
| CHA | 1 | −5 |

**HP:** 145  
**AC:** 13 (crystalline fire-shell — does not benefit from shields)  
**Speed:** 30 ft  
**Initiative:** +0  
**Proficiency:** +4

**Damage Immunities:** Fire, cold, poison, psychic  
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralysed, petrified, poisoned  
**Damage Vulnerabilities:** None (see Douse below)  
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft, Passive Perception 10

### Cold-Fire Aura
Creatures that **start their turn within 5 ft** of the golem take **2d6 cold damage** (no save). This aura cannot be suppressed.

### Absorb Flame
When targeted by a fire damage spell or attack, the golem **regains HP equal to half the fire damage** instead of taking damage.

### Douse
If the golem takes **20+ water/cold damage in a single hit**, its aura goes dormant for 1 round (no aura damage next turn) and it loses its Absorb Flame for 1 round.

### Actions

**Multiattack:** 2 attacks per action.

**Cold Flame Fist:** +9 to hit | 2d8+5 bludgeoning + 2d6 cold  

**Blue Fire Surge (Recharge 5–6):** 20 ft radius sphere centred on the golem.  
DC 17 DEX save:  
- Fail: 6d6 cold + 6d6 fire damage  
- Success: Half  
*(This affects allies and the golem's own space — it does not hurt the golem.)*

### Tactics
Positions itself in the centre of its chamber. Forces the party to come to it (aura punishes melee). Saves its Surge for when multiple players are close. If a player uses fire magic, it surges toward them.

## Golem 3 — The Amethyst Golem

**Location:** Second Floor, East Wing  
**CR:** 10 | **XP:** 5,900  
**Type:** Construct (Psionic-Infused)

> *It does not look angry. It looks like it is thinking. The amethyst that makes up its body catches the light and refracts it into tiny shards across the ceiling — like a violet sky full of stars. It watches you arrive the way a judge watches a defendant enter the room.*

### Stats

| Ability | Score | Modifier |
|---------|-------|----------|
| STR | 21 | +5 |
| DEX | 9 | −1 |
| CON | 17 | +3 |
| INT | 10 | +0 |
| WIS | 14 | +2 |
| CHA | 5 | −3 |

**HP:** 162  
**AC:** 17 (crystalline amethyst plates)  
**Speed:** 20 ft  
**Initiative:** −1  
**Proficiency:** +4

**Damage Immunities:** Poison, psychic  
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralysed, petrified, poisoned  
**Magic Resistance:** Advantage on saving throws against spells  
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft, Passive Perception 12

### Psychic Refraction (Recharge 5–6)
As a **reaction** when targeted by a spell of 3rd level or lower:  
The spell is reflected. The original caster becomes the target of their own spell, using the same attack roll or save DC.  
*(4th level and above pass through normally.)*

### Crystal Shatter
When the golem takes **30+ damage from a single hit**, it releases a burst of crystal shards:  
10 ft radius, DC 14 DEX save, **2d6 piercing** on fail. Half on success.

### Mind Fracture
When reduced **below 80 HP**, the golem emits a psychic pulse:  
All creatures within 30 ft: DC 15 INT save or have **disadvantage on concentration checks** for 1 hour.

### Actions

**Multiattack:** 2 attacks per action.

**Crystal Slam:** +9 to hit | 2d10+5 bludgeoning | Reach 5 ft

**Crushing Grip (Recharge 6):** +9 to hit | grapple attempt. On hit: 3d8+5 bludgeoning. While grappled, the target takes 1d10 psychic at the start of each of their turns (the golem's resonance).

### Tactics
Stands still and lets the party come to it. Baits spellcasters into using low-level spells (Psychic Refraction). When below 80 HP, it tries to get in the middle of the group before it triggers. If a caster is identified, it focuses all attacks on them.

## Golem 4 — The Eye Golem

**Location:** Fourth Floor, East Wing (highest room)  
**CR:** 12 | **XP:** 8,400  
**Type:** Construct (Divine — Avethis-Touched)

> *The others were Ossian's work. This one feels older. The eye carved into its chest isn't painted — it moves. It tracks. Whatever animates this golem is not just Ossian's necromancy. Avethis blessed this one directly. It is, in every sense, a witness made physical.*

### Stats

| Ability | Score | Modifier |
|---------|-------|----------|
| STR | 22 | +6 |
| DEX | 10 | +0 |
| CON | 20 | +5 |
| INT | 8 | −1 |
| WIS | 18 | +4 |
| CHA | 3 | −4 |

**HP:** 170  
**AC:** 18 (divine stone armour)  
**Speed:** 30 ft  
**Initiative:** +0  
**Proficiency:** +5

**Damage Immunities:** Radiant, poison, psychic  
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralysed, petrified, poisoned  
**Magic Resistance:** Advantage on saving throws against spells  
**Truesight:** 60 ft (sees invisible creatures, true forms, into the Ethereal)  
**Senses:** Passive Perception 14

### Avethis's Watch
The golem **cannot be surprised**. It cannot be blinded. Invisibility and illusions do not function against it.

### Gaze of the Watcher
At the start of each creature's turn, if the creature is within 30 ft and can be seen:  
DC 15 WIS save or be **restrained** until end of that creature's next turn (perceived so completely by the Eye that they feel pinned in place).

### Actions

**Multiattack:** 2 attacks per action.

**Stone Fists:** +11 to hit | 2d12+6 bludgeoning | Reach 5 ft

**Eye Ray (Recharge 5–6):** Ranged Spell Attack +9, range 120 ft.  
Hit: 6d8 radiant damage.  
Target: DC 17 WIS save or **frightened** of the golem for 1 minute (save ends at end of each turn).

**Absolute Witness (Recharge 6):** The eye in its chest flares white.  
30 ft radius. All creatures: DC 17 WIS save or be **incapacitated** until end of the golem's next turn (completely overwhelmed by the sense of being fully, utterly seen).

### Tactics
Moves to the centre of the room. Uses Gaze passively. Saves Eye Ray for clustered targets. Uses Absolute Witness as an opener if 3+ players rush it. It does not move erratically — it turns slowly, methodically, making sure everyone in the room is in its sight.

**Note:** This golem has a slight acknowledgement of Varin if Bahamut's divine aura is detectable. It looks at Varin slightly longer. It does not treat him differently in combat, but a DC 14 Insight check reveals it seems to recognize him.

## Golem Cores (for Vasskren's Door)

These are **not** the same cores as the Vasskren limb cores. The golem cores are flavour/components only. The **door-unlocking cores come from Vasskren's limbs** (see Vasskren.md).

Each golem does drop a residual magical core on death (the power source):
| Golem | Core Name | Value / Use |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Tree Golem | Verdant Core | 200 gp / crafting |
| Blue Fire Golem | Cobalt Ember | 250 gp / crafting (can relight magically as blue flame) |
| Amethyst Golem | Psionic Shard | 300 gp / used in *Headband of Intellect* crafting |
| Eye Golem | **Shard of Witnessing** | 500 gp / see note |

**Shard of Witnessing (Eye Golem Core):** This is a semi-divine artefact. It glows with faint white light. Holding it, a character can once per day sense whether a creature within 30 ft is being actively deceptive (as *Zone of Truth* for one creature, instantaneous). No charges — it regenerates this ability at dawn. Value as a magic item: significant. Selling it in Islefield would attract Second Council attention.
