# Gargoyles — Character Sheet

**Locations:**  
- Room B (Central Chamber) — 1 gargoyle, perched on column above stairs  
- Room J (Blue Fire Golem Chamber) — 1 gargoyle, on arch above doorway  
**CR:** 2 | **XP:** 450 each  
**Type:** Elemental (Construct-adjacent)  
**Count:** 2 total

> *Not carved. Collapsed into itself slightly, mouth wider than it should be. The stone is a different shade than the walls. It has been here longer than the building.*

## Special Traits

**False Appearance:** While motionless, indistinguishable from a stone statue. Creatures must make a DC 17 Perception check to recognise it as a living creature before it moves.

**Stone Camouflage:** Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made in rocky or stone terrain while motionless.

## Tactics

**Room B:** Perched on the column at the top of the stairs. Does not move until a creature gets within 20 ft **or** is attacked. Drops into melee immediately — prioritises whoever cast a spell or is wearing light armour.

**Room J:** Attacks **immediately** when a non-Tharnic-sigil creature crosses the doorway threshold. Does not wait. This one is meant to catch the party off-guard in the choke point before the Blue Fire Golem.

**On death:** Crumbles. Room B gargoyle has the cornerstone inscription inside its chest (see Overview).
