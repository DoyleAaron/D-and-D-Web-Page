## Traits

**Tunneller.**
The Sandmaw can burrow through sand and loose earth without leaving a tunnel. It can surface or submerge as part of its movement.

**Sand Sense.**
The Sandmaw automatically detects all creatures touching the ground within 60 ft. It cannot be surprised by creatures on the ground, and it cannot be hidden from by such creatures.

**Ambush Rise.**
If the Sandmaw moves while burrowed and surfaces within 5 ft of a creature, that creature must make a **DC 17 DEX save** or be knocked **Prone**.

## Actions

### Multiattack
The Sandmaw makes one **Bite** attack and one **Slam** attack.

### Bite
**+9 to hit** | Reach 10 ft | One target
**Damage:** 3d10+6 piercing
If the target is Large or smaller, it is **Grappled (escape DC 17 STR)** and **Restrained** while grappled. The Sandmaw can only have one creature grappled this way.

### Slam
**+9 to hit** | Reach 5 ft | One creature
**Damage:** 2d8+6 bludgeoning
The target must make a **DC 17 STR save** or be knocked **Prone**.

### Devour *(Recharge 5–6)*
*Requires a creature already Grappled by the Bite.*
The Sandmaw drags the creature underground. While underground, the creature is **Blinded**, **Restrained**, and begins **suffocating**. It takes **4d8 piercing damage** at the start of each of its turns.

Other creatures can use their **Action** to make a **DC 17 STR check** to pull the victim free. The Sandmaw must remain **stationary** while devouring.

---

## Reactions

### Thrash
**Trigger:** The Sandmaw is reduced to 50 HP or below.
**Effect:** It immediately burrows up to 20 ft and resurfaces at a new location. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.

---

## Combat Tactics

1. **Opens burrowed** — uses Sand Sense to track footsteps. Surfaces under the heaviest-armoured target to trigger Ambush Rise.
2. **Priority target:** whoever it can Grapple and drag underground. Isolated, low-mobility party members are ideal.
3. **Devour** the grappled target immediately if Recharged — forces the rest of the party to spend actions rescuing instead of dealing damage.
4. **Thrash** when bloodied — resurfaces near a caster or separated PC.
5. It has no self-preservation instinct. It does not flee. It fights until dead.

---

## Environment Notes

- Most effective in **open sandy terrain**, loose desert floor, or a **dungeon chamber with sandy/loose ground**.
- In tight corridors or stone-floored rooms, remove the Burrow speed and Ambush Rise trait — it becomes a straightforward grapple bruiser instead.
- If used outdoors: the tremorsense means the party gets **no surprise round** unless they fly or are otherwise not touching the ground.
