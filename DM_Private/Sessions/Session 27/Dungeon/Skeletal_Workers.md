# Skeletal Workers — Character Sheet

**Location:** Cellar / Basement (Room F)  
**CR:** 1/4 | **XP:** 50 each (×6 = 300 total)  
**Type:** Undead  
**Count:** 6

> *They move in slow, purposeless loops — pickaxe down, drag, lift, repeat. No ore moves. Nothing changes. They have been mining nothing for decades. When they notice you, the loop stops.*

## Tactics

They attack the nearest creature. No coordination. No retreat. If Ossian is defeated, they crumble mid-combat on their turn (no save — automatic).

---

## Encounter Notes

- 6 are patrolling in a loop. They do not notice the party unless disturbed or approached within 10 ft.
- The repetitive motion means a **DC 11 Stealth** check lets the party move past without triggering them.
- Destroying all 6 before Ossian is defeated has no dungeon-wide effect (unlike regular skeletons).
