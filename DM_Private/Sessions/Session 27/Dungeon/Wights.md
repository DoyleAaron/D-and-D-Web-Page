# Wights (×6) — Ossian's Worshippers

**Role:** Support enemies for the Ossian encounter. They are seated worshippers who animate when combat begins. They crumble instantly if Ossian is defeated.

**CR:** 3 each | **XP:** 700 each (but award as part of Ossian encounter, not separately)

---

## Stats

| | |
|---|---|
| **HP** | 45 (6d8+18) |
| **AC** | 14 (studded leather) |
| **Speed** | 30 ft |
| **Initiative** | +1 |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 15 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 13 (+1) | 15 (+2) |

**Saving Throws:** STR +4, CON +5, WIS +3  
**Skills:** Perception +3, Stealth +4  
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft, Passive Perception 13  
**Languages:** Common (do not speak in combat — utterly silent)

---

## Resistances & Immunities

**Damage Resistances:** Necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical weapons  
**Damage Immunities:** Cold, poison  
**Condition Immunities:** Exhaustion, poisoned  

---

## Traits

**Sunlight Sensitivity.** Disadvantage on attack rolls and Perception checks in direct sunlight. (Dungeon — irrelevant here.)

**Undead Fortitude (simplified).** When reduced to 0 HP by damage that isn't radiant or from a critical hit, the wight makes a CON save (DC = 5 + damage dealt). On a success, it drops to 1 HP instead. Does not apply if Ossian is already dead.

> **DM note:** Only use Undead Fortitude if the fight feels too easy. Skip it if the party is struggling with Ossian.

---

## Actions

### Longsword
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft, one target.  
**Hit:** 7 (1d8+2) slashing damage, or 8 (1d10+2) if wielded two-handed.

### Life Drain
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft, one creature.  
**Hit:** 9 (2d6+2) necrotic damage. The target must succeed on a **DC 13 CON save** or have its **maximum HP reduced by the damage dealt** until it finishes a long rest. If this reduction brings the target's maximum HP to 0, the target dies.

> **DM note:** Life Drain is brutal at level 9 if multiple wights use it. A good default is: wights use Longsword unless a party member is isolated or already below half HP, then switch to Life Drain. Ossian does not need the wights to kill anyone — he needs them to split attention and prevent focus-firing.

---

## Trigger — When They Animate

The wights begin the fight seated in a ring around Ossian's altar in their rotted cult robes, heads bowed. They do not appear hostile until Ossian uses his first action or the party attacks anything in the room.

**Read-aloud (when they animate):**
> *One by one, the seated figures raise their heads. No dramatic rise — they simply lift, slowly, like something remembering it has a body. The robes hang off them in folds. Their eyes are already open. They have been open the whole time.*

Roll initiative for the wights as **one group** (one initiative, they all act on the same count). Split them into two or three groups if combat gets complicated.

---

## Placement

Six wights, seated in a ring around the altar:
- 2 directly behind Ossian (between him and the reliquary)
- 2 flanking left and right of the altar
- 2 near the entrance, cutting off retreat

This forces the party to either push through or be surrounded. The two at the entrance are the highest priority to deal with early.

---

## If a Wight Uses Life Drain Lethally

If a party member's max HP is reduced to 0, they die. If not immediately revived with *Revivify* (before the end of their next turn), they **rise as a wight** on their next turn and fight against the party. Ossian does not control the newly risen wight directly — it acts on instinct.

**This almost certainly will not happen** at level 9, but the rule exists.

---

## On Ossian's Defeat

All active wights crumble immediately. No save, no drama. They simply collapse into dust and rot. Whatever they were before Ossian bound them is gone — they were never really worshipping him. They were just the last people who stayed.
