# Cloak of the Watcher's Veil

**Type:** Wondrous Item (Cloak)  
**Rarity:** Rare  
**Requires Attunement:** Yes  
**Last Known Location:** Around the neck of Necromancer Valdrek Ossian — Ossian's Chamber, the Ossian Vault  
**Who Wants It:** Islefield Second Council (via Envoy Tharion Aelindre — their given reason is "Balthazar artefact", which is a lie)  
**What It Actually Is:** An Avethis-touched intelligence tool

---

## Appearance

A deep grey cloak, almost black. The inner lining is a lighter silver-grey with a subtle pattern that only becomes visible in dim light — hundreds of tiny eyes, woven into the fabric. The hood is wide and deep. The clasp is carved bone in the shape of an hourglass.

It is old. Very old. Pre-Tharnic in origin, though Ossian acquired it during the Empire's later years.

---

## Properties

### Watcher's Sight (Passive)
While attuned and wearing the cloak, you have **advantage on Perception checks** involving watching or observing (but not hearing — the cloak affects sight only).

### Peripheral Awareness (Passive)
You cannot be **surprised** while wearing the cloak and conscious.

### Fold Into Shadow (1/day)
As a bonus action, you become **heavily obscured** while in dim light or darkness — not invisible, but effectively undetectable to creatures that rely on sight (Stealth check not required, lasts until you move or take an action).

### The Watcher Knows (1/week)
Once per week, as an action, you can focus on a creature you can see within 60 ft. For the next minute, you are aware of that creature's **exact position** at all times, even if they become invisible or move behind cover. (Think of it as temporary truesight but limited to one creature's location.)

---

## What the Islefield Council Actually Wants It For

Councillor Vel (Tharion's patron) intends to use this cloak to surveil foreign dignitaries at the upcoming summit. The cloak's **Watcher Knows** ability means a skilled operative wearing it in the room could track exactly where individuals move during what is supposed to be a private meeting.

This is the real reason. Not Balthazar. Not ancient history.

---

## Giving the Cloak to Tharion

The party receives their payment (3,000–4,000 gp) and the suspension of banishments remains in effect.

**If the party gives the cloak over:** Fine. The mission resolves cleanly. But a DC 16 Insight roll from anyone present when Tharion takes the cloak will catch the briefest moment of relief that suggests this was more important than it was presented.

**If the party keeps the cloak:** The political situation becomes more complex. Tharion is professionally unhappy. He will not escalate to violence over it — but the Second Council's goodwill is now revoked, and that affects future Islefield interactions.

**If the party figures out the real use case and confronts Tharion:** This is actually the most interesting outcome. He is genuinely caught. He will stonewall initially (DC 16 Persuasion or Intimidation to crack him). If he breaks: he offers 1,000 gp extra, an additional suspension extension, and a personal secret — the name of the Islefield advisor who is a traitor (Morjor already knows this, but now it's confirmed by an official source). He will not explain how he knows the advisor's name.
