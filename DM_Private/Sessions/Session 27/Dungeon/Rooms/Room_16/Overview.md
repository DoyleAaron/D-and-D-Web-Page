# Room 16 — Smelting Furnaces

**Enemies:** 4 Skeletons patrolling  
**Exits:** Corridor | West → Room 8 (emergency exit)

Ore smelting facilities from the Tharnic era. Three massive furnaces — cold for decades, but intact. Broken conveyor tracks. Empty ore hoppers. Iron chains hanging from the ceiling. Soot-blackened uneven floor.

---

## Hazards

**Chain Collapse:** Chain section above the west furnace. DC 14 Perception to spot the fraying.  
More than 200 lbs under it: chain falls — 2d8 bludgeoning to anyone hit.

**Furnace Gas Vent:** One furnace releases gas when struck or opened. DC 13 CON save or poisoned 1 hour. Advantage if nose/mouth covered.

---

## Emergency Egress Hint
**SW wall — three lines and a westward arrow.**  
- **Perception DC 13** to notice  
- **History DC 12** to read: Tharnic emergency egress notation → Room 8 direction  
*(One of two egress hints — the other is the survey map in Room 12.)*

---

## Skeleton Patrol
4 skeletons patrolling set routes between the furnaces. Slow, predictable. Possible to avoid with DC 12 Stealth if the party watches first.

---

## Ore Hopper (locked)
Locked iron ore hopper — left furnace row.  
**Thieves' Tools DC 12 or DC 16 STR** to wrench open.
