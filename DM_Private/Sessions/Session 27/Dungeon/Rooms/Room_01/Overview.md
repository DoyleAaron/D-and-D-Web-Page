# Room 1 — Tree Golem Chamber

**Exits:** Left wing corridor  
**Door:** Iron lock — Thieves' Tools DC 14 or DC 18 STR | Key: Room 16 (ore hopper)

Tall stone chamber with roots bursting through the floor and ceiling — an ancient elven tree, petrified and converted into a construct. Smells of old bark and copper. The roots have cracked the stonework. No daylight, but the tree still seems to reach upward.

---

## Key Mechanic — Fire

**The golem regenerates 10 HP per turn** unless it took fire damage since its last turn.  
At below 60 HP, bark splits and the **Green Core** is exposed.  
**30+ fire damage to the core in a single hit** destroys it instantly.

---

## Tactics

- Tries to grapple the most armoured target and hold them in place
- Strikes spellcasters while they are held
- Blocks the exit
- **Abandons grapples immediately** to move toward any active fire source
