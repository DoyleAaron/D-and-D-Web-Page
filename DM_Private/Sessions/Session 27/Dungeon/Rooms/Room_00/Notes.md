# Room 0 — Notes & Documents

## Tharnic Arch Inscription
**Found:** Arch stonework above the entrance  
**DC:** DC 12 History to read  
**Text:** Survey registration marks, lot numbers, colonial dating stamps. Standard bureaucratic record of the facility's founding. Nothing remarkable — except someone has scratched dozens of eye symbols over the top of the official text, overwriting the colonial authority with Ossian's Avethis iconography.
