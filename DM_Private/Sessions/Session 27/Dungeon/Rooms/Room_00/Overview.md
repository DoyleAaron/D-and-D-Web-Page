# Room 0 — Main Entrance

**Enemies:** None  
**Exits:** Forward → Room 9 | South side corridor → Room 19 (sewer)

The wide arch cut into the cliff face. Iron doors — permanently open, rusted off their hinges, overgrown with lichen. Tharnic administrative symbols cover the arch stonework; over them, dozens of carved eyes in a later and more anxious hand.

---

## Lock Mechanic

The moment the **last party member crosses the threshold**, a heavy iron bar drops across the entrance. Spring-loaded Tharnic mechanism — centuries old, still works. Very loud.

**Ways out:**
- DC 22 STR to lift the bar from inside
- Defeat all 4 golems — releases automatically
- *Knock* spell — removes for 10 minutes, then resets
- Room 8 hidden exit (far western wing)

---

## Time Dilation

**Every 1 hour inside = 1 day outside.** Do not announce this to players.

| How Found | Clue |
|-----------|------|
| Perception DC 14 | Hourglasses and eyes carved near the doorframe — the shape repeats obsessively |
| Arcana DC 13 | The air has a faint temporal aura. Magic feels slightly displaced. |
| Investigation DC 16 (worker tablets) | *"We've been down here four hours and Seral says two days have passed above."* |
| History DC 15 (Tharnic tablet, mid-dungeon) | *"One eye = one hour = one day."* |

---

## Perception / Investigation

- **Perception DC 12:** Dust disturbed — drag marks, not footprints.
- **Investigation DC 14 (lock):** Tharnic design, not Ossian's. Built to trap. Original purpose: anti-theft.
