# Room 5 — Officer's Suite

**Enemies:** None  
**Exits:** Main corridor | Connected to Room 6 area

Comfortable by dungeon standards. A bed (collapsed), a writing desk, a wardrobe, a rug (rotted). This was an officer's private room — Tharnic, not cult. Someone senior lived here during the facility's active years.

---

## Checks

| Location | Check | Result |
|----------|-------|--------|
| Mirror on south wall | Perception DC 16 | Reflection shows the room as it was — furnished, lamp lit, a figure writing. Lasts 3 seconds, then normal. |
| Wardrobe | Investigation DC 14 | False bottom. Locked — Thieves' Tools DC 12. Contains the stash. |
| Desk | Investigation DC 12 | Locked drawer — Thieves' Tools DC 12 or DC 14 STR. |
| Rug corner | Investigation DC 10 | Turned back — old bloodstain beneath. Not recent. |
