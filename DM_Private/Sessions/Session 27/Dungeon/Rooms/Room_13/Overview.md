# Room 13 — Main Work Area

**Enemies:** 6 Skeletal Workers  
**Exits:** Lower corridor | Sewer below (SW corner hatch)

The primary workspace of the facility — large, open, with remains of tool racks and work benches. Dust is very thick. The skeletal workers are still at their stations, frozen mid-task.

---

## Skeletal Workers

Not aggressive on sight. They react to Stealth vs. their Perception.  
**DC 11 Stealth** to move through the room without drawing attention.  
If noticed: all 6 animate and attack simultaneously. Standard skeletons, CR 1/4 each.

---

## Sewer Hatch (SW corner)
- Investigation DC 12 to find (buried under a workbench, mostly hidden)
- Standard lift — no STR check once found

---

## Key Location (wall rack, east side)
A wooden rack with various tools — mostly rusted. One item wrapped in a cloth on a hook.  
**Perception DC 13** to notice the hook behind a piece of canvas.  
**Room 2 key** — silver, angular, a small amethyst shard set into the bow.
