# Room 8 — Emergency Exit

**Enemies:** None  
**Exits:** Appears to be dead end | Hidden passage → cliff ledge → base of Ironwarf Heights

A short corridor that terminates at a solid wall. Nothing remarkable. Dust undisturbed. Smells faintly of outside air — very faint.

---

## The Exit

**Option 1 — Lever:**  
- **Investigation DC 15** to find it: knee height, left wall, looks like an iron pipe fitting
- Pull → panel swings inward, revealing a narrow passage (one person at a time)

**Option 2 — Break Wall:**  
- Wall: AC 17, HP 30
- Obvious force option for parties without a rogue

**Beyond the panel:**  
A narrow natural passage, rough stone, 2–3 ft wide. 10 minutes to traverse → emerges on a cliff ledge 50 ft above the Heights floor. Iron stakes hammered into rock allow a safe descent (~10 minutes). At the base: the approach road to the dungeon entrance.
