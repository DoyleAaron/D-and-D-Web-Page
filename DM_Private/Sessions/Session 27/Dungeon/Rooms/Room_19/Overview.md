# Room 19 — South Sewer Entrance

**Enemies:** None  
**Exits:** Room 0 area (short side corridor) | Sewer below

A short side corridor near the main entrance. Dead-ends at a heavy iron grate set into the floor. The air smells of mineral water and old stone. A faint trickling sound from below.

---

## The Grate

| Check | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Perception (no DC) | Immediately visible — the grate is obvious |
| DC 10 (once in reach) | Iron bolt, locked from above — unlocks easily |

Leads directly down to the Sewer System. Without the map from Room 12, it is a maze below. With the map: navigable.

---

## Note

Looking into the sewer with darkvision: the channel runs in two directions. No markings visible from here.
