# Room 4 — Eye Golem Chamber

**Exits:** Deepest corridor  
**Door:** Iron lock — Thieves' Tools DC 17 or DC 20 STR | Key: Room 18 (false-bottom desk, DC 12)

Circular chamber. Every wall surface carved with open eyes — floor to ceiling, hundreds, thousands. All oriented differently but all at rest. The air is completely still. The silence is unusual — no dust movement, no settling sound.

---

## Approach Corridor — Weeping Angels

**2 Weeping Angels.** Standard trigger: they move when unobserved. If all players look at one, the other can move.  
**Split attention is the threat.** Any round where a player is not directly observing one = it can advance.  
**Torch out, eyes down:** Angels move freely.

---

## Key Mechanic — Full Witness
**Recharge 5-6.** The golem fully opens (all eyes simultaneously). Every creature in the room.  
**DC 17 WIS save** or spend next action rigid and unseeing (not incapacitated, just locked).  
**Countermeasure: Close your eyes.** Cannot be forced open. Can still attack (disadvantage), cast (concentration DC 12).  
**Varin is immune** — she has witnessed this already.

---

## Closing Eyes
Closing eyes triggers no penalties from the golem. Tell the players they can do this.
