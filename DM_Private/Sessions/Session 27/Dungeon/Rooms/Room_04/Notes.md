# Room 4 — Notes & Documents

## Stone Marker (near arch entrance — DC 12 Arcana)
Ossian's hand:
> *"Fourth sentinel required something I was reluctant to use. Eyes are not components. Eyes are not raw material. They are recording devices \u2014 this is the principle the Eye operates on: to see is to record, to record is to preserve. I gave the fourth sentinel eyes. Not my eyes. Eyes I procured. Note: during the binding, every worker in the facility looked at the ceiling simultaneously. They could not explain this afterward. Two of them have since refused to enter this section. I have not required it of them."*

## Second Marker (central platform — Investigation DC 15)
Ossian's hand, smaller script \u2014 written later than the first:
> *"The Eye in this room is not the Eye below. One is mine. One is not. If you are reading this and you have already been to the lower room, you understand the distinction. If you have not \u2014 go. Then come back and read this again. It will mean something different."*
