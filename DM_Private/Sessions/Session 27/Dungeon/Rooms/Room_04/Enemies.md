# Room 4 — Enemies

## 2 × Weeping Angel
**CR:** 5 | **HP:** 85 each | **Found:** Approach corridor  
Move only when unobserved. Grapple and send target back in time — target disappears, reappears at dungeon entrance 1 minute later. Immediate danger: splitting party attention.

## Eye Golem
**CR:** 12 | **HP:** 170 | **Full stats:** Golems.md

**Full Witness (Recharge 5-6):** DC 17 WIS or locked (rigid, unseeing, loses action).  
**Countermeasure:** Close eyes. Angels can't trigger, golem can't force open them.  
**Varin is immune.**  
**Multi-eye attacks** — can target multiple PCs per turn.
