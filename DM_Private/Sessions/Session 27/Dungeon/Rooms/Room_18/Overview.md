# Room 18 — The Hidden Room

**Enemies:** None  
**Finding it:**
- From Room 2: **Investigation DC 17** — north wall is slightly thicker than the building geometry allows
- From Room 14: **Investigation DC 17** — east wall dimensions don't match the room's exterior
- **Opening:** A pivot door with no handle, no visible seam. Pressing the correct stone (DC 20 without prior knowledge) causes it to swing inward silently. DC drops to 14 if dimensions discrepancy was already noticed.

A small rectangular room. Stone shelves, mostly empty. A writing desk — Ossian's secondary workspace, cleaner than Room 7. Used for work he didn't want the cultists to see.

---

## Sewer Hatch
Set into the floor — iron-ringed, simple bolt lock (DC 10 to open). Ossian's private sewer access. He used this regularly.

---

## This is the highest-lore room in the dungeon
A party that finds and cracks the cipher journal gets the full picture. Designed for careful explorers only.
