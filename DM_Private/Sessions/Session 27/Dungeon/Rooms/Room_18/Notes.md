# Room 18 — Notes & Documents

## Ossian's Cipher Journal (on the desk — no check to find, DC 18 Investigation to crack cipher, or *Comprehend Languages* reads normally)

**First page (uncoded — plain Common):**
> *"If you are reading this in cipher, you are doing it wrong.*
> *If you are reading this clearly, you earned it.*
> *Everything I could not write in the other document is written here.*
> *I am sorry about approximately a third of it."*

**Decoded entries:**

> *"The Islefield surveyors who prepared the lower tunnels were not Tharnic subjects. I found their commission records in the collapsed Sector 2 archive. Paid through a Tharnic proxy. The originating account traces back to an Islefield 'Colonial Liaison Office' that does not exist in any current records \u2014 dissolved, I believe deliberately, shortly after Colony Year 31.*
>
> *Colony Year 31. I arrived in Year 34. Someone in Islefield was preparing this site three years before I came here. They knew someone like me was coming. Or they knew something was coming and wanted a person of my particular skills positioned to receive it."*

> *"The lower eastern wing was already sealed when I arrived. I had assumed I sealed it. I did not \u2014 I broke an existing seal and re-applied my own. The original seal was not Tharnic, not any method I have encountered in thirty years of practice. Much older. Applied by someone who understood what they were sealing in, and chose to seal it in rather than remove it."*

> *"Gaia. I am going to write the old name here because this is the document I will burn if I change my mind, and I have not changed my mind.*
>
> *Gaia. That is the ancient name, and the ancient name is safe — it is in a hundred dead manuscripts and frightens no one. He is not a historical figure. He is not a deity in any tradition I have studied. He is the being that the earth is when you ask it what it is. He was here. In this room. In the room below. He built in this place and he left what he built.*
>
> *What the old name connects to now — what it is called in this age, and who has been taking its instructions without knowing it — I will not write here. I have written it once, with the Eye, in the room below. That is the only place it belongs. If you want it, go down and earn it.*
>
> *The Islefield surveying party was working for someone who knew about him. Or for someone who worked for someone who knew. The chain is long and I cannot follow it further back than the second link.*
>
> *I want to record that I considered warning someone. I decided against it because I could not determine who to warn, or what the warning would be, or whether receiving it would help them or simply give them enough information to do something catastrophic in an informed way."*

> *"The Cloak is not mine. I found it. I have documented what I found in the appropriate document in the room below. What I have not documented anywhere else: the Cloak was sealed in this archive by the same hand that sealed the archive itself. Old seal, unknown method. Same person who sealed in what was below.*
>
> *Someone sealed a witnessing device in here and walked away.*
>
> *I have thought about why for eleven months. I do not have an answer that satisfies me. The most honest answer I can produce: they sealed it here so it would be found by the right person at the right time. I do not know who the right person is. I do not know if it is me. I do not know if it is whoever reads this."*
