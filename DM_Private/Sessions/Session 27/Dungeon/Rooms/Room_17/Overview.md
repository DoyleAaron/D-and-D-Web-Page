# Room 17 — The Eye Chamber

**Enemies:** None  
**Access:** Sealed door in Room 7 — all 5 Vasskren cores placed correctly in the door  
**Exits:** Room 7 only

A hexagonal chamber. Floor, walls, and ceiling are polished black stone — not Tharnic construction. This was built for Ossian, or by something working through him. In the centre: a perfect circle of white stone, ten feet across. A single eye carved in the centre, facing upward. Open, unblinking.

> *The dust on the floor is undisturbed. Whatever is here has been waiting.*

---

## The Sight of Avethis (immediate, on entry)

The eye opens — not physically. Every person in the room has the same experience simultaneously: the sensation of being seen completely, without judgment. One second. Then it's gone.

**Permanent boon — once per character, never expires until used:**  
*Once, you may choose to see into any point in time. Past or future. Any moment, any place. You see it as a witness: clearly, completely, without intervention. You cannot interact with what you see. When it ends, the ability is gone.*

**DM NOTE:** Handle privately if needed. Show the truth — not the comfortable version. If the ask would break the campaign, show a true but incomplete slice.

---

## Loot Placement

Four stone alcoves. Three contain offerings.
