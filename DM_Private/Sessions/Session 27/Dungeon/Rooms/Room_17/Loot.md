## Stone Alcoves (no check — open room, offerings in plain sight)
- **400 gp total** in pristine Tharnic gold (zero oxidation — perfectly sealed room since Ossian)

## Ioun Stone of Awareness
**Found:** Floating at rest in one alcove  
Pale blue stone. Orbits the attuned creature's head at shoulder height. Cannot be surprised. +5 passive Perception. Attunement. Full desc in Loot_Guide.md.

## Ossian's Research Codex
**Found:** Stone alcove, wrapped in oilskin  
His complete private manuscript — illuminated, Common and Old Elvish. Most important lore item in the dungeon. Contains the **Gaia** material (the maker = the earth incarnate), the Three/Trinity, the origin of the Eye, and — in the final entry only — the reveal that Gaia is **Balthazar**. Full text in this room's Notes.md; DM context in Loot_Guide.md ("Who is Gaia?").
