# Sewer System

**Enemies:** None  
**Access Points:** Room 9 (DC 14 / DC 16 STR) | Room 10 (DC 10 / DC 12 STR) | Room 13 (DC 12, unlocked) | Room 18 (DC 10, bolt) | Room 19 (DC 0, bolt) | Room 20 (DC 0, bolt)

Stone-lined passages, 8 ft wide, 6 ft tall. Central drainage channel 1 ft wide — constant slow trickle of groundwater. Mossy walls near seepage. Empty iron lamp mounts. **Total darkness without a light source.**

---

## Key Rule — Safe Zone

**No undead enter the sewer.** Groundwater carries trace consecrated mineral compound from local rock. They don't understand why. They simply don't come down.

> *The first time the party drops in and realises nothing followed them — the silence is different. Dungeon silence is a held breath. Sewer silence is just quiet.*

**Short rests are safe here.** Nothing disturbs you. Groundwater not drinkable but causes no harm.

---

## Navigation

| Condition | Check |
|-----------|-------|
| Without Room 12 map | DC 15 Survival to navigate |
| With Room 12 map | Routes clearly readable, no check |

Travel between adjacent access points: ~5 minutes (vs. 10–20 through dungeon proper).

---

## Hatches

Iron-bolt mechanism operated from above (dungeon level). Once opened: stays open. **Cannot be opened from below** — no mechanism on sewer side. Exception: DC 18 STR if hatch is incompletely bolted.
