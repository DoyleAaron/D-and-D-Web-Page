# Room 15 — Higher-Up Dining

**Enemies:** 2 Skeletons (entry-triggered)  
**Exits:** Corridor | Private Dining Quarters (south wall — Thieves' Tools DC 13 or DC 17 STR)

A proper dining room — carved chairs, iron candlesticks, pewter cups, a cracked serving platter. Better quality than the common dormitories. Upper-tier staff ate here, apart from the general workforce.

---

## Skeletons

Seated at the table as if mid-meal. The moment any creature enters the room, both stand simultaneously, knocking their chairs back, and attack. No hesitation, no delay.

---

## Hidden Compartment (kitchen area — Investigation DC 15)
Loose stone behind the remnants of the bread oven. Easy to miss. Worth checking if the party is being thorough.

---

## Private Dining Quarters
Smaller room for senior staff. Round table, two chairs, locked iron cabinet (same DC as the door: Thieves' Tools DC 13).
