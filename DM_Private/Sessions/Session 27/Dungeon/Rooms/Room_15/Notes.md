# Room 15 — Notes & Documents

## Worker's Journal (hidden compartment behind bread oven — Investigation DC 15)
Fully legible.

> *"Day 1 after the Master arrived: He says we are to remain. The colonists on the surface have been told we're on a deep survey. The work continues. He pays well."*

> *"Day 8: He walked through the whole facility last night. I heard him. He doesn't sleep. I asked Marren about it and she said she'd noticed too but not to mention it."*

> *"Day 14: I am writing this because I think I might not remember writing it. The Master makes the torches burn blue. He says it's holy. Marren says it is. I don't know what holy means anymore."*

> *"Day 17: The east wing is locked. He's been in there for three days. No food. No water. I don't know what he's doing."*

> *"Day 19: I think I'm the only one left. I found the others in the east wing. They are still standing. They are still smiling. I hid this here. If you find it, go. Now. Don't wait."*

*(No signature. The writer did not leave.)*

## Personal Ledger — Final Page (private quarters cabinet — Thieves' Tools DC 13)
Most of the ledger is routine Tharnic admin. The final page is handwritten, not formatted:
> *"He arrived today with a letter of commission I don't recognise. The seal is legitimate — I checked. The name on it is real. But he is not what they sent for. The letter says 'survey consultant.' He doesn't look at the ore. He looks at the walls. I don't think anyone sent him."*
