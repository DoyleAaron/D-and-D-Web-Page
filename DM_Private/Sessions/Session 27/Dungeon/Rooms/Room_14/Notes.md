# Room 14 — Notes & Documents

## Document on the Table (crumbling — History DC 14 to read without damaging it)
Ossian's hand:
> *"Note, for a record I will not be here to maintain:*
>
> *The vault is prepared. Four sentinels. One guardian. One door. The Eye watches everything in these walls \u2014 it always has, but now it watches something I have placed here specifically.*
>
> *Anyone who reaches the inner chamber will be marked. Not by me. I have no mark to give. By the Eye itself \u2014 which sees, and in seeing, changes what it has seen. I have been precise with myself about whether this is a curse. It is not a curse. It is a record. They will carry the fact of having been witnessed.*
>
> *They will never be deceived again. Not by others. Not by themselves. I am not certain this is comfortable. It is accurate."*

A meeting agenda. Mundane items — staffing, resource allocation — until the last line, scratched in a different hand:
> *"Item 6: the door below the drainage. Do not raise with staff."*

## Folded Note (inside locked chest, Private Section — tucked under the wax tablet)
Unsigned:
> *"They're asking questions about the east wing. I told them the noise is settling stone. They believed me because they want to."*
