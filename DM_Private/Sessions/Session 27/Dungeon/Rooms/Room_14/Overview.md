# Room 14 — Conference Room

**Enemies:** None — 6 staged robed forms do NOT animate  
**Exits:** Corridor | Private Section (east wall, behind tapestry — Thieves' Tools DC 15 or DC 19 STR)

A long stone table, intact stone chairs. A cracked window opening with iron bars — the only fresh air source on this side of the dungeon. The six sets of robes look occupied for a long moment. They are not. They will collapse to dust if touched.

> *It has the feeling of a room that was still in use until very recently. It was not. But it was used hard, and nothing in it has been disturbed since.*

## Mural Details
Full depictions of Avethis — central eye with hourglass pupil, ringed by smaller eyes showing scenes.  
**Perception DC 12:** One mural eye tracks the door — moves when no one looks directly at it. Not dangerous, Avethis bleed-through.  
**History DC 14 (murals):** One smaller eye shows a crown being placed — old Islefield regalia, pre-current dynasty.
