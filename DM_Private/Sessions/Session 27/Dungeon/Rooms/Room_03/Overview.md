# Room 3 — Blue Fire Golem Chamber

**Exits:** Upper corridor  
**Door:** Iron lock — Thieves' Tools DC 16 or DC 19 STR | Key: Room 15 (cabinet, singed leather wrap)

Cool chamber, near-freezing. Walls are streaked blue-grey from decades of cold magical flame. Blue fire burns in wall sconces — no heat, no smoke, blue, permanent. Frost on every surface.

---

## KEY RULE — No Fire Magic

Any fire spell cast in this room explodes. 10 ft radius, 2d10 fire damage, DC 13 DEX half.  
The sconces are magical — not ordinary flame. Don't touch them.

---

## Gargoyle — Immediate Attack

The gargoyle comes off the north wall the moment a creature crosses the threshold. No check needed. Straight into initiative.

---

## Blue Fire Golem — Cold-Fire Aura

Any creature within **5 ft of the golem** takes **2d6 cold damage** at the start of their turn.  
**Water or cold applied to the golem:** Aura suppressed for 1 round.

---

## Tactics

Gargoyle attacks while players deal with surprise. Golem moves to the centre to maximise aura coverage. Gargoyle has Flight (30 ft) — uses ceiling to avoid ground attacks.
