# Room 3 — Loot

## Iron Shelf (north wall, near sconces — no check)
- **Ossian's Cipher Journal** — 30 pages, personal cipher, kept in a **sealed fireproof case** that survived the forge heat. DC 18 Investigation to crack, or *Comprehend Languages* reads it normally. Full decoded content in Notes_and_Documents.md Room 3. Case: AC 19, HP 15, immune to fire/poison/psychic; opens with Thieves' Tools DC 14 or DC 16 STR.

## Fireproof Metal Plate (mounted on west wall — no check to see)
- **Scroll of Counterspell (3rd level)** — etched directly into the plate. Survived intact because the plate is fireproof. Ossian's precise hand. Plate: AC 19, HP 20, immune to fire/poison/psychic. It can be pried off the wall intact (DC 13 STR), or the scroll text can be transcribed from it (Arcana DC 15, 2 hours). If the plate is destroyed, the scroll text is ruined.

### If Kept as a Magic Item — Counterspell Chestplate
- **Type:** Wondrous item, rare; requires attunement by a spellcaster.
- **AC bonus:** While attuned and wearing the plate, the wearer gains a **+1 bonus to AC**. This stacks with worn armour and shields. It does not set the wearer's AC to 19.
- **Counterspell:** 1/day, reaction, cast *Counterspell* at 3rd level without a spell slot when the wearer sees a creature within 60 ft casting a spell.
- **Resolution:** 3rd-level or lower spells automatically fail. For 4th-level or higher spells, the wearer rolls a spellcasting ability check against DC 10 + the spell's level.
- **Recharge:** Daily at dawn.
