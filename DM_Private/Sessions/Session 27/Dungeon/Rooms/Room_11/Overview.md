# Room 11 — Upper Worker Dormitory

**Enemies:** 2 Skeletons (light-triggered, not entry-triggered)  
**Exits:** Upper corridor

A long dormitory with 8 metal lockers and 8 bunk frames (wood rotted away, iron frames remain). The lockers are dented, scratched, many with personal marks on them. This is where the upper-tier workers slept — not the common labourers.

---

## Skeletons

**They don't react to entry.** They react to light.  
The moment a torch, lamp, or light spell illuminates the far end of the room — they animate. Dark vision is fine. Dim light at range: fine. Bright light reaching them: initiative.

---

## Lockers

Lockers 1–8. Most are unlocked and empty (already looted by Ossian's people or the workers themselves).  
**Lockers 3, 5, 7 are locked.** Keys found in Room 13 or Room 16 (one set — first found wins).

| Locker | Contents |
|--------|----------|
| 3 | 45 gp, loaded dice (cheater's), letter to Mira |
| 5 | Carved wooden bird (personal, no value) |
| 7 | Guard's journal |

All other lockers: rusted open, empty or scraps of cloth.
