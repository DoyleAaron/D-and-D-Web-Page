# Room 9 — Pillar Hall

**Enemies:** 1 Gargoyle  
**Exits:** Main corridor | Sewer below (under 2nd pillar)

A wide hall with six stone pillars in two rows. Vaulted ceiling, intact. Something about the proportions is slightly off — the room is wider than it should be. Old Tharnic measuring marks on the walls. Dust thick enough to show footprints if any have been made.

---

## The Gargoyle

**Blends with the pillar** — Perception DC 16 passive to spot it. If missed, it looks like carved ornamentation.  
**Doesn't move until within 20 ft** — then drops off the pillar and attacks.  
Rolls Stealth 16 when the party enters. Any player with PP 16+ notices it's breathing.

---

## Sewer Access
**Under the 2nd pillar (left row):**  
- Iron grate in the floor — Investigation DC 14 to find (obscured by debris)
- DC 16 STR to lift (very heavy)

---

## Inside the Gargoyle's Chest Cavity
After killing it: the chest is hollow (Investigation DC 12 after death). Inside:  
A small stone carving of an eye — cornerstone of the dungeon's construction, ceremonially placed here.  
(No value, but worth noting — this was a foundation marker.)
