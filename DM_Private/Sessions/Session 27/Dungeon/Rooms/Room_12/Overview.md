# Room 12 — Salted Mine Storage

**Enemies:** None  
**Exits:** Lower corridor

A storage room with wooden shelving (mostly collapsed) and several large barrels. Smells of old salt and lamp oil. The floor is gritty. This was the supply depot for the lower mine sections.

---

## Containers

| Container | DC | Contents |
|-----------|----|----------|
| Sealed crate (locked) | Thieves' Tools DC 11 or DC 14 STR | Survey maps + sewer nav hint + mystery note |
| Open barrels | No check | Rope, chisels, lamp oil |
| Collapsed shelving | Perception DC 11 | A few intact ceramic jars — dried goods, long gone |

---

## The Maps (crate)
Three survey maps of the Ironwarf Heights exterior. One has a dotted line to an unmarked point on the cliff face: *"Emergency passage — do not use during active operations."* This marks the Room 8 exit from outside.

A second map shows the sewer system in rough form with junction labels (SE-1, SE-2, SE-3). **Without this, the sewer is a maze.** With it: navigable.
