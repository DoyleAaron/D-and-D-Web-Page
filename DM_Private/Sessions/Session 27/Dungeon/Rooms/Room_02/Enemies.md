## Amethyst Golem
**CR:** 10 | **HP:** 162 | **Full stats:** Golems.md

**Psychic Refraction:** Spells of 3rd level or lower targeting the golem are reflected back at the caster.  
**Crystal Shatter:** On single hit dealing 30+ damage — 2d6 piercing to all within 10 ft.  
**Mind Fracture:** Below 80 HP — moves to centre of group, AoE psychic blast.  
**Immunity:** Psychic, Poison.
