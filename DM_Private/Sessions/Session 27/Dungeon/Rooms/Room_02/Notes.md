# Room 2 — Notes & Documents

## Stone Tablet (chamber wall — DC 13 Arcana)
Ossian's hand:
> *"Standard binding does not work on crystal. The stone resists the death-principle — there is no death in it, so there is nothing to leverage. I spent eleven days understanding this. On the twelfth I stopped working with death and gave it memory instead. Note: memory and death are not as different as people assume. Both are records of what will not happen again. The sentinel holds the memory of everyone who died here. I did not tell the workers this. They are still dying here. The construct is still learning."*
