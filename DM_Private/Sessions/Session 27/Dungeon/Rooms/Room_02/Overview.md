# Room 2 — Amethyst Golem Chamber

**Exits:** Right wing corridor | Room 18 hidden room (DC 17 Investigation, north wall)  
**Door:** Iron lock — Thieves' Tools DC 15 or DC 18 STR | Key: Room 13 (wall rack, DC 13 Perception)

## Approach Corridor
Two Ghouls — trapped since Ossian died. Attack immediately, no hesitation.

## The Chamber
Deep purple stone — amethyst deposits throughout walls, floor, ceiling. Characters with INT 14+ feel faint pressure behind their eyes on entering.

> *It does not look angry. It looks like it is thinking. It looks like it has been thinking for sixty years and has reached some conclusions.*

---

## Key Mechanic — Crystal Shatter
**30+ damage in a single hit** → amethyst fragments burst outward — 2d6 piercing to all creatures within 10 ft including the golem.

**Do NOT open with spells of 3rd level or below.** Psychic Refraction reflects them back at the caster.

When below 80 HP: positions itself in the middle of the group, then triggers **Mind Fracture**.
