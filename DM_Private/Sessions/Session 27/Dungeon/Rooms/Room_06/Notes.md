# Room 6 — Notes & Documents

## Dais Tablet (back wall, mounted stone — DC 14 History to recognise it predates the facility)
This inscription is NOT Ossian's. The stone-type is non-local \u2014 brought in, shaped, placed, inscribed. DC 16 Arcana: the dialect is a form of Old Elvish that predates the Tharnic colonisation of this region by centuries. This was here before the mine. Before the road. Before anyone knew this place existed.

> *"The one who was first made this place last. A witness was needed. A witness was left. The eye does not sleep. The eye does not tire. When the maker returns, the eye will say: I have watched. I have not looked away. I was not alone."*

*(Ossian has written two words at the bottom of the tablet in fresh ink: "He won't.")*
