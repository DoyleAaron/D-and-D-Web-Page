# Room 6 — Grand Hall

**Enemies:** None initially. Vasskren CR15 appears after Ossian is defeated.  
**Exits:** South → Room 7 | North → upper area | East (Eye Door) → Room 7 (back passage)

The largest room in the dungeon. High vaulted ceiling. Tharnic carvings, mostly worn. Four large stone columns. An altar at the far end — repurposed, draped in rotted cloth, eye symbols scratched into every available surface.

> *It should feel important. Instead it feels abandoned. Whatever gathered here is long gone, and the room knows it.*

---

## Eye Door (east wall)
**Access to Room 7 (back passage)**  
- **Perception DC 17:** Seam in the wall — the shape of a door
- **Investigation DC 14:** Figure out how to open it. Pressing the central eye causes it to swing inward.
- No lock. Anyone who finds it can open it.

---

## Vasskren Arrival (ONLY after Ossian defeated)
Vasskren enters from the south when the party returns through the hall with Ossian's keys or the reliquary destroyed. He has been waiting. He knows they are coming.  
See Vasskren.md for full stats.
