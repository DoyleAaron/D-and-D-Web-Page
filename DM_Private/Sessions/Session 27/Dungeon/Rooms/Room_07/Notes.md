# Room 7 — Notes & Documents

## Ossian's Journal (on stone shelf beside sarcophagus — no check to find, DC 12 History to read)
Personal journal. Some pages water-stained but the main thread is legible. Written over approximately fourteen months.

---

> *Day 1: "The site is correct. The surveys are not. The lower tunnels have been worked since the Tharnic seal \u2014 recently, carefully, by someone who knew how to follow the old survey marks. I have examined fourteen disturbed blocks. Twelve reset cleanly. Two reset badly, as if in haste. Someone was here and left in a hurry. Note: the Cloak reacts to this place. Not to me. Not to proximity. To something in the stone that I cannot yet identify. I have checked this eighteen times. Eighteen."*

> *Day 8: "I have divided the facility. My people hold the east section. The Tharnic workers hold the upper levels. They are not to cross the line I have set at the east arch. I explained this clearly. Most of them understood. Three did not, this morning. Two do now. One still doesn't. I expect this to resolve."*

> *Day 20: "First sentinel placed. The elf-wood took the binding correctly \u2014 not well, correctly; I am not interested in impressions, I am interested in function. Note: it persists beyond what the binding should sustain on its own. Something here reinforces the work. I have not found the mechanism. I have noted it."*

> *Day 34: "Four sentinels placed. The fourth required eyes. I will not elaborate on the procurement. Note: during the binding, every worker in the facility looked at the ceiling simultaneously. They could not explain why. Two have since refused to enter the east section. I have not required it of them. My people did not react. My people are no longer surprised by things like this. This is one of the advantages of working with people who have been through the process."*

> *Day 51: "Vasskren is finished. I will not record what I used \u2014 the materials are in a separate log I have since destroyed. What I will say: the binding required something that had already chosen. Not been instructed \u2014 chosen. The result understood the parameters without being told the details. I find this notable. He will hold the inner door until someone places in correct order what he carries. The order is not arbitrary. The order follows a structure I found written below. If someone has read what I read, they will know the order. If they haven't, they won't."*

> *Month 3: "The Tharnic workers have begun asking questions. Expected. They have noticed that my people do not eat, do not sleep in the conventional sense, and do not age at any observable rate. I have told them it is a religious observance. Technically this is accurate. Marren has been helpful here \u2014 she smiles at them, which they find reassuring. I have not told them that Marren's smile is no longer entirely voluntary."*

> *Month 4: "I have found the origin of the Cloak. I will not record it here \u2014 this journal is not the appropriate document. What I will record: the Cloak was in the lower archive. The lower archive was sealed before the Tharnic colony existed. Someone left this here for someone who would eventually understand what they were looking at. I believe I am that person. I am not certain this is a good thing."*

> *Month 7: "I have placed what I found in the archive with the Eye, in the room below the hall. It belongs there. Anyone who reads this and wants the full record: follow the sealed door in the south wall. You will need what Vasskren carries \u2014 all five pieces, in the correct slots. He will not give them without reason. Fighting him is the reason I expected. I designed the system for someone who would fight for access, not someone who would simply ask for it. I am aware this reflects something about how I think. I do not consider it a flaw."*

> *Month 8: "I understand the time distortion now. The Eye does not only watch time pass. Its watching changes how time passes. A thing behaves differently when it is observed; this place is being observed constantly, perfectly, without blinking. The moments here are held too tightly. Inside, an hour remains an hour. Outside, the world moves on for a day. Observation is not a side effect. Observation is the mechanism."*

> *Month 11: "The workers want to leave. I have told them the survey is ongoing. This is true. I cannot tell them what I am surveying because what I am surveying cannot be safely carried out of this place. I am aware of the irony of sealing myself in to protect knowledge from being carried out while also having sealed myself in. I made this decision several months ago and am only now committing it to this document, because I kept hoping I would change my mind."*

> *Final entry: "Everything I built here was built around something that was already built. The golems, Vasskren, the door, the structure of the last fourteen months \u2014 it is a shell around something that did not need a shell. It only needed attention. Something that had been waiting in the way things wait when they have no capacity for impatience, which is to say: the waiting was not painful, it was simply time.*
>
> *I am not afraid. I want to be precise: not afraid. Finished. These are different states.*
>
> *The Cloak goes with me. It shouldn't leave this place but I cannot leave it here for someone who won't understand it. The record is below. The Eye is below. Everything I found, everything I understood, is in the room you reach by placing Vasskren's pieces in the door.*
>
> *If you are reading this: you are standing over it. Go down."*

## Loose Sheet (tucked inside the back cover — Investigation DC 13)
Ink noticeably fresher than the rest of the journal. Written last.

> *"Addition. Written because I could not not write it.*
>
> *Gaia made three things here. Not golems — I made those. Not the guardian — I made him. Three things older than this facility, older than this stone, older than the language I am writing in. Records. I have called them 'items' in other documents because 'items' implies they can be moved and handled, which they can. Records implies what they actually are.*
>
> *They are not here. They have not been here for a very long time. The transfer document I found was Colony Year 31 — three years before I arrived. Someone who knew they were not ordinary survey materials removed them and filed where they went. I found the document in a collapsed sector. I do not know if the person who filed it understood what they had. I suspect they knew only enough to hide them in a sealed administrative archive rather than a public collection.*
>
> *Who Gaia is, and what the name turns into when you follow it forward into this age, I have written once — in the room that is the appropriate place for that information. I have not written it here."*
