# Room 7 — Ossian's Chamber

**Exits:** Room 6 (front, sealed) | Eye Door from Room 6 (back passage) | Sealed door south → Room 17 (requires 5 Vasskren cores)

The cult leader's personal chamber. A stone sarcophagus in the centre — lid partly ajar, interior visible. Six worshippers arranged around it in poses of reverence, hands clasped, facing inward. All preserved — too well preserved. Rotting cloth only. No decomposition. The air here is cold and still.

Ossian himself lies in the sarcophagus — desiccated, wearing the cloak, face wrapped.

---

## CRITICAL TRIGGER — Do Not Touch

**Touching Ossian, a worshipper, or the Cloak activates the Mummy Lord encounter.**  
*Mage Hand* is safe. Reaching in carefully: safe. Physical contact from a living creature: triggers.

**Worshippers serve as verbal hint:** If a player says "I touch/move/disturb one," tell them the air gets heavier, as if waiting for something. Pause. They can pull back.

---

## Reliquary (behind north tapestry — Perception DC 15)
A stone box, locked (Thieves' Tools DC 15 / DC 19 STR — or break it: AC 13, HP 15).  
**Destroying the reliquary prevents Ossian's rejuvenation** — he is permanently dead.  
Failing this: Ossian will return in 24 hours even if defeated.

5 slots corresponding to Vasskren's cores. Place all 5 cores in the correct slots → door opens.
