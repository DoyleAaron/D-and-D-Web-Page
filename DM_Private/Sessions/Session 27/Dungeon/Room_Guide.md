The four golem rooms are locked. Each can be picked (Thieves' Tools) or forced (STR), but keys bypass both. Keys were held by different levels of the facility hierarchy:

| Golem Room | Key Location | Where Exactly |
|------------|-------------|---------------|
| Room 1 — Tree Golem | **Room 16** (Smelting Furnaces) | In the locked ore hopper with the iron keys and coinage |

## Room 0 — Main Entrance

**Enemies:** None (initially)

The wide arch cut into the cliff face. Iron doors — permanently open, rusted off their hinges, overgrown with lichen. Tharnic administrative symbols cover the arch stonework; over them, in a later and more anxious hand, dozens of carved eyes fill every spare surface.

**Lock mechanic:** The moment the last party member crosses the threshold, a heavy iron bar drops across the entrance from inside — a spring-loaded Tharnic mechanism, centuries old, still functional. The sound is very loud. The bar is thick iron: DC 22 STR to lift from inside. The only ways out once this drops:
- Find Room 8 (hidden breakable wall)
- Defeat all 4 golems — this releases the bar automatically
- *Knock* spell (removes the bar for 10 minutes, then it resets)

**Time dilation begins here.** Every 1 hour inside = 1 day outside.

**Perception DC 12:** Dust on the floor has been disturbed — drag marks, not footprints. Something moves in here.

**Investigation DC 14 (the lock mechanism):** Tharnic design, not Ossian's work. This place was built to trap people. Original purpose: anti-theft from rival mining operations.

**Loot:** Near the right wall, half-buried in debris: a rusted iron lockbox (corroded open). Inside: **28 cp, 14 sp**, a small bronze Tharnic surveying token (2 gp to a collector), and a broken compass rose stamp used by Tharnic cartographers.

## Room 1 — Tree Golem Chamber

**Enemies:** Tree Golem (CR 9, HP 178) — see Golems.md  
**Door:** Iron lock — Thieves' Tools DC 14 or DC 18 STR to force. Key found in Room 16.

A tall stone chamber with roots bursting through the floor and ceiling — an ancient elven tree, petrified and converted into a construct by Ossian. The chamber smells of old bark and copper. The roots have cracked the stonework over decades. No daylight reaches here, but the tree seems to still reach for something above.

**Tablet near the door (Ossian's writing — DC 14 Arcana):**
> *"The first sentinel is placed. The elf-wood took the binding well. It remembers what it was, and hates what it has become. That hatred makes it stronger."*

**Tactics:** The Tree Golem tries to grapple the most armoured target and hold them while striking at spellcasters. It positions to block the exit. Fire users become its priority — it will abandon the grapple to move toward active fire.

**Loot (after golem — leather satchel wedged under a root):**
- **120 gp**
- 2 × *Potions of Healing* (standard, 2d4+2)
- *Ring of Feather Falling* — clear glass-like stone set in silver. DC 13 Arcana to identify. Reaction to fall safely from any height. Attunement. Worth 250 gp.
- **Eye Golem Key (Room 4)** — angular iron key wrapped in black cloth, with a small carved eye set into the bow. The pupil is a depression, not a gem.

**Verdant Core:** If exposed and extracted (see Golems.md) — worth 200 gp to an alchemist, or usable in crafting a *Necklace of Adaptation*.

## Room 2 — Amethyst Golem Chamber

**Enemies:** 2 Ghouls (CR 1 each) in the approach corridor + Amethyst Golem (CR 10, HP 162) — see Golems.md  
**Door:** Iron lock — Thieves' Tools DC 15 or DC 18 STR to force. Key found in Room 13.

**Approach corridor:** Two Ghouls have been trapped in the east wing since Ossian died. They attack immediately — no hesitation, no waiting. They have been here a very long time.

**The Chamber:** Deep purple stone — amethyst deposits throughout the walls, floor, and ceiling. The golem stands at the far end. Faint psychic resonance: characters with INT 14+ feel a pressure behind their eyes on entering.

> *It does not look angry. It looks like it is thinking. It looks like it has been thinking for sixty years and has reached some conclusions.*

**Tactics:** The Amethyst Golem stands still and lets the party come to it. It baits spellcasters into using low-level spells — Psychic Refraction reflects 3rd-level and below back at the caster. When below 80 HP it tries to position in the middle of the group before triggering Mind Fracture.

**Tablet on the wall (Ossian — DC 13 Arcana):**
> *"The purple stone resists necromancy. Interesting. So I gave it something else — the memory of all who have died within these walls. It does not serve the dead. It remembers them."*

**Loot:**
- *Ghouls' hoard* (corner behind old ore carts in approach corridor): **180 sp, 40 gp**, a *Potion of Greater Healing* (they can't use it — just took it), a broken shortsword blade (salvageable, 5 gp), a Tharnic administrative signet ring — bronze, eagle crest (25 gp to an antiquities buyer, 8 gp generic fence)
- *After the golem:* **180 gp** in amethyst chips scattered at the golem's base (5 minutes to collect)
- **+1 Dagger** buried in crystal debris — looks like a plain iron dagger until DC 12 Arcana (blade is amethyst-veined, faint purple shimmer)

## Room 3 — Blue Fire Golem Chamber

**Enemies:** 1 Gargoyle (HP 52, CR 2) at the doorway + Blue Fire Golem (CR 11, HP 145) — see Golems.md  
**Door:** Iron lock — Thieves' Tools DC 14 or DC 18 STR to force. Key found in Room 15.

A chamber converted into a magical forge. The walls are scorched blue-black. In the centre, encased in a ring of metal rods embedded in the floor, stands the golem — wreathed in cobalt flame.

**The gargoyle** perches on the arch above the room entrance. Same stats as the Room 10 gargoyle. Attacks immediately when the threshold is crossed. The Cold-Fire Aura begins 5 ft inside the room.

> *The room is warm. Technically. The instruments would agree. But your bones say something different.*

**Cold-Fire Aura:** 2d6 cold damage to any creature that starts its turn within 5 ft of the golem. Cannot be suppressed. This combines poorly with the doorway — players who rush in cluster into the aura range immediately.

**Do not use fire magic in this room.** The golem absorbs fire and regains HP from it.

**Loot (after golem):**
- **Ossian's Cipher Journal** — 30 pages in cipher, kept in a sealed fireproof case on the west-wall plate shelf (survived the heat). DC 18 Investigation to crack, or *Comprehend Languages*. The Islefield-surveyor conspiracy thread — full text in Loot_Guide.md and Notes_and_Documents.md (Room 3)
- **Spell Scroll of Counterspell (3rd level)** — etched onto a fireproof metal plate on the wall. Can be used directly or carefully transcribed (1 minute)

## Room 4 — Eye Golem Chamber

**Enemies:** 2 Weeping Angels (CR 5 each) in the approach corridor + Eye Golem (CR 12, HP 170) — see Golems.md and Weeping_Angels.md  
**Door:** Heavy iron lock — Thieves' Tools DC 16 or DC 20 STR to force. Key found in Room 1.

**Approach Corridor — Weeping Angels:** Two angels are positioned in the corridor leading to Room 4 — one roughly 30 ft out, one at the doorway itself. They are stone-still when observed. The challenge: maintaining observation on both simultaneously while navigating the corridor. The moment a player's attention shifts, the unobserved angel moves.

*Full mechanics in Weeping_Angels.md. Key point: if both are being watched, they cannot move. Walking past them while watching is a valid solution. Make it tense.*

**The Chamber:** Hexagonal room. The ceiling is a painted dome — a massive single eye rendered in black and white, looking down. The Eye Golem stands in the centre.

> *The eye on the dome is not decorative. You realise this about three seconds after you enter. It has been watching you since the corridor.*

**Full Witness (Eye Arm Recharge 5–6):** DC 17 WIS or incapacitated 1 round — the sensation of being completely, utterly observed. **Varin is immune** — Bahamut's mark acts as a divine ward.

**Side Door:** Set into the south wall, partially hidden behind a collapsed shelf unit (DC 12 Perception to spot). Independent lock — Thieves' Tools DC 22, or break it (AC 19, HP 40, bludgeoning only). Behind it: a dead-end storage passage, old iron ore samples, broken tools. Nothing of value — the party won't know until they open it.

**Loot (after golem):**
- **Tome of Avethis** — partial manuscript, 50 pages. Grants advantage on Arcana and History checks if studied for 8 hours. Side effect: reader must succeed DC 14 WIS save or become mildly fixated on knowing things they shouldn't for 24 hours (compulsive investigation, probing questions — DM flavour only)
- **220 gp** in Tharnic gold discs

## Room 5 — Officer's Suite

**Enemies:** None

Two connected rooms: a private office and a small bedroom. Better quality than anything else in the worker areas — a real wooden desk (partially rotted), an actual bed frame, a wardrobe, a writing mirror above the desk. Whoever lived here was above the common workers but was not Ossian.

> *Someone cared about this room. The care is still here, even through sixty years of dust.*

**The Mirror (Perception DC 16):** Briefly shows the room as it was — lit, occupied, a figure at the desk. Clears immediately. Avethis bleed-through. **DC 18 Arcana** (if a character reaches toward the mirror): they see one specific memory — the officer (human, late 40s, well-dressed) placing a sealed letter in the wardrobe's false bottom and then sitting back down and never moving again. He did not leave voluntarily.

**Wardrobe — False Bottom (DC 14 Investigation):** A sealed wooden box. Inside:
- **150 gp in Islefield gold** — recognisable mint, 60+ years old. *(Why does a Tharnic mine officer have Islefield gold? This question is left open.)*
- **Ring of Warmth** — copper band, tiny flame engraved. DC 13 Arcana to identify. Cold damage resistance, advantage on CON saves vs. cold environments. Attunement.
- **Bundle of unsent letters** addressed to *"Aether, third house, Mill Row, southern camp."* Love letters. Never sent. The writer signs them only "T."

**Desk (Investigation DC 12):** Partially legible Tharnic administrative forms, some overwritten by Ossian. One document stands out: a **payment receipt from "Islefield Colonial Liaison Office"** for *"survey consulting services — Ironwarf Heights, Colony Year 31."* Ossian arrived in Colony Year 34. Islefield was paying for surveys of this area three years before Ossian took it over. The officer who lived here was being paid by Islefield.

*(This connects to Room 3's cipher journal — Ossian found the same thing and was troubled by it. Together they form a thread the party can pull.)*

## Room 6 — The Grand Hall

**Enemies:** Vasskren (CR 15) — spawns only after Ossian is defeated. Empty until then.

Long and tall — the spine of the dungeon. High arched ceiling with carved eye motifs repeated along the vault, dozens of eyes all staring forward. The Avethis symbol throughout: a single open eye with an hourglass pupil. Stone pews along the sides, facing the dais — this was a Tharnic mine processing hall, repurposed into a worship chamber.

A stone dais at the far end. On the dais: **a massive carved eye, 4 ft across.** It appears decorative.

**The Eye — Secret Door to Room 7:**
- Perception DC 17 to notice the seam around the eye
- Investigation DC 14 to find that pushing the pupil causes it to swing inward
- Unlocked from the start — the golems do not seal it.

**After Ossian is defeated:** The dungeon shakes. A low resonant tone — like a very deep bell — echoes through every corridor. The iron bar at Room 0 drops. Then, from beneath the floor of the Grand Hall, the stone separates.

> *The eye carvings along the vault go dark, one by one. And then there is movement in the floor. The stone doesn't break — it separates, like a curtain being pulled. And from the gap, something very old and very large begins to climb.*

**Vasskren rises.** See: Vasskren.md for the full encounter.

**Loot (on the dais, available before Vasskren):** Three iron offering bowls on wall brackets. Two empty. The third: **40 gp** in large Tharnic silver discs and a small *Bead of Force* (DC 15 Arcana to identify — looks like a dark glass marble, creates a 10 ft sphere of force on throw).

**Tablet on the dais wall (old Elvish — DC 12 Language check or Comprehend Languages):**
> *"He who watches all witnesses without judgment. To see is to know. To know is to transcend. The Eye does not close. The Eye does not turn away. The dead do not hide from the Eye. The living cannot."*

## Room 7 — Ossian's Chamber

**Enemies:** Valdrek Ossian — Mummy Lord (CR 11) + 6 Wights — **ONLY IF TRIGGERED**

A large square room. Thick dust. A circular altar in the centre with the dried remains of **six worshippers** arranged around it in a ritual pose — seated, heads bowed, hands flat on the altar's edge. In the centre of the altar: the body of **Valdrek Ossian** himself. Desiccated, ancient, robed in black and violet. Around his neck: the **Cloak of the Watcher's Veil**, partially folded beneath him.

> *The silence in this room is different from the rest of the dungeon. Outside, silence means nothing is moving. Here, it feels like something is waiting for you to move.*

**Trigger:** Any creature physically touches Ossian, any worshipper, or attempts to take the cloak by hand or non-Mage-Hand method. *Mage Hand* retrieval works cleanly. A thrown rope to drag the cloak does not.

**On trigger:**
- All 6 worshippers animate as **Wights** (HP 45 each, CR 3 each)
- Ossian rises as a **Mummy Lord** (see Overview.md for full stats)
- The Eye Door swings shut and magically locks until Ossian is defeated

**If Ossian is defeated:** All skeletons still active elsewhere in the dungeon crumble instantly. The door reopens.

**The Sealed Door:** In the far wall, behind a tapestry depicting Avethis's eye — the door to Room 17. Five indentations in a cross pattern (N, S, E, W, centre). Sealed at 12th-level spell. Requires Vasskren's 5 cores placed in order. See Vasskren.md.

**Loot:**
- **The Cloak of the Watcher's Veil** — the mission objective
- **Ossian's Journal** (partially legible — dates, ritual notes, increasingly fragmented near the end)
- **340 gp** in ancient Tharnic coins (face value intact; collector value ×2)
- **Scroll of Speak with Dead**

## Room 8 — Emergency Exit (Breakable Wall)

**Enemies:** None

A corridor in the far western wing that appears to dead-end at a stone wall. It does not.

**From outside the dungeon:** DC 22 Perception or Investigation to notice this section of cliff face is composite rather than natural bedrock. Effectively undetectable without prior knowledge.

**From inside:** DC 15 Investigation to notice faint scratch marks on the floor in front of the wall (old boot marks — multiple people came here over the years) and that the mortar between blocks is more worn than the surrounding walls.

**Breaking it down:** AC 17, HP 30, bludgeoning or piercing damage only. Alternatively — a lever hidden in the left-side wall at knee height (DC 15 Investigation) releases the wall panel outward silently. Original Tharnic emergency egress for workers trapped during a collapse.

**Once opened:** A narrow 3 ft passage, 20 ft long, exits onto an overgrown cliff ledge. The ledge path descends to the base of the Heights — roughly 10 minutes of careful footing. No door on the exterior side — once opened, it stays open.

**Hints pointing here:** Room 16 has the directional scratch mark. Room 12's survey maps show an "emergency egress" notation for this section of the facility.

## Room 9 — The Pillar Hall

**Enemies:** 1 Gargoyle (HP 58, CR 2)

A wide stone chamber with four cracked columns arranged in pairs. The remains of a stone administrative desk, half-buried in rubble. Ceiling higher than the corridors — vaulted, with faint remains of painted eye motifs, most now flaked away. At the far end, wide stairs lead up into the Grand Hall (Room 6).

**The gargoyle** perches on the column at the top of the stairs. It looks wrong — collapsed into itself slightly, mouth wider than it should be. It does not move until a creature gets within 20 ft or until attacked.

> *It looks like stone. It looks like it has always been here. The problem is it wasn't there when you first scanned the room.*

**Stone Camouflage:** Advantage on Stealth when motionless in a stone environment. DC 16 Passive Perception to notice it before it moves.

**Sewer Drain:** Under the second column on the left, partially obscured by debris. **DC 14 Investigation** to find it. Heavy iron grate — **DC 16 STR** to lift. Not locked from above. Leads to the Sewer System.

**Loot (behind the stairs, in a hollow at the column base):** **65 gp** in old Tharnic coins, a *Potion of Climbing* (stoppered bronze vial — DC 13 Arcana to identify), and a dead cultist's satchel — moulded leather, contains a half-legible note in Ossian's hand: *"Do not wake the stones. They remember everything."*

**When killed:** The gargoyle crumbles. Inside its chest cavity: a piece of the original Tharnic cornerstone inscription — *"the lower archive was sealed by the Master Ossian in the 34th year of the colony."*

## Room 10 — The Second Gargoyle

**Enemies:** 1 Gargoyle (HP 52, CR 2) — attacks immediately

A junction room between the left-wing corridors and the central hall approach. Lower ceiling than Room 9. The gargoyle perches on the arch above the doorway leading into the left wing. Unlike the first gargoyle, it does not wait — it attacks the moment a creature crosses the threshold.

**Sewer Drain:** Set into the floor in the far corner, no debris covering it. **DC 10 Perception** to spot. Lighter grate — **DC 12 STR** to lift. Unlocked from above.

**Loot:** The gargoyle crumbles when killed. Wedged in the wall above the arch: a small bronze Tharnic plaque (DC 12 History to read): *"Sector 3-W — Unauthorised entry will be logged."* The word "logged" has been scratched out and replaced with "punished." That has been scratched out too. Worth nothing. Grimly funny.

In the corner near the drain: an ancient worker's belt pouch — leather, intact from the dry air. Contains **22 sp**, a whetstone, and a carved bone token with a name scratched on it: *Tessim.* No other context.

## Room 11 — Upper Worker Dormitory

**Enemies:** 2 Skeletons (standard, CR 1/4 each) — slumped in bunks, animate when light hits them

Better conditions than typical Tharnic workers — bunk bed frames (collapsed but recognisable), personal storage lockers (iron, numbered 1–8), more space per person. Still shared. Still grim. But someone acknowledged these people as worth a slightly better allocation.

**The skeletons** are slumped in two bunks at the far end. The moment torchlight or a light spell hits them, they stand. The transition from still to upright is immediate.

**Storage Lockers (1–8):** Three are locked. The iron keys from Room 13 or Room 16 open lockers 3, 5, and 7.
- **Locker 3:** **45 gp**, a set of loaded dice (5 gp — any gambler recognises the weight), and an unsent letter addressed to *"Mira, in the southern camp."*
- **Locker 5:** Empty except for a small carved wooden bird. A child's toy. Carefully kept.
- **Locker 7:** A leather-bound personal log — a guard's journal. Last entry: *"Day 40: The new workers don't eat. The Master says they're from a different camp on different rations. I've been here twenty years. I know what people look like when they eat."*

## Room 12 — Salted Mine Storage

**Enemies:** None

A storage room. Iron shelving, most collapsed. Sealed barrels — Tharnic metal-banded, salt-packed. Most contain preserved tools (pickaxes, chisels, rope — ancient but serviceable). One barrel has been opened recently and resealed (DC 13 Investigation: fresher sealant — Ossian, taking rope).

> *The room smells of mineral salt and old iron. Whatever was kept here, someone cared about it. The care feels wrong in a dungeon full of death.*

**Sealed Crate (far corner, iron tag: "DO NOT OPEN — M.O."):** Thieves' Tools DC 11 or DC 14 STR to open. Inside:
- 3 intact Tharnic survey maps of the Ironwarf Heights tunnel network. One map shows passages beneath the dungeon — labelled only *"drainage system."* The passages match the sewer exactly. **(Key hint for the sewer system if the party hasn't found it yet.)**
- A small lodestone compass (5 gp)
- A folded note in an unfamiliar hand (not Ossian's, at least 60 years old): *"The tunnels run deeper than the maps show. There is a level below the drainage that the surveyors never charted. Don't go there."* No signature.

**Loot from open barrels:** Functional **rope (50 ft, hempen, good condition)**, 2 Tharnic chisels (improvised weapons 1d4 piercing, or 2 gp each to a smith), a sealed jar of lamp oil (equivalent to 3 flasks, 3 sp).

## Room 13 — Main Work Area

**Enemies:** 6 Skeletal Workers (CR 1/4 each)

The central labour area of the facility. Long stone tables for tool maintenance and meal breaks. Iron wall racks. A large central firepit, long cold. The six Skeletal Workers move in slow repetitive loops — miming tool maintenance, carrying phantom loads, sitting and standing. The loop never changes.

They do not notice the party unless approached within 10 ft or directly disturbed. **Stealth DC 11** to pass through without triggering them.

> *They move in slow, purposeless loops — pickaxe down, drag, lift, repeat. No ore moves. Nothing changes. They have been doing this for decades. When they notice you, the loop stops.*

**Sewer Hatch:** Under the large work table in the southwest corner — **DC 12 Perception** to notice the table has been moved slightly. The hatch underneath is unlocked. Leads to the Sewer System.

**Amethyst Golem Key (Room 2):** Hanging on a hook inside a wall rack, behind a torn canvas sheet — **DC 13 Perception** to spot. A heavy iron key, dark with age, with a small amethyst chip pressed into the bow.

**Loot (scattered across work tables):**
- **55 sp** in mixed Tharnic coinage, loose in a clay bowl
- A dented but functional *tinderbox* in a wall rack
- **3 iron keys on a leather cord** — matching the lockers in Room 11 *(same note as Room 16 — first found wins)*

## Room 14 — Conference Room

**Enemies:** None — 6 staged robed forms at the table do NOT animate

A large room with a long stone table, still intact. Stone chairs. A cracked window opening with iron bars — the only source of fresh air on this side of the dungeon. This is where Ossian and his senior cultists met.

**Private Section (locked door, east wall — behind a tapestry):** A small antechamber off the main conference room, Ossian's before-and-after space for meetings. Iron lock: Thieves' Tools DC 15 or DC 19 STR to force. Inside: a single high-backed chair facing a writing stand, a locked iron chest (Thieves' Tools DC 14), and an unlit brazier. **Chest contents:** **90 gp** in Tharnic administrative gold (payment for services rendered, pre-Ossian), a wax tablet with a meeting agenda — mundane mine business, except one item scratched in at the bottom in Ossian's hand: *"Item 6: the door below the drainage. Do not raise with staff."* (Plant — connects to the deeper level in Room 12. No further detail.) A folded note tucked under the wax tablet, unsealed: *"They're asking questions about the east wing. I told them the noise is settling stone. They believed me because they want to."*

**The Six Robes:** Six complete sets of rotting robes, seated as if their wearers simply vanished. They look occupied for a long moment — there's enough body structure left in the cloth. They will not animate. If touched: they collapse into dust.

**Murals on the walls:** Full depictions of Avethis — the central eye with an hourglass pupil, ringed by smaller eyes each showing a different scene (a battle, a birth, a death, a crown being placed). Remarkable, unsettling artistic work.

**Perception DC 12:** One mural eye is watching the door. It moves when no one looks directly at it. Not dangerous — residual divine bleed-through from Avethis's presence in this place.

**Loot:**
- Under the table — a locked iron document case (Thieves' Tools DC 13): **3 spell scrolls** — *Detect Magic*, *Identify*, *Comprehend Languages* (all 1st level, Ossian's precise hand, perfect condition)
- **120 gp** in old mixed coinage in a satchel hung on the back of one chair
- Wax-sealed vial of *Dust of Dryness* (DC 14 Arcana to identify)
- **Document on the table** (crumbling letter, Ossian's hand — DC 14 History): *"The vault is prepared. The Eye will watch this place until the stones dissolve. Anyone who reaches the inner chamber and proves worthy of witness will be marked. The mark is not a curse. It is a gift. They will never be deceived again."* (Foreshadows Room 17.)

## Room 15 — Higher-Up Dining

**Enemies:** 2 Skeletons (standard, CR 1/4 each) — seated at the table, animate when the room is entered

A refined dining area — better furnishings than the lower work areas. A proper table, carved chairs, wall hangings (now rotted to threads), the remnants of actual tableware: pewter cups, iron candlesticks, a cracked serving platter. This is where the upper-tier workers ate apart from the common labourers.

**Private Dining Quarters (locked door, south wall):** A smaller room reserved for the senior staff. Iron lock: Thieves' Tools DC 13 or DC 17 STR to force. Inside: a single round table with two chairs, a locked iron cabinet (same DC), better quality tableware (silver-plated, **35 gp** if collected), and a half-used bottle of something that was once very good wine — now vinegar. Wall-mounted plaque in Tharnic: *"Reserved — Sector Administration Only."* 

**Cabinet contents:**
- A personal ledger belonging to whoever ran this facility before Ossian (Tharnic admin records, mostly boring — but the last page: *"He arrived today. He is not what they sent for. I don't think anyone sent him."*)
- **Blue Fire Golem Key (Room 3):** A heavy iron key wrapped in a piece of singed leather — someone knew better than to leave it unprotected near the forge.

The two skeletons are seated at the table as if mid-meal. They don't animate until a creature enters the room — both stand simultaneously, knocking their chairs back.

**Hidden Compartment (kitchen area — DC 15 Investigation):** A loose stone behind the remnants of the bread oven. Inside:
- *Potion of Greater Healing* (4d4+4, dark amber, sealed clay vial)
- **Worker's Journal** — fully legible. Dated entries:
> *"Day 1 after the Master arrived: He says we are to remain. The work continues... Day 14: I am writing this because I think I might not remember writing it. The Master makes the torches burn blue. He says it's holy... Day 19: I think I'm the only one left. I found the others in the east wing. They are still standing. They are still smiling. I hid this here. If you find it, go. Now. Don't wait."*

**Loot:** Pewter tableware — **8 gp** total. Iron candlesticks — 3 gp. Wall hook holds a Tharnic survey satchel with 3 partial surface maps of the Ironwarf Heights (5 gp to a cartographer).

## Room 16 — Smelting Furnaces

**Enemies:** 4 Skeletons (standard, CR 1/4 each) patrolling the floor

Ore smelting facilities from the Tharnic era. Three massive furnaces — cold for decades, but intact. Conveyor tracks (broken). Ore hoppers (empty). Iron chains hanging from the ceiling at multiple points. The floor is soot-blackened and uneven.

**Hazards:**
- **Chain collapse:** A chain section above the west furnace falls if more than 200 lbs stands under it (DC 14 Perception to spot the fraying). 2d8 bludgeoning to anyone hit.
- **Furnace gas vent:** One furnace releases gas pockets when struck or opened. DC 13 CON save or poisoned 1 hour. Advantage if nose/mouth is covered.

**Exit hint:** A small mark scratched into the stone wall near the southwest door — three lines and a westward arrow. DC 13 Perception to notice. DC 12 History: a Tharnic emergency egress notation pointing toward Room 8.

**Tablet on the east furnace wall (DC 13 History):**
> *"Production output Month 7: 340 units iron ore. 12 units silver ore. Workers: 28. Deaths this month: 3. Overseer Brace notes workers are reporting sounds from the lower eastern wing. Investigation pending."*

**Loot:** In the locked ore hopper (Thieves' Tools DC 12 or DC 16 STR to wrench open):
- **45 sp** in old Tharnic coinage
- A dented but functional *tinderbox*
- **3 iron keys on a leather cord** — open the storage lockers in Room 11. *(First found between Room 13 and 16 wins.)*
- **Tree Golem Key (Room 1):** A large iron key with a carved leaf motif on the bow, tarnished green. Stored here by a foreman who managed access to the outer chambers.

## Room 17 — The Eye Chamber

**Enemies:** None  
**Access:** Sealed door in Room 7 — requires all 5 Vasskren cores placed correctly

*This room has not been entered since Ossian sealed it. The dust on the floor is undisturbed. Whatever is here has been waiting.*

A hexagonal chamber. Floor, walls, and ceiling are polished black stone — not Tharnic construction. This was built for Ossian, or by something working through him. In the centre of the floor: a perfect circle of white stone, ten feet across, a single eye carved in the centre facing upward. No hourglass pupil. Just an open eye, looking up.

When the party enters, the eye opens. Not physically — the carving doesn't move. But every person in the room has the same experience simultaneously: the sensation of being seen completely, without judgment. It lasts one second. Then it's gone.

**The Sight (Free Boon — once only, permanent until used):**
Every creature that spends 1+ minute in this room receives the Sight of Avethis.

*Once — and only once — you may choose to see into any point in time. Past or future. Any moment, any place. You see it as a witness: clearly, completely, without intervention. You cannot interact with what you see. The vision lasts as long as it needs to. When it ends, the ability is gone.*

*There is no mechanism. No word to speak, no ritual to perform. When you are ready, you close your eyes, and you see.*

**DM Note:** This is a once-per-character narrative tool. Handle it outside combat, privately if needed. The player describes what they want to see — you describe what Avethis shows them. It sees everything. It shows the truth, not the comfortable version. If a player asks to see something that would break the campaign, show them a true but incomplete slice.

**Loot:**
- Four stone alcoves along the walls. Three contain offerings: **400 gp total** in pristine Tharnic gold (still bright — perfectly sealed room, zero oxidation)
- **Ioun Stone of Awareness** — pale blue stone that orbits the attuned creature's head. Cannot be surprised. +5 to passive Perception. Attunement.
- **Ossian's Research Codex** — his complete private manuscript, illuminated, written in Common and Old Elvish. Contains:
  - His discovery that the Eye (Avethis) was *made* — a companion the earth-god Gaia created here, which became a god over the eons; it predates the Tharnic pantheon and the elven gods
  - That Gaia made the Three here (the Book / Chart of the Threefold Way, the Ring, the Crown) and that the Cloak carries Avethis's witnessing of what Gaia built beneath Islefield
  - The name **Gaia** throughout — and, in the final entry only, the revelation that Gaia is the one the present age calls **Balthazar** (the single place both names meet)
  - Final entry, the day before he died: *"The Eye does not judge. It only sees. I understand now why that is worse."*

**DM Note:** The codex is the most significant lore item in the dungeon. File it away.

- From Room 14: DC 17 Investigation — east wall dimensions don't match the room's exterior

- **85 gp** in mixed coinage under a false floorboard beneath the desk (DC 14 Investigation)

## The Sewer System

**Access Points:** Rooms 9, 10, 18, 19, 20

**Lock mechanic:** All hatches are iron-bolt mechanisms operated from above (dungeon floor level). Once opened, a hatch stays open until manually re-bolted. They cannot be opened from below — no mechanism on the sewer side. A creature already in the sewer can attempt DC 18 STR to push an incompletely bolted hatch open.

**The environment:** Stone-lined passages, 8 ft wide, 6 ft tall. Central drainage channel 1 ft wide with a constant slow trickle of groundwater. Walls are old fitted stone, mossy near active seepage. Ceiling has iron hook-mounts at regular intervals — once held hanging lamps, now empty. Total darkness without a light source.

**Navigation:** Without the survey map from Room 12, the sewer is a maze (DC 15 Survival to navigate correctly). With the map, routes between access points are clearly readable. Travel between any two adjacent access points: approximately 5 minutes (vs. 10–20 minutes through the dungeon proper).

**The safe zone:** No undead enter the sewer. This was not Ossian's design — the groundwater carries trace amounts of a naturally consecrated mineral compound in the local rock. Undead don't understand why they won't enter. They simply don't.

> *The first time the party drops into the sewer and realises nothing followed them down, the silence is different. The dungeon silence is a held breath. The sewer silence is just quiet.*

**Short rests:** Safe here. Nothing disturbs you. The groundwater is not drinkable but causes no harm.

**The deeper level (foreshadowing only):** The note from Room 12 mentions passages below the drainage that the surveyors never charted. There is no accessible way down from within this dungeon. This is a thread for a future session — do not develop it here.
