## Part 1 — Amdir's Vision (At Sea, Before Docking)

> **Trigger:** Before the ship reaches the Dawerton docks, as the crew is celebrating, run this privately with Amdir's player. Pass a note or pull them aside.

### The Message (Father Maltrius — Sending Spell)

Maltrius uses a *Sending* spell. Amdir hears a voice in his mind — not a god, unmistakably **mortal** (though ancient and cold). Amdir has heard Maltrius's voice before in older notes/sessions.

**Read-aloud (whisper to Amdir's player):**

> *A voice slips into your mind like cold water through a crack — not divine, not warm. You know this voice. Low, precise, and deeply irritated.*
>
> **"You fool."**
>
> *A pause. Like a man composing himself.*
>
> **"I built the Spehlina as a disruption. A chaos-anchor to derail their precious summit. And you burned it to ash. I should be furious. Instead, I find myself impressed — and more importantly, I find myself reconsidering."**
>
> *The voice sharpens.*
>
> **"You'll do something for me, Amdir. Travel to Lavalto Peak. You can see it even from Braewood — a bridge against the sky. You know the one. When you arrive: go to the summit. Bring your most powerful spell slot primed and ready. That is all I will say."**
>
> *A beat of silence, then — almost as an afterthought:*
>
> **"Tell no one. And Amdir — this is not a request."**
>
> *The voice is gone.*

### DM Notes — Maltrius's Full Plan (Players Don't Know)

- At the summit of Lavalto Peak there is an ancient **pedestal** (unrelated to Avethis — this one is Asmodean). 
- When Amdir slots his spellbook into the pedestal, it **drains 14 charges**, reducing the book to 5 uses.
- The pedestal harnesses those 14 charges and fuses them into a single **Meteoric Fireball** — the equivalent of 14 × Level 5 Fireballs fired simultaneously in one colossal explosion.
- **Amdir is currently on 19 charges. After the ritual: 5 charges remain.**
- Target: The bridge at Kluimont, visible from the peak.
- **What actually happens:** The explosion hits but **Trynod of Kluimont** has a city-wide arcane shield raised (he anticipated the possibility of magical attack). The fireball detonates against it — catastrophic visuals, tremors felt for miles, but the bridge survives mostly intact.
- The explosion IS however a massive enough **distraction** that:
  - **Myrelle** steals the **Book of Binding** from Queen Maera Brae in the chaos
  - The **Queen of Islefield's advisor (traitor)** steals the **Ring of Islefield** during the summit panick (Morjor was told this by Balthazar — he may or may not have connected the dots yet)
- Amdir does not know any of this. He will just be told to go to the peak and prepare.
- This is **sessions away** — do not force it this session, just plant the instruction.

## Part 1B — Divine Contacts (At Sea, Approach to Shore)

*Run these privately, back to back, while the ship approaches the docks. Amdir has just received Maltrius's message. These two scenes can overlap in timing — both happen in the last hour before shore.*

---

### Balthazar → Morjor

> **Trigger:** As the ship draws close to Dawerton. Morjor's ring pulses faintly — not threatening, just warm. If he is asleep, he dreams. If awake, his vision blurs for a moment and the world goes quiet.

**What Morjor experiences:** A brief pocket of still. Not a full vision — more like standing in a doorway into somewhere else. An old man's voice, tired but deliberate. The sense of someone who has seen many ages end and wants this one to go differently.

**Read-aloud (private to Morjor's player):**

> *The ring is warm against your hand — warmer than it should be. The ship noise fades. You are not elsewhere, exactly. But something old is looking at you.*
>
> *The voice is careful. Measured. Unhurried in the way that only centuries of patience produces.*
>
> **"You stopped the first tragedy."**
>
> *A pause.*
>
> **"I want you to understand that. What came off that lake would have unravelled the summit entirely. Many would have died. You held that back. I am grateful."**
>
> *But his tone doesn't lift.*
>
> **"You have also walked into the second one. Not through fault — through consequence. The tragedy you prevented has left a gap. Something will fill it at that summit. And the stakes now are larger than a corrupted guardian in a lake."**
>
> *The warmth in the ring increases slightly.*
>
> **"The items, Morjor. The ones that have gone missing — not just in Braewood, in every court and vault and wayshrine across the kingdoms. They are not being taken for power. They are being spent. Fed to something that has been starved for a very long time."**
>
> *A brief silence.*
>
> **"I can feel him. Not clearly — not yet. But the way you feel a fire in the next room. Something ancient is becoming less dormant. Before the queens meet, I need you to have the remaining pieces in your hands. Not for me. Because if they reach the summit and that summit breaks open—"**
>
> *He stops. Considers.*
>
> **"Get them first. That is all I ask."**
>
> *The warmth fades. The ring is ordinary again. The ship noise returns.*

**What Balthazar is hinting at:**
- The missing magical items across the kingdoms are being **funnelled as sacrificial fuel** toward Asmodeus's revival
- Balthazar can feel **Asmodeus's power growing** — not conscious yet, but stirring
- The summit on April 23rd is either the trigger event or the moment it all becomes unstoppable
- The party needs to get the Trinity pieces before that date

**What Morjor does NOT know:** That Amdir just got a message from Maltrius, the man running the revival operation. The dramatic irony here is significant.

### Bahamut → Varin

> **Trigger:** Also on the approach to shore. The moment the cursed amulet was destroyed or expended in the Spehlina fight, Bahamut's channel reopened. He has been waiting. When Varin rests his hand on his draconic arm or closes his eyes in a moment of quiet, the contact comes.

**What Varin experiences:** The connection is different from before — clearer, steadier. Like a radio that had been full of static finally tuning in. Bahamut's presence is vast but careful; he is being deliberately gentle, aware of what Varin has been through.

**Read-aloud (private to Varin's player):**

> *It is not a dream — you are awake. But your draconic arm warms from the shoulder down, and behind your eyes a pressure builds that is not pain. Then it settles, and something immense and ancient simply... arrives. Like a door you forgot was there swinging open.*
>
> *The presence does not speak in words first. There is just recognition — you, seen completely, with patience rather than judgement. Then the voice, like heat off stone:*
>
> **"I was cut off from you."**
>
> *Not an accusation. A statement of fact, delivered with something that might be grief.*
>
> **"The item you carried — the amulet — it was not cursed by an amateur or by accident. Its source was Asmodeus. His marks are made specifically to silence channels like ours. You carried it longer than most would have. You did not break under it."**
>
> *A warmth moves through the arm, deliberate, from shoulder to fingertip.*
>
> **"For that — for holding fast when I could not reach you, when the corruption was whispering at your prayers and you kept speaking anyway — I name you Varin, Aegis of the Platinum Scale. You are no longer simply Chosen. You are the shield, not merely the sword."**
>
> *A beat.*
>
> **"The Spehlina."**
>
> *He says it plainly, but there is weight in the two words.*
>
> **"It did not deserve what it became. That guardian was old — older than the current kingdoms. What was done to it was a desecration. Grieve it if you need to. It was a tragedy, and the grief is right."**
>
> *Then, carefully:*
>
> **"The amulet was one piece of something larger. Asmodeus does not create single tools. He builds toward something — and what he builds toward now, I can see the shape of it even if I cannot see the whole. The items that have gone missing from shrines and vaults and old hoards across every kingdom — they are not being collected. They are being consumed. Offered up. Each one feeds something that has been diminished for a very long time."**
>
> *The presence does not waver, but there is an edge in it now — not fear, but urgency.*
>
> **"He is not here yet. But he is less absent than he was a year ago. And the day of the summit — April 23rd — is a day of convergence. I cannot see past it clearly. That troubles me."**
>
> *The warmth recedes slowly, like a tide.*
>
> **"Use the arm wisely. You have carried enough."**

**What Bahamut is confirming:**
- The cursed amulet was **Asmodean in origin** — not just generic dark magic
- Varin resisted it; Bahamut recognises this explicitly
- **Varin's new title: Aegis of the Platinum Scale** — a formal divine rank above Chosen (DM note: this is narrative, not a mechanical reward yet — save that for when it feels right)
- The Spehlina's death was a genuine tragedy; Bahamut mourns it without blaming Varin
- The **missing magical items are being sacrificed** toward Asmodeus's revival
- The **April 23rd summit** is a convergence point Bahamut cannot see past — ominous signal

**DM Note:** Varin now knows the exact same shape of the threat as Morjor, via completely different divine channels. Whether they compare notes is up to the players. Neither knows about Amdir's Maltrius message.

**Aegis of the Platinum Scale — Mechanics (DM Reference):**

- **Open Channel:** Any prayer, question, or request Varin speaks aloud (or silently intends as a prayer) is immediately heard by Bahamut. No roll needed. Bahamut may not always respond visibly, but he always hears. Varin will often receive a faint warmth in the arm as acknowledgement — brief, deliberate, unmistakeable.
- **Call Upon Bahamut (Severe Circumstances):** Varin can invoke Bahamut's direct intervention. This draws on the divine charges stored in his draconic arm.

| Severity | What Varin Can Ask For | Arm Charge Cost |
|----------|------------------------|-----------------|
| **Minor** | A subtle sign, a moment of calm, a brief impression of guidance | 0 — Bahamut responds through the channel naturally |
| **Moderate** | Bolstering in combat, a divine shield absorbing one hit, advantage on a saving throw | 1 charge |
| **Severe** | Protection from a killing blow (drop to 1 HP instead of 0), smite a creature of great evil, turn undead area-wide | 3 charges |
| **Crisis** | Direct divine manifestation — Bahamut's presence is felt by everyone in the area; one miracle-scale effect | 5 charges |

- After each invocation above Minor, the arm grows noticeably warmer and the scales creep slightly further than before.
- **Bahamut can also act unasked** — in moments of extreme moral weight (Varin is about to die for another, or a great evil is about to win), the arm may pulse and act without Varin calling for it. This still costs charges.
- Charge total is still 10. The arm still tracks as before — each use is visible in the dimming platinum scale-runes along the forearm.

## Part 2 — Arrival at Dawerton Docks

### Scene Setup

The **Luckpenny** is already docked at Dawerton when the party's ships arrive. The crew is moored and waiting. The quartermaster has been here two hours.

The docks are **busy and tense**. The explosion of the Spehlina sent a shockwave and sound blast that reached shore. People heard it. They don't know what it was — guesses range from a shipwreck, to a volcano, to a magical detonation. There are **clusters of people on the dock wall** staring at the horizon, talking.

**Dock atmosphere:**
- Fishermen are pulled in, refusing to go back out.
- Harbour master is demanding answers from anyone off a ship.
- A dock cleric is leading a prayer group (people genuinely scared).
- Two city guard officers are asking questions at the gate.

**What the party walks into:** Controlled chaos. They're going to get stared at, maybe questioned by the guards. They have the receipts — Wayfarers Accord commission paperwork and the kill/mission confirmation from the senior ally who commissioned the hunt.

### Payment Scene

**Payer:** Garrison Commander **Aldec Tyne** (city military representative, acting for Braewood crown in Dawerton). He is waiting at the dock office — a small stone building by the wharf gate.

- He has heard the explosion. He is pale.
- He is professional but visibly shaken. He wants a full debrief.
- Once the party confirms the Spehlina is dealt with, he produces a sealed chest.

**Total Payment: 10,000 gp**

> *"The crown expected it might cost more lives than that. Consider the remainder a statement of... appreciation."*

He also quietly informs the party that **Queen Maera Brae has personally requested their escort for the summit journey**. She wants them present — not as guards, but as people who understand the full scope of what's coming. Most of her inner circle doesn't know what the party knows.

He says this visit will be arranged within the week. She is busy — do not bother the palace.

## Part 5 — First Contact: Islefield Delegation

### When to Run This

After the party has had time to settle, shop, eat — roughly mid-afternoon or the following morning. They are approached in a semi-public space (the main tavern common room, or a quieter street near the docks).

### The Approach

A small, precise group enters. Four individuals. They are dressed in the muted greens and silvers of **Islefield travelling court dress** — not flashy, but anyone who knows elven fashion reads it immediately. The insignia on the lead figure's inner collar: **a silver kingfisher pin** (Islefield council seal).

The three side guards fan out — not aggressively, but professionally. The main guard stands directly behind the lead.

> *The man who speaks first is an elf. Not young — high elves rarely look young — but there's something sharp and pointed in the way his gaze finds **Amdir** before it finds anyone else. His expression does not change, but something behind his eyes does. Recognition. And something colder.*
>
> **"I am Envoy Tharion Aelindre. I represent Councillor Vel of the Islefield Second Council. I would speak with you — all of you — privately, if the setting allows."**
>
> *He pauses.*
>
> **"Though I admit, I had not expected to find quite so many old faces."**

### Tharion and Amdir — The Tension

Tharion Aelindre was a **junior council aide** during Amdir's banishment proceedings. He was the one who read the formal charges aloud in the Islefield court. He was thorough. Professional. Amdir was arrogant and dismissive throughout, and made a remark about Tharion's family name that was publicly humiliating.

Tharion has not forgotten.

He will not be hostile outright — this is a diplomatic encounter and he is too professional for that. But:
- He addresses Amdir last in the group, deliberately.
- He does not use Amdir's name — he says "and you" or "the Aeristel"
- If Amdir is rude or provocative, Tharion will respond with precise, surgical politeness that cuts deeper than anger
- **If pressed privately**, he will say: *"I have no personal business with you. What you did to your family's name is your own concern."*

**Morjor note:** Tharion addresses Morjor respectfully as *"House Aedrent"*. He knows the name. He does not trust Morjor either, but the etiquette is extended.

### The Offer (What Tharion Says)

Run this as a proper conversation, let players react. Below is the skeleton:

**Opening:**
> *"Islefield has been watching the situation in Braewood with interest. The upcoming summit is a matter both kingdoms have a stake in. We are not here to interfere."*

**The Proposition:**
> *"We have a matter — delicate and time-sensitive — that requires capable and discreet individuals. There is an item of considerable historical significance in the south of Islefield. Southeast of Resross, near the Ironwarf Heights. We believe it may be connected to the Balthazar artefact network."*

*(This is the lie. It is NOT a Balthazar item. They want the Cloak of the Watcher's Veil from the Ossian Vault, and they want to watch the party operate.)*

**On Banishments:**
> *"I am authorised to inform you that any prior travel restrictions pertaining to members of this party are suspended for the duration of this arrangement. The Councillor's seal makes it official."*

*(He says this looking at Amdir. He phrases it as clean and bureaucratic as possible.)*

**Payment:**
He does not name a number immediately. If pressed: **3,000 gp on return with the item**. He will go to 4,000 under significant negotiation.

**What he won't say:**
- That there is no Balthazar connection
- That his team has been tracking the party for three weeks
- That the dungeon is extremely high-level and they want to measure the party's limits
- That his Councillor has their own agenda for the cloak (intelligence tool)

### Persuasion / Insight Notes

| DC | What They Learn |
|----|-----------------|
| 12 (Insight) | He's holding something back — more than diplomat's caution |
| 15 (Insight) | The "Balthazar connection" story doesn't feel fully believed even by him |
| 18 (Insight) | He's been watching you. Not just today — he knew exactly where you'd be |
| 20+ (Investigation/Arcana) | The kingfisher pin is a **Second Council** seal — this isn't the queen, this is a powerful independent faction within Islefield |

### Tharion's Tells Under Pressure
- He straightens the kingfisher pin when uncomfortable
- He shifts topic precisely when someone gets too close to the real motive
- If confronted directly with "this isn't really a Balthazar item" he goes still, then says: *"Our intelligence suggested otherwise. We may have been misled."* (Lie, DC 16 to catch)

## Session End Beat

The session ends when the party makes their decision on the Islefield offer. Either way, they now know:

1. Islefield is watching them
2. The summit is 40 days away
3. The scroll returns in 17 days
4. Amdir has a private, ominous errand that he may or may not share

**If they accept:** Plan travel south. Session 28 begins with the journey toward Resross and the dungeon.  
**If they refuse:** Tharion is gracious. He leaves a sealed card with a contact address in Dawerton. *"If you change your mind within ten days."* The Islefield agenda continues regardless — they'll find another way to test the party.
