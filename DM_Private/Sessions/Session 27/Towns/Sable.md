# Sable — The Elven Haven

**Kingdom:** Islefield  
**Population:** Impossible to count accurately — the permanent settler population is perhaps 800, but the Woods extend for miles and elves live at varying depths. Estimates range from 3,000 to 40,000 depending on how deep you count.  
**Size:** The town of Sable itself is small (a village really). The Sable Woods are vast.  
**Location:** Southwest of Yhey, on the western coast of Islefield. The only non-magical entry into the Sable Woods.  
**Notable For:** Entry to the Sable Woods, elven birthplace, ancestral home, the magical mist barrier, exclusion of outsiders

---

## Overview

The town of Sable is less a settlement and more a **threshold**. It exists at the boundary of the Sable Woods — a vast, ancient forest under a permanent magical mist that functions as a natural barrier. Any non-elf who enters the mist without permission does not emerge on the far side. They emerge from some random edge of the Woods, hours or days later, with no memory of what they saw inside. Not harmed. Just redirected.

The town at the edge — Sable proper — houses the people who manage this boundary. Gateway officials. A small garrison. A few inns for the rare official visitor who has business with the inner forest. A shrine to the forest deities.

**The real Sable is inside the mist.**

---

## The Sable Woods

The forest itself is enormous — an old-growth canopy that hasn't been touched by outside axes since before the Tharnic Empire. The deeper you go, the older the trees. The deeper you go, the more royal the elven lineage of those who live there.

**The depth social hierarchy:**

| Distance from edge | Who lives here |
|-------------------|----------------|
| Outer ring (first mile) | Common elves, those who maintain the border, mixed backgrounds |
| Middle ring (1–5 miles) | Established elven families, lesser noble houses |
| Deep ring (5–15 miles) | High noble bloodlines, those who have never left |
| The Heart | No one has formally mapped it. The royal bloodlines, theoretically. The trees here are old enough that some have never had sunlight reach their roots. |

Elven children born in the Woods spend varying time here:
- Some are born deep and immediately taken to Islefield City to be raised for politics
- Some are raised in the forest for years before entering the world
- A few never leave at all — they become the deep elves, strange and ancient-feeling even among long-lived elves

---

## Amdir and Morjor's Connection

Both Morjor (House Aedrent) and Amdir (House Aeristel) were born in the Sable Woods. They would have been born in the **middle or deep ring** given their bloodlines. They have not been back since exile / departure.

**For Amdir:** Being here — even at the town edge — would be complicated. The mist barrier is tied to elven bloodline recognition. Amdir's bloodline still resonates with the forest. He could enter. But as a banished heir, the deep elves would know he is there. Something would probably come to find him.

**For Morjor:** He left voluntarily. House Aedrent has ties here but he is not banished. He could walk in. The forest might be glad to see him — in the impersonal way old places are glad when something returns.

## The Mist Barrier

**Mechanics for a non-elf or uninvited person:**
- They walk in. It feels like fog — nothing alarming.
- After approximately 15 minutes, they emerge from a random edge of the forest with no memory of the time inside.
- They are not harmed. They are not afraid (the magic is gentle). They simply... cannot access the deep forest.
- Repeated attempts produce the same result.

**How to enter with permission:**
- A note or seal from the Queen of Islefield personally (very rare)
- Escorted by a deep-forest elf with authority to invite (blood-oathed entry)
- Amdir or Morjor vouching directly for a non-elf companion (their bloodline resonance extends to anyone they are physically touching when they enter)

---

## Key Locations in the Town

- **The Boundary Gate** — Not a physical gate. A carved arch between two ancient trees at the forest edge. Sable town is on one side. The mist begins immediately beyond it.
- **The Waymarker Inn** — The only inn in Sable. Clean, simple, elven-run. Serves food that is notably better than you'd expect from a town this small.
- **The Records Office** — Births, deaths, lineage records for all elves born in the Sable Woods are kept here. Access restricted to elves by default. The keeper is an ancient elven woman named **Liriel Osse** — she has been here so long she predates the current monarchies.
- **The Shrine of Deep Roots** — Dedicated to the forest deities (nature pantheon, not Avethis, not Bahamut). Old tradition. The priests here are quiet and strange.

## What the Party Would Find Here

**If they come without elven bloodline guidance:** They can stay in Sable town. They cannot enter the forest. The locals are polite but not interested in conversation. The atmosphere is one of respectful quiet.

**If Amdir or Morjor leads them in:** They enter the outer ring. They are not attacked. But they are noticed. Shapes in the treeline. A sense of being watched (Avethis's dungeon would feel similar — Amdir or Morjor might say so). By the time they reach the middle ring, they will have been acknowledged by a deep forest elder who will want to know why they have returned, and why they brought outsiders.

**The forest remembers them.** This is not metaphorical. The Sable Woods have a collective memory — passed between the oldest trees, translated through the deep elves who have lived among them for centuries. Amdir's departure was noted. His banishment was noted. His return will be noted.

## Liriel Osse — The Records Keeper

An extremely old high elf. She has white-silver hair and eyes that are a very pale grey, nearly colourless. She speaks slowly because she is choosing every word.

She has been the Records Keeper for approximately 340 years.

**What she would tell Amdir:**
- She pulls no record book. She simply remembers. She was there when he was born.
- She knows about the banishment. She does not say it was deserved or undeserved.
- If Amdir asks about his family: *"Your aunt has not spoken your name in formal court in twenty years. That is its own kind of keeping."*
- If Amdir asks if he can go deeper into the forest: *"The forest allows what the forest allows. You carry Aeristel blood. It remembers you. Whether it wants you back — that is a different question."*

**What she would tell Morjor:**
- *"House Aedrent. The wanderer returns."*
- She will not say more unless asked. She is not hostile. She is simply not going to give information for free when patience works just as well.

## Hooks
