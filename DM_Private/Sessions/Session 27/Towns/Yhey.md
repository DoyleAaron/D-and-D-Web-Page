# Yhey — Border Megacity

**Kingdom:** Straddles the Kluimont / Islefield border — administered jointly but culturally Islefield-dominant  
**Population:** ~28,000  
**Size:** Major city (3rd largest city in the border region, top 5 across both kingdoms)  
**Location:** Southeast — sits directly on the territorial line between Kluimont and Islefield, north of the Ironwarf Heights  
**Notable For:** Trade, classist elven supremacy, border tension, largest elven merchant class outside the capital

---

## Overview

Yhey is a city that exists because two powers refused to let the other have it. Centuries of border negotiations produced a compromise: joint administration, dual flag, one city. In practice, Islefield's money won. The elven noble class controls the upper districts, the best contracts, the administrative offices, and the social hierarchy. Kluimont's people built the lower districts, the docks, the foundries — and stay there.

The result is a city with two personalities stacked on top of each other. 

**Upper Yhey** (the Crescent, as locals call it): curved white stone buildings on the elevated plateau, wide promenades, elven architecture blending with carefully tended parks. Quiet. Impeccably maintained. Elves and high-class half-elves only in the nicer establishments without a fight.

**Lower Yhey** (the Basin): a dense, working city that could be any border town. Inns, foundries, markets, dockyards on the inland river. Louder, cheaper, more human. The foundries feed ore from the Heights up to the Crescent workshops. Most of the actual productivity of Yhey happens here.

---

## Culture — Classist Elven Supremacy

This is **not** the violent racism of northern Braewood or the aggressive tribalism of some Kluimont territories. It is something arguably more insidious: **institutional preference** dressed in manners.

- Elves are not hostile to other races. They are **politely dismissive**.
- Humans in the Crescent are tolerated as guests, clients, or servants. They are not seated next to elves at formal dinners. They are addressed last in meetings.
- Half-elves occupy a complicated middle position — accepted but quietly reminded of their "mixed nature" in small ways constantly.
- Dwarves and gnomes are received in the Basin only; the Crescent has informal "tradition" against non-elves in certain guild meetings and social clubs.

**The Kluimont Influence:** The people of Lower Yhey have spent generations watching Kluimont's worst come through — soldiers, prisoners headed to Resross, border disputes. It has made the Yhey population, even the non-elves, somewhat hard. The warmth you'd find in a southern Islefield city is absent here. People are civil. Not warm.

**Phrase the locals use:** *"We know what's on the other side of that border."* Said by elves about humans and by Lower Yhey humans about Kluimont. Everyone in Yhey is looking at someone they consider lesser.

## Key Locations

### The Crescent (Upper District)
- **The Gilded Arch** — Yhey's most prestigious inn. Elves and very wealthy humans only in practice. 30 gp/night for a private room. The dining hall serves six-course meals. If the party wants information about the Islefield council, someone here knows something.
- **The Warden's Hall** — Joint administrative building. Two wings: one flying Islefield's kingfisher, one flying Kluimont's grey tower. The two administrators famously haven't agreed on anything in 11 years.
- **Aelrath's Library** — Private elven library, technically open to the public. In practice, requires a reference from an elven patron to gain access. Contains significant Tharnic-era historical records.

### The Basin (Lower District)
- **The Broken Hitch** — The best tavern in the Basin. Cheap, loud, honest. A Kluimont ex-soldier runs it. Best stew in three kingdoms, according to its regulars.
- **The River Market** — Open-air market along the inner river dock. Runs every day until dusk. Ore, tools, cloth, black-market Tharnic artefacts (if you know who to ask, DC 16 Investigation).
- **The Gate of Two Crowns** — The actual border gate, staffed by both Islefield and Kluimont guards in alternating shifts. They hate each other mildly. Paperwork for border crossing here is extensive but doable.

## Hooks for the Party

- Morjor (House Aedrent) has a name in Yhey. His family had connections to the upper district's merchant elites. He would be recognised and received formally in the Crescent — with the ambivalence due a noble who left the kingdom.
- Amdir's name is known here. Not loudly — but anyone who follows court gossip knows the Aeristel heir who was banished. The reaction would be cool to hostile depending on the elven NPC's loyalty to the crown.
- Tharion's group likely passed through Yhey on their way south. Someone in the River Market remembers them.
- The Warden's Hall has records related to the Ossian Vault — survey documents from the Tharnic era that may point to hidden sections of the dungeon (if the party thought to investigate before entering).
