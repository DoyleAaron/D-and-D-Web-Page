# Islefield Side Guards — Stat Sheets

Three guards. All high elves. All professional. All reporting observations back to Councillor Vel after the mission.

---

## Selen Vareth — Level 6 Fighter (Battle Master)

**Race:** High Elf  
**Role:** Ranged specialist / perimeter control  
**CR:** 4

### Appearance
Lean and quiet. Long dark hair in a braid. Wears scale mail under a travelling cloak. Carries a composite longbow across her back and a shortsword at her hip. She stands slightly apart from the group and watches exits.

**HP:** 52  
**AC:** 16 (scale mail)  
**Speed:** 35 ft  
**Initiative:** +3  
**Proficiency:** +3

**Saves:** STR +6, CON +5  
**Skills:** Athletics +6, Perception +4, Survival +4  
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft, Passive Perception 14

### Actions

**Longsword:** +6 to hit | 1d8+3 slashing | Extra Attack (2 per action)  
**Longbow:** +6 to hit | 1d8+3 piercing | Range 150/600 ft | Extra Attack (2 per action)

### Battle Master Manoeuvres (4 dice — d8)

- **Precision Attack:** Add 1d8 to attack roll (after seeing the roll)
- **Menacing Attack:** On hit, 1d8 bonus damage + DC 14 WIS save or frightened 1 round
- **Trip Attack:** On hit, 1d8 bonus damage + DC 14 STR save or prone (melee only)

### Combat Behaviour
She immediately takes a flanking ranged position. She does not engage in conversation. She watches the room. If combat starts she targets casters and anyone who breaks toward Tharion.

## Donaer Thess — Level 5 Rogue (Scout)

**Race:** High Elf  
**Role:** Advance scout / surveillance  
**Note:** He is the one who was casing the Darwaeton docks last night. If the party rolled well on a rumour check, they may already know this.  
**CR:** 3

### Appearance
Short for an elf. Compact build. Plain traveller's clothing — nothing that reads as aligned to anything. Unassuming face that you forget immediately. Brown hair, green eyes. He is always slightly behind whoever he's standing next to.

| DEX | 18 | +4 |
| CON | 13 | +1 |
| INT | 14 | +2 |
| WIS | 14 | +2 |
| CHA | 11 | +0 |

**HP:** 35  
**AC:** 15 (studded leather)  
**Speed:** 40 ft (Fast Movement, Scout level 3)  
**Initiative:** +4  
**Proficiency:** +3

**Saves:** DEX +7, INT +5  
**Skills:** Stealth +10, Perception +5, Acrobatics +7, Investigation +5, Thieves' Tools +7  
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft, Passive Perception 15

### Sneak Attack
**3d6** added to one attack per turn when advantage or ally adjacent to target.

### Actions

**Shortsword:** +7 to hit | 1d6+4 + 3d6 sneak | Range —  
**Hand Crossbow:** +7 to hit | 1d6+4 + 3d6 sneak | Range 30/120 ft  

### Bonus Action
**Cunning Action:** Dash, Disengage, or Hide (bonus action)

### Scout Feature — Superior Mobility
Extra 10 ft of movement. Moving through difficult terrain costs no extra movement.

### Combat Behaviour
Donaer is not a fighter. He is a scout. If combat starts his priority is to disengage, take a hidden position, and report. He will sneak attack a priority target if Tharion gives a signal, but otherwise he stays out of the fray. If somehow cornered he fights dirty and fast, then runs.

**If confronted about last night's scouting:** He flatly denies it. DC 16 Insight to catch the lie. He is very good at this.

## Guard Group Dynamics in Combat

If combat starts against the party's will (not recommended — this ends the political relationship):

- **Round 1:** Aleth moves to intercept, Selen takes a flanking position, Donaer hides, Welan casts Spiritual Weapon
- **Round 2:** Tharion uses Shadow Step to create distance and reassess, Aleth Divine Smites the biggest threat
- **They will not fight to kill** unless the party attacks lethally first. Non-lethal intent, subdue and withdraw.

| Guard | Primary | Fallback |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Selen | Ranged suppression | Protect Tharion |
| Donaer | Disappear and flank | Extract Tharion |
| Welan | Lockdown with Command/Suggestion | Heal Aleth |
| Aleth | Absorb damage, neutralise biggest threat | Die at post |
