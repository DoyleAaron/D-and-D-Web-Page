# Commander Aleth Varne — Level 10 Paladin

**Race:** High Elf  
**Class:** Paladin — Oath of the Crown  
**Alignment:** Lawful Good (serves Islefield's sovereignty above personal morality)  
**Role:** Tharion's personal guard — the wall  
**CR:** 9

---

## Appearance

Aleth Varne is built like something carved from a mountain and then told to walk. A broad-shouldered high elf in polished silver half-plate (Islefield royal commission — kingfisher engraved on the pauldron). Grey-blond hair, close-cropped. A single thin scar across the left cheekbone from a Kluimont border skirmish twelve years ago. He keeps it unhealed. On purpose.

He speaks rarely. When he does, it counts.

## Features & Abilities

### Aura of Protection (Level 6)
All friendly creatures within 10 ft add **+4** (CHA modifier) to saving throws. This includes himself.

### Aura of Courage (Level 10)
Friendly creatures within 10 ft are **immune to the frightened condition**.

### Extra Attack
2 attacks per action.

### Divine Smite
When he hits with a melee attack, he can expend a spell slot to deal bonus radiant damage:
- 1st slot: +2d8 radiant
- 2nd slot: +3d8 radiant
- 3rd slot: +4d8 radiant
*(+1d8 additional if target is undead or fiend)*

### Lay on Hands
Pool of **50 HP** (healing). Can use as action: touch to heal any amount from pool, or expend 5 to cure disease/poison.

### Spellcasting
**Spell Save DC:** 16 | **Spell Attack:** +8  
**Slots:** 1st ×4, 2nd ×3, 3rd ×2

**Oath of the Crown spells (always prepared):**

### Channel Divinity (1/rest)
- **Champion Challenge:** Each creature of choice within 30 ft must succeed DC 16 CHA save or cannot willingly leave Aleth's presence for 1 minute.
- **Turn the Tide:** As bonus action, any creature within 30 ft with 20 HP or fewer that can hear him regains 1d6+4 HP.

### Sacred Weapon (Channel Divinity)
For 1 minute: longsword attacks count as magical, +4 to attack rolls, sheds bright light 20 ft.

## Combat Tactics

Aleth's job is to ensure Tharion survives and any threat to the envoy is neutralised. He does not initiate violence.

**If threatened:**
1. Steps directly between threat and Tharion
2. Casts *Hold Person* on the most mobile threat
3. Uses *Champion Challenge* to pin anyone trying to flank
4. *Misty Step* to close distance instantly if a caster retreats
5. Divine Smite on anything he cannot afford to let live

**If the party is well over his combat threshold:** He knows. He will say so, calmly. *"You could kill me. Understand that the consequences would follow you into Islefield for the rest of your lives."*

He will not surrender or flee. He will die at his post if it comes to that. He has made peace with this.

## Roleplaying Notes

- Says almost nothing during the diplomatic meeting. He watches.
- Stands slightly behind and to the right of Tharion at all times. Always the same position.
- If someone does something impressively skilled in front of him (combat manoeuvre, display of power), he gives the faintest nod. He respects capability regardless of allegiance.
- He dislikes Amdir only because Tharion dislikes Amdir. He has no personal investment.
- To Morjor he is politely neutral. House Aedrent is respected.
- He served in three border wars. He does not romanticise combat.
- One tell: before he thinks someone is about to do something stupid, he very quietly rolls his right shoulder. Watch for it.
