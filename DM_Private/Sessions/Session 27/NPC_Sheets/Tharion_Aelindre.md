# Envoy Tharion Aelindre — Level 7 Shadow Monk

**Race:** High Elf  
**Class:** Monk — Way of Shadow  
**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral  
**Role:** Islefield Second Council Envoy  
**CR:** 5 (significant threat if combat escalates)

---

## Appearance

Tharion is tall for an elf — lean and deliberate in every movement, like someone who has trained the waste out of every gesture. Silver-grey hair pulled back. Deep violet eyes. Court-level clothing: muted forest greens, silver trim, a pressed inner collar with the Second Council's kingfisher pin. He carries no visible weapon but moves like someone who doesn't need one.

He is impeccably composed. The only crack in that composure is the fraction of a second when his gaze first finds Amdir.

---

## Personal History with Amdir

Tharion Aelindre was a junior council aide during Amdir Aeristel's formal banishment proceedings from Islefield. He was the one tasked with reading the list of charges aloud in the Islefield high court — a humiliating duty that required standing three feet from the accused.

Amdir, mid-proceedings, made a remark about Tharion's family name. It was cutting, precise, and audible to the entire hall. The Aelindre family was a respected but middle-tier noble house — not Aeristel calibre — and Amdir had found the one wound that would sting.

Tharion finished reading the charges without missing a word. He has not forgotten.

## Combat Tactics

Tharion does **not** want to fight. He is here diplomatically. If combat is forced:
1. He immediately drops **Darkness** or **Pass Without Trace** (ki 2)
2. He moves into shadow and uses **Shadow Step** to close or escape
3. He targets whoever poses the most immediate magical threat with Stunning Strike
4. If his principal (Commander Varne) is engaged, he withdraws to guard Tharion — he is not expendable here

**He will not fight to the death** unless the party attacks first without provocation. If reduced to 20 HP he will call for a halt and remind them of the diplomatic consequences.

## Roleplaying Notes

- Addresses Amdir last and only as "the Aeristel" if at all
- Uses silence as a tool — he pauses longer than is comfortable before speaking
- Straightens the kingfisher pin when unsettled
- Never raises his voice
- If Amdir makes a scene: *"I see your exile has not changed your instinct for spectacle."*
- If treated with genuine respect: he thaws — barely, but visibly. He respects competence.
- He has been briefed on Morjor (House Aedrent, former advisor). Respects the pedigree. Trusts the house, not the man.

He keeps a mental tally. When items are gone, they're gone.

### DM Notes — Rationing

- Tharion **will not give out items proactively**. The party must ask, and he judges the request.
- He keeps a **mental count** — if players try to abuse access, he starts locking the bag.
- The **Revivify scroll** and **Potion of Invisibility** require persuasion or a strong in-fiction justification.
- If the party earns his trust over sessions, he becomes more forthcoming.
