# Avethis — The All-Seeing / God of the Undying Gaze

**Domain:** Knowledge, Fate, Observation, Death (passive)  
**Alignment:** True Neutral  
**Symbol:** A single open eye with an **hourglass pupil** (the vertical bar of the hourglass represents that time is witnessed equally in both directions — past and future are the same to Avethis)  
**Worshippers:** Scholars obsessed with forbidden knowledge, seers, certain necromancers, Tharnic-era imperial archivists  
**Attitude:** Avethis does not intervene. It watches. It does not judge. It simply *knows*.

---

## Nature and Personality

Avethis is not a god of death in the way Asmodeus or a death domain cleric understands it. Avethis is interested in the *moment of death* the way a naturalist is interested in a sunset — not because it is good or evil, but because it is *interesting*. It is the transition. The observation of what cannot be observed twice.

Avethis speaks rarely and precisely. It does not embellish. It does not comfort. It answers exactly what is asked, no more. It is not cruel. It is not warm. It is old, and it has seen too much to be surprised, and too much to be bored.

**If Avethis speaks:**
- Short sentences. No wasted words.
- Never a question — only statements.
- Tends to tell people things about themselves they did not realise it knew.
- Never threatens. Observations can be threatening enough.

**Sample dialogue from Avethis:**
- *"You have been watched since before you knew you existed."*
- *"The truth you are avoiding has a shape. You already know what it is."*
- *"I have seen this moment before. From you. From others. The outcome differs less than you would prefer."*
- *"I do not take sides. I record them."*
- *"You will remember this differently than it happened. That is always how it goes."*

---

## The Ossian Vault Connection

Necromancer Valdrek Ossian was a genuine cultist of Avethis — not a fraud. He believed that to witness all things (including death, including the dead) was the highest form of devotion. He built the vault as a **temple of witnessing** — a place where Avethis's presence was so concentrated that time itself blurred, allowing the god to observe more completely.

Ossian's error was not heresy. It was hubris. He believed his devotion would spare him. Avethis does not spare. Avethis **watches**. Including watching Ossian's death. Including watching the party's arrival now.

## The Pedestal of Avethis (Final Room)

**Location:** Behind the sealed door in Ossian's chamber  
**Room description:**

> *A circular room. The walls are covered floor to ceiling in carved eyes — all sizes, all angles, all slightly different. Some are closed. Some weep. Most are open. At the centre: a single pedestal of black obsidian, waist-height. Inlaid in its top surface: a single glowing eye, carved in pale amethyst, its pupil in the shape of an hourglass.*
>
> *It is quiet here in a way that feels deliberate. Like the room is listening.*

The pedestal is **the direct point of contact** between the physical world and Avethis's observational field in this location. Ossian built it so his most devoted followers could petition for a Vision — a single true sight, granted once.

### Using the Pedestal

A creature that touches the eye and speaks a request (aloud or in their mind) receives **one Vision**:
- They may see any single **true memory** — their own or another's
- Or they may ask to **see a truth** (one current fact about any person or situation, as *Commune* but more specific)
- The vision lasts approximately 10–15 seconds (in-game) but feels complete

**After using the pedestal:** A **symbol burns into the creature's skin** — the hourglass-pupil eye. This is permanent. Cannot be removed by *Remove Curse* or similar. It is not a curse. It is a mark.

**Mechanical effect of the mark:**
- +5 bonus to passive Insight (permanently)
- Once per day, the marked character can **instantly know if a statement made to them directly is a blatant lie** — not a half-truth, not an omission, but a deliberate falsehood. (DM adjudication on edge cases.)
- Worshippers of Avethis — if they ever meet any — recognise the mark immediately and treat the character with reverence

**Narrative effect:** The marked character is now *known to Avethis*. They exist in its record. This has no immediate mechanical weight, but it means Avethis has awareness of them specifically going forward — relevant for future plot if desired.

## Varin's Special Interaction (CRITICAL — READ CAREFULLY)

**When Varin touches the pedestal:**

The amethyst eye does not glow white. It **explodes** — not destructively, but in a burst of blinding light that fills the room. Varin vanishes from the physical world for approximately 30 seconds of real time (longer in-game). The other players see nothing. He is simply gone.

### The Liminal Realm — Between Divine Presences

Varin arrives in a space that is not a space. It looks like standing at the centre of an enormous eye — the iris around him, the pupil a dark hole below his feet, the world above the open white of a sclera. There is no horizon. There is no gravity but he stands anyway.

**Two presences are here.**

They are not entirely manifest — approaching a full divine presence would unmake him. But they are *close*. Like hearing two voices through a wall.

**BAHAMUT** (to Varin's left):
- Felt more than seen. A heat like standing near a forge. The smell of clean metal and old rain.
- *"Varin. This place was not made for my Chosen. Step back."*
- If Varin asks questions: Bahamut is brief, careful. He does not want Varin here long.
- Bahamut seems **wary** of Avethis in a way that is deeply unusual — a god of order and justice, uncertain.

**AVETHIS** (everywhere and nowhere, not a location — just present):
- Not felt. Seen — barely. A sensation of being looked at from every angle simultaneously.
- *"The Platinum Dragon's chosen. I have watched you since the arm changed. You are interesting in the way that almost nothing still is."*
- If Varin engages: Avethis speaks carefully, truthfully, in ways that are slightly too on-the-nose.
- It may tell him **one true thing** — one piece of knowledge he genuinely does not have, chosen by the DM. Suggestions:
  - Something about the summit that even Morjor doesn't know
  - Something about Amdir's current situation (the Maltrius plan)
  - A glimpse of Maltrius's goal and the fireball ritual
  - Something about Varin's arm and its final state

**The two gods do not fight.** But they are in tension — Bahamut's active presence and Avethis's passive presence do not naturally coexist. The liminal space distorts slightly. Varin can feel both pulling in different directions — not on him, exactly, but on the space itself.

Eventually **Bahamut says**: *"We are done here."* And Varin is expelled.

### Return

Varin reappears in the physical room. The pedestal is dark — the amethyst eye cracked down the middle. It will not function again.

**Varin has no mark burned into his skin.** Avethis does not mark Bahamut's Chosen without his patron's consent. Instead, Varin has a different souvenir — a **single piece of knowledge** (the DM-chosen truth from above), and an understanding that he has been placed in the record of something vast and ancient and utterly disinterested in whether he lives or dies.

## Avethis and Bahamut — The Relationship

These two gods have no natural conflict. Bahamut is a god of justice, order, and protection. Avethis is a god of observation without judgment.

The tension comes from what Avethis *knows*. It has seen Bahamut's timeline. It has watched every choice Bahamut's Chosen have made. It has seen the futures — plural. It will not tell Bahamut anything (gods cannot be *told* things by Avethis — only mortals can receive Visions). But Bahamut is aware that Avethis is watching, and that awareness is uncomfortable for a god who believes in the value of **action**.

Avethis does not act. It watches. To Bahamut, a being who watches suffering without acting is a challenge to his entire nature.

Neither will say this aloud in front of Varin. But it is there.
