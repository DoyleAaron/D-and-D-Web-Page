## Scene 1 — Arriving at Shore

The party arrives at the Darwaeton docks after the journey back. The harbour is busy — Darwaeton is always busy — but there are a few groups who are clearly waiting rather than passing through. Other people with business today.

> *The dock smells like salt and rope and money. There are ships being loaded three berths down, arguments about weights at the exchange, a harbour guard collecting a toll with the resignation of a man who has done this thirty thousand times. And then there are the people who are not doing any of that. Just standing. Watching the water. Watching you.*

**Time pressure to establish:** Remind the party (or let them calculate) that time dilation in the dungeon means time has passed outside. The scroll commissioned from [Liora / her replacement] returns March 31st — 17 days out. The Summit is April 23rd — 40 days out.

## Scene 2 — The Payment

**Aldec Tyne** (or his agent) meets the party. Private side room at the dockside warehouse — he does not do payments in the open.

**Payment: 10,000 gp** for completion of the Spehlina mission. Delivered as 10 Letters of Credit drawn on the Braewood Royal Vault, each worth 1,000 gp, in a leather case sealed with the Braewood crown stamp.

> *He doesn't count them out theatrically. He hands the case, meets eyes with whoever receives it, nods. "The queen will want to speak with you. I'll arrange it. Rest first."*

**He also mentions:** The Queen's request for the party to escort her to the Summit. He frames it as an honour, not an order. He expects them to say yes.

## DM Reminders for This Session

- **Time dilation:** Calculate how many days passed outside while the party was in the dungeon. 1 hour inside = 1 day outside. If the party spent ~8 hours in the dungeon, 8 days have passed outside. Today would then be later than March 14th — adjust if needed.
- **The Cloak:** Tharion expects delivery. The party might want to read it, copy it, or keep it. Let this tension play out before the official handover.
- **The Codex:** If they found it and studied it — they now know that **Gaia** (the maker named throughout the Codex) is **Balthazar**. The archmage they've been hunting is the earth itself, wearing a name. This is a big moment. Give it space.
- **Scroll of Speak with Dead:** If they used it on Ossian in the dungeon, they have his answers (see Loot_Guide.md). If not, they still have the scroll and could use it later on any corpse.
