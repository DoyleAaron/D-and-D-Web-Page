## Magical Items and Consumables

- **Potion of Greater Healing** — Drinking it restores **4d4+4 HP**.

- **+1 Dagger** — Magical weapon. Gives **+1 to attack rolls** and **+1 to damage rolls**. Damage is **necrotic** instead of normal piercing damage.

- **Psionic Shard** — Magical crafting component. Can be used toward creating a **Headband of Intellect** or similar mind/intellect-based magic item. No immediate combat effect by itself unless crafted into something.

- **Bead of Force** — Magical, single use. As an action, throw it up to 60 ft. On impact, it explodes in a 10 ft radius. Creatures in the area make a **DC 15 DEX save**. On a failed save, they take **5d4 force damage** and are trapped inside a sphere of force for **1 minute**. The sphere blocks damage from outside.

- **Scroll of Detect Magic** — Single use. Casts **Detect Magic**. For up to **10 minutes** with concentration, you sense magic within 30 ft and can see magical auras around visible objects or creatures.

- **Scroll of Identify** — Single use. Casts **Identify**. Touch one magic item or magically affected creature/object. You learn its properties, how to use it, whether it requires attunement, and whether spells are affecting it.

- **Scroll of Comprehend Languages** — Single use. Casts **Comprehend Languages**. For **1 hour**, you understand the literal meaning of spoken languages you hear and written languages you touch. It does not let you speak those languages.

- **Dust of Dryness** — Magical consumable. Contains **1d6+4 pinches**. One pinch can absorb up to **15 gallons of water** into a small pellet. The pellet can later be thrown up to 60 ft to release the water. Against a watery creature, one pinch forces a **DC 11 CON save**; on a failure, the creature takes **10d6 necrotic damage** and is paralysed for 1 minute.

## Lore Items and Documents

- **Jade eye stamp** — Ossian's personal seal stamp. It is not a weapon and does not have a known magical effect. It can be used to mark wax or documents with Ossian's open-eye symbol. It may matter as proof of authorship, cult connection, or collector/lore evidence.

- **Room 14 table document** — crumbling letter in Ossian's hand:

> *"Note, for a record I will not be here to maintain:*
>
> *The vault is prepared. Four sentinels. One guardian. One door. The Eye watches everything in these walls — it always has, but now it watches something I have placed here specifically.*
>
> *Anyone who reaches the inner chamber will be marked. Not by me. I have no mark to give. By the Eye itself — which sees, and in seeing, changes what it has seen. I have been precise with myself about whether this is a curse. It is not a curse. It is a record. They will carry the fact of having been witnessed.*
>
> *They will never be deceived again. Not by others. Not by themselves. I am not certain this is comfortable. It is accurate."*

- **Room 16 production tablet** — tablet on the east furnace wall:

> *"Production output Month 7: 340 units iron ore. 12 units silver ore. Workers: 28. Deaths this month: 3. Overseer Brace notes workers are reporting sounds from the lower eastern wing. Investigation pending."*

- **Room 16 egress scratch mark** — three lines and a westward arrow scratched into the southwest wall. It appears to be a Tharnic emergency egress mark pointing toward a hidden exit route.
