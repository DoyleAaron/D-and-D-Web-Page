# Vasskren the Undying Sentinel — Character Sheet (Final Boss)

**Location:** Grand Hall (centre dungeon) — rises after Ossian is defeated
**CR:** 15 | **XP:** 13,000
**Type:** Undead Construct (Avethis-Anchored)
**This fight is optional** — see the Vasskren decision in the progress tracker.

> *The floor of the Grand Hall cracks in four places at once. Stone slabs push upward and topple as something below pushes through. What rises is not one creature — it is assembled. A spine twenty feet long. Ribcage wide as a doorway. Three arms pulling itself out of the stone like a man climbing out of cold water. A skull that turns, slowly, and stops when it finds you.*

---

## Multi-Limb Combat System

Vasskren has **five parts**, each with its own HP pool. **Each arm rolls its own initiative** at combat start. Players may target parts individually (torso, left claw, right maul, spectral arm, skull).

**Kill condition:** Torso reaches 0 HP, OR all 3 arms are destroyed (Vasskren collapses even if the torso has HP — it cannot support itself). Destroying limbs weakens him but does not kill him.

## Tactical Phases

**Phase 1 (all limbs):** Left Claw grapples the tank; Right Maul hits clusters; Spectral Arm phases onto kiters; Skull uses Death Wail on 4+ in range; Torso slams. No Legendary Actions until at least one arm is destroyed.

**Phase 2 (1–2 limbs down):** Torso uses Limb Emergency on the most critical destroyed limb. Remaining arms turn erratic. Bone Collapse at 150 HP.

**Phase 3 (3+ limbs down):** Torso is vulnerable. Skull uses Necrotic Gaze every round. If only skull + torso remain, the skull begins visually weeping.

## Death Scene

> *The last piece of it falls. The sound is not metal or stone — it is a hundred bones settling at once, the way a forest sounds when the last tree falls. The eye carvings along the Grand Hall begin to glow again — all of them. Clean, pale white. Watching. Still. Five fragments remain on the floor, each pulsing faintly. You know, without knowing how, where they belong.*

All **five Shards** must be placed into the Sealed Door's five indentations in Room 7 to open the way to Room 17.
