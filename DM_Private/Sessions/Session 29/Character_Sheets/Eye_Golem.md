**Location:** Room 4 — Eye Golem Chamber (last remaining golem)
**CR:** 12 | **XP:** 8,400
**Type:** Construct (Divine — Avethis-Touched)

## Traits

**Avethis's Watch:** The golem **cannot be surprised**. It cannot be blinded. Invisibility and illusions do not function against it.

**Gaze of the Watcher:** The golem's facing matters. Creatures inside the cone of its open chest-eye are subject to Full Witness. Creatures outside the cone are not affected, even if they can see the golem.

---

## Actions

**Multiattack:** 2 attacks per action.

**Stone Fists:** +11 to hit | 2d12+6 bludgeoning | Reach 5 ft

**Eye Ray (Recharge 5–6):** Ranged Spell Attack +9, range 120 ft.
Hit: 6d8 radiant damage.
Target: DC 17 WIS save or **frightened** of the golem for 1 minute (save ends at end of each turn).

**If the Eye Looks at Them:** Anyone caught in the chest-eye cone rolls a **DC 17 Wisdom save**. On a failure, they are **stuck/incapacitated for 1 turn** — until the end of the golem's next turn.

**Full Witness (Recharge 5–6):** The eye in its chest flares white in a **60 ft cone** in the direction the golem is facing. Each creature in the cone must make a **DC 17 WIS save** or be **incapacitated** until the end of the golem's next turn, completely overwhelmed by the sense of being fully, utterly seen.

The cone remains obvious until the start of the golem's next turn: pale white light, all eye carvings in that direction open and tracking. Characters can work around it by moving behind the golem, spreading out, breaking line of sight, or using cover.

---

## Player Counterplay — Work Around the Cone

- The eye's effect is positional, not visual consent. Closing eyes or looking away does **not** prevent Full Witness.
- The safe play is to stay out of the cone, get behind the golem, use pillars/cover, or force it to choose between clustered targets.
- **Varin is immune** — Bahamut protects him from the Eye's incapacitating witness. A DC 14 Insight check reveals the golem seems to recognise him (Bahamut's divine aura). It does not treat him differently in combat.

---

## Tactics

Moves to the centre of the room. Turns slowly and deliberately, trying to catch the largest cluster in its Full Witness cone. Uses Eye Ray on targets outside the cone or on anyone using cover too effectively. It does not move erratically — the players should be able to read its facing and reposition around it.
