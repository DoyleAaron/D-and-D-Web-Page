## Special Traits

**Legendary Resistance (2/day):** If Ossian fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

**Magic Resistance:** Advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects.

**Borrowed Witness — Cloak of the Watcher's Veil (1/day):** At dawn, or when the Cloak is first attuned that day, the wearer chooses one creature sample in the Cloak's hidden pouch. The choice lasts until the next dawn and cannot be changed or ended early. Ossian's current sample is an Avethis-touched eye-stone/golem fragment, so for the current day magic perceives him partly as the Eye Golem for damage vulnerabilities, resistances, and immunities only. Radiant/holy damage does **not** trigger his normal undead vulnerability while this remains active. If the Cloak is removed or the sample is taken, Ossian becomes **vulnerable to radiant/holy damage** like other undead at this table.

**Rejuvenation:** If destroyed and his heart is not destroyed separately, he reforms in 24 hours. His heart is a small black iron reliquary behind the tapestry on the altar's back face (DC 14 Investigation). Destroying the heart prevents rejuvenation.

### Cantrip Quick Use

**Thaumaturgy:** No damage. Use for menace and control of the room: booming voice, flickering lights, slamming doors, harmless tremors, whispers from the altar, or his dead eyes flaring open. Good for intimidation or showing divine/undead presence.

**Chill Touch:** Ranged spell attack **+8**, range 120 ft. Hit: **2d8 necrotic**, and the target **cannot regain HP** until the start of Ossian's next turn. If the target is undead, it also has disadvantage on attack rolls against Ossian until the end of Ossian's next turn.

**Guidance:** Touch a willing creature, concentration up to 1 minute. The target adds **1d4 to one ability check**. Mostly useful before combat, for ritual work, or if Ossian directs a Wight/servant to perform a task.

### Disease / Curse Quick Use

**Mummy Rot from Rotting Fist:** On hit, target makes **DC 16 CON**. On a failure, they are cursed with Mummy Rot. They cannot regain HP, and every 24 hours their maximum HP drops by **10 (3d6)**. If maximum HP reaches 0, they die and turn to dust. Cure: *Remove Curse*.

**Contagion:** Make a **+8 melee spell attack**. On a hit, the target is **poisoned**. At the end of each of that target's turns, it makes a **DC 16 CON save**. Track successes and failures separately:

- **3 successes:** the spell ends.
- **3 failures:** the target is diseased for 7 days. The poisoned condition ends, but the disease remains.

Useful disease choices for Ossian:

| Slimy Doom | Disadvantage on CON checks and CON saves; when damaged, target is stunned until end of its next turn. | Very dangerous boss move; use if you want real pressure. |
| Blinding Sickness | Target is blinded and has disadvantage on WIS checks/saves. | Casters or anyone relying on sight. |
| Mindfire | Disadvantage on INT checks/saves and confused behavior in combat. | Wizards or tactical casters. |

## Actions

**Multiattack:** 2 attacks with Rotting Fist. Can substitute one for a spell.

**Rotting Fist:** +8 to hit, reach 5 ft, one target.
Hit: (2d6+4) bludgeoning + (6d6) necrotic. Target: DC **16 CON** or **Mummy Rot.**

**Mummy Rot:** A cursed creature cannot regain HP and its maximum HP drops by 10 (3d6) every 24 hours. If max HP hits 0, it dies and turns to dust. Cure: *Remove Curse* (Lesser Restoration does not work).

- **Blinding Dust (2):** 5 ft radius around Ossian. DC 14 CON or blinded until end of next turn.

- **Channel Negative Energy (3):** Each non-undead within 60 ft loses 10 (3d6) HP; each undead within 60 ft regains 10 HP.

## Tactics

**Phase 1 (above 50 HP):** Opens with *Spirit Guardians*, uses Blasphemous Word to push frightened creatures into the aura. Wights focus isolated targets.

**Phase 2 (below 50 HP):** Drops *Spirit Guardians*, casts *Blight* on the most damaged PC, uses Channel Negative Energy (legendary) to heal himself while draining the party. *Contagion* on a martial character who relies on ability checks.

**Ossian never retreats.** He holds Room 7 and does not pursue fleeing creatures.

**Turn Undead:** Ossian makes a CHA save (+7). On a fail he is turned — he will spend Legendary Resistance to avoid it.

---

## On Defeat

HP reaches 0 → he collapses, all active Wights crumble, and the Eye Door from Room 7 swings open. Roughly 1 minute later (if not already triggered), the Grand Hall responds and **Vasskren** begins to rise.
