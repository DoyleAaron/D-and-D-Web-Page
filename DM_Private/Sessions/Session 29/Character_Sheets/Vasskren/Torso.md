# Vasskren — Torso

**Door Core:** Shard of Vision (#4)  
**Role:** Main body, collapse trigger, primary melee pressure

## Stats

**HP:** 240  
**AC:** 18 (divine bone plating)  
**Speed:** 30 ft  
**Initiative:** +4

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 24 (+7) | 10 (+0) | 22 (+6) | 5 (-3) | 14 (+2) | 1 (-5) |

**Saves:** STR +12, CON +11  
**Damage Immunities:** Poison, necrotic, psychic  
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralysed, petrified, poisoned  
**Magic Resistance:** Advantage on saves vs spells  
**Senses:** Darkvision 120 ft, Truesight 30 ft | Passive Perception 12

## Traits

**Legendary Resistance (2/day):** If Vasskren fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

**Bone Collapse (Triggered, once):** At **150 HP or below**, the rib cage fragments outward. All within 20 ft: DC 17 CON, fail 4d8 piercing, success half.

## Actions

**Spine Slam (2/action):** +12 to hit | 2d12+7 bludgeoning | Reach 15 ft

## On Destruction

If the torso reaches 0 HP, Vasskren dies/collapses.

**Drops:** Shard of Vision (#4 — pale white core set behind the sternum, carved with a tiny eye)
