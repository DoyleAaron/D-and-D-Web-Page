# Vasskren — Skull

**Door Core:** Shard of Witnessing (#5)  
**Role:** Fear, necrotic pressure, controls legendary actions

## Stats

**HP:** 120  
**AC:** 16  
**Initiative:** +3  
**Acts:** Independently

## Actions

**Death Wail (Recharge 5-6):** 60 ft cone: DC 17 WIS, fail frightened 1 minute (repeat save) + deafened.

**Necrotic Gaze:** +9 to hit, range 80 ft | 4d8 necrotic

## On Destruction

Vasskren loses Legendary Actions and Truesight for the rest of combat.

**Drops:** Shard of Witnessing (#5 — violet gem in forehead, warm, humming. Only drops when the skull is destroyed.)
