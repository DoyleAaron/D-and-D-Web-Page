# Vasskren — Left Claw Arm

**Door Core:** Shard of Rending (#1)  
**Role:** Grapples and holds targets in place

## Stats

**HP:** 100  
**AC:** 15  
**Initiative:** +2

## Actions

**Claw Rake:** +11 to hit | 2d8+7 slashing | Reach 10 ft

**Grapple:** On a hit, DC 16 STR or grappled (Escape DC 16 STR). While grappled, target takes 2d8+7 slashing automatically at the start of its turn.

## On Destruction

Detonates. 10 ft radius: DC 14 DEX, fail 2d8 piercing, success half.

**Drops:** Shard of Rending (#1 — red-tinged bone, warm)
