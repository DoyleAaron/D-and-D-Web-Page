# Vasskren — Spectral Arm

**Door Core:** Shard of Withering (#3)  
**Role:** Backline pressure, necrotic burst, phasing movement

## Stats

**HP:** 90  
**AC:** 13  
**Initiative:** +3  
**Trait:** Partially translucent, phases in and out

## Actions

**Wither Touch:** +9 to hit | 2d10 necrotic | Reach 10 ft. DC 15 CON or disadvantage on attacks until end of next turn.

**Necrotic Burst (Recharge 5-6):** 20 ft radius: DC 15 CON, fail 5d8 necrotic, success half. 

## Movement

**Phase-Through:** Ignores opportunity attacks; can move through walls, but cannot attack from inside them.

## On Destruction

15 ft radius: DC 14 CON or frightened 1 round.

**Drops:** Shard of Withering (#3 — black bone, leaves frost)
