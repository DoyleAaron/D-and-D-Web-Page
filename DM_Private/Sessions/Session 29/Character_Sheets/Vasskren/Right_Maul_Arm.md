# Vasskren — Right Maul Arm

**Door Core:** Shard of Breaking (#2)  
**Role:** Knockback, prone, cluster disruption

## Stats

**HP:** 100  
**AC:** 15  
**Initiative:** +1

## Actions

**Crushing Blow:** +11 to hit | 3d8+7 bludgeoning | Reach 10 ft. On hit: DC 17 STR or knocked back 15 ft and prone.

**Shockwave (Recharge 6):** 30 ft cone: DC 17 STR, fail 4d6 bludgeoning + prone, success half no prone.

## On Destruction

Falls and pins. DC 14 DEX or restrained until a DC 16 STR check (action).

**Drops:** Shard of Breaking (#2 — iron-grey bone, heavy)
