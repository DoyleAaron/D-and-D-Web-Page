# Vasskren — Part Overview

Use this as the quick table during the fight. Each body part has its own file for full mechanics.

## Fight Structure

Vasskren has **five targetable parts**:

| Part | Init | HP | AC | Main Role | Door Core |
|------|-----:|---:|---:|-----------|-----------|
| [Torso](Torso.md) | +4 | 240 | 18 | Main body, collapse trigger, melee pressure | Shard of Vision (#4) |
| [Skull](Skull.md) | +3 | 120 | 16 | Fear cone, gaze, controls legendary actions | Shard of Witnessing (#5) |
| [Spectral Arm](Spectral_Arm.md) | +3 | 90 | 13 | Necrotic burst, phases through terrain | Shard of Withering (#3) |
| [Left Claw Arm](Left_Claw_Arm.md) | +2 | 100 | 15 | Grapples and holds targets in place | Shard of Rending (#1) |
| [Right Maul Arm](Right_Maul_Arm.md) | +1 | 100 | 15 | Knockback, prone, cone shockwave | Shard of Breaking (#2) |

## Kill Condition

Vasskren is defeated if either:

- The **Torso** reaches 0 HP.
- All **3 arms** are destroyed, causing the body to collapse even if the torso still has HP.

Destroying the **Skull** does not kill Vasskren, but it removes Legendary Actions and Truesight for the rest of combat.

Legendary Actions escalate only after arms are destroyed: 0 at the start, 1 after the first arm falls, and 2 after the second arm falls.

## DM Flow

- **Phase 1:** All parts active. Claw grapples, Maul disrupts clusters, Spectral pressures backline, Skull frightens, Torso slams. No legendary actions yet.
- **Phase 2:** 1-2 limbs down. Torso uses Limb Emergency once, if available. Bone Collapse triggers at 150 torso HP.
- **Phase 3:** 3+ limbs down. Torso becomes the clear focus. Skull uses Necrotic Gaze every round if still active.

## Door Cores

All five shards are needed for the sealed Room 7 door unless the party uses Ossian's ceremonial iron key fallback.
