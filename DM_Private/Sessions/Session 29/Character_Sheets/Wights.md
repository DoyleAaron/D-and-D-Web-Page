# Ossian's Followers — Wights

**Location:** Room 7 — Ossian's Chamber
**Count:** 6
**CR:** 3 each | **XP:** 700 each | **Total XP:** 4,200
**Type:** Undead
**Trigger:** Activate when Ossian awakens.

These are the preserved worshippers arranged around Ossian's sarcophagus. They look reverent until the trigger happens. Then all six animate at once.

## Attacks

**Longsword:** +4 to hit | 1d8+2 slashing

**Life Drain:** +4 to hit | 2d6+2 necrotic

Target's maximum HP is reduced by the necrotic damage taken until the target finishes a long rest. If this reduces a creature's maximum HP to 0, the creature dies and rises as a Wight.

---

## Tactics

- They activate together when Ossian is triggered.
- They spread pressure rather than all dogpiling one target.
- They focus isolated or frightened targets first.
- If Ossian uses *Spirit Guardians*, they try to keep enemies inside the aura.
- If Ossian is defeated, all active Wights crumble immediately with no save.
