# Room 4 — Notes & Documents

## Stone Marker (near arch entrance — DC 12 Arcana)

Ossian's hand:

> *"Before this, I believed observation was an abstract principle: a mind noticing a thing. I was wrong. Observation is not only thought. It is pressure. It is contact. It is one of the physical conditions by which the universe decides what is true."*

---

## Time Dilation Marker (east wall — DC 13 Arcana or Religion)

Ossian's hand, written after repeated measurements:

> *"I understand the time distortion now. Avethis does not only watch time pass. Avethis's watching changes how time passes. Inside, an hour remains an hour. Outside, the world moves on for a day. Observation is not a side effect. Observation is the mechanism."*

---

## Second Marker (central platform — Investigation DC 15)

Ossian's hand, smaller script — written later than the first:

> *"The Eye in this room is not the Eye beside Ossian's chamber. One is mine. One is not. If you are reading this and you have already been to the sealed south chamber, you understand the distinction. If you have not — go. Then come back and read this again. It will mean something different."*
