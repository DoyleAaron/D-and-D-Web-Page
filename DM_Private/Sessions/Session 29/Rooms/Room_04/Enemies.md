# Room 4 — Enemies

## 2 × Weeping Angel

**CR:** 5 | **HP:** 85 each | **Found:** Approach corridor

They move only when unobserved. If all players watch one, the other can advance.

- **Split attention is the whole threat.** Any round a player is not directly observing one angel, that angel can move.
- **Torch out / eyes down:** Angels move freely.
- On a grapple, they send a target back in time — target vanishes and reappears at the dungeon entrance 1 minute later.
- Valid solution: walk past while keeping both watched. Make it tense.

## Eye Golem

**CR:** 12 | **HP:** 170 | **Full stats:** ../../Character_Sheets/Eye_Golem.md

**Full Witness Cone (Recharge 5–6):** 60 ft cone in the direction the golem is facing. Creatures in the cone make a DC 17 WIS save or become incapacitated until the end of the golem's next turn.

**Counterplay:** Closing eyes or looking away does not stop it. Move out of the cone, get behind the golem, use cover, or spread out.

**Varin is immune** — Bahamut protects him from the incapacitating witness.
