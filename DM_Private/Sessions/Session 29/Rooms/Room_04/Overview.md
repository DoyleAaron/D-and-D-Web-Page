# Room 4 — Eye Golem Chamber

**Enemies:** 2 Weeping Angels (approach corridor) + Eye Golem — stat sheet in ../../Character_Sheets/Eye_Golem.md
**Door:** Iron lock — Thieves' Tools DC 17 or DC 20 STR | Key: the party has the Eye Golem key (from Room 1)
**Exits:** Deepest corridor

Circular chamber. Every wall surface carved with open eyes — floor to ceiling, hundreds, thousands. All oriented differently but all at rest. The air is completely still. The silence is unusual — no dust movement, no settling sound.

---

## Key Mechanic — Full Witness Cone (Eye Golem)

**Recharge 5–6.** The golem's chest-eye fully opens in a **60 ft cone** in the direction it is facing. Every creature in the cone makes a **DC 17 WIS save** or becomes **incapacitated** until the end of the golem's next turn.

- **Counterplay:** Closing eyes or looking away does **not** stop it. Players need to move out of the cone, get behind the golem, use cover, or spread out so the golem cannot catch everyone.
- **Telegraph:** The cone is visible as pale white light; every carved eye in that direction opens and tracks.
- **Varin is immune** — Bahamut protects him from the incapacitating witness.
