# Room 4 — Loot

## Stone Plinth (centre — no check after the golem is defeated)

- **Tome of Avethis** — large, iron-bound, Old Elvish. DC 16 to read without aid. Lore on Avethis, the watching principle, and the cult's founding.
- **220 gp** in old Tharnic gold, wrapped in cloth.

### Tome of Avethis — Mechanics

**Type:** Wondrous item, rare  
**Appearance:** 50-page illuminated manuscript with unsettling imagery. Single open eyes appear in the margins of every page. The eyes seem to follow the reader peripherally.

**Study Benefit:** If a character spends **8 hours studying** the Tome, they gain **advantage on Arcana and History checks** for 30 days. Studying it again refreshes the duration.

**Side Effect:** After studying, the reader makes a **DC 14 WIS save**. On a failure, they become mildly fixated on knowing things they should not know for 24 hours. This is DM flavour only: compulsive investigation, probing questions, staring too long at symbols, etc. No mechanical penalty.

**Value:** Significant to theologians, ancient historians, Avethis cult researchers, and the Second Council.

## Golem Drop

- **Shard of Discernment** — faintly glowing white stone, cool and smooth, pulses ~once every 2 seconds. Value 500 gp as a component.

### Shard of Discernment — Item Mechanics

**Type:** Wondrous item, very rare  
**Appearance:** Faintly glowing white stone, cool and smooth. Its light pulses slowly, about once every two seconds.

Holding it, a character can **1/day** sense whether one creature within 30 ft is being actively deceptive. Treat this as *Zone of Truth* for one creature, instantaneous: the holder feels the truth-pressure rather than hearing a clear yes/no from a god. No charges to track; it recharges at dawn.

**Warning:** Selling it in Islefield attracts Second Council attention immediately.
