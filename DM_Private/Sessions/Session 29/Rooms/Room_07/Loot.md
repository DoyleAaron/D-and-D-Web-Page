## On / In the Sarcophagus (TRIGGERS the fight if touched directly — use Mage Hand or cloth)

- **Cloak of the Watcher's Veil** — the mission objective, around Ossian's neck. DC 12 Arcana to sense magic.

### Cloak of the Watcher's Veil — Mechanics

**Type:** Wondrous item, rare  
**Attunement:** Required  
**Appearance:** Deep grey, almost black cloak. Inner lining is silver-grey with tiny woven eyes visible only in dim light. Wide hood. Bone clasp shaped like an hourglass. Very old — pre-Tharnic origin.

**Properties:**
- **Watcher's Sight:** Advantage on Perception checks involving watching or observing with sight. Does not help hearing-based checks.
- **Peripheral Awareness:** While wearing it and conscious, you cannot be surprised.
- **Fold Into Shadow (1/day):** Bonus action. While in dim light or darkness, become heavily obscured. No Stealth check required. Ends when you move or take an action.
- **The Watcher Knows (1/week):** Action. Choose one creature within 60 ft. For 1 minute, you know that creature's exact position even if it is invisible or behind cover.
- **Borrowed Witness (1/day):** At dawn, or when the Cloak is first attuned that day, the wearer chooses one creature sample kept in the Cloak's hidden inner pouch: bone, scale, tooth, hair, blood, stone fragment, etc. Until the next dawn, magic perceives the wearer as that creature for the purpose of **damage vulnerabilities, resistances, and immunities** only. This choice cannot be changed or ended early. The wearer gains that creature's magical damage weaknesses and protections, but does not gain its AC, HP, attacks, senses, condition immunities, movement, legendary traits, or creature type.

**Ossian's Current Sample:** Ossian has an Avethis-touched eye-stone/golem fragment in the pouch. For the current day, magic perceives him partly as the Eye Golem, so radiant/holy damage does **not** trigger his normal undead vulnerability. This cannot be changed until the next dawn. If the Cloak is removed or the sample is taken, Ossian is vulnerable to radiant/holy damage like other undead at this table.

**DM Note:** The Islefield Second Council wants this for political surveillance and leverage at the summit.

## Reliquary (behind north tapestry — Perception DC 15 / Thieves' Tools DC 15 / break AC 13 HP 15)

- Destroying the reliquary **permanently prevents Ossian's rejuvenation.**

### Scroll of Speak with Dead — Mechanics

This is a **ritual scroll**, not a normal spell scroll. It cannot be transcribed into a spellbook. Any creature can use it with 10 minutes of preparation.

**Use:** Single use. Casts *Speak with Dead* on one corpse. The corpse answers up to **5 questions**. It answers truthfully, but only with what it knew in life. It is not compelled to be helpful beyond answering the question asked.

**Best target:** Ossian's corpse. He is not hostile after death; his voice is quiet, precise, and formal.

**Good questions:** Who built the original facility? What is the Cloak for? Who is Balthazar? Why did you seal yourself in here? What does the vault beneath Islefield contain?
