# Room 7 — Notes & Documents

## Ossian's Journal (stone shelf beside the sarcophagus — no check to find, DC 12 History to read)

Personal journal over ~14 months. Key entries:

> *Day 1: "The site is correct. The surveys are not. The lower tunnels have been worked since the Tharnic seal — recently, carefully, by someone who knew how to follow the old survey marks... the Cloak reacts to this place. Not to me. To something in the stone that I cannot yet identify."*

> *Day 20: "First sentinel placed. The elf-wood took the binding correctly... it persists beyond what the binding should sustain on its own. Something here reinforces the work. I have not found the mechanism."*

> *Month 4: "I have found the origin of the Cloak... the Cloak was in the lower archive. The lower archive was sealed before the Tharnic colony existed. Someone left this here for someone who would eventually understand what they were looking at. I believe I am that person. I am not certain this is a good thing."*

> *Month 7: "I have placed what I found in the archive with the Eye, in the chamber beyond the south wall... follow the sealed door. You will need what Vasskren carries — all five pieces, in the correct slots."*

> *Final entry: "Everything I built here was built around something that was already built... The record is nearby. The Eye is beyond the south wall. Everything I found, everything I understood, is in the room you reach by placing Vasskren's pieces in the door. If you are reading this: you are standing beside it. Open the way."*

## Loose Sheet (inside the back cover — Investigation DC 13)

Ink fresher than the rest — written last:

> *"Gaia made three things here. Not golems — I made those. Not the guardian — I made him. Three things older than this facility... Records. They are not here. They have not been here for a very long time. The transfer document I found was Colony Year 31 — long before I arrived, before this place became the facility I inherited...*

> *Who Gaia is, and what the name turns into when you follow it forward into this age, I have written once — in the room that is the appropriate place for that information. I have not written it here. Only the worthy can know.."*
