# Room 7 — Ossian's Chamber

**Enemies:** Ossian (Mummy Lord) + 6 Wights — stat sheets in ../../Character_Sheets/Ossian.md and ../../Character_Sheets/Wights.md
**Exits:** Room 6 (front, sealed) | Eye Door from Room 6 (back passage) | Sealed door south → Room 17 (requires 5 Vasskren cores)

The cult leader's personal chamber. A stone sarcophagus in the centre — lid partly ajar, interior visible. Six worshippers arranged around it in poses of reverence, hands clasped, facing inward. All preserved — too well preserved. Rotting cloth only. No decomposition. The air here is cold and still.

Ossian himself lies in the sarcophagus — desiccated, wearing the cloak, face wrapped.

---

## CRITICAL TRIGGER — Do Not Touch

**Touching Ossian, a worshipper, or the Cloak activates the Mummy Lord encounter.**
*Mage Hand* is safe. Reaching in carefully is safe. Physical contact from a living creature triggers the fight.

**Worshippers as a warning:** If a player says "I touch/move/disturb one," describe the air getting heavier, as if waiting for something. Pause. Let them pull back.

---

## Reliquary (behind north tapestry — Perception DC 15)

A stone box, locked (Thieves' Tools DC 15 / DC 19 STR — or break it: AC 13, HP 15).
**Destroying the reliquary prevents Ossian's rejuvenation** — he is permanently dead.
If it survives: Ossian returns in 24 hours even if defeated.

---

## Sealed Door (south wall — toward Room 17)

Five slots corresponding to Vasskren's cores. Place all 5 cores in the correct slots → the door opens.

See ../../Character_Sheets/Vasskren.md for which core is which. DM fallback: Ossian's ceremonial iron key can substitute — not hinted to players.

---

## On Ossian's Defeat

All active Wights crumble. The Eye Door swings open. About 1 minute later (if not already triggered), the Grand Hall responds and **Vasskren** begins to rise.
