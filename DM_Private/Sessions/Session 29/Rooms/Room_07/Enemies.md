# Room 7 — Enemies

*(Only if triggered — physical contact with Ossian, a worshipper, or the Cloak.)*

## Ossian — Mummy Lord / Necromancer

**CR:** 11 | **HP:** 135 | **Full stats:** ../../Character_Sheets/Ossian.md

Mummy Lord with Avethis-themed lair actions and 6 Wights. Rejuvenates in 24 hours unless the reliquary/heart behind the north tapestry is destroyed.

---

## 6 × Wight

**CR:** 3 | **HP:** 45 each | **AC:** 14 | **Vulnerability:** radiant/holy | **Full stats:** ../../Character_Sheets/Wights.md

Animate simultaneously when Ossian activates. Each has Life Drain on hit, reducing max HP until long rest.

If Ossian is defeated, all Wights still active crumble immediately (no save).

---

## Encounter Does NOT Trigger If

- Party uses *Mage Hand* to take items.
- Party observes but does not touch anything.
- Party takes the Cloak using a tool, cloth, or object to avoid direct contact.
