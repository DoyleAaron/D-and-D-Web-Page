# Room 17 — Notes & Documents

## Ossian's Research Codex (stone alcove, wrapped in oilskin — no check)

DC 12 History for Common sections. Old Elvish sections require a Language check or *Comprehend Languages*.

---

> *"Entry 4 — What was here first. The inscription on the dais in the Grand Hall predates this facility by centuries... The inscription reads: 'a witness was needed.'"*

> *"Entry 7 — The maker. The old texts give them a name: Gaia... They are the earth. The literal earth... They came here — made themself small enough to come here — because they wanted to make something."*

> *"Entry 9 — What they made. Three powers... The Chart of the Threefold Way is a guide, a star-coded wayfinder made to locate the place where the powers were hidden. The true works were three: a ring, a crown, and an amulet. They were made here. They are no longer here."*

> *"Entry 10 — The first constraint. I thought a maker such as Gaia could simply remember what they had made and where they had placed it. I was wrong. Creating power on this scale required conditions even they did not fully anticipate. The works could not remain safe if their maker could point to them. So the act of hiding them became part of the making: once they were placed, even Gaia had to lose the exact knowledge of where they rested. They could arrange signs. They could make paths. They could leave a Chart. But they could not tell anyone the answer directly, because the answer was no longer allowed to exist inside them."*

> *"Entry 11 — The Eye. Gaia made it here, in this room, as a companion — something to watch while they worked... My followers call it Avethis... they are worshipping a thing made out of loneliness... The Eye is — functionally, for anyone who encounters it — a power that does not have a smaller word than deity."*

> *"Entry 14 — The part I swore I would not write. Gaia did not die... They are still here. Still acting. Still making arrangements... And they wear a name now. A present name...*
>
> *Gaia is the one they call Balthazar.*
>
> *Balthazar is not a man who became powerful. Balthazar is what the soul of the world is calling itself in this age... I have written this nowhere else. If you are holding the Codex, you are holding the only place the two names sit on the same page."*

> *"Final entry. I am going to stay here... The Eye has already seen you. It sees everything. Welcome to the record."*

## Slip of Paper (tucked between the last pages)

Ossian's hand, smaller and more cramped — the storage-location clue:

> *"The Eye gave me one vision. Not a dream. Not a metaphor. A sight. The same kind of sight it gives to those it chooses when it wants a truth carried forward.*
>
> *I saw where Gaia placed the Three. I saw the vault from the inside: the ring, the crown, and the amulet held apart from one another, each sealed in its own cradle. I saw the walls around them — old stone, root-veined, damp with the mineral sheen I know from Islefield's underground works. I have walked those walls before. I know their cut. I know their silence.*
>
> *The records of Gaia are in Islefield. Under it, to be precise in a sealed lower archive. I do not know if anyone there knows what they are. I only know what the Eye showed me."*
