# Room 17 — Loot

## Stone Alcoves (no check — offerings in plain sight)

- **400 gp total** in pristine Tharnic gold (zero oxidation — perfectly sealed since Ossian).

## Ioun Stone of Awareness

Pale blue stone, floating at rest in one alcove.

### Ioun Stone of Awareness — Mechanics

**Type:** Wondrous item, rare  
**Attunement:** Required  
**Appearance:** Pale blue stone that orbits the attuned creature's head at shoulder height.

**Properties:**
- The wearer **cannot be surprised** while conscious.
- The wearer gains **+5 to passive Perception**.
- The stone orbits visibly unless hidden by magic or heavy clothing/cover.

## Ossian's Research Codex

Wrapped in oilskin. The most important lore item in the dungeon — the Gaia material, the Three/Trinity, the origin of the Eye, and (final entry only) the reveal that **Gaia is Balthazar**. Full text in Notes.md.

### Ossian's Research Codex — Mechanics

**Type:** Lore item / wondrous item  
**Appearance:** Complete illuminated manuscript in Common and Old Elvish. Ossian's private life's work.

**Contents:**
- The Eye/Avethis was made by Gaia as a companion and witness.
- Gaia made the Three here: the Ring, the Crown, and the Amulet. The Chart of the Threefold Way is not one of the Three; it is the star-coded guide that can lead someone toward where the Three were hidden.
- The Three were removed in the early Tharnic colonial period, long before Ossian arrived, and are now stored beneath Islefield's old administrative quarter.
- The final entry reveals that **Gaia is Balthazar**.

**Study Benefit:** A character who studies the Codex for **8 hours** gains permanent advantage on History checks related to Avethis, the Tharnic Empire, Gaia/Balthazar, the Three, or Islefield's founding.

**DM Note:** This is the most important lore item in the dungeon. Do not rush the full implications at the table; let the party carry it forward.

## The Sight of Avethis (boon, not an item)

Received by every creature in the room on entry after spending 1+ minute in the chamber.

### The Sight of Avethis — Boon Mechanics

Each creature receives this once:

*Once — and only once — you may choose to see into any point in time. Past or future. Any moment, any place. You see it as a witness: clearly, completely, without intervention. You cannot interact with what you see. The vision lasts as long as it needs to. When it ends, the ability is gone.*

No ritual is needed. When the character is ready, they close their eyes and see. Handle privately if needed. Show the truth, but if the question would break the campaign, show a true but incomplete slice.
