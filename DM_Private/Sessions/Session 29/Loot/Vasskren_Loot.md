# Vasskren — Loot

## Door Cores (one per part — needed to open Room 17)

| Part | Shard |
|------|-------|
| Left Claw | Shard of Rending (#1 — red bone, warm) |
| Right Maul | Shard of Breaking (#2 — iron-grey, heavy) |
| Spectral Arm | Shard of Withering (#3 — black bone, frost) |
| Torso | Shard of Vision (#4 — pale white core set behind the sternum, carved eye) |
| Skull | Shard of Witnessing (#5 — violet gem, humming) |

All five shards must be placed into the five indentations in Room 7's sealed door to open Room 17.

### Door Core Mechanics

The shards are magical keys, not combat items. Each one is warm/cold/weighted according to its nature and faintly pulls toward the sealed door in Room 7.

- **Shard of Rending:** red-tinged bone fragment, warm to the touch.
- **Shard of Breaking:** iron-grey bone, unnaturally heavy.
- **Shard of Withering:** black bone fragment, leaves frost on fingers.
- **Shard of Vision:** pale white core from behind the sternum, perfectly smooth, carved with a tiny eye.
- **Shard of Witnessing:** violet gem from the skull's forehead, warm and faintly humming.

**Use:** Place all five into the matching indentations in the sealed door. When all are placed correctly, the door opens. They are not consumed by the opening unless you want them to be.

---

## Torso (after death)

- 4 × large bone segments — crafting material, **50 gp each**.
- **Belt of Giant Strength (Storm)** — found fused into the spine.

### Belt of Giant Strength (Storm) — Mechanics

**Type:** Wondrous item, legendary  
**Attunement:** Required  
**Effect:** While wearing the belt, your **Strength score becomes 29**. It has no effect if your Strength is already 29 or higher.

**Appearance:** Massive belt of dark leather, bone plates, and storm-grey metal. It hums faintly when the wearer grips a weapon or braces to lift something heavy.

---

## Skull (after death)

- The gem in the skull is the **Shard of Witnessing (#5)** listed above. It is one of the five door cores, not a separate loot item.
