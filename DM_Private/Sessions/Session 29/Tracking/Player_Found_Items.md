## Magical Items and Consumables

- **2 Potions of Healing** - each restores **2d4+2 HP**.

- **Potion of Greater Healing** - Drinking it restores **4d4+4 HP**.

- **Potion of Greater Healing** - second one from Room 15, restores **4d4+4 HP**.

- **+1 Dagger** - Magical weapon. Gives **+1 to attack rolls** and **+1 to damage rolls**. Damage is **necrotic** instead of normal piercing damage.

- **Ring of Feather Falling** - Magical ring. When the wearer falls, they descend slowly and take no falling damage, as the *Feather Fall* spell.

- **Ring of Warmth** - Requires attunement. Grants resistance to cold damage and comfortable survival in cold environments down to extreme cold.

- **Psionic Shard** - Magical crafting component. Can be used toward creating a **Headband of Intellect** or similar mind/intellect-based magic item. No immediate combat effect by itself unless crafted into something.

- **Bead of Force** - Magical, single use. As an action, throw it up to 60 ft. On impact, it explodes in a 10 ft radius. Creatures in the area make a **DC 15 DEX save**. On a failed save, they take **5d4 force damage** and are trapped inside a sphere of force for **1 minute**. The sphere blocks damage from outside.

- **Scroll of Detect Magic** - Single use. Casts **Detect Magic**. For up to **10 minutes** with concentration, you sense magic within 30 ft and can see magical auras around visible objects or creatures.

- **Scroll of Identify** - Single use. Casts **Identify**. Touch one magic item or magically affected creature/object. You learn its properties, how to use it, whether it requires attunement, and whether spells are affecting it.

- **Scroll of Comprehend Languages** - Single use. Casts **Comprehend Languages**. For **1 hour**, you understand the literal meaning of spoken languages you hear and written languages you touch. It does not let you speak those languages.

- **Dust of Dryness** - Magical consumable. Contains **1d6+4 pinches**. One pinch can absorb up to **15 gallons of water** into a small pellet. The pellet can later be thrown up to 60 ft to release the water. Against a watery creature, one pinch forces a **DC 11 CON save**; on a failure, the creature takes **10d6 necrotic damage** and is paralysed for 1 minute.

- **Counterspell Chestplate / Counterspell Plate** - If removed intact from Room 3, it can be worn as a rare wondrous item requiring attunement by a spellcaster. While attuned and worn, it gives **+1 AC** and lets the wearer cast *Counterspell* **1/day** at 3rd level as a reaction without a spell slot. Recharges at dawn.

## Lore Items and Documents

- **Jade eye stamp** - Ossian's personal seal stamp. It is not a weapon and does not have a known magical effect. It can be used to mark wax or documents with Ossian's open-eye symbol. It may matter as proof of authorship, cult connection, or collector/lore evidence.

- **Ossian's Cipher Journal** - 30 pages in Ossian's personal cipher, found in Room 3 inside a sealed fireproof case. The party has now decrypted it. Full decoded text below.

**First page (plain Common, uncoded):**

> *"If you are reading this in cipher, you are doing it wrong. If you are reading this clearly, you earned it. Everything I could not write in the other document is written here. I am sorry about approximately a third of it."*

**Decoded entries:**

> *"The Islefield surveyors who prepared the lower tunnels were not Tharnic subjects. I found their commission records in the collapsed Sector 2 archive. Paid through a Tharnic proxy. The originating account traces back to an Islefield 'Colonial Liaison Office' that does not exist in any current records - dissolved, I believe deliberately, shortly after Colony Year 31.*
>
> *Colony Year 31. I arrived long after that — after the Tharnic Empire had already turned this place into a minor facility and forgotten why the lower eastern wing mattered. Someone in Islefield was preparing this site before the Tharnic version of it existed. They did not know me. They knew something was coming and wanted the right sort of mind positioned to receive it."*

> *"The lower eastern wing was already sealed when I arrived. I had assumed I sealed it. I did not - I broke an existing seal and re-applied my own. The original seal was not Tharnic, not any method I have encountered in thirty years of practice. Much older. Applied by someone who understood what they were sealing in, and chose to seal it in rather than remove it."*

> *"Gaia. I am going to write the old name here because this is the document I will burn if I change my mind, and I have not changed my mind.*
>
> *Gaia. That is the ancient name, and the ancient name is safe - it is in a hundred dead manuscripts and frightens no one. He is not a historical figure. He is not a deity in any tradition I have studied. He is the being that the earth is when you ask it what it is. He was here. In this room. In the sealed chamber beside Ossian's room. He built in this place and he left what he built.*
>
> *What the old name connects to now - what it is called in this age, and who has been taking its instructions without knowing it - I will not write here. I have written it once, with the Eye, in the sealed chamber beside Ossian's room. That is the only place it belongs. If you want it, open the way and earn it.*
>
> *The Islefield surveying party was working for someone who knew about him. Or for someone who worked for someone who knew. The chain is long and I cannot follow it further back than the second link."*

> *"The Cloak is not mine. I found it. I have documented what I found in the appropriate document in the sealed chamber beside Ossian's room. What I have not documented anywhere else: the Cloak was sealed in this archive by the same hand that sealed the archive itself. Old seal, unknown method. Same person who sealed what waits beyond the south wall.*
>
> *Someone sealed a witnessing device in here and walked away. I have thought about why for eleven months. The most honest answer I can produce: they sealed it here so it would be found by the right person at the right time. I do not know who the right person is. I do not know if it is me. I do not know if it is whoever reads this."*

- **Room 5 letters / Islefield payment receipt** - links Islefield money and old administrative influence to the site.

- **Room 11 guard's log / letters** - mundane guard records with hints of worker fear and dungeon decline.

- **Room 12 mystery note** - points toward the drainage / lower-door thread.

- **Room 14 table document** - crumbling letter in Ossian's hand:

> *"Note, for a record I will not be here to maintain:*
>
> *The vault is prepared. Four sentinels. One guardian. One door. The Eye watches everything in these walls - it always has, but now it watches something I have placed here specifically.*
>
> *Anyone who reaches the inner chamber will be marked. Not by me. I have no mark to give. By the Eye itself - which sees, and in seeing, changes what it has seen. I have been precise with myself about whether this is a curse. It is not a curse. It is a record. They will carry the fact of having been witnessed.*
>
> *They will never be deceived again. Not by others. Not by themselves. I am not certain this is comfortable. It is accurate."*

- **Room 16 production tablet** - tablet on the east furnace wall:

> *"Production output Month 7: 340 units iron ore. 12 units silver ore. Workers: 28. Deaths this month: 3. Overseer Brace notes workers are reporting sounds from the lower eastern wing. Investigation pending."*

- **Room 16 egress scratch mark** - three lines and a westward arrow scratched into the southwest wall. It appears to be a Tharnic emergency egress mark pointing toward a hidden exit route.
