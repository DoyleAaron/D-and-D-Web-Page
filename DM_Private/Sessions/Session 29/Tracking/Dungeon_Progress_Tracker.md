## Planned Session 29 Flow

This session is expected to run in three beats:

1. **The Eye Golem fight (Room 4).** The last of the four sentinels. Party has the Eye Golem key. On defeat they can also claim the Tome of Avethis, 220 gp, and the Shard of Discernment core.
2. **The necromancer fight (Ossian).** The main boss encounter. On his defeat, all active undead in the dungeon crumble and the Eye Door reopens.
3. **The Vasskren decision.** Whether or not the party fights Vasskren. His five cores (skull, torso, maul arm, left claw arm, spectral arm) are what open the sealed door to Room 17. If they skip the fight, the DM-only ceremonial iron key from Ossian is the fallback way to open Room 17.

### DM Reminders

- Let the party decide whether to backtrack to **Room 9** before pushing on; the loot there is minor.
- **Room 17** only opens after the five Vasskren cores are placed correctly (N, S, E, W, centre), or via the ceremonial iron key fallback.
- Keep the Gaia = Balthazar reveal reserved for **Room 17**, not Room 7.
