# Lavalto Peak — Journey Prep

**When:** Travel window is approximately **April 10–22** (10-12 days before the April 23 summit)  
**From:** Dawerton (or wherever the party is based by then)  
**To:** Lavalto Peak — the active volcanic island/peninsula where the summit takes place  
**Context:** Maltrius has granted the party **unimpeded passage** through vampire territory along the route. No vampire ambushes. The undead simply do not engage. This will feel strange — wrong — to the party if they've been expecting trouble.

---

## Route Overview

```
Dawerton → [Coastal Road South] → Resross → [Heights Track] → Ironwarf Pass → [Volcanic Foothills] → Lavalto Road → Summit Gate
```

**Approximate distances and travel time (on foot / horse):**

| Leg | Distance | Time (foot) | Time (horse) | Notes |
|-----|----------|-------------|--------------|-------|
| Dawerton → Resross | ~30 miles | 2 days | 1 day | Coastal road, maintained |
| Resross → Ironwarf Pass | ~18 miles | 1.5 days | 1 day | Heights track, rougher |
| Ironwarf Pass → Volcanic Foothills | ~22 miles | 2 days | 1.5 days | Unmaintained, narrow paths |
| Volcanic Foothills → Lavalto Gate | ~12 miles | 1 day | — (too steep for horses) | Steep, ash-covered, hot |
| **Total** | **~82 miles** | **~6–7 days** | **~4–5 days (part of route)** | |

---

## The Maltrius Passage — How It Feels

> *The road south of Resross should be dangerous at night. Everyone who travels these paths knows that. But nothing comes. The party passes through a ravine where something watches from the dark — shapes on the upper ridges, still. Not attacking. Just watching. And then the shapes are gone.*

**Signs the party notices:**
- Fresh claw marks on trees along the road — recent, at head height
- An abandoned campsite they pass on day 2: two dead horses, untouched by scavengers, drained of blood. The riders are nowhere
- On night 3, the watch-keeper sees a pale figure standing at the treeline — humanoid, very still, watching the camp. When they blink it's gone. This happens once per night for the rest of the journey
- None of them attack. This is Maltrius's passage in effect

**Morjor/Amdir note:** Amdir knows what this means, if he has shared anything with the party. The vampires aren't ignoring them randomly.

### Leg 4 — Volcanic Foothills to Lavalto Gate (On Foot Only)

*No roll needed — guaranteed encounters on this leg. It's the final approach.*

**Encounter A — The Ash Road (2 miles out):**
The path narrows to a single file track along a ridge. Loose volcanic rock. Sulphur smell. Visibility drops.
> *The rock here is black and glassy — obsidian, not stone. It's warm underfoot through boot leather.*

Hazard: the path is **treacherous** — DC 12 Athletics or Acrobatics each 30 minutes of travel or lose footing (10 ft fall, 1d6 bludgeoning, DC 13 CON save or stunned 1 round from the impact).

**Encounter B — The Kluimont Forward Watch (1 mile out):**
Three Kluimont soldiers in volcanic-ash-grey armour. They are posted here specifically to watch who approaches the summit.
- They are not hostile — the summit is meant to be diplomatic
- But they are thorough (DC 14 Persuasion to get through without full identity disclosure)
- **Amdir's presence is notable.** A banished Islefield heir at a joint summit is a diplomatic incident waiting to happen. The soldiers note him and one of them leaves immediately — presumably to report

**Encounter C — The Lava Field Crossing (30 minutes from gate):**
A cooled lava field. It holds. Mostly. Random plate sections are soft — the crust is thin.
- DC 13 Perception per 10 ft of movement to spot soft sections
- Failure: fall through 2 ft of crust into lava-heated mud — 4d6 fire damage, DC 15 STR save to pull free or 2d6 more per round

**Encounter D — Lavalto Gate (arrival):**
A large carved stone arch — old, pre-summit. The Lavalto Peak manor complex is visible beyond. Guards from all three kingdoms are already present (Braewood, Islefield, Kluimont). The diplomats have been arriving for two days. The party is late by summit-organiser standards. Someone from Braewood's household meets them at the gate — relief visible on their face.

## The Vampire Corridor — DM Note

Along the entire journey, **do not put vampires on the encounter table**. If the party explicitly searches at night and succeeds on a DC 17 Perception, they see the figures watching at the treeline. If pressed, they disappear. They are real. They are numerous. They are honouring Maltrius's passage guarantee.

The significance: **Amdir knows Maltrius gave this passage.** He received the Sending spell. He knows they're travelling unmolested because Maltrius allowed it. Whether he tells the party why is up to his player.

## Liora's Door — Position Note

*No prep needed yet.*

The hidden door is in the volcanic foothills section, set into a vertical cliff face approximately 1 mile south-east of the Ironwarf Pass exit and 4 miles west of the Lavalto Gate. The cliff is non-descript — no markings, no path leading to it, no worn ground. The party passes within 200 ft of it if they take the main route.

**Morjor touching the cliff face at that point** causes the door to open silently. No arcana check needed. No sensation of magic. The door simply opens. What's inside is Liora's prepared dungeon — content pending.
