Counting rules

1 pp = 10 gp = 20 ep = 100 sp = 1,000 cp. Every complete 16 cp in the full deposit plus previous carry gives 1 XP to each character. XP is not divided by party size. Remaining copper carries forward. Crew payout uses only the new deposit and is rounded down to whole copper; it never reduces the XP calculation.
